Document templates get a screen, and the payslip PDF renders through them

The store, the safe renderer and the Blade importer have been finished for a while, but
nothing rendered through any of it - so importing iLearn's payslip changed nothing anyone
could see. This is the front door and the last connection.

Settings > Document templates lists what exists, with preview, edit, activate and delete.
Import takes a pasted Blade file or plain HTML, translates it, and shows exactly what could
not be carried over rather than failing silently or - much worse - passing it through to
something that executes. The editor lists the fields that document kind provides and inserts
them at the cursor, because guessing a field name is how a template ends up rendering blank.

PayrollDocumentController now renders an active template when the agency has one. The
built-in layout stays as the fallback in two senses: an agency that never opens this screen
keeps getting a working payslip, and a template that throws falls back rather than handing
somebody an error page in place of their pay.

Values are formatted before they reach the template - money, dates, periods - because the
renderer deliberately performs no arithmetic and no formatting. That restraint is what keeps
it unable to execute anything, and it is the reason named totals like regular_amount and
gross_with_vacation exist.

The screen follows the house table pattern established by the earlier sweep: data-kt-pretty,
a plain table inside .kt-card, no inline widths.

Verified end to end: a payslip PDF was 20,813 bytes on the built-in layout and 21,288 through
the imported iLearn template, with the test template removed afterwards so nothing was left
activated.
