Room assignments: a screen for the table nobody could fill in

educator_rooms decides who sees which children on several screens, and it held ONE row for
the entire platform. Everything keyed on it silently returned nothing, which is why the
educator daily summary had to fall back to the centre and why the scorecard reports
sign-outs per centre rather than per person. It was a data job nobody could do, because
there was no screen for it.

Everyone on one page, saved in one go. Each educator is offered only the rooms at their own
centre - offering every room in the agency is how somebody ends up attached to a room three
sites away by a mis-click - and a banner says how many still have none.

Saving REPLACES a person's assignments rather than adding to them, because the screen shows
the complete set and what it submits is therefore the complete answer; adding would leave a
room silently attached after somebody unticked it. Only the people actually changed are
sent, so untouched rows keep who assigned them and when. Saving the same thing twice is a
no-op rather than a delete-and-reinsert.

Verified end to end, including the case that matters: assigning works, repeating changes
nothing, and a room belonging to another agency is refused rather than stored.
