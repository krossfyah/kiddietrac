Payroll documents become a record instead of a calculation

A payslip was computed on demand from time punches and never written down, so there was
no answer to "what did we issue this person, and when" - the one question a payroll
record exists to answer. Worse, a recomputed payslip silently changes when a punch is
later edited or a rate corrected, so last month's payslip stopped matching what the
person was actually paid.

payroll_documents stores the units, rate and gross AS ISSUED. Backfilled from the punch
and visit history KiddieTrac already holds: 62 documents, verified against the live
calculation with zero mismatches. Re-running the backfill is idempotent on external_key
and leaves anything already marked paid alone, so it stays safe to re-run as more
history arrives.

It appears in two places: a Documents subsection under Payroll for admins, split into
educators and other staff the same way the hours report is, with view and mark-paid; and
on the staff pay screen, where an educator sees their own issued documents beside the
rolling 12-week calculation. Both carry a PDF.

PayController's profile() and units() move to App\Support\Payroll and the controller now
delegates, so the stored ledger and the live payslip screen cannot drift into quoting
different numbers for the same week.

Isolation verified with two tokens: a single-agency admin forging X-Active-Agency-Id at
another agency sees only their own documents, and a foreign PDF 404s. The self-view is
pinned to the caller's user id and cannot be widened by a parameter.
