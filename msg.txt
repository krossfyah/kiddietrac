Payroll sits under Accounting, and reads as two payrolls

The payroll summary carried no role, so it could not be separated into educators and
everyone else - which is the first cut anybody makes when reading it. The API now
resolves each person's role (lowest rank wins, so somebody who is both an educator and
a director reads as a director) and the screen renders a section per group, each with
the subtotal that actually gets copied out. The grand total stays underneath so nothing
appears to have moved.

Payroll also moves out of Staff and sits beneath Accounting in the sidebar, where the
rest of the money lives.
