Overtime travels from iLearn to the payslip

iLearn's payslip has always carried an overtime line - hours, a multiplier, the resulting
amount - and the template imported from it refers to those fields. Nothing carried them
across, so that row rendered blank whatever the pay period held.

Worth stating plainly: none of the 64 pay periods currently record overtime, and only seven
record hours at all - the rest are flat amounts. So this changes nothing you can see today.
It is wired anyway because the alternative is silently dropping the first overtime shift
somebody enters and noticing a pay run later.

Nullable rather than zero-defaulted throughout. "No overtime recorded" and "zero overtime
hours" are different statements, and a payslip should not assert the second one; a template
using {{#if ot_hours}} then hides the row instead of printing "0.00 hours at x0".

Verified by setting overtime on one document, rendering the PDF through the template, and
putting the document back: 21,765 bytes rendered, row restored, totals unchanged at
$121,321.86 across 68 documents.
