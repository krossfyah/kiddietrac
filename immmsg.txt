Immunisation report: what each child has, and what is due

The digest already flagged children with NOTHING on file, which is the crudest form of the
question. This is the whole picture per child: doses held, the last one given, what the
agency schedule says should have been given by now, what is coming within three months, the
next recorded due date, and any exemption.

Due is worked out from the child's AGE against the agency schedule rather than a calendar
date, because that is how the schedule is written (due_at_age_months) and it is the only way
the answer stays right for a child who joined last week. A recorded next_due_on always wins
over anything inferred - somebody entered that from the real record. Rows are sorted worst
first, so a report read top-down starts with the children somebody has to act on.

A correctness bug worth naming, caught by checking the output against the underlying rows
rather than trusting the summary. The schedule and the records are written by different
people at different times: the schedule says "4th dose (booster)" where a record says "4th
dose", and "1 dose" where a record says "1st". Comparing the raw labels matched ONE of
fourteen schedule entries, so children were reported overdue for doses they had already
received - the worst possible failure in a report used to chase families or satisfy an
inspector.

Both sides are now reduced to vaccine plus dose NUMBER before comparing: bracketed
qualifiers dropped, the word "dose" dropped, ordinals to digits. Verified against a child
holding a 4th dose and an MMR: both correctly drop off her overdue list, while the first and
second doses she genuinely lacks remain.
