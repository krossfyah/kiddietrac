Tax fields line up

The row was a flex with align-items:flex-end and two columns of unequal height - only
"Called" carries a hint under its input. Bottom-aligning them put the two inputs 21px out
of line, and the % suffix sitting beside the rate input took width from that field alone,
leaving them 20px different in size.

It is a two-column grid aligned to the start now, so both cells begin on the same line and
the inputs land level. The % moved inside the rate field, where it cannot steal width, and
both columns carry a hint of reserved height so neither collapses and drags the panel out
of shape when the worked example below re-renders.

Measured after: both inputs at the same y, both 300px wide, both 28px tall.

The page title now says "Billing" to match the nav entry it was merged into.
