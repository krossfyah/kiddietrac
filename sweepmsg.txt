Date-only values stop rendering a day early across the portal

Measured in the browser rather than assumed: new Date("2026-08-11T00:00:00"),
new Date("2026-08-11") and the explicit-Z form ALL parse to UTC midnight in this engine, and
rendering any of them in America/Toronto gives "Aug 10". Every screen formatting a date-only
value that way has been showing it a day early to everyone west of UTC.

Fixed at seven display sites through one shared helper, KT.dayLabel, which formats from the
string's own parts. A value with no time in it denotes a calendar DAY and has nothing to
convert between timezones, so nothing is converted and nothing can shift:

  payslip periods (my pay)          home-visit dates, short and long forms
  HCC inspection form dates         payroll document periods
  sales follow-up dates             what's-new dates

screen-care.js was deliberately LEFT ALONE. It appends Z and also passes timeZone:"UTC", so
UTC midnight is rendered in UTC and the day is already right. It matches the pattern and is
not the bug - which is exactly why each site was read before being changed.

Arithmetic sites are also untouched. They parse and format consistently within themselves,
and rewriting date maths is a different job with different risks.

This is the third and fourth time this shipped today - closure ranges on the calendar, then
the closures list - so the rule is already in CONVENTIONS.md. The helper now makes following
it easier than not.
