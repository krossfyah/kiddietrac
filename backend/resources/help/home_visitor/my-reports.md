# Viewing your reports

**My reports** lists every home visit you've logged, newest first. Each card shows
the family or child, the centre and date, the visit type, your summary, and any
next steps you recorded.

## Tips
- Use **+ New report** at the top right to log another visit.
- Set a **Follow-up date** on a report so you remember to check back.
- Reports are saved to your agency's record — keep summaries factual and
  respectful, and note concerns clearly so the right people can follow up.
