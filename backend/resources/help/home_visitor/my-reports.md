# Viewing your reports

**My reports** lists every home visit report you have filed, newest first. Open it to check what you wrote, finish a
draft, download a report as a PDF, or see which families need a follow-up.

[[open: #home-visits | Open My reports]]

## Reading the list

1. Open **📋 My reports** from the menu, or press **My reports** on **Home**.
2. The page is titled **My home visits** and says how many reports it is showing.
3. Each card shows the family (or child) name, the centre and visit date, the visit type (for example **Routine visit**),
   your summary, and your **Next steps** in a grey box.
4. A yellow **Draft** label means the report is not submitted yet. **✎ Edited 2×** means a director or admin has
   amended it since you submitted it.

## Opening a report and downloading the PDF

1. On the report's card, press **⋮ > View report**.
2. The **Home Visit Report** opens with its status (**Draft**, **Submitted** or **Edited**), the details (home visitor,
   centre, family / child, who was present, location, duration, follow-up date) and the **Visit summary**,
   **Provider strengths**, **Concerns / observations** and **Next steps** sections.
3. Press **⬇ Download PDF** to save or print a copy.
4. Press **← Back** to return to the list.

> ⚠️ A report is part of the family's record at your agency. Keep a downloaded PDF somewhere private and delete it when
> you no longer need it.

## Finishing a draft

1. Find the card with the **Draft** label.
2. Press **⋮ > Edit draft**.
3. Complete the form. **Centre**, **Visit date** and **Visit summary** are needed to submit.
4. Press **Submit report**, or **💾 Save as draft** again if you are still not done.

## Filing another report

1. Press **+ New report** at the top right of the list.
2. Fill in the form and press **Submit report**. See [Filing a home visit report](filing-a-home-visit-report).

## Who can do what

- **Home visitor**: sees only their own reports; edits a report only while it is a **Draft**.
- **Centre director / agency admin**: see every home visitor's reports for their centres or agency on their own
  **Home visit reports** screen, can amend a submitted report (with a note saved to its **Activity log**) and download
  the PDF.
- Families do not see home visit reports in their portal.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? The list says "No reports yet". | You have not filed a report in this agency yet. Press **+ New report** to file your first one.
??? There is no Edit draft on my report. | Only drafts can be edited by you. Once a report is submitted, ask your director or agency admin to change it; their change is logged on the report.
??? I see "Could not load reports". | The list could not be fetched. Check your connection and refresh the page.
??? The PDF says "Download failed". | Try again in a moment. If it keeps failing, raise a ticket from Help with the family and visit date.
??? Why does my report say Edited? | A director or admin amended it after you submitted it. Open it to see the latest version.
??? Where do follow-ups show? | The **Follow-ups due** number on **Home** counts reports whose follow-up date is today or earlier.
