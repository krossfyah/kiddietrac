# Inspection forms

Two standardised inspection forms, digitised field for field from the paper originals:

- **Monthly Home Visitor Monitoring & Inspection Report**
- **Standard Home Visitor Checklist** — the quarterly inspection under O. Reg. 137/15

They are separate from a [home visit report](filing-a-home-visit-report). A visit report
is your account of a visit; these are the regulated monitoring forms your agency files.

## Finding them

**Home → the 🗂️ Inspection forms tile.** They are not in the sidebar.

The tile only appears if your agency has inspection forms switched on. If you do not see
it, your agency does not use them — nothing is wrong with your account.

## Filling one in

1. Open the tile and pick the form.
2. Work down it. The layout matches the paper form, section by section, so you can fill it
   in alongside a printed copy without hunting for anything.
3. Sign at the bottom.
4. **Submit.**

The form is built from one definition held on the server, which means the form on your
screen, the PDF that gets emailed and the answers that are stored are always the same
thing. There is no version of it that can drift.

On a phone, wide sections scroll sideways within the page rather than pushing the whole
screen out of shape.

## Drafts and your own submissions

Both live on the same screen, underneath the two form tiles:

- **Drafts** — a form you started and did not finish. Open it and carry on where you left
  off. Useful when an inspection is interrupted.
- **Your submitted forms** — everything you have filed, oldest to newest, searchable.

## What happens when you submit

- The completed form is turned into a **PDF and emailed to your agency's directors and
  admins**.
- A copy is stored, so the submission exists independently of anybody's inbox.
- Reviewers see every submission in a table and can open a read-only copy or download the
  PDF.

## Worth knowing

- **Fill it in on the day.** The form carries the date you enter, not the date you submit,
  but an inspection written up from memory a week later is worth less.
- If you are interrupted, leave it as a draft rather than submitting something incomplete.
  A draft is yours; a submission has already been emailed.
- Once submitted, a form is a filed record. If something is wrong, tell your director
  rather than filing a second one — a duplicate in the reviewers' table is worse than a
  correction.
