# Getting started as a Home Visitor

Welcome! As a **home visitor** you are attached to your **agency** (not a single
centre). You visit families and log a report for each visit, choosing whichever
centre in your agency the family belongs to.

## Your home screen
Your launcher has everything you need:

- **New visit report** — log a home visit.
- **My reports** — review the visits you've logged.
- **Notifications** — your alerts.
- **Settings** — your account and sign-in.
- **Help** — these guides.

## What you can and can't see
You only see the tools for your role. Home visitors do **not** see other roles'
screens (classroom rosters, billing, staff scheduling, etc.) — just home visits
and your own account. If something looks missing, it's simply not part of the
home-visitor role.
