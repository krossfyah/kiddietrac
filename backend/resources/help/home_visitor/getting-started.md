# Getting started as a Home Visitor

As a **home visitor** you belong to your **agency**, not to one centre. You visit families and file a report for each
visit, choosing the centre in your agency that the family belongs to. Your **Home** screen is where you start: your
visit numbers at the top, then a tile for each thing you do.

[[open: #home | Open Home]]

## Reading your numbers

1. Open **🏠 Home** from the menu.
2. Under the **Home visits** banner, read the row of numbers:
   - **Reports completed**: every report you have filed;
   - **Follow-ups due**: reports whose follow-up date is today or earlier;
   - **Visits this month**: visits dated this month;
   - **Open tasks**: tasks assigned to you;
   - **This week's pay**: the gross amount on your latest payslip.
3. Press a number to open the screen behind it (**My reports**, **My tasks** or **My pay**).

> ℹ️ On a phone the numbers sit in one compact row with short names (Reports, Follow-ups, Month, Tasks, Pay).

## Filing your first visit report

1. On **Home**, under **Quick access**, press **📝 New visit**.
2. Choose the **Centre** and the **Visit date**, write the **Visit summary**, and fill in anything else you know.
3. Press **Submit report** (or **💾 Save as draft** to finish later).
4. You land on **My reports**, where your new report is at the top.

[[show: #home @ New visit | Show me]]

The full form is explained in [Filing a home visit report](filing-a-home-visit-report).

## Finding your way around

1. Use the tiles under **Quick access** on **Home**: **New visit**, **Play Spark**, **My reports**, **My tasks**,
   **Report a hazard**, **Inspection forms**, **Forms to sign**, **Message Centre**, **Documents**, **My pay**,
   **Time off**, **News**, **Inbox**, **Settings** and **Help**.
2. On a computer, the menu on the left has the same screens plus **My documents**, **My HR**, **My training**,
   **My certificates**, **My points**, **Policies to review**, **Household residents**, **Files**, **Walkie-talkie**
   and **Notes**.
3. Press the **?** in the top-right corner of any screen's banner to open the guide for that screen.

## Who can do what

- **Home visitor**: files and reads their own visit reports, and can edit a report only while it is a **Draft**.
- **Centre director / agency admin**: read every report for their centres or agency, amend submitted reports (each
  change is logged) and get an email when a report is submitted, if the agency has turned that alert on.
- Home visitors do **not** see classroom rosters, billing, staff scheduling or other families' records. If a screen
  you expect is missing, it is not part of the home-visitor role or your agency has not turned it on.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? My numbers all show 0. | You have not filed a report yet, or the numbers could not load. Refresh the page. If they stay at 0 after you have filed reports, raise a ticket from Help.
??? This week's pay shows $0. | Your agency has not issued you a payslip yet, or does not run payroll in KiddieTrac.
??? The Centre list in a new report is empty. | Your agency has no open centres you can file against, or the list could not load ("Could not load centres"). Refresh, then ask your agency admin.
??? A tile I see on a colleague's phone is missing. | Some screens depend on what your agency has turned on (for example Play Spark ideas made by AI need the AI Bundle, and My HR needs KiddieTrac HR).
??? Who is told when I submit a report? | The people your agency chose to receive home-visit alerts get an email with the family, date and summary. Drafts are not sent to anyone.
??? I'm signed in but see an older home screen. | Your app may be out of date. Close and reopen it, or refresh the browser page.
