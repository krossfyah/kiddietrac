# Message Centre

The **💬 Message Centre** is your private chat inside KiddieTrac. Use it to message the families you visit and the
colleagues your agency lets you reach: your director, the agency admins, and any group you are part of. Every message
is kept with the family's or staff member's record, so nothing lives only on your own phone.

[[open: #chat | Open the Message Centre]]

## Getting there

1. On a phone, tap **💬 Message Centre** in the bottom bar (Home · My reports · New visit · Message Centre · Inbox).
2. On a computer, open **Message Centre** from your Home page.
3. A red count on the button means you have unread messages.

## Reading your conversations

1. The **💬 Inbox** tab lists your conversations, newest first, with the number of unread messages.
2. Type in **🔍 Filter…** to find a family, a colleague or a group by name.
3. Tap a conversation to open it. Under your own messages you see **Sent — not read yet** until the other person
   opens it, then **Read** and the time.
4. Inside a conversation, use **🔍 Search messages…** to find a word.

## Starting a conversation

1. Press **+ New chat**. The **✉️ New message** window opens.
2. Under **To**, pick a family or a colleague. Each person shows their role (Admin, Director, Educator, Parent …).
3. Optionally fill **Subject (optional)**, for example "Visit moved to Thursday".
4. Write your **Message**, and add an emoji, a photo or a voice note if you like.
5. Press **Send message**. The conversation opens and moves to the top of your inbox.

To talk with several colleagues at once, press **👥 New group**, give the group a name, tick the people and press
**Create group**.

## Replying and reacting

1. Beside each message there is a small **＋ React** and a **↩ Reply**.
2. Press **React** to add an emoji to a message.
3. Press **Reply** to answer one message in particular. The message you are answering shows above your reply. On a
   phone you can also swipe the message to the right.

## Who you can message

Your agency admin chooses who home visitors can message, in the **Message Centre** rules. By default that is the
families at your centre (or in your rooms) and the admins and directors. If a family or colleague is missing from
**To**, ask your director. A conversation the rules no longer allow stays as history and shows
"This conversation is read-only."

## Privacy

- Messages are **sealed (encrypted) when they are stored**, and only people in the conversation can read them.
- Phone notifications and missed-message emails say **who** wrote, not **what** they wrote.
- For anything urgent about a child's safety, phone the family or your director as well. A message is not a call.

## Questions and problems

??? I can't find a family under To. | The Message Centre rules limit who home visitors can message. Ask your director or agency admin to check the rules or your centre.
??? "Could not send. Please try again." | The connection dropped. Your text is still in the box; press send again.
??? Why doesn't the notification say what was written? | For privacy, chat notices say who wrote, not what. Open the Message Centre to read it.
