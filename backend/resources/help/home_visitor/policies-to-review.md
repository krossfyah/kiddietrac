# Policies to review

Your centre's policies must be read before you work with children, and again whenever one changes. **📘 Policies to review** lists the policies you still need to sign, and keeps a copy of every one you have signed so you can read it again.

[[open: #my-policies | Open Policies to review]]

## Getting there

1. On a computer, open the menu on the left and go to **Compliance**, then **Policies to review**.
2. On a phone, tap the **Open Policies to review** button at the top of this guide, or the reminder that pops up on your first visit of the day.

## Signing a policy

1. Under **To sign**, find the policy. Its version and date are under the title; *required* means you must sign it.
2. Tap **📖 Read policy**. It opens in the document viewer.
3. Read it, then close the viewer. The note beside the button changes from **Open it before signing** to **Opened ✓**.
4. Tick **I have read and understand this policy**.
5. Type your full name in **Type your full name**.
6. Tap **✍️ Sign**. A green **Signed** message appears and the policy moves to **Signed**.

[[show: #my-policies @ 📖 Read policy | Show me]]

> ℹ️ The tick box and the name stay greyed out until you have opened the policy. Your signature is saved with the date and time.

## When a policy changes

When a policy is updated, it comes back to **To sign** with a yellow **Updated** note saying which version you signed before and what changed. Read it again and sign the new version the same way.

You get a notice in your 🔔 **Inbox** and an email when that happens, a reminder on your first visit of the day, and a weekly email on Mondays while anything is still waiting.

## Reading a policy you already signed

1. Scroll to **Signed**.
2. Tap **View** beside the policy.

When there is nothing left to sign you see ✅ **You are all caught up** — *Every policy that applies to you is signed.*

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Educator / home visitor | Read and sign the policies that apply to them |
| Centre director | Signs their own policies here, and sees who has signed on the **Policies** screen |
| Agency admin | Publishes policies and new versions, and sees who has signed |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? It says "Tick the box to confirm you have read and understand it." | Tick **I have read and understand this policy** before you tap **✍️ Sign**.
??? It says "Type your full name to sign." | Type your full first and last name (at least three letters), then sign.
??? The Sign button is greyed out. | Open the policy first with **📖 Read policy**.
??? The policy will not open. | Check your connection and try again. If it still fails, tell your director — the file may need to be uploaded again.
??? It says "Policy sign-offs are for staff working with children." | Your account is not set up as staff at a centre. Ask your director to check your role.
