# Policies to review

Your centre's policies must be read before you work with children, and again whenever one changes. Open **Policies to review** in your menu.

1. Press **Read policy**. It opens in the document viewer.
2. Close the viewer, tick **I have read and understand this policy**, and type your full name.
3. Press **Sign**.

When a policy is updated, it comes back to your list with a note saying what changed. You get a bell and an email when that happens, a reminder on your first visit of the day, and a weekly email on Mondays while anything is still waiting.

Everything you have signed is listed under **Signed**, where you can read it again.
