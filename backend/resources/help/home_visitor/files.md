# Files — sharing printables and papers with your families

**Files** (📎 in the menu) is where you send printables, activity sheets, handouts and notes to the families you
visit, and where you see what they send back — a finished worksheet, a signed note, a photo of a document. Open it
before a visit to share material, or when a family tells you they sent you something.

[[open: #files | Open Files]]

The screen has three tabs: **Received** (files families sent), **Sent** (files you sent) and **All with my families**
(every file exchanged with your families, whoever sent it).

## Sending a file to a family

1. Open **Files** and press **📤 Send files**.
2. Drop files into the box, or press **choose files**. You can send up to 10 at a time, 20 MB each: PDF, pictures,
   Word, Excel, PowerPoint or text.
3. Add a **Title (optional)**, for example "Bedtime routine chart", and a **Note (optional)**.
4. Under **Send to**, tick one or more families. Each family shows its children's first names.
5. Press **Send**. A green **Sent** message says how many families got it, and the screen moves to **Sent**.

> 💡 You can only send to the families of the children you visit — the same children you see in KiddieTrac.

## Opening a file a family sent you

1. Open the **Received** tab. It shows how many are new, for example **Received (2 new)**.
2. New files have a dot. Each row shows the file, who sent it and from which family, the date and the size.
3. Press **View** to open it inside KiddieTrac, or **Download** to save it to your device.

On a phone, the buttons are in the row's **⋮** menu.

## Checking what your families have received

1. Open **All with my families**.
2. The **From → To** column shows who sent each file and to which family — including files your director or a
   colleague sent to one of your families.
3. Press **View** to see exactly what the family received.

## Deleting a file

Only the person who sent a file can delete it. Find it in **Sent** and press **Delete**. It disappears for every
family it was sent to.

## Who is told

- A family gets a notification in their app when you send a file and, unless your agency switched it off, an email
  saying a file is waiting. The file is never attached to the email.
- When a family sends you a file, you get a notification and, usually, an email.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Home visitors and educators | Send to and receive from the families of the children in their care |
| Centre directors and agency admins | The same, plus **All files** for every family they look after |
| Parents | Send files to their child's provider and open what they were sent |

## Questions and problems

??? "No families to send to". | No children are linked to you yet. Ask your director to link you to the children you visit.
??? "Each file can be at most 20 MB — remove the large one." | One of the files is too big. Remove it (×), or shrink it before sending.
??? "Choose who to send to." | Tick at least one family under Send to.
??? "Choose at least one file." | Drop a file in the box or press choose files first.
??? A family can't find the file. | Ask them to open Files from their menu and look under Received. The email only says a file is waiting.
??? Can I take a file back? | Delete it from Sent. If the family already downloaded it, they keep their copy.
