# Filing a home visit report

Tap **New visit report** from your home screen. The form is grouped into three
short sections.


## Quick steps

1. From your home screen, tap **New visit report**.
2. Pick the **Centre** and the **Visit date** — these two are required.
3. Choose the visit type, location and duration if you know them.
4. Add the family and child, and who was present.
5. Write the **Visit summary** — the third and last required field.
6. Add strengths, concerns, next steps and a follow-up date where they apply.
7. Press **Submit report**. You land on **My reports** with a confirmation.

Only Centre, Visit date and Visit summary are required. Everything else is worth filling
in when you have it, but never a reason to delay filing.

## Visit details
- **Centre** *(required)* — the centre in your agency this family belongs to.
- **Visit date** *(required)* and **Visit type** (initial, routine, follow-up,
  assessment, or other).
- **Location** (family home, virtual/phone, community) and **Duration** in minutes.

## Family
- **Family name** and **Child name** (optional).
- **Who was present** — e.g. "Mother, child".

## Observations
- **Visit summary** *(required)* — what happened during the visit.
- **Strengths observed** and **Concerns**.
- **Next steps** and an optional **Follow-up date**.

When you're done, press **Submit report**. You'll get a confirmation and land on
**My reports**. Only the **Centre**, **Visit date** and **Visit summary** are
required — everything else is optional but helpful.
