# Filing a home visit report

Use **New visit report** after every home visit to record what happened, what went well, any concerns and what comes
next. Only three things are required (**Centre**, **Visit date** and **Visit summary**), and you can save a draft at
any point and finish it later.

[[open: #new-home-visit | Open New visit report]]

## Filling in the visit details

1. Open **📝 New visit report** from the menu, or press **New visit** on **Home**. The form is titled
   **New home visit report**.
2. Under **Visit details**, choose the **Centre** *(required)*: the centre in your agency this family belongs to.
3. Check the **Visit date** *(required)*. It starts on today.
4. Pick the **Visit type**: **Initial visit**, **Routine visit** (the default), **Follow-up**, **Assessment** or
   **Other**.
5. If you know them, choose the **Location** (**Family home**, **Virtual / phone**, **Community** or **Other**) and
   type the **Duration (minutes)**.

## Recording the family and the visit

1. Under **Family**, type the **Family name** (for example *The Lee family*) and, if it helps, the
   **Child name (optional)**.
2. In **Who was present**, list who took part (for example *Mother, child*).
3. Under **Observations**, write the **Visit summary** *(required)*: what happened during the visit, in plain, factual
   words.
4. Add **Strengths observed** and **Concerns** where you have them.
5. Write the **Next steps** and, if you need to check back, set a **Follow-up date (optional)**.

> 💡 A follow-up date puts the report in the **Follow-ups due** count on your **Home** screen once that day arrives.

## Submitting or saving a draft

1. Press **Submit report** when the report is complete. A green **Report submitted** message appears and you land on
   **My reports**.
2. Or press **💾 Save as draft** to keep an unfinished report. A **Draft saved** message appears ("Come back any time
   to finish it."). A draft does not need the required fields.
3. To finish a draft later, open **My reports** and press **⋮ > Edit draft** on its card.

> ⚠️ Write as if the family could read it: facts, what you saw and heard, and clear concerns. A submitted report is part
> of your agency's record and only a director or admin can change it afterwards.

## Who can do what

- **Home visitor**: files reports for any centre in their agency, and edits their own report only while it is a draft.
- **Centre director / agency admin**: can also file a report from this form, read every report on
  **Home visit reports**, and amend a submitted one. Each change asks for an optional note and is listed in the
  report's **Activity log**.
- When a report is **submitted** (not saved as a draft), the people your agency set to receive home-visit alerts get
  an email with the family, visit type, centre, date, follow-up date and summary.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? I see "Please choose a centre." | Pick a centre in the **Centre** list before pressing **Submit report**.
??? I see "Please set the visit date." or "Please add a visit summary." | Those fields are required to submit. Fill them in, or press **💾 Save as draft** to finish later.
??? The Centre list says "Could not load centres". | The list did not load. Check your connection and refresh the page. If it stays empty, ask your agency admin which centres you can file against.
??? I see "Could not save: ..." | The report was not saved. Your answers are still on the screen: check your connection and press the button again. If it keeps failing, copy your summary somewhere safe and raise a ticket.
??? Will I lose my work if the screen refreshes? | The form is protected while you type, so a background refresh waits for you. Saving a draft is the safest way to keep a long report.
??? I submitted with a mistake. | Ask your director or agency admin to amend it. Their change and note are logged on the report.
