# Reporting a hazard

You are often the first to see something unsafe in a provider's home or centre — a broken gate, a loose stair rail, a smell of gas, a spill that will not dry. Report it so the director can deal with it and, if needed, tell parents.

## Report it

1. Open **Report a hazard** from your home screen or menu.
2. Choose the **centre** it is at.
3. Say what the hazard is, where it is, and pick how serious it is (low, medium or high).
4. Check the date and time it was found.
5. Add what was already done (for example, "gate tied shut, children kept inside").
6. Press **Report hazard**.

[[open: #hazards | Report a hazard]]

The centre's directors and your agency admins get a notice and an email straight away. Underneath the form you can see the hazards still open and what has been done about them.

## When parents are told

If the director sends parents a notice about a hazard, or tells them it is resolved, you get a copy too — a notice in the app and an email marked **Copy**. It is exactly what parents read.

> **Tip:** Make sure children are safe first, then report. For an injury, the provider or director should also file an incident report.
