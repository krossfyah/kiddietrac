# Reporting a hazard

You are often the first to see something unsafe in a provider's home or centre — a broken gate, a spill that will not dry, a loose board, a smell of gas — so report it on **🚧 Report a hazard** so your director can deal with it and, if needed, tell parents. Your director is told straight away.

[[open: #hazards | Open Report a hazard]]

## Getting there

1. On a phone, tap **Home** in the bottom bar (Home · My reports · New visit · Message Centre · Inbox).
2. Tap the **🚧 Report a hazard** tile.
3. On a computer, open **Hazards** from the menu on the left, under **Operations**.

## Reporting a hazard

> ⚠️ Keep children away from the hazard first. Report it as soon as they are safe.

1. In **Centre**, check the centre it is at.
2. In **Hazard**, say what it is in a few words (for example, "Broken fence board").
3. In **Where**, say the room or area.
4. Pick the **Severity**: low, medium or high.
5. Check **Identified** — the date and time it was found (it starts at now).
6. In **What is it**, describe what you saw.
7. In **Steps taken**, say what you have already done (for example, "taped off the area, children moved inside").
8. Under **Photo (optional)**, take a photo. On a phone this opens the camera.
9. Tap **Report hazard**. A green **Hazard reported** message says your director has been told.

[[show: #hazards @ Report hazard | Show me]]

## Seeing what happened next

Under the form, **Open hazards at your centre** lists the hazards still open, with their severity, where and when they were found, and the **Steps taken** so far. Tap **📷 Photo** to see a photo. When everything is dealt with, it says *Nothing open. Thank you for keeping an eye out.*

## When parents are told

If your director sends parents a notice about a hazard, or tells them it is resolved, you get a copy too — a notice in the app and an email marked **Copy**. It is exactly what parents read, so you know what to say if a provider or parent asks. Parents are never texted about hazards.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Home visitor | Report a hazard and see the open hazards at the centres they visit |
| Centre director | See every hazard at their centres, edit it, record the steps, **Mark resolved**, and send the parent notice |
| Agency admin | The same for every centre |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? It says "Say what the hazard is." | The **Hazard** box is empty. Type a few words and tap **Report hazard** again.
??? Someone was hurt. | Report the hazard, and ask the provider or director to file an incident report for the injury.
??? Is it urgent, like a gas smell? | Get everyone out and call for help first (911 or the gas company). Report it in KiddieTrac once everyone is safe.
??? The photo will not upload. | Check your connection and try a smaller photo. You can send the report without a photo and show your director in person.
??? I reported it but it's still not fixed. | Your director sees it under **Open** until it is resolved. Ask them in person or in the **Message Centre** if it is still unsafe.
