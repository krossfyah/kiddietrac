# My tasks

Admins and directors can assign you tasks. They appear on your home screen under the **My tasks** tile (with a live open-count badge) and in an "open tasks" card at the top when something is outstanding.

Open **My tasks** to see everything assigned to you — with its priority and due date. Use **Start** and **Mark done** as you work through them. Completed tasks drop off your open count.


## Quick steps

1. Open **My tasks** from your home screen — the tile carries a badge with how many are
   still open.
2. Read the priority and due date on each.
3. Press **Start** when you pick one up.
4. Press **Mark done** when it is finished. It drops off your open count straight away.

Anything outstanding also shows in an "open tasks" card at the top of your home screen,
so you do not have to go looking.
