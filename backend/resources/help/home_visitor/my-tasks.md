# My tasks

Your director or agency admin can assign you tasks — things like "update the allergy board" or "restock the art supplies". **My tasks** keeps them in one place so nothing slips through, and lets you show your team what you are working on.

[[open: #my-tasks | Open My tasks]]

## Getting there

1. On a phone, tap **Home** in the bottom bar (Home · My reports · New visit · Message Centre · Inbox).
2. Tap the **My tasks** tile. When you have open work, the tile shows a red count and an open-tasks card appears near the top of Home.
3. On a computer, open **My tasks** from the menu on the left.

## Reading a task

Each task is a card with:

- the title and any details your director added;
- the priority chip — **High**, **Normal** or **Low** — and the status chip — **Open**, **In progress** or **Done**;
- 📅 the due date (in red with *overdue* when it is past), the centre, and who it is from.

Newest and most urgent tasks come first.

## Working through a task

1. Tap **▶ Start** when you begin. The status changes to **In progress**, so your team can see you are on it.
2. Do the work.
3. Tap **✓ Mark done**. A **Task completed** message appears, the title is crossed out, and it drops out of your open count.
4. Marked one done by mistake? Tap **↺ Reopen** and it goes back to **Open**.

[[show: #my-tasks @ ✓ Mark done | Show me]]

> 💡 You can tap **✓ Mark done** straight away without starting — **▶ Start** is only there to show others you have picked it up.

When everything is finished you see 🎉 **No tasks right now** — *You are all caught up.*

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Educator / home visitor | See their own tasks, start them, mark them done or reopen them |
| Centre director / agency admin | Assign tasks on the **Tasks** screen, set the priority and due date, and track who has done what |

You cannot create, edit or delete an assigned task — only the person who assigned it can. If a task is wrong or no longer needed, tell them.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? It says "Could not load tasks." | The connection dropped. Check your internet and open **My tasks** again.
??? It says "Could not update task". | The change did not save. Wait a moment and tap the button again.
??? A task is overdue but I can't do it in time. | Tell the person it is **From** (in person or in the **Message Centre**), so they can change the due date.
??? Does my director know when I finish? | Yes. The task shows as **Done** on their **Tasks** screen straight away.
??? Where do new tasks show up? | On this screen and as a red count on the **My tasks** tile. You may also get a notice in your **Inbox**.
