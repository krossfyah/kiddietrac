# My training, checks and certificates

Two screens keep your own paperwork up to date. **My training & checks** shows what your licensing rules ask of you (background checks, required training and their deadlines) and your training hours for the year. **My certificates** holds your CPR, first aid and other certificates with their expiry dates. Your director sees the same records, so keeping them current saves everyone a chase before an inspection.

[[open: #my-certificates | Open My certificates]]
[[open: #my-training | Open My training & checks]]

## Getting there

1. On a computer, open the menu on the left and go to **Compliance**.
2. Choose **My certificates**, or **My training** (it only shows when your province or state sets staff training rules).
3. On a phone, tap the buttons at the top of this guide. Both screens work the same on a phone; the cards stack one under the other.

## Adding a certificate (CPR, first aid …)

1. Open **My certificates**.
2. Tap **＋ Add certificate**.
3. Pick the **Certificate** type (CPR, first aid, …). If you pick Other, type the **Name on the certificate**.
4. Fill in **Issued by** (for example, Canadian Red Cross) and the **Issue date**. The **Expiry date** fills in from the rules when it can; check it against your card.
5. Under **Certificate file**, attach a PDF or take a photo of the card.
6. Tap **Add certificate**. The new card appears with its status and **Not yet checked** until your director verifies it, then **✓ Verified**.

[[show: #my-certificates @ ＋ Add certificate | Show me]]

> 💡 Renewed your CPR? Add the new certificate rather than editing the old one, so the history stays on file.

To change or look at one, tap the card's **⋮**: **📎 View certificate**, **✏️ Edit**, or **🗑️ Remove** (you can only remove one that has not been verified yet).

## Reading your requirements

1. Open **My training & checks**.
2. Read the **Requirements** card: each line shows the **Requirement**, the **Rule** behind it, its **Status** and **Date**.
3. Act on anything marked **Due**, **Overdue**, **Expired** or **Not on file** — bring the document to your director.

If it says your hire date isn't on file yet, ask your director to add it: deadlines are counted from your hire date.

## Logging training hours

1. In the **Training hours** card, set the **Date** you took the training.
2. Type the **Training** name (for example, Child abuse prevention) and who it was **Given by**.
3. Enter the **Hours** and, if you have one, attach the **Certificate**.
4. Tap **Add hours**. A **Training hours added** message says it is waiting for your director to approve.

Your hours show **Waiting for review** until approved, then **Approved** (they count toward your yearly total) or **Not approved**. You can remove an entry with **✕** while it is still waiting.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Educator / home visitor | Add and edit their own certificates and training hours; see their own requirements |
| Centre director / agency admin | See and verify everyone's certificates, approve or not approve hours, set hire dates |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? I can't find My training in the menu. | It only appears when your agency's province or state sets staff training and check rules. **My certificates** is always there.
??? It says "No current CPR on file". | Add your CPR certificate with **＋ Add certificate**. A photo of the card is fine.
??? It says "Pick the issue date first." | Fill in the **Issue date** before the **Expiry date**.
??? My hours are not counted in my total. | Only **Approved** hours count. Ask your director to review them.
??? Will I be reminded? | Yes. You get a reminder in your 🔔 Inbox when a requirement is due soon or overdue, so there is time to renew.
