# My tasks

Your director or agency admin can assign you tasks — things like "update the allergy board" or "restock the art supplies." They show up in one place so nothing slips through.

## Where to find them

- The **My tasks** tile on your home screen. When you have open work, the tile shows a red count and an **"open tasks"** card appears at the top of the home screen.
- Open **My tasks** to see the full list, newest and most urgent first.

## Working a task

Each task shows the title, any details, who assigned it, the priority (High / Normal / Low) and a due date (overdue ones are flagged in red).

- **▶ Start** — marks it *in progress* so your team knows you're on it.
- **✓ Mark done** — completes it. It drops out of your open count.
- **↺ Reopen** — if you marked it done by mistake.

You can't create or delete assigned tasks — only the person who assigned them can. Your job is to get them done.
