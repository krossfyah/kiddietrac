# My hours and my pay

**My hours** (you may see it called **Payroll**) is your own record: what you have worked,
what that work is worth, and what the agency has actually issued you.

## Two questions, kept apart

The screen answers two different questions and never adds them together, because the same
work appears in both.

**🧾 Issued** — what the agency has actually issued and paid you. This is the
authoritative one. When somebody asks "have I been paid", this is the answer, which is why
it leads. Each payslip is listed, with whether it is still outstanding.

**📈 Earned by week** — worked out live from the hours you have clocked (or the visits you
have filed) over the last twelve weeks. It is what the work is worth, whether or not
anything has been issued for it yet.

Adding the two together would count the same work twice. They are separate tabs for that
reason.

## If the hours look wrong

Your clock only toggles in and out — you cannot edit your own shifts, and that is
deliberate. Tell your director:

- **"I forgot to clock in."** They can enter the shift by hand, with a note saying why.
- **"I forgot to clock out and it ran overnight."** They can close it at the right time.
  Until they do, the shift shows as open and counts as **0h**, so it is not quietly
  overpaid or underpaid — it is waiting.
- **"That shift is not mine."** They can remove it.

Every one of those is a couple of taps for them on **Timesheets**, and every one is
recorded with their name and the reason. It is a normal thing to ask for — ask early
rather than at the end of the pay period.

## Where your pay goes

**Settings → My profile & security → How you are paid.** Choose one:

- **Interac e-Transfer** — the name on the account and the address to send to.
- **Direct deposit** — the name on the account, institution number (3 digits), transit
  number (5 digits) and account number.

Worth knowing:

- Only your administrator and centre director can see it, and it is stored encrypted.
- **The page only ever shows you the last few digits** — "•••• 4821". That is enough to
  confirm the right account is on file and useless to anyone reading over your shoulder.
- **Leaving a box blank leaves the stored value alone**, so correcting a typo in your name
  cannot wipe your account number. To take the details off entirely, use **Remove**.
- Until something is on file, payroll has nowhere to send your pay.

## Worth knowing

- Only **closed** shifts count towards hours. One left open is named rather than silently
  dropped.
- Glance at the week's total on a Friday. A missed punch is easy to fix the same week and
  annoying to reconstruct a month later.
- Breaks are not recorded by the clock, so the hours are the clock's hours, unadjusted.
