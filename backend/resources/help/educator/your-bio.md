# Your bio — tell families about yourself

Families want to know who will care for their child. Your bio is how they meet you:

- it goes in every **new family's welcome email** when a child joins you;
- it shows on **your provider card** in the parents' app, every time they check in on their child.

Parents who feel they know their provider settle in faster and trust you sooner.

## Where to write it (about 2 minutes)

1. Open **Settings → Profile**.
2. Find **Your bio**.
3. Write at least a sentence or two (40 characters or more).
4. Press **Save bio**.

If you haven't written one, you'll see a reminder card, *"Families would love to meet you"*.
Its **Write my bio** button opens the box for you.

## What to say

A few lines on any of these is plenty:

- how long you've cared for children, and any training (ECE, First Aid, CPR);
- what a day looks like in your home: play, outdoor time, meals, naps;
- what makes your care special: languages you speak, music, nature, a garden, pets;
- something personal, so families feel they know you.

*For example: "Hi, I'm Maria! I have run a licensed home daycare in Orangeville for 8 years.
Our days are full of outdoor play, music and story time, and I speak English and Spanish."*

## While you're there

Add a **profile photo** too (Settings → Profile). It appears beside your bio, and families love
putting a face to the name.
