# My training and checks

**My training** shows what your province or state's licensing rules ask of you, and lets you log
your training hours. It appears in your menu when your agency's rules include staff deadlines or
training hours (Ohio today).

## Requirements

A list of what you need — for example first aid, CPR, child abuse prevention training, orientation,
background checks and your registry number — each marked **Done**, **Due by** a date, **Overdue**,
**Expired** or **Not on file**. Deadlines count from your **hire date**; if yours isn't recorded,
ask your director to add it.

Certificates and checks are recorded by your director on your staff record, and this list updates from them.

## Training hours

1. Enter the **date**, the **training** title, who **gave** it (optional) and the **hours**.
2. Attach the **certificate** if you have one (PDF or photo).
3. Press **Add hours**.

Hours you add count toward the year's total straight away. The bar shows your hours against what your rules require.
Your director or admin can remove an entry that is wrong, and you can remove your own.

You'll get a reminder in your bell when something is due soon or overdue.
