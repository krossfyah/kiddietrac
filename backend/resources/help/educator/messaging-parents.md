# Messaging parents

Each child has a conversation thread between their parent(s) and the room. Use it for things that don't need urgent attention but benefit from a personal touch.

## What to use messages for

- Sharing a moment from the day that's worth more than a one-line photo caption
- Responding to a parent's question
- Letting them know about something they should ask their child about
- Coordinating a pickup time change

## What NOT to use messages for

- **Urgent safety issues** (call the parent directly)
- **Medical incidents** (use the incident report flow + call)
- **Complaints or concerns about a child** (talk to the director first)
- **Anything you'd be uncomfortable having on record** (everything is logged)

## How to send a message

1. From the room view, tap **Conversations**
2. Pick a parent's thread (the child's name and parent's name appear)
3. Type your message
4. Tap **Send**

Parents see the message in their app and (if they have notifications on their phone enabled) get a push notification.

## Texting or emailing a parent

On a child's record, use **💬 Message parents**, or **Text** / **Email** beside a parent's
**Call** button:

1. Choose who to send to and how: text, email, or both.
2. Write your message. A text can be up to **480 characters**; for anything longer, send it by email.
3. Send. You'll see, for each parent, whether it was sent.

Good to know:

- Messages go from **your agency's SMS number and email, never your own** number or address.
- A parent who hasn't agreed to receive texts shows *"Has not agreed to texts — email instead"*. You can still **email** them.
- **Replies go to the agency**, not to you.
- **Every message is kept on the child's record** (and in the audit log), and the parent also sees it in their app.

## Response time expectations

- During care hours: try to respond to parents within an hour
- After hours: it's fine to respond the next morning
- Be kind to yourself — you're not on call 24/7

## Tone

- Warm but professional
- Specific over generic ("Aria built a tall tower today" is better than "had a great day!")
- Don't use parent's first name unless they've asked you to
- Avoid emojis except for one occasional 😊 or 🎨 to add warmth

## Examples

**Good:**

> "Hi! Just wanted to mention that Aria had a really focused session at the easel today — she experimented with mixing yellow and red and was thrilled when she made orange. Photo attached. Ask her about it tonight!"

**Less good:**

> "Aria had a great day!"
>
> (Tells parent nothing specific)

**Avoid:**

> "I'm a bit worried about Aria's behaviour today, she was difficult with the other kids..."
>
> (Talk to the director first — concerns should go through proper channels)

## What if a parent asks me something I can't answer?

- Operational questions (fees, schedule changes) → "Let me check with [Director] and get back to you"
- Medical advice → "I'm not qualified to give medical advice, but if you'd like I can connect you with [Director] who can refer you"
- About another child → "I can't share information about other children, but happy to chat about [their child]"

## Privacy notes

- Everything you send is logged and visible to your director
- The parent sees your name on every message
- Be professional — these messages can be reviewed in the future
