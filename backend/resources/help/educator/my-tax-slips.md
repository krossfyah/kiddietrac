# My tax slips (T4, T4A, RL-1)

When your employer pays you through KiddieTrac payroll, your tax slips for the year appear in **My HR › My tax slips** once they are finalized (by the end of February). You get a notice in the bell when they are ready.

## Open a slip
Tap a slip to open it. You can save or print it for your income tax return. It shows the CRA box numbers (for example box 14 employment income, box 22 income tax deducted) in your employer's own layout, and your full social insurance number. Your employer also files the slip with the CRA.

## The kinds of slip
- **T4**: employment income and the deductions taken from your pay (CPP or QPP, EI, income tax).
- **T4A**: fees paid to you as a contractor (box 048).
- **RL-1 information** (Quebec): the RL-1 boxes. Your official RL-1 comes through Revenu Québec.

## Amended or cancelled slips
If your employer corrects a slip, the new version is marked **Amended** and the old one shows **Replaced by a newer slip**. Use the one that is in force. A **Cancelled** slip reports nothing.

## Questions
If an amount looks wrong, contact the office. Only your employer can correct a slip.
