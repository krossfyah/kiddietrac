# Requesting time off

**Time off** is where you ask for a day off, several days, or just part of a day, and follow what happens to each
request. Your director is told straight away, and you are told their decision in the app and by email.

[[open: #time-off | Open Time off]]

## Asking for time off

1. Open **🌴 Time off** from the menu (or the **Time off** tile on **Home**).
2. Press **🌴 Request time off**. The **New time-off request** box opens.
3. Pick the **Type**: **Vacation**, **Sick**, **Personal**, **Bereavement**, **Jury duty** or **Other**. If your
   agency uses KiddieTrac HR, the list shows your agency's own leave types instead.
4. Choose the **Start date**, then the **End date**. The end date can never be before the start.
5. For part of a day, switch **All day** off and set **From** and **To** (part of a day is always one date).
6. Add a **Reason** if you want to explain.
7. Press **Submit**. The box closes and the request appears under **Your requests** as **Pending**.

> 💡 A 2 pm dentist appointment is a part-day request, not a day off, so the rota does not show you away all day.

## With KiddieTrac HR: balances, clashes and sick notes

1. Above **Your requests**, read your balance for each leave type (for example *Vacation 6 days*, with any
   *pending* amount under it).
2. In the request box, after you pick a type and dates, a line shows what you have *available* and what is left
   *after this request*. If it says *not enough left*, your agency may refuse it.
3. If others at your centre are already off or asking on those days, a yellow box says how many.
4. Under **Note or photo (optional)**, press **📷 Take a photo** or **📎 Choose a file** to attach a sick note, then
   set the dates it covers. Only you, your director and your agency admin can see it.
5. Some leave types need no approval: you see **Recorded** and the time comes off your balance straight away.

> ℹ️ Forgot the note? Press **📎 Add a note or photo** on the request under **Your requests**.

## Checking and withdrawing a request

1. Look at **Your requests**: **Type**, **Dates**, **Decided by** and **Status** (**Pending**, **Approved** or
   **Denied**).
2. To take a request back, press **⋮ > Withdraw** on its row, then **Withdraw** in the box that asks you to confirm.
3. You can withdraw while it is pending, and also after it is approved: the calendar goes back to normal and the people
   who were told are told you are working as usual.

## Who can do what

- **Educator**: asks for time off, attaches notes, and withdraws their own requests. Educators do not see other
  people's requests.
- **Centre director / agency admin**: see **Team requests**, approve or deny, and decide whether the centre closes.
  They get a notification and an email with **Approve** and **Decline** buttons for each new request.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? I see "Pick the dates." | Choose both a **Start date** and an **End date** before pressing **Submit**.
??? I see "The end time has to be after the start." | For part of a day, the **To** time must be later than **From**.
??? I see "Could not send the request. Please try again." | Nothing was sent. Check your connection and press **Submit** again.
??? There is no Withdraw on my request. | Denied and cancelled requests cannot be withdrawn. Ask your director if plans change.
??? I see "Sent, below zero". | Your agency lets balances go below zero, so the request was sent, but you are asking for more than you have.
??? Can I ask for part of a day over two dates? | No. Part of a day is always one date: make two requests.
??? Will my centre close while I'm away? | Your director or admin decides when they approve. If they close it, families are emailed.
??? Who sees my reason? | Your director and the agency admins who decide on the request.
