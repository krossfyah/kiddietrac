# Requesting time off

Ask for time off from the portal — a whole day, several days, or just part of a day —
and your director is told straight away.

[[open: #time-off | Open Time off]]

## Asking for time off

- [ ] Open **Time off** and press **🌴 Request time off**.
- [ ] Pick the **Type**: Vacation, Sick, Personal, Bereavement, Jury duty or Other.
- [ ] Choose the **Start date** and **End date**.
- [ ] For part of a day, switch **All day** off and set **From** and **To**.
- [ ] Add a **Reason** and press **Submit**.

> **Tip:** A 2pm dentist appointment is a part-day request, not a day off, so the rota does not show you away all day.

Your director and the agency admins get a notification and an email with **Approve**
and **Decline** buttons. You're told their decision in the app and by email.

## Changing your mind

You can withdraw a request while it is waiting, and even after it has been approved.
Withdrawing puts the calendar back to normal and lets the right people know.

??? Can I request part of a day across two dates? | No — part of a day is always a single date. Make two requests if you need both.
??? Who sees my reason? | Your director and the agency admins who decide on the request.
