# Headcounts

A headcount is a name-to-face check: you look at each child and tick them off. From 1 January 2027, Ontario centres must count children at set times and keep a record (O. Reg. 137/15 s.11). The riskiest moments are transitions between areas, washroom trips, arrival and dismissal, field trips, going in and out of the playground, and parking lots. The **Headcounts** screen shows each of your rooms and the history of counts.

[[open: #headcounts | Open Headcounts]]

## Taking a headcount

1. On **Today**, tap **👀 Headcount** above the roster, or open **Headcounts** and tap **👀 Headcount** on the room. On a walk, tap **Count** on the floating walk bar.
2. Pick the moment: **Routine check**, **Transition**, **Washroom**, **Outdoor out**, **Outdoor in**, **Arrival**, **Dismissal**, **Field trip** or **Other**.
3. Tap each child as you **see** them. The top shows how many are accounted for, for example "9 of 12 accounted for".
4. If you like, open **Where / note (optional)** and add where you are (for example "playground gate") and a note.
5. Tap the save button. It says **Save — all 12 accounted for**, or **Save — 2 not seen** when someone is missing.

The list shows the children signed in at that moment. If a child is signed in while you are counting, the app adds them and asks you to find them too before it saves.

## If a child is not accounted for

The screen turns red and lists who was not seen.

1. Search right away: the last place the child was seen, washrooms, cubbies, the playground and gates, and the parking lot. Follow your centre's missing-child procedure.
2. Tap **🚨 Alert director now**. Your director, the agency admins and the acting supervisors get a bell notice and a phone push at once. No text message is sent.
3. If the supervisor is away, the red screen shows the **acting supervisor** to contact.
4. If it is a serious occurrence, tap **Serious occurrence report**: a new incident form opens for that child, already filled in.
5. When everyone is found, tap **✅ Found — all accounted for**, say where the child was found and tap **Mark found**. Anyone you alerted is told.

## The Headcounts screen

- **👀 Rooms now** — each of your rooms, how many children are signed in, when the last count was done (or **No count today**), and a **👀 Headcount** button.
- **📜 Logs** — the history of counts, with filters for date, room, moment and result.

[[show: #headcounts @ 📜 Logs | Show me the logs]]

Need it as a document? **Reports → Headcounts** gives one day or any range for your rooms, as PDF, Excel or CSV.

## Reminders

Your agency can switch on headcount reminders. If a room goes a set time without a count, the educators clocked in to that room get a reminder. Reminders stop at the end of your rostered shift that day, or at the centre's closing time if you have no roster, so staying late does not bring a stream of reminders.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Educator | Count the rooms they work in, raise and clear a missing-child alert, read the logs for their rooms. |
| Centre director / agency admin | All of that for their centres, plus name the acting supervisors and switch reminders on or off. |
| Parent | Not shown. |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? My room is not on Rooms now. | You only see the rooms you are assigned to. Ask your director to assign you to the room.
??? A child I can see is not on the list. | The list shows children signed in. Check the child in first; the count then picks them up.
??? I tapped Alert director now by mistake. | Tap **✅ Found — all accounted for** and say it was a mistake. Everyone you alerted is told.
??? There is no acting supervisor on the red screen. | None has been named for your centre. A director or agency admin names them on the **Headcounts** screen.
??? It says "Could not save". | The count did not reach the server. Check your connection and tap save again.
