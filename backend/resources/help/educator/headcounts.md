# Headcounts

A headcount is a name-to-face check. You look at each child and tick them off. From 1 January 2027, Ontario centres must count children at set times and record it (O. Reg. 137/15 s.11). The higher-risk moments are transitions between areas, washroom trips, arrival and dismissal, field trips, going in and out of the playground, and parking lots.

## Taking a headcount

1. On **Today**, tap **👀 Headcount** above the roster. On a walk, tap **Count** on the floating walk bar.
2. Pick the moment: Transition, Washroom, Outdoor out, Outdoor in, Arrival, Dismissal, Field trip, Routine check or Other.
3. Tap each child as you **see** them. The top shows "9 of 12 accounted for".
4. You can add where you are (for example "playground gate") and a note.
5. Tap **Save**.

The list shows the children signed in at that moment. If a child is signed in while you are counting, the app adds them to your list and asks you to find them too before it saves.

## If a child is not accounted for

The screen turns red and lists who was not seen.

- Search right away: the last place the child was seen, washrooms, cubbies, the playground and gates, and the parking lot. Follow your centre's missing-child procedure.
- Tap **🚨 Alert director now**. Your director, the agency admins and the acting supervisors get a bell and a push notification straight away. No text message is sent.
- The red screen also shows who to contact if the supervisor is away (the **acting supervisor**).
- **Serious occurrence report** opens a new incident form for that child, already filled in.
- When everyone is found, tap **✅ Found — all accounted for** and say where. Anyone you alerted is told.

## Reminders

Your agency can switch on reminders. If a room has gone a set time without a count, the educators clocked in to that room get a reminder.
