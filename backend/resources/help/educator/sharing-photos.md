# Sharing photos

Photos are one of the highest-value things you can do for parents. A single photo of a child engaged in something specific is more meaningful than five paragraphs of text.

## How to share a photo

1. Tap any child's card
2. Tap **Share photo**
3. Either **take a new photo** with the tablet camera, or **choose from gallery**
4. Add a caption (optional but recommended)
5. Tap **Share photo**

The photo appears in the parent's app within seconds.

## Tagging multiple children

If a photo includes more than one child (group activity, two friends playing together):

1. Tap the first child's card → **Share photo**
2. After uploading, tap the other children in the room — they'll be added as "tagged in this photo"

Or use the batch option from the room view (tap the camera icon, then check which children are in the photo).

## What makes a good photo

- **In focus.** Blurry photos add no value.
- **Shows the child engaged** — building, painting, sleeping, reading. Not random snapshots.
- **Captures expression** — happy, focused, curious
- **Includes context** — the materials they're using, the activity

Avoid:

- Group shots where everyone looks bored
- Photos taken from awkward angles
- Photos showing children who aren't fully clothed (changing, in the bathroom, etc.)

## What about consent?

By enrolling, families consent to photos for their own child within Kiddietrac. **Do not** share photos:

- Outside Kiddietrac (Facebook, Instagram, etc.) without explicit written consent
- Of children whose families have opted out of photo sharing (your director will tell you)
- Of any child not enrolled in the room (other children visiting, parents visiting)

## What about captions?

A good caption gives the parent a starting point for conversation. Examples:

- "Block tower we built together! Aria asked for help with the top piece."
- "First time at the easel — she mixed yellow + red and got really excited about orange."
- "Quiet moment during story time."

Bad captions:

- "Cute!" (tells the parent nothing)
- Long essays (just write a sentence)

## How often

Aim for 1-3 photos per child per week. Daily is overwhelming for parents (lots of notifications). Weekly is fine.

For special moments — first time walking, first time using a new word, big achievement — definitely capture and share.
