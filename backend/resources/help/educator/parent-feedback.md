# Parent feedback — what families said about their child's day

**Parent feedback** shows the notes families leave about their child's day, for the children you care for. Parents
send them from a link in their daily summary email: a star rating, a comment, and sometimes a note **For tomorrow**.
Open it when the menu shows a new count, or at the end of the day to see how families felt.

[[open: #parent-feedback | Open Parent feedback]]

## Reading new feedback

1. Open **Parent feedback** from the menu. A number beside it means there is something new.
2. At the top, a blue line says how many are **new since you last looked**.
3. Each card shows the child's name and the day the family is talking about, the stars they gave (★★★★☆), and their
   comment.
4. New cards have a blue edge and the word **NEW**.
5. Read each one. A moment after the list appears, the new ones are marked as read and the count in the menu clears.

> 💡 The day on a card is the day the family is commenting on, not the day they wrote it.

## Acting on a "For tomorrow" note

Some families add an orange **For tomorrow** box, for example "She slept badly, please let her nap early".

1. Read the **For tomorrow** note.
2. Pass it to whoever is with the child tomorrow if that will not be you (your room's handover or a quick note).
3. If it needs a reply, write to the family in the **Message Centre**.

[[open: #chat | Open Message Centre]]

## What you see, and what waits for your director

- **Praise and good news** reach you the moment the family sends it.
- **Anything critical** waits for your director or agency admin to read it first. They decide how to pass it on, so
  you never meet unfiltered criticism of yourself on this screen.
- You only see feedback about the children you care for. Directors and agency admins see all feedback, released or
  not, on their own Feedback screen.

There is no reply button on this screen: feedback is a note to you, not a conversation. To answer a family, use the
Message Centre.

## Who can do what

| Role | Sees |
|---|---|
| Educators and home visitors | Released feedback about the children in their care |
| Centre directors and agency admins | All feedback for their centres, including what is held back, and decide what is released |
| Parents | Leave feedback from the daily summary email |

## Questions and problems

??? The screen says "No feedback yet". | No family has left a note about a child in your care yet, or what they sent is still waiting for your director. It appears here once it is released.
??? "Could not load feedback: …" | Your connection dropped or the server was busy. Refresh the page.
??? A parent told me they left a comment, but I don't see it. | It may be waiting for your director to read it first. Ask your director.
??? Can I reply to the feedback? | Not from here. Write to the family in the Message Centre.
??? Is the family told I read it? | No. Reading it only clears your own NEW markers and the count in your menu.
