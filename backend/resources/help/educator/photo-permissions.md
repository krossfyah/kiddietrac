# Checking a child's photo permission

**🤳 Photo permissions** shows what each family allows for photos and videos of their child. You hold the camera, so check it before you take or share a photo — especially a group photo. Each child's primary parent chooses; until they answer, the child is in photos for their own family only.

[[open: #photo-permissions | Open Photo permissions]]

## Getting there

1. On a computer, open **Photo permissions** from the menu on the left, under **Administration**.
2. On a phone, tap the **Open Photo permissions** button at the top of this guide. The screen works the same on a phone; the list turns into one card per child.

## What the three answers mean

| Answer | What you do |
|---|---|
| 🚫 **Do not photograph** | Do not take or share photos or videos of this child. KiddieTrac will not let you tag them, and any photo that already tags them is hidden from every family. |
| 👪 **Family only** | Share photos with their own family only. A photo with this child in it is not shown to the other families tagged in it. |
| ✅ **Group photos OK** | They can be in group photos the other families see. |

Under a child whose primary parent has not answered yet you see *until the primary parent answers* — treat them as **Family only**.

**Marketing** is a separate yes or no: only a **Yes** means the centre may use the child's photo outside KiddieTrac (website, flyers, social media).

## Checking your room before a photo

1. Open **Photo permissions**.
2. Tap a coloured count at the top to see only those children: **All children**, **Do not photograph**, **Family only**, **Group photos OK** or **Primary parent not answered**.
3. Or type a name in **Find a child…** to check one child.
4. Read the **Photo permission** column. **Parents' answers** shows who answered and when; an earlier answer from a parent who is not the primary parent shows greyed as not counted.
5. Before a group photo, tap **Do not photograph** and make sure none of those children are in the shot.

[[show: #photo-permissions @ Do not photograph | Show me]]

> 💡 Taking a group photo? Frame it so the 🚫 children are out of shot, rather than tagging around them afterwards.

> ⚠️ Never post a child's photo outside KiddieTrac (your own social media, a group chat) whatever the answer is. Marketing use is the centre's decision, made by a director.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Educator | See the permission for every child they can see |
| Centre director / agency admin | The same, plus **✍ Paper form** to record a primary parent's signed paper answer, and **Show … hidden** to show again photos that were hidden for no permission once the family allows them |
| Parent | Answers for their own child in the portal (they are asked once, and can change it in their settings) |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? A child I work with is not on the list. | The list shows the children you can see in KiddieTrac. If a child in your room is missing, ask your director to check your room assignment.
??? It says "No children match." | Your filter or search hides everyone. Tap **All children** and clear **Find a child…**.
??? A parent told me in person they changed their mind. | Ask them to change it in the portal, or to sign a paper form that your director records. You cannot change an answer yourself.
??? I already shared a photo with a "Do not photograph" child in it. | KiddieTrac hides a photo that tags them from every family. If they are in it but not tagged, remove the photo and tell your director.
??? Why is a child "Family only" when nobody chose that? | Until the primary parent answers, KiddieTrac is careful by default and treats the child as **Family only**.
