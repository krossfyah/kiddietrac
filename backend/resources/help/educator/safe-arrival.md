# Safe Arrival — when a child hasn't arrived

When your agency has Safe Arrival on, KiddieTrac watches for children who were
expected today but haven't been signed in. It asks the parents first. If they don't
answer, **you** get an alert — *"⚠️ Safe Arrival: Maya not here"* — so someone calls.

[[open: #safe-arrival | Open Safe Arrival]]

## When you get an alert

- [ ] Tap the alert — it opens the **Safe Arrival** board for your rooms.
- [ ] Under **Needs attention**, tap a guardian's 📞 number to call them.
- [ ] Record what you found out: **Record absence** (with the reason), **Parent contacted** with a note like *"on the way, arriving 10:15"*, or **Other…**.

The case is saved with your name and note. When the child arrives and is signed in, it
closes by itself.

> **Important:** An alert means the parents were asked and nobody has answered. Please call — don't wait for the child to turn up.

## What you see

The board shows the children in **your rooms** who are scheduled today: who has arrived
and when, who is absent and why, and who isn't due yet. It refreshes every minute.

??? Why did I get an alert for a child who's on holiday? | Nobody reported the absence. Record it with **Record absence** so the parents aren't asked again and the roster is right.
??? A child arrived but still shows as overdue. | They haven't been signed in yet. Sign them in and the case closes.
