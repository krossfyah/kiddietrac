# Safe Arrival — when a child hasn't arrived

When your agency has Safe Arrival on, KiddieTrac watches for children who are expected today but have not been signed in. It asks the parents first, and your agency may also have it **phone** them. If nobody answers, **you** get an alert — *"⚠️ Safe Arrival: Maya not here"* — so someone calls. The **Safe Arrival** board shows the children in your rooms who are scheduled today.

[[open: #safe-arrival | Open Safe Arrival]]

## Getting to the board

1. Tap the Safe Arrival alert on your phone or in your **Inbox** — it opens the board.
2. Or, on a phone, tap **Home** in the bottom bar (Home · Daily log · One-Tap · Message Centre · Inbox) and then the **Safe Arrival** tile ("Who is due in").
3. On a computer, open **Safe Arrival** from the menu on the left, under **Operations**.

The board has two tabs: **Today** and **🕘 History**. Under the short On/Off line, five counts show **EXPECTED TODAY**, **ARRIVED**, **ABSENT**, **NOT DUE YET** and **NEEDS ATTENTION**. Tap **↻ Refresh** for the latest; it also refreshes by itself.

## When you get an alert

1. Look under **Needs attention**. Each child card shows the room, the expected time, when the parents were asked and when staff were alerted.
2. Tap a guardian's 📞 number on the card to call them.
3. Record what you found out:
   - **Record absence** — pick the **Reason** (**Sick**, **Appointment**, **Holiday**, **Family** or **Other**) and add a note;
   - **Parent contacted** — with a note such as *"on the way, arriving 10:15"*;
   - **Other…** — anything else, with a note.
4. Save. The case is kept with your name and note, and the card leaves **Needs attention**.

When the child arrives and is signed in, the case closes by itself.

> ⚠️ An alert means the parents were asked and nobody has answered. Please call — don't wait for the child to turn up.

## When a parent tells you the child will be late

1. Find the child under **Everyone else today** (or on their **Needs attention** card).
2. Tap **⏰ Late drop-off**.
3. Set **Expected by** to the time the parent gave you, and add a **Note (optional)** (for example, "Mum called, doctor's appointment").
4. Tap **Save**. Nobody is asked or alerted before that time. If the child still is not signed in by then, Safe Arrival checks as usual.

To change the time, tap **Change** on the row; **Take it back** returns to the usual check time.

## When a parent answers the phone call

If your agency uses the Safe Arrival phone call, the parent presses a key and you're told what they chose:

- **"Safe Arrival: Maya is absent today"** — the absence is recorded. Nothing to do.
- **"Safe Arrival: Maya is on the way"** — she's still expected. Sign her in when she arrives. If she still hasn't arrived a while later, you'll get an alert after all.
- **"🚨 Safe Arrival: Maya dropped off but NOT signed in"** — **act now.** The parent says the child is at the centre, but she isn't on the register. Find her and sign her in. If she can't be found, follow your missing-child procedure.

> ⚠️ A child is never signed in because a parent pressed a key. Only a sign-in by staff or the parent at the door puts her in your count.

## At the end of the day

If your agency checks departures too, **🕔 End of day — still signed in** lists children still signed in after their pickup time. For each one, record **Picked up at…** (and the time), **Still here**, **⏰ Late pickup**, or **Other…**.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Educator | See the board for their rooms, call families, record absences and late drop-offs |
| Centre director | The same for their centre, plus they can be alerted too |
| Agency admin | Everything, plus **⚙️ Settings** (times, phone calls, who is alerted) |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? Why did I get an alert for a child who's on holiday? | Nobody reported the absence. Record it with **Record absence** so the parents aren't asked again and the roster is right.
??? A child arrived but still shows as overdue. | They haven't been signed in yet. Sign them in and the case closes.
??? The card says "No guardian on file." or "no phone". | Call the centre office or your director, who can look up other contacts in the child's record.
??? It says "Off. Nobody is being checked automatically." | Safe Arrival is switched off for your agency. The board still shows who hasn't arrived, so check it yourself after drop-off.
??? A child shows "Counted … Please sign them in." | They were ticked in a headcount but never signed in. Sign them in now so your attendance is right.
