# Safe Arrival — when a child hasn't arrived

When your agency has Safe Arrival on, KiddieTrac watches for children who were
expected today but haven't been signed in. It asks the parents first, and your agency
may also have it **phone** them. If they don't answer, **you** get an alert —
*"⚠️ Safe Arrival: Maya not here"* — so someone calls.

[[open: #safe-arrival | Open Safe Arrival]]

## When you get an alert

- [ ] Tap the alert — it opens the **Safe Arrival** board for your rooms.
- [ ] Under **Needs attention**, tap a guardian's 📞 number to call them.
- [ ] Record what you found out: **Record absence** (with the reason), **Parent contacted** with a note like *"on the way, arriving 10:15"*, or **Other…**.

The case is saved with your name and note. When the child arrives and is signed in, it
closes by itself.

> **Important:** An alert means the parents were asked and nobody has answered. Please call — don't wait for the child to turn up.

## When a parent answers the phone call

If your agency uses the Safe Arrival phone call, the parent presses a key and you're told what they chose:

- **"Safe Arrival: Maya is absent today"** — the absence is recorded. Nothing to do.
- **"Safe Arrival: Maya is on the way"** — she's still expected. Sign her in when she arrives. If she still hasn't arrived a while later, you'll get an alert after all.
- **"🚨 Safe Arrival: Maya dropped off but NOT signed in"** — **act now.** The parent says the child is at the centre, but she isn't on the register. Find her and sign her in. If she can't be found, follow your missing-child procedure.

> **Important:** A child is never signed in because a parent pressed a key. Only a sign-in by staff or the parent at the door puts her in your count.

## What you see

The board shows the children in **your rooms** who are scheduled today: who has arrived
and when, who is absent and why, and who isn't due yet. A parent who said they dropped
the child off is at the very top in red; **On the way (parent)** shows who is coming
late. It refreshes every minute.

??? Why did I get an alert for a child who's on holiday? | Nobody reported the absence. Record it with **Record absence** so the parents aren't asked again and the roster is right.
??? A child arrived but still shows as overdue. | They haven't been signed in yet. Sign them in and the case closes.
