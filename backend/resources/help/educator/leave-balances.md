# Your leave balances

If your agency uses KiddieTrac HR, **Time off** shows your leave balances at the top: how much of each type you have, and how much is waiting for approval.

## Requesting time off

Tap **Request time off** and pick a leave type. Under it you see what you have available and what you will have after this request. If the request would take you below zero, your agency either refuses it or sends it with a warning.

Some leave types need no approval: they are recorded and taken off your balance straight away, and your manager reviews them afterwards. The others go to your director or admin as before.

Only working days count: weekends, and the holidays and closures on your centre's calendar, are not taken off.

If others at your centre are already off or asking for the same days, the form tells you how many, so you can plan. Your director sees the same before deciding.

## After a decision

An approved request is taken off your balance. If it is denied, or you withdraw it, the time goes back.

Each of your requests shows how much it uses and your balance. If someone adjusts your balance, you get a notice with the reason.

## Time in lieu

Overtime that your director banks as time in lieu goes into your **Overtime bank** balance (the hours times your agency's overtime rate). Book it like any time off. Log overtime in **My HR > Overtime and time in lieu**.
