# Your leave balances

If your agency uses KiddieTrac HR, **Time off** shows your leave balances at the top: how much of each type you have, and how much is waiting for approval.

## Requesting time off

Tap **Request time off** and pick a leave type. Under it you see what you have available and what you will have after this request. If the request would take you below zero, your agency either refuses it or sends it with a warning.

Some leave types are approved as soon as you send them. The others go to your director or admin as before.

## After a decision

An approved request is taken off your balance. If it is denied, or you withdraw it, the time goes back.

Each of your requests shows how much it uses and your balance. If someone adjusts your balance, you get a notice with the reason.
