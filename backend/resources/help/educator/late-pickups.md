# Logging a late pickup or late arrival

Use **⏰ Late pickups** when a family collects a child after the centre has closed, or brings a child in late. You log it as it happens; KiddieTrac works out the suggested fee from your agency's policy, and a director or admin then approves it onto the family's invoice, waives it or declines it.

[[open: #late-pickups | Open Late pickups]]

## Getting there

1. On a phone, tap **Home** in the bottom bar (Home · Daily log · One-Tap · Message Centre · Inbox).
2. Tap the **🌙 Late pickup** tile.
3. On a computer, open **Late pickups** from the menu on the left, under **Finance**.

## Logging a late pickup

1. Tap **＋ Log late pickup** in the banner. The **Log a late pickup** window opens.
2. In **What**, keep **Late pickup**.
3. Pick the **Child**.
4. Set **Picked up at** to the date and time the child actually left.
5. Check **Centre closed at** (it starts at the centre's closing time; change it if your room closed at a different time).
6. Add anything useful in **Notes (optional)**, for example "Parent called at 5:50, stuck in traffic".
7. Read the suggested fee in the grey box, then tap **Log for review**.

A green message says **Logged — waiting for a director or admin to decide**, and the record appears on the **Pending** tab. If the child was picked up within the grace period, you see **Logged — within the grace period, no fee** instead.

## Logging a late arrival

1. Tap **＋ Log late pickup**.
2. In **What**, choose **Late arrival**.
3. Pick the **Child** and enter the **Minutes late**.
4. Add **Notes (optional)** and tap **Log for review**.

## Reading the list

The tabs across the top filter the list: **Pending**, **Charged**, **Waived**, **Declined** and **All**. A red number on **Pending** shows how many are waiting for a decision. Each row shows the **Child**, **What**, **Picked up / arrived**, **Late by**, the **Suggested** fee, the **Status** and who logged it.

> 💡 Log it the same day, while the times are fresh. The pickup time you type is the centre's own clock time.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Educator | Log late pickups and late arrivals for review, and see the list |
| Centre director / agency admin | Everything above, plus **Log & charge** in one step, and **Approve & charge**, **Waive** or **Decline** a pending record |

Educators never charge a family directly; the fee only reaches an invoice after a director or admin approves it.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? It says "The pickup time must be after the close time." | The **Picked up at** time is earlier than **Centre closed at**. Check both times, including the date.
??? It says "Choose a child." or "Enter how many minutes late." | A required box is empty. Fill it in and tap **Log for review** again.
??? The child is not in the Child list. | The list shows children enrolled at your centre(s). If a child is missing, ask your director to check their enrolment.
??? It says "Could not load the children list". | The connection dropped. Close the window, check your internet and try again.
??? Does the family get a message? | Not from you logging it. The family sees the fee on their invoice only if a director or admin approves it.
