# Parent issues — answering a family's report

When a parent uses **Report an issue** in their app (a lost item, a question about a bite, a worry about their
child's day), it lands on **Parent issues**. As an educator you see the reports about the children in your own rooms.
Open one to reply to the family, leave a note for your team that the family never sees, or mark it resolved.

[[open: #parent-issues | Open Parent issues]]

You are told about a new report by a bell notification and a push on your phone. Pressing it opens the report
straight away.

## Finding a report

1. Open **Parent issues** from the menu.
2. The tiles at the top count the reports that are **Open**, **In progress**, **Resolved** and **Closed**.
3. The list starts on **Open & in progress**. Use the first drop-down to show **Open**, **In progress**, **Resolved**,
   **Closed** or **All**, and **All categories** to narrow it to one kind of issue.
4. Each row shows the category, its status, who reported it, the family, the child it is about, when, who it is
   assigned to and how many messages it has.
5. Press **↻ Refresh** to check for new reports.

## Replying to the family

1. Press the report to open it. You see the parent's message, their phone and email, and every message since.
2. Type in **Reply** ("Write to the family…").
3. If the issue is dealt with, change **Status** to **Resolved** at the same time.
4. Press **Save**. **Reply sent to the family** appears.

The family is told by a bell notification and an email, and sees your reply on their **Report an issue** screen. A
reply and a status change saved together go as one email, not two.

## Leaving an internal note

1. Open the report.
2. Type your note in **Reply**.
3. Tick **🔒 Internal note — the family will not see it**.
4. Press **Save**.

The note shows in the thread in amber with **🔒 internal note**. Only staff who can open the report see it — use it
for "Checked the cubby, not there" or "Spoke to Dad at pick-up".

## Changing the status

1. Open the report.
2. Choose a new **Status**: **Open**, **In progress**, **Resolved** or **Closed**.
3. Press **Save**. The family is told when the status changes.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Educators | See and answer reports about the children in their rooms; change the status; add internal notes |
| Centre directors | Everything above for their centres, plus **Assigned to** (give the report to a person) |
| Agency admins | All centres |
| Parents | Send reports from **Report an issue** and read every reply there |

> ⚠️ A concern a parent raises about a **staff member** never reaches educators. Only directors and admins see it, so
> nobody reads a complaint about themselves first.

## Questions and problems

??? The list says "Nothing here. ✓". | No reports match the filter. Try All in the first drop-down.
??? A parent says they reported something, but I can't see it. | It may be about a child who is not in your room, about the family in general, or about a staff member. Your director sees those.
??? I can't assign a report to someone. | Assigning is for directors and admins. Ask your director.
??? "Could not open that report" | It may have been moved to another centre, or you no longer have that child in your room. Refresh the list.
??? Does the family see my internal note? | No. Only notes without the 🔒 Internal note tick are sent to the family.
??? Will the family get an email for every note? | Only for replies and status changes, never for internal notes.
