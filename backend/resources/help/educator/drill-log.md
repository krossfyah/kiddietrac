# Recording a fire or safety drill

The **Drill log** is where you record every fire, severe weather, lockdown, vehicle, shelter or missing-child drill as soon as it is done. It also shows which drills your centre still owes this month, so nobody has to keep a paper binder. You see the centres you work in.

[[open: #drills | Open Drill log]]

## Getting to the Drill log

1. On a phone, tap **Home** in the bottom bar (Home · Daily log · One-Tap · Message Centre · Inbox).
2. Tap the **🚒 Drill log** tile ("Record a drill").
3. On a computer, open **Drill log** from the menu on the left, under **Operations**.

The screen has four tabs across the top: **🚒 Drills**, **🗺️ Fire plan**, **👩‍🏫 Educators** and **⚙️ Settings**.

## Reading what is owed

On the **🚒 Drills** tab each centre has one card. Every drill the centre must run is a row:

- the drill type with its sign (🔥 fire, 🧒 missing child, ⛈️ weather, 🔒 lockdown, 🚐 vehicle, 🏠 shelter);
- **Last** — when it was last done and by whom;
- the next due date, and a status chip: **On track**, **Due**, **Due soon**, **Overdue**, **Done**, **Missed** or **Not due**.

Under each row you see the drills recorded in the chosen month. To look at another month, change the month box at the top of the tab.

> ⚠️ A red **Overdue** or **Missed** chip means the drill was not recorded in time. Run it as soon as you safely can and record it, then let your director know.

## Recording a drill

1. Tap **＋ Record** on the row of the drill you just ran (or **＋ Record drill** at the top of the tab).
2. Check the **Centre**, and pick the **Room (optional)** if the drill was for one room.
3. Check the **Type** and the **When** date and time (it starts at now).
4. Enter the **Evacuation time (seconds)** and the number of **Children evacuated** (or **Children present**).
5. Type the names in **Staff present** and anything to fix in **Notes** (exit used, door that stuck, child who was upset).
6. For a fire drill, pick the **Plan used** and the **Route used** (**Primary route**, **Alternate route** or **Other**).
7. For a missing-child drill, fill in the **Room(s) / area**, **Scenario**, **Led by**, **Time until child located (seconds)**, and tick **911 call simulated** or **Parent call simulated** if you practised them.
8. Tap **Record drill**. A green **Drill recorded** toast appears and the row updates.

[[show: #drills @ Drills > ＋ Record drill | Show me]]

## Looking at or removing a drill

1. Find the drill under its row and tap it, or tap its **⋮**.
2. Choose **👁 View details** to see the whole record.
3. If you entered it by mistake, choose **🗑 Remove drill** and confirm with **Remove**.

A drill can only be removed within 24 hours of entering it; after that the menu shows **🔒 Kept until …** because the record must be kept.

## Your fire drill details

Open the **👩‍🏫 Educators** tab to see your own fire drill training dates (**Renewed** and **Expires**), your status (**Current**, **Expires soon**, **Expired** or **No dates yet**), your fire drill schedule with the **Next** date, and your drill map (**🗺️ View map**). Your director keeps these up to date.

The **🗺️ Fire plan** tab shows your centre's drawn fire plan.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Educator | Record drills for their centres, view details, remove their own drill within 24 hours, see their own training dates and map |
| Centre director | All of the above for their centres, plus set each educator's schedule, training dates and map, and mark a drill not required |
| Agency admin | Everything, for every centre, plus the **⚙️ Settings** tab |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? The Settings tab says "Nothing to set up here for your centres." | That is normal for educators. Drill rules and notifications are set by your director or agency admin.
??? A drill I need is not listed. | The list follows your province or state's rules. You can still record any drill with **＋ Record drill** and pick the **Type**. Ask your director if a required drill is missing.
??? It says "No drill rules for this jurisdiction yet." | Your agency's province or state has no built-in drill rules. Record your drills anyway; ask your agency admin to check the agency's location.
??? I put the wrong time on a drill. | Remove it within 24 hours (**⋮ > 🗑 Remove drill**) and record it again. After 24 hours, ask your director.
??? Does anyone get told when I record a drill? | Your director can choose to get a bell, phone or email notice when a drill is recorded, due or missed. You may get a reminder before your own scheduled fire drill.
