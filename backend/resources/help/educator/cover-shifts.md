# Cover shifts — taking a substitute shift

When your director has put you on the agency's **substitute list**, you are told when a centre you cover needs
someone for a shift. **Cover shifts** lists those shifts and lets you take one.

[[open: #cover-requests | Open Cover shifts]]

**Where to find it:** side menu → **Cover shifts**. The screen opens under **🤚 Cover shifts**. The "Cover needed"
notice and email open it at that shift.

## Taking a shift

1. Open **Cover shifts**. **Open shifts** lists each shift: the day and time, the centre and room, and the reason.
2. Press **Take this shift**.
3. Usually the first person to take a shift gets it, and it moves to **Your cover** marked **Yours**. If your agency
   asks a director to confirm, it shows **Waiting for a director** until they do. You get a notice either way.

If a shift has no **Take this shift** button, the reason is shown under it: for example you are already working then,
or it has started.

## Giving a shift back

1. Find it under **Your cover**.
2. Press **Give it back** and confirm. The shift opens again for the rest of the list and your director is told.

You can give a shift back only before it starts. After that, call your director.

## Not on the list?

The screen says **You are not on your agency's substitute list**. Ask your director to add you. They choose the
centres you cover and whether you are told in the app, by email or both.

## FAQs

??? I was told about a shift but it is not listed. | Someone else took it first, or it is no longer needed.
??? I stopped getting cover notices. | Your director may have set you to inactive or changed how you are told. Ask them.
??? Does a shift I take go on my schedule? | When your agency uses KiddieTrac HR and the shift names a room, yes: it goes on the rota.
