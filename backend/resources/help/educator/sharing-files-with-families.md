# Sharing files with families

**Files** (📎 in the menu) is where you send printables, homework and notes to the families you work with, and where you see what they send back.

## Sending a file

1. Open **Files** and press **📤 Send files** — or, on a child's record, press **📎 Send a file** to start with that family already picked.
2. Drop files into the box, or choose them. You can send up to 10 at a time, 20 MB each: PDF, pictures, Word, Excel, PowerPoint or text.
3. Add a title and a note if you like.
4. Choose who gets it: tick one or more families, or press **All families in [your room]**.
5. Press **Send**.

You can only send to the families of children in your care — the same children you see on your roster.

## What families see

Parents get a notification in their app (and, unless your agency has switched it off, an email saying a new file is waiting — the file itself is never attached to the email). They open it in **Files**, where they can view or download it.

## Files families send you

When a parent sends something back — finished homework, a signed note — it appears under **Received** with a dot until someone opens it, and the staff who care for that family get a notification.

## Deleting

Only the person who sent a file can delete it (**Delete** in the row's menu). Deleting removes it for everyone it was sent to. Every download and deletion is recorded in the audit log.
