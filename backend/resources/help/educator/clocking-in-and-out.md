# Clocking in and out

The **Time clock** records the hours you work and tells the centre who is on the floor, which keeps ratios honest. Most centres need you clocked in before you can check children in or out or log care. You can clock in from the **Time clock** screen or from the clock strip at the top of your Home and Today screens.

[[open: #time-clock | Open Time clock]]

## Clocking in

1. Open **Time clock** from the menu (or tap the clock strip at the top of **Today**).
2. Tap the green **Clock in** button.
3. The line above it changes to "You're clocked in since …" with the hours so far, and the button turns red and says **Clock out**.

> ℹ️ If your agency uses the HR add-on with a location check, you may be asked to share your location once for this clock-in.

## Taking lunch or a short break

1. While clocked in, tap **🍽️ Lunch** or **☕ Short break** under the big button.
2. The screen shows "You're on lunch" (or "a break") and the button says **I'm back**. You stay on today's shift; you are just off the floor.
3. When you return, tap **I'm back** and confirm.

> ⚠️ While you are on a break you cannot check children in or out: the ratio no longer counts you on the floor.

## Clocking out

1. Tap the red **Clock out** button (on the clock strip, choose **👋 End my day**).
2. If children you signed in are still signed in, a warning lists them. Sign them out first, or tap **Go ahead anyway** if someone else is taking over.
3. The shift closes and appears under **Recent punches** with its length.

## Checking your hours

**Recent punches** lists your shifts: the date, the in and out times, lunch or break marks, and the hours. A shift still open says **In progress**. For weekly totals and payslips, open **My hours** (it may be called **Payroll** on your screen; see [My hours and my pay](my-hours-and-pay)).

## Reminders and automatic clock-outs

Your agency can send reminders by email, push or both, plus a notice in the app:

- **Still clocked in** — after your rostered shift ends (or your usual day plus about an hour, never more than 8 hours) you get an evening reminder. A shift left open on an earlier day gets one too.
- **Children still signed in** — a list of children you signed in who were never signed out. Sign each one out, or use **⏱ Fix** to record the real pick-up time.
- **Clocked out automatically** — if you still did not clock out, the agency's auto sign-off closes your shift that evening. You, your director and the agency admins are told. The time it used is a placeholder, not when you left.

If this happens 3 or more times in 7 days, your director and the agency admins are told it has become a pattern.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Educator | Clock themselves in and out, take breaks, see their own punches. |
| Centre director / agency admin | The same for themselves, plus correct anyone's punch from timesheets. |
| Parent | Not shown. |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? I tapped a check-in and got "Clock in first". | You need to be clocked in before checking children in or out. Tap **Clock in now**, or **Not now** to leave it.
??? The strip says "Couldn't check your shift". | The app could not reach the server for a moment. Tap **Retry**. If it keeps failing, tell your director rather than starting the day unclocked.
??? I forgot to clock in or out. | Tell your director. They can add or fix the punch. Do not clock out the next day to close an old shift: that records every hour in between.
??? I got an email saying I was clocked out automatically. | Your agency closes open shifts each evening. Tell your director the time you actually left so they can correct it.
??? It says "Could not update the clock." | The change did not save. Wait a moment and tap the button again; the screen refreshes to your real state.
??? Does clocking out sign me out of the app? | No. It only ends your shift. Use **Sign out** on a shared tablet so the next person signs in as themselves.
