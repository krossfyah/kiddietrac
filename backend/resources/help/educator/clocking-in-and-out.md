# Clocking in and out

Your time clock is how your hours are recorded and how the centre keeps ratios honest. It takes one tap.


## Quick steps

1. Open your **Home / Today** screen.
2. Tap the **time clock strip** across the top. It flips to **Clocked in** and starts counting.
3. Care for your room as normal — check-ins and care logs work once you are clocked in.
4. At the end of your shift, tap the strip again and **confirm**.
5. Check **My hours** if you want to see the week's running total.

If a check-in does nothing, you are almost certainly not clocked in yet — start at step 2.

## Clocking in

At the start of your shift, tap the **time clock strip** at the top of your Home/Today screen. It flips instantly to **"Clocked in"** and starts counting your shift.

You don't need to open a separate screen — the strip does it in place, so you're ready to start with the children right away.

## Clocking out

At the end of your shift, tap the strip again. It asks you to confirm (so you can't clock out by accident), then closes your shift.

## Why you clock in before caring

Most centres require you to be clocked in before you can **check children in or out** or log care. This isn't red tape — it ties every logged action to a real educator who is actually on the floor, which matters for ratios and for safety records.

If you tap a check-in and nothing happens, check that you're clocked in first.

## Seeing your hours

Your recorded shifts live under **My hours** (and **My schedule** if your centre schedules shifts). There you can see:

- Hours you've actually clocked
- Your scheduled shifts, if set
- The running total for the week

## If the strip says it could not check

Sometimes the strip reports **"Couldn't check your shift"** instead of your status. That
means the app could not reach the server for a moment — a lift, a dead spot, a slow
morning on the network. It is not a sign that your account is wrong.

Tap **Retry**. The strip is tappable in that state, so you can also clock in straight from
it once the connection comes back. If it keeps failing, tell your director rather than
starting the day unclocked.

## Where to see your pay

**My hours** has an **Issued** tab showing the payslips the agency has actually issued
you, alongside what you have earned by week. Your payment details — e-Transfer or direct
deposit — live under **Settings → My profile & security**. See
[My hours and my pay](my-hours-and-pay).

## "Payroll" means hours, not pay

In Kiddietrac, the time clock records **hours worked** — it is not a payroll or pay-rate system. Your centre uses these hours in whatever way they run payroll. If a shift looks wrong, tell your director; they can correct a punch.

## Reminders and automatic clock-outs

Your agency can send you reminders, by email, push notification or both, plus a notice in the app.

- **Still clocked in.** If you are still on the clock after your rostered shift has ended, or after your usual day plus about an hour (never more than 8 hours), you get a "you're still clocked in" reminder in the evening. A shift left open on an earlier day gets a reminder too, asking you to have your director set the correct out time.
- **Children still signed in.** At the same time, if children you signed in have not been signed out, you get a list of them. Sign each one out at pick-up. If they left earlier, use **⏱ Fix** to record the real pick-up time.
- **Clocked out automatically.** If you still did not clock out, the agency's auto sign-off closes your shift that evening. You get a notification, a push and an email saying so, and your director and the agency admins get a copy.

If it happens **3 or more times in 7 days**, your director and the agency admins are told it has become a pattern.

Headcount reminders stop at the end of your rostered shift, or the centre's closing time if you have no roster, so staying late does not mean a stream of reminders.

## Common questions

**"I forgot to clock in this morning."**
Tell your director as soon as you remember — they can add or adjust the punch so your hours are right.

**"I forgot to clock out and it ran overnight."**
Same thing — let your director know and they'll fix the shift length. Don't clock out the next day to close it: that would record every hour since you clocked in.

**"I got an email saying I was clocked out automatically."**
Your agency closes shifts nobody clocked out of, at a set time each evening. The time it used is a placeholder, not when you left. Tell your director or administrator the time you actually left so they can correct it. See **Reminders and automatic clock-outs** above.

**"The clock says I'm clocked in but I'm sure I clocked out."**
Tap the strip to check the current state, then clock out if needed. If it's genuinely stuck, refresh the app; if it persists, tell your director.

**"Does clocking out sign me out of the app?"**
No. Clocking out just ends your shift. To leave the app entirely, use **Sign out** — do that on a shared tablet so the next educator signs in as themselves.

## Good habits

- Clock in the moment you arrive on the floor, and out when you leave.
- On a shared tablet, sign out at the end of your day so hours never get logged under the wrong name.
- Glance at **My hours** at the end of the week to catch any missed punches early.
