# Running a report for your room

**📋 Reports** gives you ready-made, print-ready reports for the room(s) you are assigned to: attendance, a daily overview, staff hours, observations, incidents and headcounts. Use it when your director asks for a sign-in sheet, when you prepare for a licensing visit, or to look back over a week.

[[open: #reports | Open Reports]]

## Getting there

1. On a phone, tap **Home** in the bottom bar (Home · Daily log · One-Tap · Message Centre · Inbox).
2. Tap the **📊 Reports** tile ("Your rooms & hours").
3. On a computer, open **Reports** from the menu on the left, under **Administration**.

## Running a report

1. In **Room**, keep **All my rooms** or pick one room.
2. Set the dates in **From** and **To**. They start at the last 30 days. The dates apply to dated reports (attendance, staff hours, observations, incidents).
3. Under **Centre reports**, tap the report you want:
   - 🕘 **Attendance log** — every child check-in and check-out for the period;
   - 🗓️ **Daily overview** — one line per child per day: attendance plus meals, naps, diapers and activities;
   - ⏱️ **Staff hours** — clock-in and clock-out hours worked;
   - 🔭 **Observations** — the learning observations recorded;
   - 🩹 **Incidents & injuries** — incidents logged in the period;
   - 👀 **Headcounts** — every headcount taken, expected against counted, anyone not seen and the follow-up (pick a single day for the daily log).
4. Wait for **Generating report…**. The report appears under the cards, with your agency's logo, the room, the dates and the number of rows.

## Printing or saving a report

1. Scroll to the bottom of the report.
2. Tap the print button (**Print**) to print it or save it as a PDF from the print window.
3. Or use **Export to PDF**, **Export to CSV** or **Export to Excel** to download a file.

> ⚠️ Every report is marked **PRIVATE & CONFIDENTIAL**. Keep printed copies in the centre and never share them outside your team.

> 💡 For a single day's sign-in sheet, set **From** and **To** to the same date before you tap **Attendance log**.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they see |
|---|---|
| Educator | The six room reports above, for the rooms they are assigned to only |
| Centre director | All reports for their centre, plus **📅 Scheduled reports** to email a report automatically |
| Agency admin | Every report, for every centre (billing, enrolment, waitlist and more), plus scheduling |

Educators do not see billing or family reports, and cannot schedule reports.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? It says "No rooms are assigned to you yet." | Your reports cover only your rooms. Ask your director to assign your room(s); the reports appear right after.
??? A report is empty. | Nothing was recorded in that room for those dates. Widen **From** and **To**, or pick **All my rooms**.
??? It says "The start date is after the end date." | **From** is later than **To**. Swap them and try again.
??? I need a family or billing report. | Those are for directors and admins. Ask your director to run it.
??? Does running a report send anything to families? | No. A report is only shown to you; nothing is emailed unless a director sets up a scheduled report.
