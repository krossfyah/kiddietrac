# Quick notes

**Notes** is a notepad for the things you need to remember. Jot a note in seconds and keep it just for you, or share it with your staff. You can pin it, colour it, add pictures, link it to a child, family or staff member, and set a reminder.

[[open: #notes | Open Notes]]

## Writing a note

1. Open **Notes** from the menu.
2. Add a **Title (optional)**, then write the note in the box below it (**Write a quick note…**).
3. Choose who sees it: **🔒 Just me** or **👥 Agency**.
4. Pick a colour if you like.
5. Save it. On a computer press **Enter** (**Shift+Enter** starts a new line); on a phone or tablet tap **Save**. A **Note saved** message appears and the note joins the list.

The list shows each note's title on one line; a note with no title is listed by its first line. Tap a title to open the whole note.

## Adding pictures

1. While writing, or in **Edit note**, press **🖼 Add picture**. On a phone this opens your camera or photos.
2. On a computer you can also paste a screenshot (**Ctrl+V**, or **Cmd+V** on a Mac) or drag a picture onto the note.
3. Tap a picture to see it full size, or press **✕** on it to remove it.

A note can have up to 5 pictures of up to 10 MB each. They are made smaller on your device and their location details are removed before they are sent. A picture is seen by exactly the people who can see its note and is deleted with it. In the list, **🖼 3** means the note has 3 pictures.

## Pinning, colour, links and reminders

1. Press **📌** (**Pin to top**) to keep a note at the top of the list.
2. Press **🎨** (**Colour**) to colour it.
3. Press **✏️** (**Edit**) to change the text, link the note to a child, family or staff member, or set a **Reminder** date. A linked note also shows on that record's page for the staff who can see that record.
4. On the reminder date the note shows in your calendar and in Tasks in the right-hand panel. A private note's reminder is only ever shown to you.

## Finding a note

1. Switch between **🔒 My notes** and **👥 Agency notes** at the top of the list.
2. Type any word from the title or text in **Search notes**.

## Who sees a note

**Just me** notes are private: only you can ever see them, not your director, not an admin, not KiddieTrac support. **Agency** notes are for the staff of your agency: any staff member who can see one can edit it, and every change is kept. A shared note can be for the whole agency or for one centre (a note linked to a child or family belongs to that child's centre). A shared note stays shared.

Press **🕘** (**History**) on a shared note to see who created it and who changed what, and when. Changes to shared notes are also kept in the audit log.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Any staff member | Write private notes and shared notes, edit shared notes they can see, see the history of shared notes. |
| The note's writer | Delete their own note with **🗑️**. Directors and agency admins can also delete shared notes in their centres. |
| Parents | Do not have Notes and never see staff notes. |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? I don't see Agency notes or the 👥 Agency button. | Sharing needs an active agency. If you work for more than one, pick the agency first: "Pick an agency to see and share agency notes."
??? It says "Write something first." | The note is empty. Type some text before saving.
??? It says "A note can have up to 5 pictures." | Remove a picture with **✕** before adding another.
??? It says "The note is saved, but a picture could not be added". | The text is safe. Open the note with **✏️** and try the picture again; check it is under 10 MB.
??? Can I make a shared note private again? | No. A shared note stays shared. Copy what you need into a new **🔒 Just me** note, then delete the shared one if you wrote it (or ask your director).
??? I deleted a shared note by mistake. | Staff no longer see it, but its history stays in the audit log. Ask an admin if it must be recovered.
??? Does anyone get a notification when I write a note? | No. Notes never send emails, texts or pushes. Only your own reminders appear, on the day you set.
