# Fire plan

Draw your centre's or home's fire escape plan, write down who does what, and send it for review. The approved plan is the one everyone follows and prints.

## Where it is

Sidebar → **Drill log** → **🗺️ Fire plan** tab. Each centre in the Drills tab also shows its plan state (no plan, waiting for review, current, review due).

## Draw the plan

1. Select **＋ New fire plan** and choose the centre (and a room, if the plan is for one room).
2. **Drawing**: start from a template (⋮ → Start from: Home or Room) or a blank grid. Use **Wall** and **Room** (they snap to the grid), **Pen**, **Stamps** (exit, extinguisher, smoke alarm, meeting spot, first-aid kit, electrical panel, hazard), the green **Primary route**, the amber **Alternate route** and **Text**. Undo, redo, erase, zoom and pinch all work with a finger or a mouse. You can trace over a photo of your floor plan (⋮ → Photo underlay).
3. **What we do**: fill in who, what and where for each step, including the two "if" branches: *primary exit blocked* and *child missing*.
4. **Details**: children and ages, staff, number of exits, date reviewed and notes.
5. **Save draft** keeps it private to you. **Submit for review** sends it to your director or admin.

## Review (directors and admins)

Plans waiting for review show at the top of the tab and in your submissions feed. Open one, then choose **✅ Approve** or **↩️ Ask for changes** with a comment. The educator sees the comment and makes a new version. Every version is kept.

## Drills

When you record a fire drill, pick the plan you used, the route (primary or alternate), how many children were here today and how many you evacuated, and any issues found.

## Print and post

**🖨 Print / PDF** gives the drawing with its legend on page 1 and the steps as a flow chart with the details on page 2. **📌 Post this plan** prints one landscape page to put up by the door.

## Yearly review

A plan not reviewed in 12 months shows **Review due**. Make a new version and send it for review.
