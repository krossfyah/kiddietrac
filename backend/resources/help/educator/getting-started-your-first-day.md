# Getting started — your first day

Welcome to the team! This guide gets you from your first login to confidently running your room. Give it five minutes before your first shift and you'll be ahead.

## 1. Signing in

Your director creates your account and sends an email invitation to set your password. If you can't find it, check spam or ask your director to re-send it.

On a shared classroom tablet, always sign in as **yourself** — everything you log is recorded under your name, which is exactly how it should be for accountability.

## 2. Clock in

At the start of your shift, tap the **time clock** strip (top of the Home/Today screen) to clock in. Your hours come straight from this. Clock out at the end of your shift. See **Clocking in and out** for details.

Most centres require you to be clocked in before you can check children in or out — it ties every action to a real, on-shift educator.

## 3. Find your room

The **Today** screen is your home base. At the top you'll pick your **room** (if you cover more than one) and see your live ratio. Below that is your **roster** — a card for every child, colour-coded by whether they're in, gone home, or not arrived yet.

## 4. Know your day at a glance

Right at the top of Today is the **"Today at a glance"** brief — the handful of things that need your attention: who's not checked in yet, whether a lesson plan is set, meals still to log, and more. Start your day there. See **Today at a glance**.

## 5. The rhythm of the day

Your core daily loop is simple:

1. **Check children in** as they arrive (or confirm the parent's scan)
2. **Log moments** through the day — meals, naps, diapers, activities
3. **Share a photo or two** so parents feel connected
4. **Record observations** when you notice something worth remembering
5. **Check children out** at pickup
6. **Clock out**

Each of these has its own guide — start with **Checking children in and out** and **Quick logging events**.

## 6. Safety first, always

Before anything else, know your children's **allergies and medical alerts**. Open any child's record to see them — see **Child records, allergies and safety**. If something goes wrong, **When something happens** walks you through incident reporting.

## Tips for a smooth start

- **Log as you go**, not all at once at the end — it's faster and more accurate.
- **Keep the tablet handy** but children first, screen second.
- **Ask your director** anything — they'd far rather answer a question than have a guess.
- **Don't stress about a perfect record.** Log what you can; the tool is here to help you, not to pile on work.

## Where to get help

Every Help page has an **Ask** button — our assistant reads all these guides and your role, and answers in plain English. For anything it can't solve, your director is your first stop.
