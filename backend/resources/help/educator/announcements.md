# Announcements — reading and posting notices

**Announcements** (**Operations › Announcements**) is where you read the notices your centre and agency send to
staff, and where you post your own notice to the families in your centre — a reminder about pyjama day, a note that
the room is going on a walk, a request to bring sunscreen. Open it when the menu shows a count of new notices, or
when you want to tell families something.

[[open: #announcements | Open Announcements]]

## Reading notices

1. Open **Announcements**. The list shows every notice for you and every notice you sent, newest first.
2. A new notice has a dot. Tap a row to read it; it is marked read.
3. Type in **🔍 Filter…** to find a notice by its words.
4. Press **✓ Mark all read** to clear every new dot at once.

A notice with a **⏰ Due** date is asking you to do something by then. If it has a **✓ Mark as done** button, press it
when you have done it; the sender sees who is done.

## Posting a notice to families

1. Press the **+** button (**New announcement**). On a phone, press **+ New alert**.
2. Under **Where**, pick your centre (**📍 Centre: …**). Only agency admins can send to every centre.
3. Under **Who**, choose the audience. **All parents** is the default; you can also choose **All educators**,
   **All contractors**, **All admins and directors** or **Everyone**. The line underneath reads your choice back.
4. Add a **Picture (optional)** or a **PDF (optional)** if you have one.
5. Type the **Title** and the message. Use the toolbar for bold, lists and links.
6. Tick how it goes out: **📧 Send email**, **📱 Send SMS** and **🔔 In-app notification**.
7. To send it later, open **Schedule for later (optional)** and pick a date and time.
8. Press **Send**. The notice appears at the top of your list.

> 💡 Keep a text message short: SMS sends plain text only, and only to people with a mobile number on file.

## Asking families to do something

1. In the new notice, open **⏰ Asking for something? Due date & reminders (optional)**.
2. Set **Due by** to the date it is needed.
3. Under **Remind them (up to 2)**, pick **3 days before**, **1 day before**, **Morning of** or **Custom…**.
4. Under **Who gets the reminder**, choose only the people who have not tapped **✓ Done** yet, or everyone again.
5. Press **Send**. Families see **⏰ Please do this by …** and a **✓ Mark as done** button.

To see who has done it, open the notice's **⋮** menu and press **👁 View details**: **Who’s done?** lists each family.

## Managing your list

Each row has a **⋮** menu:

- **👁 View details** — the full notice, how it was sent and, for a request, who is done.
- **🔁 Send again** — sends a notice you manage once more.
- **✓ Mark read** / **● Mark unread** — for your own list only.
- **🗄 Archive** — hides it from your list. **🗄 Archived** at the top shows archived notices; **↩ Unarchive** brings one back.
- **🗑 Delete** — only on notices you are allowed to remove. It also removes it from every family's News.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Educators | Read staff notices; post to their own centre; email, text and in-app |
| Centre directors | All of that, plus **📞 Voice call** |
| Agency admins | All of that, plus **🏢 Everywhere — all centres** |
| Home visitors | Read only |
| Parents | Read the notices sent to them in **📰 News** |

## Questions and problems

??? I can't see the + button. | Home visitors read announcements but cannot post. If you are an educator and the button is missing, ask your director to check your centre assignment.
??? "No centres available for this account." | You are not attached to a centre yet. Ask your director to add you to one.
??? Why isn't Voice call offered? | Phone-call announcements are for centre directors and agency admins only.
??? A family says they didn't get the text. | Texts only reach people with a mobile number who agreed to texts, and only when your agency's texting is set up. They still see the notice in the app.
??? Can I take back a notice I sent? | Use ⋮ › 🗑 Delete if it is offered: it disappears from families' News. An email or text already sent cannot be recalled.
??? A notice says "⏱ scheduled". | It goes out at the time you picked, by every channel you ticked.
