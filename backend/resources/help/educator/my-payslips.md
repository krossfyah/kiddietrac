# My payslips and tax forms

If your agency runs payroll in KiddieTrac, your pay is in **My HR**.

## Tax forms

Open **My HR → Tax forms** and choose **Fill in my TD1** (in Quebec, the TD1 and the TP-1015.3). It goes to **My forms** for you to sign. Once signed, it is used for every pay that tax year. Until you sign one, only the basic personal amounts are used.

## My payslips

**My HR → My payslips** lists each payslip with the pay date, gross and net pay, and your totals for the year. Open one to see every deduction (CPP, EI, income tax and any others), your year to date and what your employer paid on your behalf.

When a payslip is ready you get an email saying so. For your privacy it has no amounts in it: sign in to see them. Questions about your pay go to your office.
