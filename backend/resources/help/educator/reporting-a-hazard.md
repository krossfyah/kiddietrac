# Reporting a hazard

If you spot something unsafe at the centre — a broken gate, a spill that will not dry, a loose board, a smell of gas — report it so your director can deal with it and, if needed, tell parents.

1. Open **Report a hazard** from your menu or home screen.
2. Say what it is and where it is, and pick how serious it is.
3. Add what you have already done (for example, "taped off the area, children moved inside").
4. Press **Report hazard**.

[[open: #hazards | Report a hazard]]

Your centre's directors and your agency admins get a notice and an email straight away. Underneath the form you can see the hazards still open at your centre and what has been done about them.

## When parents are told

If your director sends parents a notice about a hazard, or tells them it is resolved, you get a copy too — a notice in the app and an email marked **Copy**. It is exactly what parents read, so you know what to say if a parent asks at pick-up.

Keep children away from the hazard first — report it as soon as they are safe. For an injury, use **Incidents** as well.
