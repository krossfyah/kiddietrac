# Logging outdoor play

Outdoor play is a moment you can log like any other, so the day a parent reads back is
the day the children actually had.

## Logging it

1. Open **Log a moment** on your room or on a child.
2. Choose **Outdoor play**.
3. Set the time if it was not just now, add a note, and save.

Log it for the whole room in one go, or for one child when only they went out.

## Why bother

- Parents see it in the daily summary, which otherwise looks like a day spent indoors.
- Licensing visits ask how much outdoor time children get, and a logged moment is the
  answer.
- It appears in the daily log beside naps, meals and nappy changes.

## Worth knowing

- The time is recorded in **your agency's timezone**, not your device's.
- Add a note ("splash pad", "walk to the park") — parents read these.
- A field trip or a walk with GPS tracking is a separate thing; use those when you
  leave the grounds.
