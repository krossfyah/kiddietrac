# Giving medications safely

The **Medications** screen (its heading says **Medications to administer**) lists the active standing orders for the children in your rooms: what the medicine is, the dose, how often, the dates, and how many doses have been logged. You record every dose you give here, right after you give it. The record supports your care; it does not replace your training or your centre's medication procedure.

[[open: #medications | Open Medications]]

## Before you give anything

1. Find the child's card on **Medications**. Only medications a director has marked as authorized by the parent show here, and only while they are active.
2. Check the five rights: right child, right medication, right dose, right time, right route.
3. Read the card's **For:** line and any instructions, and check the child's allergies and alerts on their record.
4. Look at the card's last line: "3 doses logged · last dose …" tells you whether a dose was already given today.

## Recording a dose

1. On the child's card, tap **+ Log dose**.
2. In **Dose given**, type exactly what you gave (for example "5 mL").
3. Pick the **Outcome**: **Given as prescribed**, **Partial dose**, **Refused by child** or **Missed (not given)**.
4. Add **Notes** if anything was unusual (spat some out, given with food).
5. For a narcotic or where your policy asks for it, fill in the witness field.
6. Tap **Record dose**. The card's dose count goes up and the time of the last dose changes.

> 💡 Log the dose at once, not at the end of the day. It is what stops a second educator from giving the same dose again.

## Checking what has been given

1. On the child's card, tap **View dose log**.
2. **Dose history** opens: **When**, **Dose**, **Outcome**, **By** and **Notes** for every dose.
3. Tap **Close** to go back.

## Safety rules that never change

- Never give a medication that is not on this screen, even if a parent asks at the door. It needs written authorization first.
- Never guess a dose. If an instruction is unclear, stop and ask your director and the parent.
- Store medication securely and out of children's reach.
- Watch for reactions after giving, especially a first dose, and know your emergency procedure.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Educator | See active medications for children in their rooms, log doses, read the dose history. |
| Centre director / agency admin | Add a **+ New medication**, record the parent's authorization, discontinue a medication, log doses. |
| Parent | Ask the centre to give a medication and see the doses given to their own child. |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? The medication a parent told me about is not here. | It has not been set up or authorized yet. Tell your director; do not give it until it shows on this screen.
??? The screen says "No active medications." | None of the children in your rooms has an active, authorized medication right now.
??? It says "Medication is not active … Director must authorize before doses are recorded." | The order is waiting for the parent's authorization, has ended or was discontinued. Ask your director.
??? I recorded the wrong dose or time. | Tell your director straight away so the record can be corrected properly. Do not log a second dose to "fix" it.
??? A dose went wrong or the child reacted. | Care for the child first, tell your director and the parent, and file an incident report with **Medication error** as the type (see [When something happens](when-something-happens)).
??? Is the family told when I log a dose? | Yes. The child's guardians get an in-app notice and a phone push, unless the agency or the parent switched these off.
??? I see a Discontinue button on the card. | Discontinuing is a director's job. Ask your director if a medication should stop.
