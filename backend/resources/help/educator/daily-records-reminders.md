# Your daily records reminder

Some evenings you may get an email called **Daily records reminder**. It means something
from your day was not recorded. This article explains what it says and how to clear it.

## When it arrives

Your agency chooses the time, usually early evening. It only comes:

- on a day you worked (you clocked in, signed a child in, or logged something);
- on a day the centre was open;
- when something was missing. If your records were complete, nothing is sent.

You get one at most each day. Your director and the agency admins are usually copied on
the same email.

## What it checks

Your agency sets what is expected. It can include:

- **Observations** — at least a set number each day.
- **Daily logs** — meals, naps, toileting, activities and notes together.
- **Photos and videos** shared with families.
- **Meals and snacks** — at least one logged when children were in your care.
- **This week's lesson plan** for your room or centre.
- **This week's menu**, written and published.

The first four are only checked when children were in your care that day.

The numbers match your 7 p.m. **Your day at a glance** email, so you can check one
against the other.

## Reading the email

For each missing item, the email shows what you recorded and what was expected, for
example "0 recorded (expected at least 1)", and why it matters to families.

It also says how many working days in a recent window (often 30 days) had incomplete
records. After a set number of those days, the reminder is **escalated**: the subject
starts with **Escalated:**, and the email says it has been shared with your director and
administrator under your agency's documentation policy.

At the bottom are **Ideas for tomorrow**: tips for what you missed and one for busy days.
They change every day.

You also get a notification in KiddieTrac. Tapping it opens your **Daily log**.

## What to do

1. Open KiddieTrac. The **Open KiddieTrac** button in the email takes you to the
   **Daily log**.
2. Add the entries you can still add, that evening or first thing the next day.
3. For a lesson plan or menu, talk to whoever writes them at your centre.
4. If something made recording hard that day, such as being short-staffed, tell your
   director.

> **Tip:** Logging as you go is easier than catching up. Record meals right after clean-up and naps as children wake.

??? Can I turn the email off? | No. Your agency switches it on or off for everyone. Talk to your director if you have questions about it.
??? I did record everything. Why did I get it? | The check counts what you recorded under your own name. If a co-worker logged it, it counts for them. Tell your director if the numbers look wrong.
