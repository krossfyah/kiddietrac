# Sleep room and sleep checks

In Ontario, every sleeping child under 24 months needs a **direct visual check** at the times your centre's sleep policy sets, and each check is written down. A baby monitor does not replace a check. Infants under 12 months sleep **on their back** unless a physician has directed otherwise in writing. **😴 Sleep room** lists the children under 24 months booked into your room today, who is asleep, and when each next check is due.

[[open: #sleep-room | Open Sleep room]]

## Getting there

1. On a computer, open **Sleep room** from the menu on the left, under **Operations**.
2. On a phone, tap the **Open Sleep room** button at the top of this guide. The screen works the same on a phone, one card per child.
3. Read the line at the top: it says how often your sleep policy wants under-12-month-olds and 12–24-month-olds checked.

## Putting a child down

1. Find the child's card.
2. Tap **😴 Asleep now**.

A nap logged from **Today** ("Nap down") or the nap quick-log counts too — you don't need to do both.

## Recording a check

1. Look at the child: breathing, colour and position.
2. Tap **✓ Checked** on their card.
3. Pick the **Position**, leave **Breathing normal** and **Colour normal** ticked if they are, and add a note if anything was unusual.
4. Tap **Save check**. The countdown starts again.

With several children asleep, **😴 Check all** records the whole room in one go. Each child still gets their own position and observations.

[[show: #sleep-room @ ✓ Checked | Show me]]

If a baby under 12 months is on their side or tummy, turn them onto their back and add a note saying so. You do not need the note if the child has a physician's direction on file, shown as a green **Physician direction** tag.

> ⚠️ If a child is not breathing normally, get help right away. Record the check afterwards.

## The countdown

Each sleeping child shows **Next check in**. It turns amber in the last 3 minutes and red when the check is overdue. If a check is overdue, the educators in the room get a notification. If it is still not done after a few minutes, the director is told as well.

## When the child wakes

1. Tap **🌅 Woke up** on their card.
2. The checks for that nap stop.

Signing a child out also ends the nap.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Educator | Put children down, record checks and wake-ups for their room |
| Centre director / agency admin | The same, plus the **📋 Report** tab: every check and every gap longer than the interval, with **⬇ Check log (CSV)** and **⬇ Inspector report (PDF)** |
| Agency admin | Sets the sleep policy (how often to check) in settings |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? It says "You are not assigned to a room yet." | The screen shows your own room. Ask your director to assign you to it.
??? It says "No children under 24 months are booked into this room today." | Sleep checks only cover children under 24 months. Older children's naps are logged as usual in the **Daily log**.
??? It says "Sleep checks are switched off for this centre." | An agency admin can turn them on in **Settings → Room Controls → Sleep checks**.
??? A child shows "Not signed in". | Sign them in first, so their nap and checks are recorded against today's attendance.
??? I missed a check. | Do it now and record it. The report shows the gap; tell your director what happened.
