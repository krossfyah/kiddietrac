# Sleep room and sleep checks

In Ontario, every sleeping child under 24 months needs a **direct visual check** at the times your centre's sleep policy sets, and each check is written down. A baby monitor does not replace a check. Infants under 12 months sleep **on their back** unless a physician has directed otherwise in writing.

## Putting a child down

Open **Sleep room** from the menu. Tap **😴 Asleep now** beside the child. A nap logged from Today ("Nap down") or the nap quick-log counts too.

## Recording a check

1. Look at the child: breathing, colour and position.
2. Tap **✓ Checked**. Pick the position, leave **Breathing normal** and **Colour normal** ticked if they are, and add a note if anything was unusual.
3. Tap **Save check**.

With several children asleep, **✓ Check all** records the whole room in one go. Each child still gets their own position and observations.

If a baby under 12 months is on their side or tummy, turn them onto their back and add a note saying so. You do not need the note if the child has a physician's direction on file, shown as a green **Physician direction** tag.

If a child is not breathing normally, get help right away. Record the check afterwards.

## The countdown

Each sleeping child shows **Next check in**. It turns amber in the last 3 minutes and red when the check is overdue. If a check is overdue, the educators in the room get a notification. If it is still not done after a few minutes, the director is told as well.

## When the child wakes

Tap **🌅 Woke up**. The checks for that nap stop. Signing a child out also ends the nap.
