# Checking children in and out

The roster is the heart of your day. Every child is a card, and checking them in and out keeps attendance accurate, ratios safe, and parents informed in real time.

## Reading the roster

On the **Today** screen, each child's card shows their presence at a glance:

- **Green — IN** with the arrival time: here now
- **Amber — OUT** with the time: signed out / gone home
- **Grey — NOT IN**: not arrived yet

The card's colour and a bold status pill make it easy to scan the whole room in a second.

## Checking a child in

When a child arrives:

1. Find their card (grey, "Not in yet").
2. Tap the green **Check in** button.
3. Confirm — the card flips to green **IN** with the time, and the parents are notified automatically.

The preferred path is actually the **parent** scanning the centre QR at drop-off — it's clearly them. Your **Check in** button is the backup for when a parent's hands are full or their phone is dead. Either way, the record is the same.

## Checking a child out

At pickup:

1. Tap the amber **Check out** button on the child's card.
2. Confirm — the card flips to **OUT** with the departure time.

Always confirm the person collecting is a **guardian or an authorised pickup person** (see the child's record). If someone you don't recognise arrives, check the record and, if in doubt, call the director.

## You must be clocked in

Checking children in or out usually requires you to be clocked in first — it ties the action to an on-shift educator. If a button doesn't respond, check your own clock.

## The QR check-in poster

Your centre has a check-in QR poster (by the door or sign-in desk) that parents scan. The code **rotates daily**, so a screenshot from yesterday won't work. If a parent can't scan, you can always check the child in from the roster.

## Multiple check-ins

Parents with more than one child sign them all in with a single scan. On your side, each child still appears as their own card — check the ones the roster shows as still out.

## Common questions

**"I checked a child in but the card didn't change."**
It should flip immediately. If it didn't, pull to refresh. Tapping again may tell you they're "already checked in" — that means the first one worked.

**"I checked the wrong child in / out."**
Just tap the opposite button to correct it. If you're unsure, your director can fix any attendance record.

**"A child never got checked out yesterday."**
Tell your director — they can close the record. Getting into the habit of a final roster glance at the end of the day prevents this.

## Why it matters

Attendance drives ratio safety, informs parents, and can affect billing. A quick, accurate check-in and check-out for every child is one of the most important things you do — and it takes seconds.
