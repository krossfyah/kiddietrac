# Using the tablet view

The Kiddietrac tablet view is designed for big buttons and quick taps. You'll spend your day in this view, logging children's activities.

## Logging in

1. Open `https://app.kiddietrac.com` on the tablet
2. Use the email and temporary password from your welcome email
3. Change your password after first login

## Your home screen

When you log in, you see:

- **Room pills** at the top — tap to switch between rooms (if you work across multiple)
- **Child cards** for every child enrolled in the selected room
- Each card shows: child's photo or initials, age, current status (at centre vs not), urgent flags (allergies in red)

## What to do first each day

1. **Clock in.** Tap your name in the top-right → **Clock in**. This is critical — without it, the director sees you as not working, and rooms show as BREACH.
2. **Check in arriving children.** As parents drop kids off, tap their card → **Check in**. Record their mood at arrival.
3. **Note any handoff info** the parent shared (slept poorly, has a cold, etc.) in the notes field.

## What to do throughout the day

For each child, log events as they happen:

- **Meal / snack** — what they ate and how much
- **Diaper change** — wet, BM, or both
- **Bathroom** — for older kids
- **Nap start / end** — when they fell asleep, when they woke
- **Activity** — what they did, what skill it builds
- **Mood** — happy, calm, tired, upset, sick

Parents see these events in real time on their phone. Don't worry about being perfect — log what you can.

## At end of day

1. **Check children out** as parents pick them up
2. **Record mood at departure** so parents know how their day ended
3. **Clock out** when you leave

## Things to avoid

- Don't share login credentials — your name is attached to every event you log
- Don't log activities for children in other rooms — they should be logged by their own educator
- Don't take photos of children whose parents haven't been invited yet — those photos sit in the system with no audience

## What's coming

In future versions:

- Voice logging ("Aria had her nap from 1 to 2:45") — coming in v5
- Real-time push alerts when a parent messages you
- Batch operations (log nap for the whole room at once)
