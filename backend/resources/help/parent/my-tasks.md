# My tasks

Sometimes your child care centre asks you to do something — for example "bring extra diapers", "send in a change of clothes" or "sign the field-trip form". These requests are called tasks. **My tasks** keeps them in one list, so you can see what is still to do and tell the centre when it is done.

[[open: #my-tasks | Open My tasks]]

## Finding your tasks on a phone

1. Tap **Home** at the bottom of the screen (the bar shows Home · Photos · One-Tap · Message Centre · Billing).
2. Tap the **📋 My tasks** tile.
3. When you have something to do, the tile shows a red number.

On a computer, click **My tasks** in the menu on the left.

## Reading a task

Each task is a card. It shows:

- what the centre is asking, and any extra details;
- how important it is — **High**, **Normal** or **Low**;
- where it is up to — **Open**, **In progress** or **Done**;
- 📅 the date it is due (it turns red and says *overdue* if the date has passed), and who sent it.

## Telling the centre you have done it

1. Tap **✓ Mark done** on the task.
2. A green **Task completed** message appears. The task is crossed out and the red number on the tile goes down.

The centre sees straight away that it is done.

[[show: #my-tasks @ ✓ Mark done | Show me]]

## If you need more time

1. Tap **▶ Start** to let the centre know you are working on it. The task now says **In progress**.
2. Tap **✓ Mark done** when it is finished.

Tapped **✓ Mark done** by mistake? Tap **↺ Reopen** and the task comes back.

> 💡 When there is nothing left, you see 🎉 **No tasks right now** — *You are all caught up.*

## Who can do what

| Who | What they can do |
|---|---|
| You (parent) | See the tasks sent to you, and mark them started or done |
| Your centre's staff | Send you tasks, choose the due date, and see when you have done them |

You cannot add or delete a task yourself. If a task does not make sense, send the centre a message.

## Questions and when something goes wrong

??? I have a task I don't understand. | Tap **Message Centre** at the bottom of the screen and ask your child's educators.
??? It says "Could not load tasks." | Your phone lost its connection. Check your Wi-Fi or data and open **My tasks** again.
??? It says "Could not update task". | The change did not save. Wait a moment and tap the button again.
??? The red number on the tile did not go away. | Go back to **Home**; the number updates when the screen opens again.
??? Does the centre know I finished? | Yes. As soon as you tap **✓ Mark done**, the centre sees it as **Done**.
