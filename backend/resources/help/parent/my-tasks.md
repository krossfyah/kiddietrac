# My tasks

Your centre can send you tasks — for example "bring extra diapers" or "sign the field-trip form." They collect under the **My tasks** tile on your home screen, and an "open tasks" card appears at the top when you have something outstanding.

Tap **My tasks** to see the list, then use **Start** / **Mark done** as you handle each one. Anything you complete drops off your open count.
