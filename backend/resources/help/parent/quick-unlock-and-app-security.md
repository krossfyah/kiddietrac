# Quick unlock and app security

Your child's information is private. Kiddietrac gives you fast, secure ways to get into the app without typing your password every time — while keeping a nosy passer-by out.

## Two quick-unlock options

| Option | How it works | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Fingerprint / Face unlock** | Uses your phone's built-in biometrics | The fastest, most secure everyday option |
| **PIN** | A short numeric code you set | Phones without biometrics, or as a backup |

You can turn either (or both) on in **Settings**.

## Setting up fingerprint / face unlock

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Find **Biometric unlock** and turn it on.
3. Confirm with your fingerprint or face when prompted.

Now, when you open the app, it asks for your fingerprint or face instead of your password. If the sensor can't read you, you can fall back to your PIN or password.

## Setting up a PIN

1. Go to **Settings → Quick unlock / PIN**.
2. Choose a numeric PIN and confirm it.

Your session is then locked behind that PIN. Important: your PIN actually **encrypts** your saved session, so it can't be bypassed — which also means if you forget it, you'll simply sign in again with your password (no harm done).

After several wrong PIN attempts, the app clears the quick-unlock and falls back to a full password login, just to be safe.

## Signing out vs locking

- **Locking** (closing the app) keeps you signed in behind your PIN/biometric for a fast return.
- **Signing out** ends the session completely and removes your saved login from the device — use this on a shared or public device.

You'll find **Sign out** at the bottom of the Menu / Everything-else screen, and in Settings.

## On a shared device

If more than one person uses the same phone or tablet:

- Prefer **Sign out** when you're done, rather than just closing the app.
- Don't enable biometric unlock if someone else's fingerprint is also registered on that device.

## Keeping your account safe

- Use a strong, unique password (see **Changing your password**).
- Don't share your login. If another guardian needs access, ask the director to give them their **own** account.
- If you ever think someone else has access, change your password immediately and tell your director.

## Common questions

**"It's not asking for my fingerprint anymore."**
Fully close and reopen the app. If it still doesn't prompt, toggle biometric unlock off and on again in Settings — and check the app has permission to use your phone's biometrics.

**"I forgot my PIN."**
No problem — enter your password to sign in, then set a new PIN in Settings.

**"Is my data safe if I lose my phone?"**
Your session is protected by your phone's lock plus your PIN/biometric. Still, if you lose your device, change your Kiddietrac password from another device and tell your centre.
