# I don't see my child's data

If you log in and your child isn't showing, or their data looks empty, here's what to check.

## "I don't see my child at all"

Most common cause: the centre director linked you to the **family** but the **child** isn't enrolled in the family yet.

- Contact your director
- Ask them to confirm your child is enrolled in the same family that you're linked to as a guardian

## "I see my child but no events / no activity"

Today's data only appears once educators log it. Possible reasons:

- **It's early in the day** — wait until after the morning check-in / first events
- **Educators are using paper today** — sometimes the tablet is down or they're behind
- **Your child isn't checked in yet** — until they're checked in for the day, no events will be logged
- **You're looking at the wrong date** — check the date selector

## "The timeline is missing things I know happened"

- **Hard refresh** the app: pull down the page (mobile) or Ctrl+Shift+R (desktop)
- **Check if you have a stale cache** — log out and back in
- If after that the data is still missing, it's possible the educator missed logging it. Ask your educator directly via Messages.

## "My child is shown in the wrong room"

This happens when your child is moved between rooms. The director can fix it:

- Contact your director
- Ask them to update the room assignment

The change takes effect immediately.

## "I see another family's child or info"

This should never happen. If you do see it, **screenshot it and email your director immediately** — there may be a data sharing bug we need to fix.

## "The Billing tab says 'No invoices yet'"

This is normal if your director hasn't generated this month's invoices yet, or you've only been enrolled for less than a full month.

Once your director generates invoices, you'll see them appear automatically.

## "I lost my password"

Right now, only the centre director can reset your password. Contact them. Self-service password reset is coming soon.

## When to contact whom

| Problem | Who to contact |
|---|---|
| I can't see my child | Your director |
| The timeline is missing events | Your educator (Messages) |
| The bill seems wrong | Your director |
| The app is slow / broken | Take a screenshot and email support@kiddietrac.com |
| I want to add another guardian | Your director |
| I want to change my contact info | Your director |

## Still stuck?

Click the **Ask** button at the top of any Help page. The AI assistant has access to all this documentation and can give you a more specific answer for your situation.

Or email support@kiddietrac.com with:

- Your name and centre
- What you were trying to do
- What you saw instead
- A screenshot if possible
