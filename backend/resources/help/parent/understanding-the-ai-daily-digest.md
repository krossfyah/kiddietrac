# Understanding the AI daily digest

The daily digest is a 100-150 word summary of your child's day, written by Claude (an AI) based on what the educators logged. It's designed to give you a meaningful sense of your child's day in 30 seconds of reading.

## When it appears

- **Before 4 PM** — Says "Aria's digest will be ready after 4 PM today." The day isn't over yet; not worth summarizing.
- **After 4 PM** — Auto-generates. Updates as more events are logged.
- **End of day** — Once the child is checked out, the digest is "final" and cached.

## Why AI?

A typical day for a child has 20+ events: meals, naps, activities, mood notes, observations. Reading the raw timeline takes a few minutes. The digest highlights:

- Their general mood and energy
- What they ate (and didn't eat)
- How long they napped
- The most notable activities
- Any growth moments

Plus a conversation starter — something specific to ask your child about ("ask Aria about the orange paint she made today").

## What the digest can't do

- **Make up events.** If meals weren't logged, the digest won't mention meals. Sparse logs = sparse digest.
- **Predict the future.** It only describes what happened today.
- **Diagnose medical issues.** If you're concerned about a pattern, talk to your educators or pediatrician.
- **Replace human communication.** If your child had a big day, the educators may message you directly.

## Tone

The digest is written to be:

- Warm but not saccharine
- Specific about events, not vague platitudes
- Honest about quiet days
- Focused on what's useful to you as a parent

## When the digest looks wrong

If something in the digest doesn't match what you experienced (your child says they didn't eat lunch but the digest says they did):

1. Check the raw timeline — the digest reflects what was logged
2. If the timeline shows an event that didn't happen, that's an educator entry error — message the room
3. If the digest is mischaracterizing the day, you can mention it to the director — we can adjust the AI prompts based on feedback

## What's coming

In future versions:

- Choose your digest length (brief / detailed)
- Weekly summaries (every Sunday: this week with Aria)
- Year-end developmental portfolio

## Privacy

The digest is generated from your child's data only. The AI sees:

- Your child's name, age, room
- The events logged that day

Nothing else. Other children's data is never included. The digest text is stored alongside the events and visible only to you and centre staff.
