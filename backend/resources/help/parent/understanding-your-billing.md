# Understanding your billing

The **Billing** tab shows your monthly invoices and payment history.

## How invoices work

Each month, your centre director generates invoices for all enrolled families. Your invoice shows:

- **Subtotal** — the full monthly tuition before any subsidy
- **CWELCC subsidy** — the Ontario subsidy applied to reduce your cost
- **Your portion** — what you owe (subtotal minus subsidy)
- **Due date** — when payment is expected (usually 15 days after issue)
- **Status** — sent / paid / partial / overdue

## What is CWELCC?

CWELCC stands for the Canada-Wide Early Learning and Child Care system. Ontario joined the system in 2022. It significantly reduces what families pay:

- **Most families pay about 40-50% less** than the pre-CWELCC rate
- The reduction is automatic if your child is enrolled in a CWELCC-participating centre
- No application needed for the basic subsidy

Your invoice shows the math: full tuition minus subsidy equals what you pay.

## Paying your invoice

How you pay depends on what your centre has switched on:

- **Online card or bank payment** — if your centre uses online payments, you'll see a **Pay** button inside the invoice, and you can save a method for **autopay**. See **Paying invoices and autopay** for the full walkthrough.
- **E-transfer** — usually the easiest offline method. Your centre will share their e-transfer email.
- **Cheque / bank transfer / cash** — accepted by many centres.

For offline methods, the centre director records the payment in Kiddietrac and your invoice status updates. Online card payments update automatically.

## What if I'm behind?

Talk to your director. Centres typically have policies about late payments — some charge late fees, some offer payment plans. Communication is key. Don't ghost your centre.

## Split billing (separated/divorced parents)

If you and your child's other parent split tuition, your centre can set up the billing accordingly:

- **Single** — one parent gets the full invoice
- **Split equally** — both parents get half-sized invoices
- **Custom** — any percentage split (e.g. 60/40)

Ask your director to set this up. Both parents need to be invited and marked as "Receives billing emails."

## When do invoices come out?

Most centres generate invoices on the 1st of each month, due by the 15th. Your specific centre may vary — ask your director if you're not sure.

## What if my child is absent for a month?

Most CCEYA centres have an "attendance fee" policy where you still pay even if your child is absent (vacation, illness). This keeps their spot. Check with your director about specifics.

## Annual childcare tax receipt

At year-end, your director can generate an annual receipt showing total fees paid. Useful for Canadian tax filing (claim under the Child Care Expense Deduction line). Ask your director in January or February.
