# Files from your provider

Your child's provider can send you printables, homework and notes. You'll find them in **Files** (📎 in the menu or on your home screen).

## Opening a file

New files have a dot beside them. Press **View** to open a PDF or picture in the app, or **Download** to save it. Other kinds of file (Word, Excel) download and open in their own app.

You'll get a notification when a new file arrives, and usually an email too — the email only tells you a file is waiting; the file stays safely in KiddieTrac.

## Sending something back

1. Open **Files** and press **📤 Send files**.
2. Drop in or choose up to 10 files (20 MB each): PDF, pictures, Word, Excel, PowerPoint or text.
3. Add a note if you like — for example "Homework for Monday".
4. Press **Send**. It goes to your child's provider.

You can delete a file you sent from the **Sent** tab.
