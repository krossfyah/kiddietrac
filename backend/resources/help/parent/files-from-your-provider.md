# Files from your provider

**Files** (📎 in the menu, or the **📎 Files** tile on your home screen) is where your child's educator or provider
sends you printables, homework, activity sheets and notes — and where you send things back, such as finished homework,
a signed note or a photo of a document. Open it when you get a "new file" notification or email, or when you want to
send something to the centre.

[[open: #files | Open Files]]

The screen has two tabs: **Received** (files sent to you) and **Sent** (files you sent).

## Opening a file sent to you

1. Open **Files**. You start on **Received**. The tab shows how many are new, for example **Received (1 new)**.
2. New files have a dot. Each row shows the title, any note, who sent it, the date and the size.
3. Press **View** to open a PDF or picture inside the app.
4. Or press **Download** to save it to your phone or computer. Word, Excel and other files open in their own app after
   downloading.

On a phone, **View** and **Download** may be in the row's **⋮** menu.

> ℹ️ The email you get only says a file is waiting. The file itself is never attached: it stays safely in KiddieTrac.

## Sending something back

1. Open **Files** and press **📤 Send files**.
2. Drop files into the box, or press **choose files**. Up to 10 files, 20 MB each: PDF, pictures, Word, Excel,
   PowerPoint or text. A photo from your phone is fine.
3. Add a **Title (optional)** and a **Note (optional)**, for example "Homework for Monday".
4. Check the line under the note: **Goes to your child's provider.**
5. Press **Send**. A green **Sent** message confirms it, and the screen moves to **Sent**.

The staff who care for your child get a notification and, usually, an email saying you sent a file.

## Deleting a file you sent

1. Open the **Sent** tab.
2. Find the file and press **Delete**.
3. Confirm. It disappears for the centre too.

You can only delete files you sent yourself. A file the centre sent you stays until they remove it.

## Who sees your files

- Files you send go to the staff who care for your child, and your centre's director and admins can see them.
- Other families never see your files.
- Every download and deletion is recorded in the centre's audit log.

## Questions and problems

??? I got an email about a file but the list is empty. | Make sure you are signed in to the account the email was sent to. If you have children at two centres, the file may be under the other one.
??? "Each file can be at most 20 MB — remove the large one." | One of your files is too big. Remove it (×) and send a smaller copy, for example a photo instead of a scan.
??? "Choose at least one file." | Drop a file in the box or press choose files before pressing Send.
??? The file won't open. | Press Download instead of View, then open it from your downloads. Word and Excel files need an app that can open them.
??? Can I send a file to one educator only? | Files go to the staff who care for your child. For a private message, use the Message Centre.
??? Is this where I send forms the centre asked for? | If the centre emailed you a link asking for documents (an immunization card, ID), use that link. For a form to sign, use Forms to sign.
