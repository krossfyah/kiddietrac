# Messaging your educators

The Messages tab is your way to communicate with your child's room. Each child has its own conversation thread between you and the room's educators.

## What to use messages for

- Sharing important info about your child (slept poorly, has a cold, dealing with something at home)
- Asking about something you noticed in the timeline
- Coordinating pickup (different person picking up, late pickup)
- Following up on something from the day
- Thanking your educator (they appreciate it!)

## What NOT to use messages for

- **Emergencies** — call the centre directly (your director's phone is on the centre profile)
- **Booking absences or changing enrollment** — talk to the director, not the educators
- **Complaints** — direct these to the director, not the room educator

## How to send a message

1. Tap the **Messages** tab
2. Pick the conversation (if you have multiple children, pick the relevant one)
3. Type your message in the box at the bottom
4. Tap **Send**

The educator sees the message in their tablet view.

## Response time

Educators are caring for your child during the day — they can't always reply quickly. Reasonable expectations:

- **During care hours**: within an hour for non-urgent messages
- **After hours**: next business day
- **Weekends**: not until Monday morning

If something needs an immediate response, call the centre. Don't wait for a message reply.

## Tips for good messages

**Good message:**

> "Hi Maria! Aria had a tough night and woke up at 3 AM with a stuffy nose. She's not sick, just snuffly. She's been clingy this morning so might need extra reassurance at drop-off. Thanks!"

**Less helpful:**

> "Aria didn't sleep well."

(Vague, doesn't tell educator what to do with the info)

**Avoid:**

> "Why didn't you give her more food at lunch?? She came home starving!"

(Confrontational — if you have a concern, ask first to understand. The educator might have logged "ate most" because she did, but had a snack timing issue. Lead with curiosity.)

## What if I have an issue?

If something is wrong (your child is regularly coming home hungry, or you don't like how an educator is handling something), talk to the **director**, not the educator. The director can investigate and address it properly. Educators read all messages, so a difficult message to them can affect your relationship — it's much better to go through the director.

## Privacy

- Messages are visible to: you, your co-guardian (if both invited), the room's educators, and the centre director
- They're not visible to other parents or to anyone outside your centre
- Don't share login info — your name is on every message you send
