# Sending in an immunization record

**Immunization** in the menu shows what your centre has on file for your child, what is
due, and gives you a way to send in a new record without emailing anybody or bringing
paper to the door.

## Two tabs

- **💉 What is due** — the schedule your centre follows and what is outstanding for your
  child's age.
- **📄 Records you have sent** — the cards you have already sent in, and what the centre
  read off each one.

## Sending one in

1. **📷 Send in a record.**
2. Take a photo of the card, or choose a PDF. Up to 10 MB — a phone photo is normally well
   under.
3. Send.

That is the whole thing. A photo of the card is fine; it does not need to be scanned or
flattened, only readable.

## What happens next

Your centre is told straight away. **A member of staff reads the card and records the
doses from it** — that is the step that clears anything showing as due.

This is deliberate and it is not a delay for its own sake. Deciding that a particular line
on a card means a particular dose is a clinical judgement, and it is the centre's to make.
It also means an unclear photo is caught by somebody who can ask you for a better one,
rather than being silently recorded wrong.

So: after you send a record, **your child may still show as due for a short while**.
Nothing has gone missing. Once staff have read it, the status updates and the doses appear
under **Records you have sent**.

## Worth knowing

- **Sending the same card twice is harmless.** A dose already recorded is not recorded
  again, so a clearer second photo will not double anything up.
- A record you have sent stays on file — it is part of your child's health record, so it
  is not something you can delete afterwards. Ask your centre if something needs removing.
- Opening a record shows it inside the app, with a **Print** button. See
  [Opening and printing documents](opening-and-printing-documents).
- If the due list looks wrong for your child's age, ask your centre — the schedule is
  theirs to set, and it varies by province and by agency.
