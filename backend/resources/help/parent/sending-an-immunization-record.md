# Sending in an immunization record

**Immunization** in the menu shows what your centre has on file for your child, what is
due, and gives you a way to send in a new record without emailing anybody or bringing
paper to the door.

## Two tabs

- **💉 What is due** — the schedule your centre follows and what is outstanding for your
  child's age.
- **📄 Records you have sent** — the cards you have already sent in, and what the centre
  read off each one.

## Sending one in

1. **📷 Send in a record.**
2. Take a photo of the card, or choose a PDF. Up to 10 MB — a phone photo is normally well
   under.
3. Optionally, under **What does this record show?**, tick each immunization you can see on
   the card, with a date if it is readable. Anything not on the list can be added with
   **+ Add a dose that is not listed**.
4. Add a note if something needs explaining, then **File record**.

A photo of the card is fine; it does not need to be scanned or flattened, only readable.

### What your ticks do

Ticking what the card shows helps the centre read it faster. It does **not** record the
doses and it does not change what shows as due: staff confirm each one against the card
before it counts. If you are not sure, leave the ticks blank and just send the photo.

## What happens next

Your centre is told straight away. **A member of staff reads the card and records the
doses from it** — that is the step that clears anything showing as due.

This is deliberate and it is not a delay for its own sake. Deciding that a particular line
on a card means a particular dose is a clinical judgement, and it is the centre's to make.
It also means an unclear photo is caught by somebody who can ask you for a better one,
rather than being silently recorded wrong.

So: after you send a record, **your child may still show as due for a short while**.
Nothing has gone missing. Until staff have read it, the record shows **Details pending**
under **Records you have sent**, with anything you ticked listed as *not confirmed*. Once
they have, the status updates and the doses appear against the record.

## Exemptions

If your child has a religious, conscientious or medical exemption, send the exemption form
in the same way. Your centre records the exemption from it. Exempt doses show as **Exempt**
on **What is due**, with the reason, and you are not reminded about them. Only the centre
can record or remove an exemption.

## Reminders from your centre

Your centre may choose to email you — and send an app notification — when a dose is
overdue or coming up, or when they do not have a record for your child yet. If you have
sent a record and nothing has been read off it yet, you are not asked for it again — it is
waiting for the centre. A reminder only lists what the centre has recorded, so if it names
a dose your child has already had, send the updated record.

## Worth knowing

- **Sending the same card twice is harmless.** A dose already recorded is not recorded
  again, so a clearer second photo will not double anything up.
- A record you have sent stays on file — it is part of your child's health record, so it
  is not something you can delete afterwards. Ask your centre if something needs removing.
- Opening a record shows it inside the app, with a **Print** button. See
  [Opening and printing documents](opening-and-printing-documents).
- If the due list looks wrong for your child's age, ask your centre — the schedule is
  theirs to set, and it varies by province and by agency.
