# Sending in an immunization record

**Immunization** shows, for each of your children, what is still needed, what the centre has on file, and a place to send in a new record. You no longer need to email anybody or bring paper to the door. Your child's educator and the centre are told as soon as a record arrives.

[[open: #immunizations | Open Immunization]]

## Checking what is due

1. Open **Immunization** from the menu or the **Immunization** tile (**Send in your records**).
2. Find your child's card. Each child has their own.
3. Stay on **💉 What is due**. Each dose shows **Overdue**, **Due**, **Due soon**, **Not yet due**, **Recorded** or **Exempt**.
4. Press **Show the rest of the schedule** to see doses that are not due yet.

The list is worked out from your child's date of birth and the schedule your centre follows, so it is complete even when nothing has been typed in yet.

## Sending a record in

1. Press **📷 Send in a record** on your child's card.
2. Take a photo of the immunization card, or choose a PDF. You can choose several files at once (up to 10, 10 MB each). A phone photo is fine as long as it is readable.
3. If you like, under **What does this record show?**, tick each immunization you can see on the card, with a date if it is clear. Use **+ Add a dose that is not listed** for anything missing from the list.
4. Add a note if something needs explaining, then press **File record**. **Thank you — the centre has been told.** appears.

### What your ticks do

Ticking what the card shows helps the centre read it faster. It does **not** record the doses and does not change what shows as due. Staff confirm each dose against the card before it counts. If you are not sure, leave the ticks blank and just send the photo.

## What happens next

1. The centre is told straight away.
2. A member of staff reads the card and records the doses from it. That is the step that clears anything showing as due.
3. Until then the record shows **Details pending** on **📄 Records you have sent**, with anything you ticked listed as not yet confirmed.

So your child may still show as due for a short while after you send a record. Nothing has gone missing. Deciding that a line on a card means a particular dose is the centre's call, and an unclear photo gets caught by someone who can ask you for a better one.

## Exemptions

If your child has a religious, conscientious or medical exemption, send the exemption form the same way. The centre records the exemption from it. Exempt doses show as **Exempt** on **💉 What is due**, with the reason, and you are not reminded about them. Only the centre can record or remove an exemption. In some provinces and states only medical exemptions are accepted; the screen says so under the title.

## Who can do what

| Who | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Parents and guardians | See what is due, send records and exemption forms |
| Educators | See records and what is due for their children |
| Centre director and agency admins | Record doses and exemptions from what you sent, set the schedule, send reminders |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? I ticked a dose but my child still shows it as due. | Your ticks are a note for the centre. The dose counts once staff have checked it against the card.
??? Do I have to tick anything? | No. A readable photo is enough; the centre reads it.
??? It says "Up to 10 files at a time, please." | Send the first 10, then press Send in a record again for the rest.
??? It says "Could not file the record." | Check your connection and that each file is under 10 MB, then try again.
??? I sent the same card twice. | That is harmless. A dose already recorded is not recorded again.
??? Can I delete a record I sent? | No. It is part of your child's health record. Ask the centre if something needs removing.
??? The due list looks wrong for my child's age. | The schedule is the centre's to set, and it varies by province and agency. Ask the centre.
??? Why did I get a reminder email? | Your centre may email you, and send an app notification, when a dose is overdue or coming up, or when they have no record yet. If you have already sent a record that is still waiting to be read, you are not asked again.
