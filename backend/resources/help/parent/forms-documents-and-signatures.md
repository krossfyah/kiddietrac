# Forms, documents and signatures

From time to time your centre will ask you to read something, fill in a form, or sign an agreement — a consent form, a policy update, a photo-permission, an incident acknowledgement. Kiddietrac keeps all of this in one place so nothing gets lost in a backpack.

## Where to find forms and documents

Look in the menu for:

- **Forms to sign** — things the centre needs you to complete, sign or acknowledge
- **Documents** — a personal file of things you've signed, sent in, or the centre has shared with you

If something needs your attention, you'll usually also get a **notification**, an email and a badge, so you don't have to go looking.

[[open: #my-forms | Open Forms to sign]]

## The three tabs

**Forms to sign** has three tabs:

- **To sign** — what is waiting for you. When you're done, it says you're all caught up.
- **Drafts** — forms you started and saved but haven't signed yet. Tap **Continue** to pick up where you left off.
- **Submitted** — everything you've sent. **View my copy** opens it. Your signed copies are also kept in **Documents**.

## Filling in a form

1. Open **Forms to sign**, or tap the link in the email or notification.
2. Tap **📄 Read the form** to read it through first.
3. Then either:
   - **📝 Fill & sign** — for a form with boxes to type into. Fill it in on screen and sign it in the same place. You can save your work and come back to it: it waits under **Drafts**.
   - **✍️ Sign** — for a notice you only need to read and sign. Draw your signature and tap **Submit signature**.

Once submitted, a copy is saved to **Documents** and the centre is notified. You can reopen it any time to see what you agreed to and when.

> **Tip:** If your centre sent you forms before you had an account, the link in their email lets you fill in and sign without a password.

## When a form is sent back

Sometimes the centre needs something corrected — a missing date, a phone number with a digit missing. When that happens, the form reappears under **To sign** with a yellow **Sent back for correction** note that says exactly what to change. You'll also get an email and a notification.

Your earlier answers are still in the form, so you only need to fix what the note asks for, then sign again. Your first submission is kept as it was; the corrected one arrives at the centre as a new submission.

## When the centre counter-signs

Some forms are reviewed and signed by the centre as well. When that happens, you'll usually receive the finished copy by email, with the centre's signature on an extra page at the end. Nothing you signed is changed. If the centre corrected or completed anything on the form, the counter-signature page lists what they changed.

## When the centre asks for files

Your centre may ask you to send documents you already have — an immunization card, a photo ID, proof of address. You'll get an email with a link and one button for each thing they asked for.

- You don't need to sign in: the link works on its own for 21 days.
- Each file can be up to 15 MB. A photo from your phone is usually fine.
- The email names the person at your centre who asked, so you know it is genuine.
- Everything you send is filed in your **Documents**, and you get a receipt when the last item arrives.

## Signing and acknowledging

Some items just need you to confirm you've read them (for example, an **incident report** about your child, or a policy change). These show an **Acknowledge** or **I have read this** button. Tapping it records:

- That it was you (you're signed in)
- The exact date and time

That timestamped acknowledgement is the centre's proof that you were informed — so please only acknowledge once you've actually read it.

## Your documents are private

Anything in **Documents** belongs to you and your family. Other families can't see it, and it's tied to your account. If you ever see a document that isn't yours, screenshot it and email your director right away — that would be a bug we need to fix.

## Saving a copy

Open any document and use your device's share or save option to keep a PDF — handy for subsidy applications, tax time, or your own records.

## Common questions

??? The centre says they sent a form but I don't see it. | Check your notifications and Forms to sign. If it's not there, ask the director to re-send it.
??? Can my partner sign instead of me? | If they're a listed guardian with their own login, yes — whoever is signed in is who the signature records. For legal agreements, sign from your own account.
??? I signed something by mistake. | Contact your director. They can see what was signed and can send it back to you or withdraw it so you can sign again.
??? A form I already signed is back in To sign. | The centre sent it back. Read the yellow note — it says what to change.

## Keeping your details current

Many forms pre-fill from your profile. Keeping your **contact info, address, and emergency contacts** up to date in **Settings** means less typing and fewer mistakes when a new form arrives.
