# Forms, documents and signatures

From time to time your centre will ask you to read something, fill in a form, or sign an agreement — a consent form, a policy update, a photo-permission, an incident acknowledgement. Kiddietrac keeps all of this in one place so nothing gets lost in a backpack.

## Where to find forms and documents

Look in the **Menu / Everything else** area for:

- **Forms** — things the centre needs you to complete or acknowledge
- **My documents** — a personal file of things you've signed or the centre has shared with you

If something needs your attention, you'll usually also get a **notification** and a badge, so you don't have to go looking.

## Filling in a form

1. Open the form from your notifications or the Forms area.
2. Read it through — some forms have required fields marked with an asterisk.
3. Complete the fields and, if it asks, add your signature or tick the acknowledgement box.
4. Tap **Submit**.

Once submitted, a copy is saved to **My documents** and the centre is notified. You can reopen it any time to see what you agreed to and when.

## Signing and acknowledging

Some items just need you to confirm you've read them (for example, an **incident report** about your child, or a policy change). These show an **Acknowledge** or **I have read this** button. Tapping it records:

- That it was you (you're signed in)
- The exact date and time

That timestamped acknowledgement is the centre's proof that you were informed — so please only acknowledge once you've actually read it.

## Your documents are private

Anything in **My documents** belongs to you and your family. Other families can't see it, and it's tied to your account. If you ever see a document that isn't yours, screenshot it and email your director right away — that would be a bug we need to fix.

## Saving a copy

Open any document and use your device's share or save option to keep a PDF — handy for subsidy applications, tax time, or your own records.

## Common questions

**"The centre says they sent a form but I don't see it."**
Check your notifications and the Forms area. If it's genuinely not there, ask the director to re-send it — occasionally a form is sent to the wrong guardian on a shared family.

**"Can my partner sign instead of me?"**
If they're a listed guardian with their own login, yes — whoever is signed in is who the signature records. For legal agreements, sign from your own account.

**"I signed something by mistake."**
Contact your director. They can see what was signed and sort out a correction.

## Keeping your details current

Many forms pre-fill from your profile. Keeping your **contact info, address, and emergency contacts** up to date in **Settings** means less typing and fewer mistakes when a new form arrives.
