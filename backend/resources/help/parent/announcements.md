# Announcements — news from your centre

**Announcements** is where you read the notices your centre sends families: a closure, a pyjama day, a reminder to
bring sunscreen, a form that is due. The screen is called **📰 News**. You usually also get a notification, and an
email or text if your centre chose to send one. Open it when the menu shows a count of new items, or whenever you
want to look back at what your centre said.

[[open: #announcements | Open News]]

## Reading your news

1. Open **Announcements** from the menu. The banner says how many updates there are and how many are new.
2. New items have a teal dot and a light-blue card. They are listed newest first.
3. Tap a card to read it. It is marked read and the dot goes.
4. If the notice has a document, press the **📄** link under the message to open the PDF.
5. If you have more than one new item, press **✓ Mark all read** to clear them all.

> 💡 Notices also arrive in your notification inbox (🔔). Opening them from there marks them read here too.

## When the centre asks you to do something

Some notices ask for something by a date, for example "bring a family photo".

1. Look for the amber **⏰ Please do this by …** line on the card.
2. Do what is asked.
3. Press **✓ Mark as done** on the card. It turns green and reads **✓ Done · Undo**.

The centre sees who is done, and its reminders can skip families who have marked it done. Pressed it by mistake? Press
**✓ Done · Undo**.

## Notices just for you

A card marked **🔒 Just for you** is about your own family only, for example a new invoice. Nobody else sees it. Its
buttons take you straight to the right place.

## Tidying your list

Each card has a **⋮** menu:

1. Press **⋮** on the card.
2. Choose **✓ Mark read**, or **● Mark unread** to keep it as a reminder (offered for about a month after it was sent).
3. Or choose **🗑 Delete** and confirm. It leaves *your* list only: the centre and other families keep it.

## Who sees what

- **Parents** see the notices sent to families at their child's centre, or to the whole agency, plus their own
  **🔒 Just for you** items.
- If you have children at two centres, you see both centres' notices.
- Only the centre posts announcements. To reply or ask a question, use the **Message Centre**.

## Questions and problems

??? The screen says "No news yet". | Your centre has not posted anything for families yet. New notices appear here as soon as they are sent.
??? I got an email about a notice but can't find it here. | It may be older than your list shows, or you deleted it from your list. Ask your centre to send it again if you need it.
??? Can I reply to an announcement? | Not on this screen. Send a message to your educator or director in the Message Centre.
??? I deleted a notice by mistake. | Deleting only hides it from your list. Ask your centre to send it again.
??? Why don't I get these by text? | The centre chooses the channels for each notice, and texts only go to parents who agreed to texts. You can change what you receive in My settings › Notifications.
??? "Could not save. Try again." | Your connection dropped while marking a notice. Try again in a moment.
