# Account status — waiting for your centre

You see **⏳ Account status** when your sign-in works but your agency has not yet linked your account to your family. This often happens when the centre sends you a form to sign before it has finished setting up your family. Until the link is made, your child's updates, photos, messages and billing cannot show, so the menu only offers what works without them.

[[open: #pending | Open Account status]]

## What the screen tells you

1. Open **Account status** from the menu. The card reads **Your account is pending approval** and names your agency.
2. Read the yellow box. If you have been waiting a while, it asks you to contact your agency administrator.
3. Use the email or phone buttons on the card (when your agency has added them) to reach the agency directly.

## What you can do while you wait

1. Press **✍️ Forms to sign** to fill in and sign any forms the centre has sent you, for example an enrolment or consent form.
2. Press **📄 Documents** to open reports or files the centre has shared with you.
3. Press **👤 My account** to check your name, phone number and password, and to set up a passkey or quick unlock.
4. Check **Notifications** for messages about your account.

> 💡 Filling in the forms the centre sent you often helps them finish setting up your family sooner.

## When your family is linked

1. The agency links your account to your family (you do not need to do anything).
2. Refresh the page, or sign out and sign in again.
3. The full menu appears: **Home**, **Today**, **Photos & video**, **Message Centre**, **Billing** and the rest, with your child's information.

## Who can do what

- **Parents and guardians** waiting to be linked see only **Account status**, **Forms to sign**, **Documents**, **Notifications**, **My settings** and **Help**.
- **Agency admins and centre directors** link a parent to a family from the family's record. Only they can end the wait; KiddieTrac support cannot do it for your agency.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? I have been on this screen for days. | Your agency has not linked you yet. Use the email or phone shown on the card, or speak to the centre's director.
??? There is no email or phone on the card. | Your agency has not added contact details. Contact the centre the way you normally would.
??? My family was linked but I still see Account status. | Refresh the page or sign out and back in. On the phone app, close it fully and open it again.
??? I tried to open another screen and came back here. | Other screens need a family, so they are not available yet.
??? Will I get an email when it's done? | Not from this screen. Your centre may let you know; otherwise check again after a refresh.
