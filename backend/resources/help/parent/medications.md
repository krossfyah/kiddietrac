# Your child's medications (Health)

The **💊 Health** screen shows the medications the centre has on file for each of your children, and lets you ask the centre to give your child a medication while they are in care. Open it when your child starts a new medicine, or to check what the centre has recorded.

[[open: #medications | Open Health]]

## Seeing what the centre has on file

1. Open **Health** from the menu. The page is titled **Medications**.
2. Each of your children has a card. Under the child's name, every active or waiting medication is listed with its strength, dose and how often.
3. Read the coloured label beside the medication name: **ACTIVE** means the centre has authorised it and educators can give it; **PENDING PARENT AUTH** means the centre is still waiting on the written consent it needs before any dose.
4. The grey line under each one shows the start and end dates (or **no expiry**) and how many doses have been logged so far.

> ℹ️ Stopped and expired medications are not listed here. If a card says **No medications on file.**, the centre has nothing active or waiting for that child.

## Asking the centre to give a medication

1. On your child's card, press **💊 Ask the centre to administer a medication**. A short form opens on the card.
2. Fill in **Medication** (required), for example the name on the pharmacy label.
3. Add the **Strength** if it is on the label (for example 250 mg/5 ml).
4. Fill in **Dose** (how much each time) and **How often** (for example "twice daily, after meals"). Both are required.
5. Check **Starts** (required; today is filled in) and, for a short course, set **Ends**.
6. Optionally fill in **Reason**, **Prescribed by** (the doctor's name, if it is a prescription) and **Anything the educator should know**.
7. Press **Send request**. A green message says "Request sent — the centre has been notified and will authorise it before any dose is given." The medication then shows on the card as **PENDING PARENT AUTH** until the centre authorises it.

> ⚠️ Educators will not give a dose until the centre has authorised the medication. Bring the medicine to the centre in its original labelled container, and follow your centre's rules for any paper consent they still need.

## Who can do what

- **Parents and guardians** see the medications for their own children and can send a request. You cannot change or stop a medication here; message the centre instead.
- **Directors and agency admins** review your request, record the authorization and mark the medication active, changed or stopped.
- **Educators** see the active medications for the children in their care and log each dose they give. The dose count on your card goes up as they do.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? I see "Medication, dose, how often and the start date are all needed." | Fill in the four fields marked with a star (*) and press **Send request** again.
??? I see "This child is not linked to a centre yet". | Your child is not placed at a centre, so there is nobody to send the request to. Contact your agency.
??? My request still says PENDING PARENT AUTH. | The centre has not authorised it yet. Message the centre or speak to the director at drop-off.
??? The medicine has finished early or the dose changed. | Send a message to the centre so the director can stop or update it. A new request can be sent for a new dose.
??? A medication I gave the centre is not listed. | It may have ended or been stopped, or not been recorded yet. Ask the centre.
??? Who is told about my request? | The centre's staff (directors, admins and educators) get a notice in the app. No email is sent to you; the green message on screen is your confirmation.
??? I see "Could not load". | Check your connection and open **Health** again. If it keeps happening, use **Report an issue**.
