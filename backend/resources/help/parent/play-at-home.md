# Play at home (Play Spark)

**🧩 Play at home** shows the Play Spark activities your child joined in at the centre, month by month, with a picture of what they made and an easy idea to try the same kind of play at home. Open it when you want to know what your child has been playing, or need an activity for a rainy weekend.

[[open: #play-spark | Open Play at home]]

## Seeing what your child played

1. Open **Play at home** from the menu. The page opens on the current month.
2. Scroll the cards. Each card shows the date, the name of the activity and, when one was drawn, a picture of the end result.
3. Read the coloured tags under the picture. They name the learning areas the activity builds, such as language, movement or problem-solving.
4. To look at another month, change the month in the box at the top. The list reloads for that month.

## Trying it at home

1. Find a card with **🏡 Try at home:** at the bottom.
2. Read the short idea. It is written for a home, with everyday things you are likely to have.
3. Try it with your child, and ask them to tell you how they did it at the centre.

> 💡 Talking about the activity ("What did you build today?") helps your child remember and use the words they learned.

## Who can do what

- **Parents and guardians** see the activities for their own children only. You can read and try the ideas; you cannot change them.
- **Educators** choose the activities in Play Spark and record who joined in. Only activities logged with **We did it** for your child appear here.
- **Play at home** shows only when your agency's plan includes Play Spark. If it is not in your menu, your centre has not switched it on.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? I see "No Play Spark activities this month yet." | No Play Spark activity has been logged for your child this month yet. Try last month, or check again later.
??? A card has no picture. | Not every activity has a picture of the end result yet. The activity and the home idea are still shown.
??? I see "That picture is not available." | The picture is not ready or is no longer kept. The rest of the card is still correct.
??? Play at home is not in my menu. | Your agency has not switched Play Spark on. Ask the centre if you would like it.
??? Will I get a notice each time? | No. Play at home is a page you open when you want; the activities also show in your child's day as usual.
??? I have more than one child. | Activities for all your children at the centre are listed together, by date.
