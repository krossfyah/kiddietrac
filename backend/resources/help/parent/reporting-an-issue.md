# Reporting an issue to the centre

Something not right? A sign-out time that looks wrong, a bump nobody mentioned, a lost lunch box, a question about an invoice. **Report an issue** sends it straight to the centre, keeps a record of it, and shows you every reply in one place.

For an emergency, call the centre. A report is for things that need looking into, not for urgent help.

[[open: #report-issue | Open Report an issue]]

## Where it is

Menu → **Report an issue**. The top of the screen is the form. Underneath, **Your reports** lists everything you have sent.

## Sending a report

1. **Who is it about?** Choose your child, or **My family in general**.
2. **What is it about?** Choose a category.
3. **Reason** — choose the closest match from the list. (**Something else** has no list.)
4. **What happened?** Write it in your own words. Include the day, the time and anything that helps the centre look into it. At least a sentence is needed.
5. Press **Send to the centre**.

> **Tip:** The more detail you give the first time, the faster it gets sorted.

## The categories

- **Billing & payments** — an invoice amount, autopay, a subsidy or CWELCC fee, a receipt or tax slip.
- **Health, medication or allergy** — medication not given or given wrongly, an allergy or diet not followed, illness, a health record to update.
- **Injury or incident follow-up** — an injury you weren't told about, a question about an incident report, a bump, bite or scratch.
- **Behaviour or social** — bullying or conflict, your child's behaviour, how a situation was handled.
- **Food & meals** — not enough food, a menu question, food quality.
- **Communication** — a missing daily report or photos, a message not answered, a notice that wasn't clear.
- **Concern about a staff member** — how a staff member spoke or acted, supervision.
- **Safety or the building** — a hazard, cleanliness, doors, gates or security.
- **Pick-up & drop-off** — a wrong sign-in or sign-out time, a problem with a pick-up person, a late pick-up fee.
- **Lost or damaged item** — clothing, a bottle, lunch box or bag, a comfort item or toy.
- **KiddieTrac app or website** — can't sign in, notifications not arriving, something not working.
- **Something else** — anything that doesn't fit.

## What happens next

- The centre's **director** and your agency's **admins** are told straight away, in the app and by email.
- For a report about a child, that child's **room educators** are told too, so the people who were there can help.
- A **concern about a staff member** goes to directors and admins **only**. Educators never see it.

When the centre replies or changes the status, you get a notification and an email. Open **Report an issue** to read the whole conversation.

## Statuses

- **Open** — sent, not yet answered.
- **In progress** — the centre is looking into it.
- **Resolved** — the centre has dealt with it.
- **Closed** — finished.

## Adding more, or reopening

Under each report there is a box to add more. Type and press **Send**. The centre is told.

If a report is **Resolved** or **Closed** but it isn't sorted for you, write in the same box. Your report **reopens** and the centre sees that it came back.

## Questions

??? I can't see the Billing category. | If your login is a pick-up login rather than a paying parent's, billing questions go to the parents who pay the account. Ask them, or message the centre.
??? Can the educators read my report about a staff member? | No. Those go to the director and admins only.
??? I don't see my child in the list. | Only children currently enrolled are listed. Choose **My family in general**, or message the centre.
??? Who can see my report? | The centre staff who handle it, and you. Staff can also keep internal notes on it that you won't see.
