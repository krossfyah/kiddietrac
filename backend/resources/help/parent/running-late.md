# Running late for drop-off or pickup

Use **⏰ Running late** when you will drop your child off later than usual, or pick them up later than planned, today. You give the centre a time; until that time nobody chases you with a "where is your child?" check, and your child's educators are told straight away.

[[open: #running-late | Open Running late]]

## Telling the centre you'll be late for drop-off

1. Open **Running late** from the menu (or tap the **+** on your child's photo on **Home** and choose **Running late**).
2. Find your child's card. Under the name it says where your child is today, for example **Expected today · usually in by 8:30 AM**.
3. Press **Running late** on that card. A window opens titled **⏰ Running late:** and your child's first name.
4. If both choices are offered, press **🚗 Drop-off**.
5. In **We'll be there by**, set the time you expect to arrive. The app suggests a time about 30 minutes from now.
6. Optionally type a **Note for the centre (optional)**, for example "Doctor's appointment".
7. Press **Tell the centre**. A message confirms "The centre knows … will be there by …" and the card now shows **Arriving by** and your time.

> 💡 You can only tell the centre about today. For a whole day off, use **Not attending today** or **Sick today** from the **+** on your child's photo instead.

## Telling the centre you'll be late for pickup

1. Open **Running late**. When your child is at the centre, the card says **At the centre** and the button reads **Late for pickup**.
2. Press **Late for pickup** (the window opens on **🏠 Pickup**).
3. In **I'll pick up by**, set the time you will be there.
4. Optionally add a note, for example "Stuck in traffic".
5. Press **Tell the centre**. The card now shows **Pickup by** and your time.

## Changing the time or taking it back

1. On the child's card, press **Change or take back**.
2. To give a new time, change it and press **Save new time**.
3. If you will be on time after all, press **We're on time**. The centre is told your child is on time and the late notice is removed.

## Calling in instead

When your agency has the KiddieTrac phone line, a **📞 Call in instead** box shows under the cards with the number to call. Call from a phone number saved on your profile to report an absence or a late drop-off or pickup; a code is texted to you during the call to make sure it's you.

1. Calling from a landline, or a phone that can't get texts? Press **Set a phone PIN**.
2. Type 4 digits in **New PIN** and again in **Type it again** (not 1234, a repeated digit, a year or the end of your phone number).
3. Press **Save PIN**. You can now enter the PIN on the call instead of the texted code. **Change phone PIN** and **Remove PIN** appear once a PIN is set.

## Who can do what

- **Parents and guardians** of the child can send, change or take back a late notice for today, for their own children only.
- **Educators** in your child's room, and directors or admins as your agency chooses, get a notice in the app. They see your time and note on their Safe Arrival board.
- Pick-up contacts who are not guardians cannot send a late notice.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? The button is grey and a message says my child "is not expected at the centre today". | Your child isn't booked in today, so there is nothing to be late for. If this is wrong, message the centre.
??? It says my child "is reported absent today". | An absence is already on file for today. Message the centre if your child is coming in after all.
??? It says my child "has already gone home today". | Your child was signed out, so a pickup time no longer applies.
??? I see "Enter a time." | Pick a time in the time box before pressing **Tell the centre**.
??? Will I still get a Safe Arrival call or text? | Not before the time you gave. If your child still hasn't arrived (or been picked up) by then, the usual checks start again.
??? Does the centre get an email? | No. Staff are told in the app (bell and phone notification). You see a confirmation message on screen.
??? The Call in instead box says to add my phone number first. | The line knows you by the number you call from. Add it in **My settings**, then call from that phone.
