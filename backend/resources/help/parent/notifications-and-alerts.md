# Notifications and alerts

Kiddietrac keeps you in the loop as your child's day unfolds — a new photo, a message from an educator, a new invoice. This guide explains what you'll be notified about and how to make sure the alerts actually reach you.

## What you get notified about

| Notification | When it fires |
|---|---|
| 📷 New photo or video | An educator shares a moment from your child's day |
| 💬 New message | An educator replies to you or starts a thread |
| 🧾 New invoice | Your centre generates a bill |
| 🏆 New award | Your child earns a certificate |
| ⚠️ Important notices | Incident reports, announcements, or things needing your acknowledgement |

## Three places alerts show up

1. **Badges in the app** — little red counters on the Home, Photos, Messages, and Billing buttons. They clear automatically once you open that section.
2. **The notification bell / inbox** — a running list of everything, newest first.
3. **Push notifications on your phone** — pop up even when the app is closed (phone app only).

## Turning on phone push notifications

Push notifications only work in the installed phone app, and only if you allow them:

1. Open the app.
2. When it asks to send notifications, tap **Allow**. (If you tapped "Don't allow" earlier, you can re-enable it in your phone's **Settings → Apps → KiddieTrac → Notifications**.)
3. That's it — you'll now get alerts with sound and vibration even when the app is closed.

If you're using the website in a browser instead of the app, push is limited — install the app for reliable alerts.

## Not getting notifications?

Work through this checklist:

- **Are you using the installed app?** Browser tabs rarely deliver push reliably.
- **Is notification permission granted?** Check your phone's app settings.
- **Is the phone in Do Not Disturb / silent?** Alerts still arrive but won't make a sound.
- **Battery saver** on some phones suspends background apps — allow KiddieTrac to run in the background.
- **Did you fully close and reopen the app** after installing an update? That refreshes the connection.

## Too many notifications?

You can't yet mute individual categories from the app, but you can:

- Adjust the KiddieTrac channel in your **phone's** notification settings (e.g. silence photos but keep messages loud)
- Ask your centre to be mindful of announcement volume

We're adding per-category controls — until then, your phone's own notification settings give you fine control.

## A note on timing

Educators log the day as it happens, in between caring for children. A quiet stretch doesn't mean nothing happened — it means their hands were full. Most photos and notes appear in bursts around nap time and the end of the day.
