# Running a live demo

Nothing sells KiddieTrac like seeing it work. The **Launch demo** screen opens the
live **Test Agency** in a new tab, signed in as a demo administrator, so you can walk
a prospect through the real product.

## How it works

1. Open **Launch demo**.
2. Press **🚀 Open demo in a new tab**.
3. A new tab opens already signed in to the Test Agency — no login needed.

From there you can show attendance and check-in, billing and invoices, the parent
app, messaging, reports and more. It's the full product, using demo data only — **no
real family information** is ever shown.

## Tips for a good demo

- Have the prospect's use case in mind and head straight for the features that solve
  it (e.g. billing for an agency frustrated with spreadsheets).
- The demo session is temporary. If it expires mid-demo, just launch a fresh one.
- Follow up afterwards by adding a **Proposal** to their lead while the conversation
  is warm.
