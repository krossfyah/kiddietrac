# Running a live demo

Nothing sells KiddieTrac like seeing it work. This guide is the sales team's playbook for a good demo: how to prepare,
what to show in what order, and how to follow up. The **Launch demo** screen itself (the button that opens the Test
Agency) is explained in [Launch demo](sales-demo).

[[open: #sales-demo | Open Launch demo]]

## Before the call

1. Open the lead and read its notes, so you know their centres, their size and what hurts today.
2. Pick the two or three screens that answer that pain. Do not try to show everything.
3. If they want to see pricing or plans under confidentiality, send the NDA from **Sales › Prospect NDAs** first.
4. Press **🚀 Open demo in a new tab** a few minutes early and check the demo opens on the dashboard.

## During the demo

1. Start with the day at a glance: who is in, who is due, what needs attention.
2. Show their pain first. For example: attendance and check-in for a busy centre, invoices and autopay for an agency on spreadsheets, or messaging and photos for one that wants happier parents.
3. Show the parent side for a minute: families see their child's day, pay invoices and message the room.
4. Leave time for their questions, and write them down in the lead's notes as you go.

> 💡 Ask "What would you want to see next?" instead of guessing. The answer tells you what goes in the proposal.

> ⚠️ The demo is shared by the whole team. Never type the prospect's real details into it, and delete anything you
> added for the call.

## After the demo

1. Update the lead's stage on the **Pipeline** (for example from **Qualified** to **Proposal Sent** once you send the proposal).
2. Set a follow-up date so the lead shows in your follow-ups.
3. Build the proposal on the lead's **📄 Proposals** tab while the conversation is warm.
4. Email it with **📧 Email to prospect**.

## FAQs

??? The demo signed out in the middle of the call. | The demo session is temporary. Go back to Launch demo and press the button again; it takes a few seconds.
??? Can the prospect try it themselves? | Not with the demo button: it signs in as a demo administrator in your browser only. Share your screen instead.
??? Can I show a feature the demo has no data for? | Add a little demo data before the call, and tidy it up afterwards so the next rep's demo stays clean.
