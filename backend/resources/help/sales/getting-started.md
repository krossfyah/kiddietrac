# Getting started with the Sales workspace

The Sales workspace is where KiddieTrac's sales team takes prospective childcare agencies from first contact to a
signed deal. Everything is shared by the sales team and superadmins: one pipeline, one mailbox, one price list. Your
**Home** screen is your sales command centre: the numbers that matter, the pipeline by stage and shortcuts to
everything else.

[[open: #home | Open Home]]

## Reading your Home screen

1. Read the tiles across the top: **Open leads**, **Pipeline value / yr**, **Weighted forecast / yr**, **Won**, **Won revenue**, **Follow-ups due** and **My open** (the open leads that are yours).
2. Look at **📊 Pipeline by stage**: how many leads sit in each stage (**New**, **Contacted**, **Qualified**, **Proposal Sent**, **Negotiation**, **Won**…) and what they are worth.
3. Check **🏆 Won vs lost**: your win rate, with **Won**, **Lost**, **Won revenue** and **Avg won deal** beside it.
4. Use the **Quick access** tiles to jump to **Pipeline**, **Website chat**, **Leads**, **Bookings**, **Calendar**, **Plans & pricing**, **Launch demo**, **Inbox** or **Help**.

> 💡 **Follow-ups due** above zero is your to-do list for the day: open **Leads** or the **Pipeline** and work them first.

## Your first day

1. Open **Settings** and check your name, phone and notification sounds.
2. Tick **Available for website chat** in **Website chat** when you are on duty, so visitors reach a person.
3. Open **Sales email** and read the team inbox.
4. Open **Pipeline**, click a lead's card and change its **Stage** when it moves on.

## The Sales menu

- **Pipeline** — every lead as a board, by stage. See [[sales-pipeline]].
- **Website chat** — answer visitors on kiddietrac.com. See [Website chat](website-chat).
- **Leads** — the same leads as a searchable list. See [Managing leads](managing-leads).
- **Proposals** — every proposal, with stats. See [[sales-proposals]].
- **Customers** and **Expenses** — won leads and the team's spending. See [[sales-customers]] and [[sales-expenses]].
- **Campaigns** and **Sales email** — intro emails and the shared mailbox. See [[sales-campaigns]] and [[sales-email]].
- **Bookings** and **Calendar** — website meeting requests and the team calendar. See [[sales-bookings]] and [[sales-calendar]].
- **Prospect NDAs** and **Launch demo** — the NDA before a deep dive, and the live demo. See [[prospect-ndas]] and [[sales-demo]].

## Proposals, start to finish

1. Open the lead and go to its **📄 Proposals** tab, then press **+ New**. See [Building quotes and proposals](quotes-and-proposals).
2. Email it with **📧 Email to prospect**. The prospect gets the PDF and a **Review & sign online** button.
3. When they sign, countersign it. The lead moves to **Won**. See [Signing proposals online](signing-proposals-online).

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Sales rep | Everything in the Sales menu. Not the **Files repository** (superadmins only). |
| Platform admin (superadmin) | The same Sales screens from the platform menu, plus the Files repository. |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? "Could not load: …" on Home | The numbers did not load. The Quick access tiles still work; refresh the page in a minute.
??? My numbers look low. | **My open** counts only the leads assigned to you; the other tiles count the whole team.
??? Where do my notifications go? | New chats, bookings and mail ring the bell (**Inbox**) and, on the app, your phone.
??? Who do I ask for access to a screen? | A KiddieTrac superadmin manages the sales team's accounts.
