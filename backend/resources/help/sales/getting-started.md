# Getting started with the Sales workspace

The Sales workspace is where you manage prospective childcare agencies from first
contact through to a signed deal. Everything is shared across the sales team and
superadmins — one pipeline, one source of truth.

## The main areas

- **Pipeline** — a kanban board of every lead, grouped by stage, with win/loss
  analytics across the top.
- **Leads** — the same leads as a searchable list.
- **New lead** — add a prospect.
- **Follow-ups** — the leads that need a touch, soonest first.
- **Plans & pricing** — the preset products you drop into proposals.
- **Launch demo** — open the live Test Agency to walk a prospect through KiddieTrac.

## The pipeline at a glance

The top of the pipeline shows your key numbers: open leads, pipeline value, deals
won, follow-ups due, and your own open leads. Below that is a win/loss strip — your
**win rate**, **won revenue**, **average won deal** and **lost** count — so you can
see how the pipeline is converting without leaving the page.

Click any card on the board to open the full lead.
