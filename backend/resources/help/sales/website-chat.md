# Website chat

Visitors on **kiddietrac.com** can chat with us from the bubble in the corner of every
page. **Sales → Website chat** is where the team answers them, live. When nobody is
available, **Maya**, our virtual assistant, answers — and she always says she is the
virtual assistant, never a person.

[[open: #sales-webchat | Open Website chat]]

## Going online

Tick **Available for website chat** at the top of the screen. While it is ticked and the
portal is open, you count as online and visitors are told a person is there. Close the
portal or untick it and you go offline within a couple of minutes.

[[show: #sales-webchat @ Available for website chat | Show me the availability switch]]

When a visitor writes, the **Website chat** item in the menu shows a badge, and the portal
pops up an alert with a chime — even if you are on another screen.

## Answering a visitor

- [ ] Pick the conversation from the **Open** list on the left.
- [ ] Press **Join** — the chat is now yours, and Maya stops answering.
- [ ] Type your reply and press **Send**. The visitor sees you typing.
- [ ] If they are a real prospect, press **🎯 Add as lead** (or **🎯 Open lead** if they already are one). The transcript is attached to the lead.
- [ ] Press **Close** when the conversation is finished.

Simply typing a reply also takes the chat from Maya. If a colleague already holds it, the
button reads **Take over**.

## Maya covers for you

- When **nobody is online**, Maya answers straight away.
- When people are online but **nobody picks up within about 45 seconds**, Maya answers so
  the visitor is not left waiting.
- **Never while a person holds the chat.** Press **Hand back to Maya** when you have to
  step away; she picks up the conversation, including anything the visitor asked that
  you had not answered yet.

## What the list tells you

- **Wants a person** — the visitor asked for a human.
- **Maya is answering** — nobody has joined yet.
- **On the website now** / **Left the page** — whether the visitor is still there.
- **Closed** — writing in a closed chat reopens it for the visitor if they are still on
  the page.

Switch between **Open** and **Closed** at the top right to find earlier conversations.

## Transcripts and alerts

A visitor who arrives while nobody is online triggers a push alert to the team. A
"visitors are waiting" email and finished transcripts go to the address set in
**Website → Chat transcripts to** (a platform admin setting); if that is blank, alerts are push only.

## Questions

??? Does the visitor know when they are talking to Maya? | Yes. Maya always introduces herself as the virtual assistant, and a person joining is announced in the chat.
??? I joined a chat and need to leave. | Press Hand back to Maya. She takes over and answers anything still unanswered.
??? Can I work a chat from my phone? | Yes. On a phone the conversation opens full-screen; use ‹ Chats to go back to the list.
