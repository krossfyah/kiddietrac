# Signing proposals online

A prospect can read, sign or decline a proposal online, with no account and no printing.
KiddieTrac then countersigns in the Sales workspace, and the deal is done.

## How the prospect gets the link

- **Email it:** open the proposal's **⋮** menu → **📧 Email to prospect**. The email has a
  **Review & sign online** button.
- **Copy it:** **⋮** → **🔗 Copy signing link**, then send it to the prospect yourself.

The link is private. **Anyone who has it can sign for the prospect**, so share it only with them.

## What the prospect sees

1. They open the link and see the full proposal, with a **Download PDF** button.
2. They read the proposal and its terms and conditions.
3. Under **Sign the proposal** they enter the **Organisation (legal name)**, **Your full
   name** and **Your title**. These start filled in from the proposal.
4. They draw their signature (they can **Clear signature** and try again).
5. They tick the box to accept the proposal and its Terms & Conditions, confirming they are
   authorised to sign and that an electronic signature counts as a handwritten one.
6. They press **✍️ Sign the proposal**.

They get a thank-you email right away saying KiddieTrac will countersign next.

**Not going ahead?** At the bottom they can press **Not going ahead? Let us know**, add a
reason if they want, and press **Decline this proposal**.

## When the link stops working

- **After Valid until.** The link works up to the end of the proposal's **Valid until**
  day. After that the prospect sees *Expired* and is asked to contact you. A proposal with
  no Valid until date doesn't expire.
- **After it is declined.** The prospect sees that it was declined.
- **After it is signed.** The page shows that it is signed and waiting for KiddieTrac, then
  *Signed by both parties* with the signed copy to download.

## Follow it on the proposal card

On the lead's **📄 Proposals** tab, each card tells you where things stand:

- **👁 Opened by the prospect** with the date and time. Not signed yet.
- **Declined online** with the date and their reason, if they gave one.
- **✍️ Signed — countersign**: who signed, their title and when. Waiting for you.
- **✅ Signed by both**: both signatures and when.

Each of these is also logged on the lead's **Activity & history**.

## Once it is signed, it is locked

As soon as the prospect signs, the proposal can't be edited or emailed again. What they
signed is fixed. If something needs to change, **create a new proposal**.

## Countersign

1. Open the lead's **📄 Proposals** tab.
2. Press **✍️ Countersign** on the card, or use **⋮** → **✍️ Countersign**.
3. Press **👁 View what they signed** if you want to check it first.
4. Check **Your name** and **Title**, and draw your signature.
5. Tick *I countersign this proposal on behalf of KiddieTrac and I am authorised to do so.*
6. Press **✍️ Countersign & send**.

## What happens next

When you countersign:

- The **fully signed PDF** is emailed to the prospect, with the sales contact copied.
- The signed PDF is saved to the lead's **📁 Documents** tab.
- The lead moves to **Won**.
- The proposal shows **✅ Signed by both**, and **⋮** → **⬇ Download signed PDF** gets the
  signed copy.

The signed PDF shows both signatures, names, titles and dates on the acceptance page, and an
**Electronic signature record**: who signed, when, from where, and a fingerprint of the
document they signed.

- [ ] Countersigned with **✍️ Countersign & send**
- [ ] Signed PDF in the lead's **📁 Documents**
- [ ] Lead is **Won**

??? Can I change a signed proposal? | No. Once the prospect signs, it is locked. Create a new proposal and send that instead.
??? What if the link expired? | Edit the proposal, set a later Valid until date and email it again. The same link works again until the new date.
??? Who can sign with the link? | Anyone who has it, including people you copied on the email. Send it only to the prospect.
??? The prospect declined. Can they still sign? | Not from the link as it stands. If they change their mind, create a new proposal and email it to them.
??? Who can countersign? | Anyone on the sales team, from the proposal card.
