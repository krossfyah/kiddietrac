# When a lead misses a meeting (no-shows)

Once a meeting with a lead has started, its **⋮** menu (on the Sales calendar, in the
list view and on the lead's **🤝 Meetings** tab) gains three choices:

- **Mark as no-show**: the lead did not turn up.
- **Mark as held**: the meeting happened.
- **Undo**: put it back to scheduled if you picked the wrong one.

Each one shows as a chip on the meeting and adds a line to the lead's timeline.

## Asking for a new time

**Mark as no-show** opens a short window that shows you exactly what will go out:

- **Email**: a short, friendly "Sorry we missed you" note signed with your first name.
  Replies go to sales@. It has a big **Pick a new time** button.
- **Text**: a short message from the KiddieTrac line with the same link and
  "Reply STOP to opt out".

The lead's own email and mobile are shown next to each box. A box is greyed out, with
the reason, when the lead has no email, no valid mobile, has replied STOP, or is a test
lead. Press **Send**, or **Mark only, don't send** to just record the no-show. You can
send later with **Ask for a new time** in the ⋮ menu.

## When they pick a new time

The link opens the booking page with their details already filled in. When they book:

- the new meeting is logged on the **same lead**;
- the missed meeting is marked **Rescheduled**;
- you (or the lead's owner) get a 🔔 notice.

The link works for 45 days.

## Automatic asks (off unless you turn it on)

A platform setting can ask automatically 30 minutes after a missed meeting that nobody
marked. It is **off** by default.
