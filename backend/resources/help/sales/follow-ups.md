# Follow-ups and staying on top of leads

Deals are won in the follow-up. There are two ways to keep track.

## The Next follow-up date

Every lead has a **Next follow-up** date. Set it on the lead's **Details** panel (or
when you create the lead). The **Follow-ups** screen lists every open lead with a
follow-up date, soonest first, and flags anything **overdue** in orange. Click a row
to jump straight to the lead.

## Follow-up tasks in the timeline

On a lead's page, the **Activity & follow-ups** panel lets you log what happened and
schedule what's next:

1. Pick a type — **Note**, **Call**, **Email**, **Meeting** or **Follow-up task**.
2. For a follow-up task, choose a **due date**.
3. Write a short note and press **Add**.

Follow-up tasks show in the timeline with their due date. When you've done one, press
**mark done**.

## Email reminders

Each morning, KiddieTrac emails the lead's owner about any follow-up that's **due
today or overdue** — a one-time nudge so nothing slips. If a lead is unassigned, the
reminder goes to the superadmins.
