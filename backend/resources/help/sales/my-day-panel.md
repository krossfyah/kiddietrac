# My day - the right panel

On a computer, agency admins, centre directors, super admins and the sales team have a panel on the right edge of every screen, like the one in Outlook. It keeps your calendar, meetings, tasks and today's numbers one click away while you work.

## Open and close it

- The thin bar on the right has an icon for each section: **Calendar**, **Meetings**, **Tasks** and (for admins and directors) **Today at the centre**. Click one to open the panel on that section.
- Click the same icon again, or the **›** arrow at the top of the panel, to fold it back to the thin bar.
- KiddieTrac remembers whether you like it open or closed, and which section you used last.
- On a wide screen the panel sits beside the screen and the screen makes room for it. On a smaller laptop screen it opens over the right side of the screen and closes when you click anywhere else.
- It isn't shown on phones or narrow windows - the bottom bar is there instead.

## Calendar

A small month calendar. Dots mark days with meetings (teal), sales bookings and follow-ups (pink), centre dates such as birthdays and absences (amber) and task due dates (purple). Click a day to see that day on its own.

Underneath, **Coming up** lists everything from today through the days ahead you chose. **New meeting** books a meeting, and **Open calendar** goes to the full Calendar.

It reads like Outlook: each day is headed **Today • Wed, Nov 25**, every event shows its start time and how long it lasts (or **All day**), and a coloured bar on the left tells you what it is - teal for meetings, pink for sales, amber for centre dates, purple for tasks. An outline bar means it still needs your reply or a decision. Events that have already finished today drop off, and the next one is tinted with an **Up next** label. When nothing is left today you'll see **No more events scheduled today** with **Create new event**, and **+ Add a task due today** adds a private to-do for today. Today's date is tinted on the month calendar.

## How far ahead (days ahead)

Choose how far ahead **Coming up** and **Meetings** look: **Today**, **3 days**, **1 week** (the default), **2 weeks** or **1 month**. Change it with the gear at the top of the panel, the **Change** link, or **My settings → My day panel**. It is saved to your account, so it follows you to any computer.

## Meetings

Your meetings for the days ahead, grouped by day. Click one to see the details and reply; **Join** opens the video link. **New meeting** opens the same meeting form as the Calendar.

## Sales calendar (super admins and sales)

Super admins and the sales team also see the Sales calendar in the panel, in pink: website bookings (⏳ means it still needs a decision), lead meetings and follow-ups. Click one to open it in Sales. Agency admins and directors never see sales items - and neither does a super admin using **View as** an agency admin or director.

## Tasks

- **My to-dos** are private tasks for yourself. Type in **Add a task…** - **Me (private)** is already picked - add an optional due date, then **Add task**. Only you can see a private to-do: it never appears in anyone else's lists, reports or digests, and it never sends a notification or reminder.
- Admins and directors can pick someone else instead of **Me** to assign them a task, the same as assigning from Staff → Tasks. **Assigned to me** and **Assigned by me** list those open tasks.
- Click the circle to mark a task done. On the Tasks screen your private to-dos show **Me · Private** and a ✅ button to mark them done.
- **All tasks** opens the Tasks screen.

## Today at the centre

Children **At Centre** (of those enrolled), **Staff on floor**, Safe Arrival children **Not arrived** yet, and **Ratio alerts**. A centre director sees their centre; an agency admin sees every centre.

Ratio alerts and **Check staffing** come from the same live ratio check as the Room ratios screen, room by room, grouped by centre. Click one to open Room ratios. The section refreshes on its own every couple of minutes while it's open; the **↻** button refreshes it now.

Everything in the panel follows what you can already see - your agency, and for a director, your centres.

## Outlook or Google calendar (optional)

If you use a Microsoft 365 (Outlook) or Gmail mailbox in **Email**, you can connect its calendar. Nothing changes until you do.

- Go to **My settings → Calendar → Outlook or Google calendar** and choose **Connect calendar**. Microsoft or Google asks you to allow KiddieTrac to see and change the calendar; choose **Accept**. If you haven't added a mailbox yet, add it in **Email → Manage accounts** first.
- **My calendar** is your own. Its events show in this panel with a blue bar, and only you see them. Meetings you organise in KiddieTrac go on it: for an agency meeting, Outlook or Google sends the invitations from your own mailbox and KiddieTrac reads everyone's answers back. With your calendar connected, the meeting form also offers **Teams meeting** (Outlook) or **Google Meet** (Google) to make the video link for you.
- **Agency calendar** (agency admins connect it - for example a shared office mailbox): admins and directors see its events in the panel, and private ones just say **Busy**. The agency's meetings are copied onto it.
- Click an Outlook or Google event in the panel to open it there. **Disconnect** stops KiddieTrac reading and adding to the calendar; meetings already added stay on it.
- If the panel shows **Calendar needs reconnecting**, open **My settings → Calendar** and choose **Reconnect**.
