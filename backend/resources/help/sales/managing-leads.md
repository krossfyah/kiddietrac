# Adding and managing leads

## Add a new lead

From **New lead** (or the **+ New lead** button on the pipeline), fill in the
prospect's details. Only the **Contact name** is required — everything else is
optional but helps you and the team:

- **Company / childcare** — the agency or centre you're selling to.
- **Email** and **Phone**.
- **Stage** — where they are in the pipeline (defaults to *New*).
- **Owner** — the rep responsible (defaults to you).
- **Est. value** — the expected deal size, used in the pipeline value and win stats.
- **Expected close** and **Next follow-up** dates.
- **Notes** — what they're looking for.

Press **Create lead**. The superadmin and the sales team are notified by email, and
you land on the new lead's page.

## Move a lead through the pipeline

Open a lead and change the **Stage** dropdown at the top — the move is saved
immediately and logged to the activity timeline. The stages are:
**New → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal Sent → Negotiation → Won / Lost.**

Marking a lead **Won** or **Lost** closes it and feeds the win/loss analytics on the
pipeline.

## Edit or delete

The **Details** panel on the lead page is fully editable — change any field and
press **Save changes**. **Delete** removes the lead for everyone, so use it only for
duplicates or test entries.
