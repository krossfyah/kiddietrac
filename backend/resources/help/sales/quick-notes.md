# Quick notes

A private notepad for the things you need to remember between calls: a prospect's question, a number to call back, a
pricing idea or a screenshot from a meeting. Open **Notes** in your menu (under **Account**). Your notes are just for
you: nobody else on the sales team, and no superadmin, can read them.

[[open: #notes | Open Notes]]

## Writing a note

1. Open **Notes** from the **Account** part of your menu.
2. Add a title in **Title (optional)** if you like.
3. Write the note in the big box below it (**Write a quick note…**).
4. Pick a colour dot if you like, and add pictures with **🖼 Add picture** (see below).
5. Press **Save**. On a computer, **Enter** also saves (**Shift+Enter** starts a new line). On a phone or tablet, Enter starts a new line and **Save** saves. A **Note saved** toast confirms it.

The list shows each note's title only, on one line. A note with no title is listed by its first line. Tap a title to
open the whole note.

## Pictures

Add up to 5 pictures to a note, such as a screenshot of a prospect's email or a photo of a whiteboard:

1. On a computer, paste a screenshot into the note (**Ctrl+V**, or **Cmd+V** on a Mac), drag a picture onto it, or press **🖼 Add picture**.
2. On a phone, **🖼 Add picture** opens your camera or your photos.
3. Tap a picture to see it full size; press **✕** on it to remove it.

Pictures are made smaller on your device and their location details are removed before they are sent. Each picture
can be up to 10 MB (PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF). In the list, **🖼 3** means the note has 3 pictures.

## Pin, colour and remind

1. Press **📌** (**Pin to top**) to keep a note at the top; press **📍** to unpin it.
2. Press **🎨** (**Colour**) to colour a note, for example one colour per prospect.
3. Press **✏️** (**Edit**) to open **Edit note**. Change the text, or set a **Reminder** date, then press **Save**.
4. On the reminder day the note shows in your calendar and Tasks in the right-hand panel.

## Finding and deleting notes

1. Type in **Search notes** to find a note by any word in its title or text.
2. Press **Delete** on a note and confirm **Delete this note?** to remove it. "This note will be gone for good."

## Who can do what

- **Sales reps**: notes are always **Just me**. Sales reps do not belong to an agency, so there are no shared agency notes here.
- **Superadmins** have the same private notes, and shared agency notes inside an agency.
- Customers, parents and agency staff never see your notes.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? It says "Write something first." | A note needs some text before it can be saved.
??? A picture won't add. | Only PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF pictures up to 10 MB can be added, up to 5 per note. A phone HEIC picture can't be read here: share it as a JPG, or take a screenshot of it.
??? Can I share a note with the team? | No. For something the team needs, add it to the lead's notes, or post it in the team chat (💬 in the top bar).
??? I deleted a note by mistake. | A deleted note is gone for good and cannot be brought back.
