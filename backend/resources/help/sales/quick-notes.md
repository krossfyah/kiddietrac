# Quick notes

A notepad for the things you need to remember: jot a note in seconds and keep it just for you, or share it with your staff.

## Where it is

Open **Notes** in your menu (under Account). Your notes are just for you.

[[open: #notes | Open Notes]]

## Writing a note

1. Add a **Title** if you like, then write the note in the big box below it. On a computer press **Enter** to save (**Shift+Enter** starts a new line). On a phone or tablet, Enter starts a new line and the **Save** button saves.
2. The list shows each note's title only, on one line. A note with no title is listed by its first line. Tap a title to open the whole note.

## Pictures

Add up to 5 pictures to a note, such as a screenshot or a photo of a notice:

- On a computer, paste a screenshot into the note (**Ctrl+V**, or **Cmd+V** on a Mac), drag a picture onto it, or press **🖼 Add picture**.
- On a phone, **🖼 Add picture** opens your camera or your photos.
- You can add pictures while writing a new note or in **Edit note**. Tap a picture to see it full size; press **✕** on it to remove it.

Pictures are made smaller on your device and their location details are removed before they are sent. A picture is seen by exactly the people who can see its note, and is deleted with it. In the list, **🖼 3** means the note has 3 pictures. Each picture can be up to 10 MB.

## Searching

Use **Search notes** to find a note by any word in its title or text.
