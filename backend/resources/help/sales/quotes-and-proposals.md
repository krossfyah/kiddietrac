# Building quotes and proposals

Proposals live on the lead's page, in the **Proposals** panel on the right.

## Create a proposal

Press **+ New** to open the proposal builder. Give it a title, then add line items
two ways:

- **Add preset plan** — pick from your **Plans & pricing** presets (Starter, Growth,
  Agency, Setup, or whatever you've configured). The name and price fill in for you.
- **+ Blank line** — a free-form line for anything custom. Set the quantity and unit
  price.

Add a **Discount** and a **Valid until** date if you need them. The **total**
recalculates live as you edit. Press **Create proposal** to save.

## Send it to the prospect

Re-open a saved proposal to:

- **⬇ PDF** — download a branded PDF of the proposal.
- **📧 Email** — email the PDF straight to the lead's email address. Sending marks
  the proposal as *Sent*.

## Track the outcome

Use the **Status** dropdown on a saved proposal to move it through
**Draft → Sent → Accepted / Declined**, so the team can see where each proposal
stands.
