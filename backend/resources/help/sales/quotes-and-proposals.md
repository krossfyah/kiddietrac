# Building quotes and proposals

Proposals live on the lead's page, in the **📄 Proposals** tab. Anyone on the sales team can
create, edit and send them. Each proposal gets its own number, such as *KT-Q-000123*.

## Create a proposal

1. Open the lead and go to the **📄 Proposals** tab.
2. Press **+ New**. The proposal builder opens, already filled in with your
   [proposal defaults](proposal-defaults).
3. Give it a **Title** (it starts as *KiddieTrac Proposal*).
4. Check the **Prepared for** and **Prepared by** boxes (see below).
5. Choose the **Currency** and **Billing** (**Monthly** or **Annual**).
6. Add your line items, discounts and taxes.
7. Set **Valid until**, and add any **Notes**.
8. Press **Create proposal**.

The **total** updates as you type, so you always see the figure the prospect will see.

## Prepared for and Prepared by

- **Prepared for** fills in from the lead: organisation, contact name, job title,
  address, email and phone. Change anything that should read differently on this proposal.
- **Prepared by** starts with **your name** and the KiddieTrac sales **title, email and
  phone** from the proposal defaults. It never uses your personal login email, so replies
  come to the shared sales inbox. You can change it for one proposal if you need to.

## Currency and billing

- Pick from 14 currencies (CAD, USD, GBP, EUR and more). Prices are typed in that currency;
  **nothing is converted**. Your last choice is remembered for your next proposal.
- **Monthly** or **Annual** sets how the total is shown (*per month* or *per year*). On an
  annual proposal, monthly preset plans are priced **×12**. If you switch between Monthly
  and Annual, the preset plan lines re-price for you.

## Line items

Add lines two ways:

- **+ Add preset plan or discount…** — pick a plan from **Plans & pricing**. The name,
  description and price fill in for you.
- **+ Blank line** — a free-form line for anything custom. Set the quantity and price.

Long descriptions wrap onto more lines. Press **×** to remove a line.

## Discounts

There are two kinds:

- **Discount plans** — set up once in **Plans & pricing** under **🏷️ Discounts**, then
  picked from the same **Add preset plan or discount…** menu (they start with 🏷️). A
  discount can be a percentage or a fixed amount off, and can apply to **the whole
  proposal**, only **recurring items (monthly / yearly)**, or only **one-time items**. It
  shows as its own line with a minus, and a percentage updates as you change the other lines.
- **Extra discount (amount)** — a one-off amount taken off this proposal's subtotal.

> **Tip:** To add a discount plan, go to Plans & pricing → **➕ Add discount**, give it a name, the amount, **% off** or **$ off**, and what it **Applies to**.

## Taxes

Under **Taxes (on the total after discount)**, press **+ Add tax** and enter a name (for
example *HST*) and a rate. You can add up to 5. New proposals start with your default taxes.

## Notes, terms and conditions

- **Notes** — anything you want to say to the customer. Shown on the proposal under the prices.
- **Proposal terms** — the short terms under the prices, such as how long the price holds.
- **▸ Terms & conditions** — press it to open the full terms. They print on their own page,
  before the signatures. Start a line with `##` for a heading or `-` for a bullet. Leave it
  empty to leave the page out.

Each proposal keeps its own copy, so changing the defaults later never changes a proposal
you already made.

## The ⋮ menu on each proposal

Every proposal card has a **⋮** menu:

- **👁 View** — see the proposal exactly as it prints. Clicking the card does the same.
- **✏️ Edit** — reopen the builder.
- **⬇ Download PDF** — save the branded PDF.
- **📧 Email to prospect** — send it (see below).
- **🔗 Copy signing link** — copy the private link where the prospect can sign online.
- **✍️ Countersign** — appears once the prospect has signed.
- **🗑 Delete** — removes the proposal. The lead's history keeps a note that it was deleted.

Once the prospect has signed, **Edit**, **Email** and **Copy signing link** are no longer
offered: what they signed can't change. See [Signing proposals online](signing-proposals-online).

## The PDF

The PDF is branded with the KiddieTrac logo and shows the proposal number, issue date and
valid-until date, the Prepared for and Prepared by details, the billing and currency, the
line items, totals, notes and proposal terms. Then come:

- the **Terms & Conditions** page, and
- an **Acceptance** section with signature, name and title, and date lines for the customer
  and for KiddieTrac.

Every page shows *Page n of m* and *Private & confidential. Not for distribution.*

## Email it to the prospect

1. Open the **⋮** menu and choose **📧 Email to prospect**.
2. Add anyone to **CC** or **BCC** if you want (separate addresses with commas, up to 10 each).
3. Press **Send email**.

The email goes to the lead's email address, so add one to the lead first. A draft proposal
is marked **Sent**, and the send is logged on the lead's history.

What the prospect receives:

- A branded email that thanks them for their interest and introduces KiddieTrac.
- **No prices in the email.** All figures are only in the attached PDF.
- The proposal PDF attached, with the terms and conditions.
- A **Review & sign online** button that opens their private signing page. Anyone who opens
  the email, including people you copied, can use it.
- A **private and confidential** notice asking them not to share the proposal.

When the prospect replies, the reply goes to the **Prepared by** email (the sales contact),
not to a no-reply address.

## Track the outcome

Each proposal card shows its status: **Draft**, **Sent**, **✍️ Signed — countersign**,
**✅ Signed by both** or **Declined**. Under it you also see when the prospect opened or
declined it online.

You can still set the **Status** by hand in the builder (**Draft**, **Sent**, **Accepted**,
**Declined**) for a proposal handled on paper.

??? Can I change a proposal after sending it? | Yes, until the prospect signs it. Edit it and email it again. Once they sign, create a new proposal instead.
??? Why is the Prepared by email not mine? | Proposals use the shared sales email and phone from Proposal terms & defaults, so replies reach the whole team.
??? Does the email show the price? | No. Prices, totals and dates are only in the attached PDF.
