# Privacy and data security

Childcare data is sensitive. Here's how Kiddietrac handles it.

## What we collect

For **children**: name, date of birth, room enrollment, medical notes (allergies, conditions), dietary restrictions, daily activity logs, photos shared by educators, immunization records (if added by the centre).

For **parents/guardians**: name, email, phone, address, relationship to child, billing preferences.

For **staff**: name, email, role, certifications (RECE, First Aid, etc.), shift records.

## What we don't collect

- Payment card details — Kiddietrac doesn't process credit cards; payments happen offline (e-transfer, cheque, etc.) and the director records them
- Browsing or location data outside of what's needed to operate the app
- Data for advertising — we don't have ads and we don't sell data to advertisers

## Who can see what

- **Parents** see only their own children's data
- **Educators** see all children at their centre, but only basic info for children not in their room
- **Director** sees everything for their centre
- **Other centres** never see your data — every centre is isolated

## Photos

Photos shared by educators are visible to: the children's parents/guardians, all educators at the centre, and the director. They're not posted publicly anywhere. You can ask the centre director to remove specific photos at any time.

## Data export

You can ask your centre director to export all your child's data at any time. The director has access to compliance reports and attendance records that can be shared with you.

## Questions?

If you have privacy questions, contact your centre director or reach out to support@kiddietrac.com.
