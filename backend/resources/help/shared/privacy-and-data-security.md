# Privacy and data security

Childcare data is sensitive. Here's how Kiddietrac handles it.

## What we collect

For **children**: name, date of birth, room enrolment, medical notes (allergies, conditions), dietary restrictions, daily activity logs, photos shared by educators, immunization records (if added by the centre).

For **parents/guardians**: name, email, phone, address, relationship to child, billing preferences.

For **staff**: name, email, role, certifications (RECE, First Aid, etc.), shift records.

## What we don't collect

- Raw payment card / bank numbers — where your centre enables online payment or autopay, your card is handled by our secure payment processor (Stripe), which tokenizes it; Kiddietrac never sees or stores the full number. Offline payments (e-transfer, cheque) are recorded by your director
- Browsing or location data outside of what's needed to operate the app
- Data for advertising — we don't have ads and we don't sell data to advertisers

## Who can see what

- **Parents** see only their own children's data
- **Educators** see all children at their centre, but only basic info for children not in their room
- **Director** sees everything for their centre
- **Other centres** never see your data — every centre is isolated

## Messages

Messages between families and the centre, Team chat, message translations and the in-app notifications that copy a message are **stored encrypted** on our servers (AES-256), with a key kept apart from the database. A copy of the database or a backup on its own shows scrambled text, not what anybody wrote. Inside KiddieTrac they read as normal for the people in the conversation, and search still works.

Phone notifications and emails say **who** sent a message, not **what** it says — open the app to read it. 🚨 Emergency messages still ring loudly and still say they are urgent.

This is encryption at rest, not end-to-end encryption: KiddieTrac's servers decrypt messages to show them to the people allowed to read them (and, for your agency's administrators, in the Chat audit).

## Photos

Photos shared by educators are visible to: the children's parents/guardians, all educators at the centre, and the director. They're not posted publicly anywhere. You can ask the centre director to remove specific photos at any time.

## Data export

You can ask your centre director to export all your child's data at any time. The director has access to compliance reports and attendance records that can be shared with you.

## Questions?

If you have privacy questions, contact your centre director or reach out to support@kiddietrac.com.
