# Walkie-talkie

The **Walkie-talkie** lets staff talk live to a colleague, a room's educators, a centre's staff or a centre's
leaders. You hold a button, speak, and let go: everyone on the channel hears you straight away, out loud. Use it for
quick, spoken messages during the day ("Can someone cover the toddler room for five minutes?"). It is for your
agency's own staff only. Parents are never on it.

[[open: #walkie | Open Walkie-talkie]]

Find it under **Operations › Walkie-talkie** (admins and directors) or **Walkie-talkie** in your menu (educators and
home visitors). The screen has **Channels** and **People** on the left, the talk button in the middle and **History**
underneath.

## Talking on a channel

1. Under **Channels**, press the channel you want, for example your room or your centre's staff.
2. Press and **hold 🎙 Hold to talk**. The button changes to **Talking…** once the microphone is on.
3. Speak, then let go to finish. On a computer you can hold the **spacebar** instead of the button.
4. The first time, your browser or phone asks to use the microphone: allow it.

Only one person talks on a channel at a time. If someone already is, the channel shows "*name* is talking": wait a
moment and try again. A talk stops by itself after one minute.

> ℹ️ A channel marked **Listen only** is one you can hear but not talk on, for example **All staff** for educators.

## Talking to one person

1. Under **People**, type in **Search names** to find the colleague.
2. Press their name.
3. Hold **🎙 Hold to talk**, speak, and let go.

## Channels and who hears them

| Channel | Who hears it |
|---|---|
| 🚨 Emergency | Every staff member of the agency. **Always plays, even with Do not disturb on.** Directors and admins talk on it (an admin can make it **Admins only**). |
| 🏢 All staff | Every staff member. Admins talk, everyone listens. |
| 🏫 *Centre* staff | The staff of that centre. |
| 🧭 *Centre* leaders | That centre's directors and the agency's admins. |
| 🧸 *Room* educators | The educators of that room. |
| People | One person. |

Admins reach the whole agency. Directors and educators reach the staff of their own centres and their leaders.

## Hearing and answering a talk

- **Android app:** your phone listens all the time, even with the screen off, and plays the talk out loud. The
  notification bar shows "Walkie-talkie on".
- **iPhone:** a notification says "Walkie-talkie from *name*": tap it to listen.
- **Browser:** you hear talks only while a KiddieTrac tab is open. If your browser blocks sound, tap **▶ Listen** on
  the card.

When a talk arrives, a card shows who is talking:

1. Listen. Tap **▶ Listen** if the card asks you to.
2. Press **🎙 Reply** to open the same channel and answer, or
3. Press **Busy, can't talk** to let the speaker know you heard but cannot talk right now.

## Do not disturb

1. On the Walkie-talkie screen, find the **Do not disturb** drop-down.
2. Choose **For 1 hour**, **For 4 hours** or **Until I turn it off**. The note beside it says until when.
3. To end it early, set the drop-down back to **Off**.

Talks sent to you while it is on are kept in **History** so you can listen later. The emergency channel still plays.

## Listening again (History)

1. Select a channel or person.
2. Look under **History**: each talk shows **When**, **Who**, **Length** and what happened to you: **Heard**, **Busy**, **Do not disturb**, **Not on shift** (it arrived while your phone only listens when clocked in) or **Not played**.
3. Press **▶ Play** to listen again.

If you were the speaker, you also see how many people heard it and who said they were busy.

> 🔒 Talks are encrypted on the way and stored encrypted, then deleted after the number of days your agency chose (30
> by default). The audit log keeps who talked to whom, when and for how long — never what was said.

## Agency settings (agency admins)

At the bottom of the screen, **Agency settings** (changes save as you make them and are recorded in the audit log):

- **Walkie-talkie** — on or off for the agency.
- **Phones listen** — **All the time (24/7)** or **Only while clocked in**.
- **Keep recordings for (days)**.
- **Emergency channel** — **Off** or **On (always plays)**, its **Emergency channel name**, and **Who can talk on
  it**: **Directors and admins** or **Admins only**.
- **Educators can call** — **Staff at their own centres** or **Anyone on staff in the agency**.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Educators and home visitors | Talk on their rooms' and centres' channels and to people they may call; listen to All staff and Emergency |
| Centre directors | Everything above for their centres, plus talk on Emergency (unless it is Admins only) |
| Agency admins | Talk to the whole agency and change the agency settings |
| Parents | Never on the walkie-talkie |

## Questions and problems

??? "The walkie-talkie is switched off for your agency." | An agency admin turns it on under Agency settings at the bottom of this screen.
??? "Microphone blocked" or "Allow the microphone for KiddieTrac to talk." | Your browser or phone refused the microphone. Allow it in the site or app permissions, then try again.
??? "Someone is talking" when I press the button. | One person talks per channel. Wait until they finish, then hold the button again.
??? I can't talk on a channel. | It shows Listen only: your role can hear that channel but not talk on it.
??? I don't hear talks on my computer. | Keep a KiddieTrac tab open, and tap ▶ Listen if the browser blocked sound. The Android app listens all the time.
??? Someone is not in People. | They may be at another centre: by default educators reach staff at their own centres. An admin can change Educators can call.
??? Can parents hear us? | No. Only your agency's staff are on the walkie-talkie.
