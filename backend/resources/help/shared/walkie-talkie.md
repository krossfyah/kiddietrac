# Walkie-talkie

Talk live to a colleague, a room's educators, your centre's staff or your centre's leaders. Hold the button, speak, let go. Everyone on the channel hears you straight away, out loud.

## Where to find it

**Operations › Walkie-talkie** (admins and directors) or **Walkie-talkie** in your menu (educators and home visitors).

## Talking

1. Pick a channel on the left, or a person under **People**.
2. **Hold 🎙** while you speak and let go when you finish. On a computer you can hold the **spacebar** instead.
3. One person talks on a channel at a time. If someone is already talking, you see **"Amna is talking"**: wait a moment and try again.

A talk stops by itself after one minute.

## Channels

| Channel | Who hears it |
|---|---|
| 🚨 Emergency | Every staff member of the agency. **Always plays, even with Do not disturb on.** Directors and admins talk on it (an admin can make it admins only). |
| 🏢 All staff | Every staff member. Admins talk, everyone listens. |
| 🏫 *Centre* staff | The staff of that centre. |
| 🧭 *Centre* leaders | That centre's directors and the agency's admins. |
| 🧸 *Room* educators | The educators of that room. |
| People | One person. |

Admins reach the whole agency. Directors and educators reach the staff of their own centres and their leaders. Parents are never on the walkie-talkie.

## Hearing a talk

- **Android app:** your phone listens all the time, even with the screen off, and plays the talk out loud. The notification bar shows "Walkie-talkie on".
- **iPhone:** a notification says "Walkie-talkie from Amna": tap it to listen.
- **Browser:** you hear talks only while a KiddieTrac tab is open. If your browser blocks sound, tap **▶ Listen** on the card.

A card shows who is talking, with **Busy, can't talk** (tells them you can't talk right now) and **Reply** (opens the channel so you can answer).

## Do not disturb

Choose **Do not disturb** on the Walkie-talkie screen: for 1 hour, 4 hours, or until you turn it off. Talks to you are kept in the history so you can listen later. The emergency channel still plays.

## History

Under the talk button, **History** lists the talks on the selected channel that you gave or were sent: when, who, how long, and whether you heard it. Press **▶ Play** to listen again. If you were the speaker, you see how many people heard it and who said they were busy.

Talks are stored encrypted and deleted after the number of days your agency chose (30 by default). Who talked to whom, when and for how long is kept in the audit log; what was said never is.

## Settings (agency admins)

At the bottom of the Walkie-talkie screen:

- **Walkie-talkie** on or off for the agency.
- **Phones listen**: all the time (24/7), or only while the person is clocked in.
- **Keep recordings for (days)**.
- **Emergency channel**: on or off, its name, and who can talk on it.
- **Educators can call**: staff at their own centres, or anyone on staff in the agency.
