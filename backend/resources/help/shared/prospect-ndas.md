# Prospect NDAs

**Sales › Prospect NDAs** sends KiddieTrac's non-disclosure agreement to someone who is not a customer yet — before a
deep-dive demo, or before you share pricing or a roadmap. The prospect signs from a private link and adds their
business legal name, full legal business address and role. The signed PDF goes to them and to sales@kiddietrac.com,
and is noted on the lead when you link one. Sales reps open it from the Sales menu; superadmins find the same list as
the **🤝 Prospect NDAs** tab in **Forms Manager**.

[[open: #prospect-ndas | Open Prospect NDAs]]

## Sending the NDA

1. Press **🤝 Send NDA to a prospect**. The **🤝 Send our NDA to a prospect** window opens.
2. Pick the lead in **Link to a sales lead (optional)**. The name, email and company fill in from the lead.
3. Check **Prospect’s name** and **Email** (both are needed).
4. Type the **Company (optional — they confirm the legal name)** if it is not filled in.
5. Add a **Personal note (optional)** for the email if you like.
6. Press **Send NDA**. A toast says "NDA sent to …" and the row appears with the status **Sent**.

> 💡 Press **👁 Preview NDA** on the row to see exactly what the prospect will sign.

## Following it up

1. Watch the **Status** column. **Sent** shows "Opened …" under it once the prospect opens the link.
2. If they lost the email, press **📤 Resend link**. A fresh link goes out ("New link sent to …").
3. When they sign, the status becomes **✅ Signed** and the **Business** column shows the legal name, signer and role they entered.
4. Press **📄 View signed PDF** to open the signed copy.

## Cancelling or replacing a link

1. Press **✖ Cancel** on a row that is not signed.
2. Confirm **Cancel NDA**. Their link stops working; you can send a new one later.
3. A link works for **30 days**. After that the row shows **Link expired**: press **📤 Send a new link**.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Sales rep | Send, resend, cancel and view every prospect NDA from **Sales › Prospect NDAs**. |
| Platform admin (superadmin) | The same, from the **🤝 Prospect NDAs** tab in Forms Manager. |
| Agency staff | Do not see this screen: it is KiddieTrac's own sales paperwork. |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? "A name and a valid email are needed." | Fill in **Prospect’s name** and a real email address, then press **Send NDA** again.
??? "Saved, but the email did not go: …" | The NDA was saved but the email failed. Check the address, then press **📤 Resend link**.
??? "A signed NDA is a record and cannot be cancelled." | Once signed, the NDA is kept for good. Nothing more is needed.
??? The prospect says the link has expired. | They see "This link has expired. Please ask your KiddieTrac contact for a new one." Press **📤 Send a new link**.
??? "No NDAs sent yet." | Nobody has sent one. Press **🤝 Send NDA to a prospect** to send the first.
??? Who gets the signed copy? | The prospect (by email) and sales@kiddietrac.com. If a lead was linked, the lead's history notes it.
