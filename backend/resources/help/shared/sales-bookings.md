# Sales meeting bookings

**Sales › Bookings** is where meeting requests from the KiddieTrac website land. Nothing is confirmed to the prospect
until someone on the sales team accepts it here. Accept, move or decline each request, and the prospect gets an email
with an Outlook invitation and a Teams link. You can also book a meeting yourself.

[[open: #sales-bookings | Open Bookings]]

## Answering a meeting request

1. Open **Sales › Bookings**. New requests are under **Needs a decision**, each showing when **They asked for** it and the **Reason given:**.
2. Press **Accept** to keep the time, **Modify** to offer a different one, or **Decline**.
3. For **Accept**: check the **Time (Eastern)** and **Minutes**, add anyone under **Also invite (optional)**, and leave the Teams link blank so a Teams meeting is created automatically (or paste one).
4. Keep **Email them (recommended)** ticked so the prospect gets the invitation.
5. Press **Accept & send confirmation**. A **Meeting confirmed** toast appears with "Email sent to …", and the booking moves to **Upcoming**.

> ℹ️ If you see "Saved, but the email to … No email sent.", the meeting is booked but the prospect was not told. Email them yourself or press **Change time** to send again.

## Changing or cancelling a meeting

1. Find the meeting under **Upcoming**.
2. Press **Change time**, set the new time and press **Confirm the new time** — or press **Cancel meeting** and then **Cancel the meeting**.
3. Add a **Reason (optional, one line)** if you want it in the email.
4. The prospect is emailed (if **Email them (recommended)** is ticked) and the booking updates.

Declined and past meetings are under **Past & declined**.

## Booking a meeting yourself

1. Press **New meeting**.
2. Pick the lead, the time and the people to invite.
3. Save. The invitation goes out from the sales mailbox and the meeting appears in **Upcoming** and on the **Calendar**.

## Setting a standing Teams link

1. Press **Booking settings**.
2. Paste a **Default Teams link**. It is used only if a Teams meeting cannot be created automatically.
3. Press **Save**.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Sales rep | Accept, modify, decline, cancel and book meetings for any lead. |
| Platform admin | The same, plus booking settings. |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? A prospect says they got no invite. | Open the booking: if "No email sent." shows, the email failed. Check the prospect's email on the lead and use **Change time** (same time) to send again.
??? The Teams link is missing. | KiddieTrac could not create the Teams meeting. Paste a link when you accept, or set a **Default Teams link** in **Booking settings**.
??? What happens if they don't show up? | Mark it on the lead; see the no-show guide for the follow-up steps.
??? Where do replies go? | Replies to the invitation go to the sales mailbox.
