# Play Spark in your room

Play Spark gives you a fresh play idea in one tap, pitched so every age in your room can join in, using the materials you actually have. When you have run it, one tap logs it to each child's daily record and drafts an observation for each child. Open it when you need an activity now, to see the room's plan for the week, or to find an idea you saved.

[[open: #play-spark | Open Play Spark]]

The tabs you see depend on what your agency has switched on: **✨ Right now**, **📅 This week**, **📚 Library** and **📊 Monthly report**.

## Getting an idea right now

1. Open **Operations > Play Spark** and press **✨ Right now**.
2. Check the **Room**. Under it you see how many children are signed in now, this week's theme and the learning areas the room needs more of lately.
3. Under **Ages**, tap the age groups joining in (**Infants**, **Toddlers**, **Preschool**, **School-age**).
4. Choose **Where** (**🏠 Indoor**, **🌳 Outdoor** or **Either**), the **Time** (10 to 45 min) and **Your involvement** (**Children lead**, **Guided** or **Hands-on**).
5. Tick the **Materials on hand**. To add one, type it in **Add a material…** and press **Add**, or press **📷 Scan** and photograph the materials (materials only, no people; the photo is not kept).
6. Optionally type a **Theme (optional)**, or tap an activity under **On today's plan · tap to spark it**.
7. Press **✨ Spark an idea**. The idea appears with a picture of the end result, what you need, how to set it up and steps for each age.

> 💡 Not quite right? Press **🔄 Another idea** for a different one with the same choices.

## Running it and logging it

1. Read the **🛡️ Safety check** box (it says **⚠️ Safety check - read before you start** when extra care is needed).
2. Tick **I have read the safety check**. The **✅ We did it** button becomes available.
3. After the activity, press **✅ We did it**. Under **Who joined in? Signed-in children are ticked.** untick anyone who sat it out.
4. Optionally add **A note (optional)**, and keep **Draft an observation for each child (not shared until you review it)** ticked if you want the drafts.
5. Press **Log it**. A message says how many children it was logged for. Families see the activity in their child's day and on **Play at home**.

Other buttons on an idea: **☆ Save** (adds it to **★ Favourites**), **📚 Share to library** (shares your idea with your agency), **📅 Add to this week** (puts it on a day of the room's weekly plan) and **🖨️ Print**.

## Using the weekly plan

1. Press **📅 This week** to see the room's lesson plan, day by day.
2. Press **✏️ Edit week**, then **+ Add activity** under a day, or change what is there.
3. Choose **Draft** (staff only) or **Published** (families can see it).
4. Press **💾 Save week**.

## Finding saved ideas and the monthly report

1. Press **📚 Library** and choose **My ideas**, **★ Favourites** or **📚 Agency library**.
2. Press an idea to open it and use it again, with the same **✅ We did it** button.
3. Press **📊 Monthly report**, pick the room and the month to see how many Play Spark activities ran, how many children joined in and which learning areas they covered. Press **🖨️ Print** to print it.

## Who can do what

- **Educators** see **✨ Right now**, **📅 This week**, **📚 Library** (your ideas, favourites and the agency library) and **📊 Monthly report**.
- **Home visitors** see the same tabs except **📅 This week**, and cannot add an idea to a weekly plan. Play Spark needs a room, so a home visitor who is not placed in one sees a message instead of the form.
- **Directors and admins** also see **🗓️ Plan the week**, **👥 Team ideas** and **💡 Activity ideas**.
- **✨ Spark an idea**, **📷 Scan** and AI week drafts need AI switched on for your agency. Without AI, saved ideas, their pictures and **✅ We did it** still work.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? I see "You are not placed in a room yet. Ask your director to add you to one." | Play Spark works per room. Ask your director to place you in a room.
??? I see "AI is switched off for this agency". | New ideas and scans are paused. Your saved ideas are still in **📚 Library**. Ask your agency admin about AI.
??? The ✅ We did it button is greyed out. | Tick **I have read the safety check** first.
??? Scan says "That photo has a person in it." | Take a new photo of just the materials.
??? I see "No children are placed in this room." | No children are in the room's list, so there is nobody to log it for. Ask your director.
??? Saving a favourite says "Only the person who made this idea can save it." | Only the person who made an idea can add it to their favourites. Ideas you made yourself are in **My ideas**.
??? I see "Nothing here is switched on for your agency." | None of Play Spark's features are on. Ask your agency admin.
