# Opening the app, signing in and unlocking

## Opening KiddieTrac

Open the app, or go to **app.kiddietrac.com** in your phone's or computer's browser. A short
start-up screen shows while KiddieTrac loads. If your agency uses its own brand, you see its
name and logo instead of KiddieTrac's.

## Signing in

1. Enter your **Email or username** and your password.
2. Press **Log in**.
3. If you have two-factor authentication, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app
   and press **Verify code**.

Other ways in:

- **Sign in with a passkey** — your face, fingerprint or device PIN instead of a password.
  Add one under **Settings → Security**.
- **Forgot your password?** — get a 6-digit code by email, text or phone call and set a new one.

If one email belongs to more than one account, you are asked for your **username**.

## Unlocking quickly

In the app you can skip the password next time:

- **Fingerprint / Face sign-in** — turn it on under **Settings → Security**.
- **Quick-unlock PIN** — a 4–6 digit PIN, also under **Settings → Security**.

On the lock screen, use **Use PIN instead** or **Use password instead** if the sensor will not
read you. After too many wrong PINs you are asked to sign in with your password.

## Why you were signed out

The sign-in page tells you why:

- **You signed out.** You pressed Sign out.
- **You were signed out after a period of inactivity.** For security, sessions end when the
  app is left unused.
- **For security, sessions are capped at 12 hours.**
- **You were signed out in another window.** Signing out in one tab signs out the others.
- **Your access has changed.** Your role or agency changed; sign in again to pick it up.

??? I forgot my PIN. | Use your password to sign in, then set a new PIN under Settings → Security.
??? How do I change the language? | Under **My settings → Language**. It is saved for your next sign-in, on any device.
