# Sales email campaigns

**Sales › Campaigns** sends KiddieTrac's own email campaigns to prospects and subscribers — for example an introduction
to childcare centres in one province. Each campaign has its email (written by you or from a KiddieTrac preset), its
people, and its results: who opened it, clicked, replied, booked a demo or unsubscribed. Campaigns go out in small
batches, and only once they have been approved.

[[open: #sales-campaigns | Open Campaigns]]

## Starting a campaign

1. Open **Sales › Campaigns** and press **New campaign**.
2. Enter a **Campaign name** (only your team sees it, for example "Ontario centres: October intro").
3. Under **Start with**, choose the KiddieTrac introduction or a template you pick next.
4. Under **When it goes out**, pick a date and time (**Send at (Eastern Time)**) or schedule it later.
5. Press **Create campaign**. The **Email** tab opens.

## Writing the email

1. Pick a **Layout**: **Standard introduction (with the film)**, **Write your own words**, or one of the finished KiddieTrac designs (only the subject changes).
2. Write the **Email body** with the editor: headings, lists, links (**Add a link**), pictures, **Add a film** and **Add a Book-a-demo button**.
3. Open **Design: header, footer and look** to choose the **Header**, **Footer**, **Signature**, colours, width and spacing.
4. Use **Save as template** to keep it for next time, or **Choose a template** to start from one (**Manage templates** edits the list).
5. Press **Send me a test** from the campaign's **Actions** menu and check the email in your own inbox.

## Adding the people

1. Open the **People** tab and press **Add people**.
2. Choose **Add people from Leads** (filter and tick), paste a list of addresses, or **Add all subscribers** to the KiddieTrac newsletter list.
3. Check the count. People who unsubscribed before are skipped automatically.

## Approving and sending

1. Press **✅ Approve to send**. The campaign shows **Approved**, with when it goes out.
2. If it is scheduled, it goes out at that time. Otherwise press **📤 Send campaign** — "Send to … people".
3. To change the email after it starts, press **Pause** ("Nobody else gets it until…"), edit, then **Resume sending**. Changes need approval again.

## Reading the results

1. Open the **Results** tab for the funnel: **Emails sent**, **Opened**, clicks and **Responses**.
2. Each person's status shows: **Waiting**, **Sent**, **Failed**, **Skipped**, **Replied**, **Booked a demo** or **Unsubscribed**.
3. Leads with a follow-up task are listed so you can call them; press **Add to Leads** to turn a responder into a lead.

From the card's **Actions** you can also **Copy to a new campaign** (its own schedule and results; nothing is sent
until it is approved) or **Delete campaign** (nobody on its list will receive it; its results stay in the audit log).

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Sales rep | Create campaigns, write emails, add people, send tests and read results. |
| Platform admin | The same, for every campaign. |

A campaign is approved by someone the sales team allows to approve, usually a superadmin.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? The send button says "Add people first". | The campaign has nobody on it yet. Open **People** and add them.
??? It says "It will not go out until approved". | Someone who can approve must press **✅ Approve to send**.
??? Clicks are not showing. | Only links added with **Add a link** are tracked. Opens count when images load.
??? Someone asked to be removed. | Their unsubscribe is recorded automatically and they are skipped in every future campaign.
