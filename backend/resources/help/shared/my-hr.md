# My HR

If your agency uses KiddieTrac HR, **My HR** brings together your onboarding checklist, your courses, your reviews and your goals.

## Your onboarding

Your checklist shows each task and when it is due. Some tasks tick themselves off: signing a policy, adding a certificate under **My certificates**, finishing a course. For a document, press **Upload** and choose the file. Press **Mark done** for anything else.

## Your courses

Press **Start the course** to open it. When you have finished, press **Mark complete** and, if asked, type your name to confirm you understood it. You get a certificate to download. Some courses also add PD hours for you.

## Your reviews

Open a review to write your self review: a rating and a comment for each section, and an overall comment. **Save draft** keeps it for later and **Submit** sends it to your reviewer. Once your reviewer submits theirs you can read it, and you both sign by typing your names.

## Goals and one-to-ones

Your goals show their status and progress. Press **Check in** to say how a goal is going. One-to-one notes are private to you, your manager, your agency admins and your centre's director.

You get a reminder before a task or a course is due and when a review is waiting for you.

## My documents

Your documents are in **My documents**: papers your manager shared with you, and company documents such as the handbook. Press ⋮ to **Preview** or **Download** one. If a document asks you to, read it and press **I have read and accept it**.

When you ask for time off you can add a note or a photo, for example a sick note taken with your phone camera, and say which days it covers. You can also add one later from the request with **📎 Add a note or photo**. Sick notes are seen only by you, your director and your agency admins.

If your bank or e-Transfer details for pay are changed, you get an email. If it wasn't you, tell your director straight away.

## Team directory and kudos

Press **Team directory** to see your colleagues and the org chart. Your email and phone are hidden unless you switch on **my email** or **my phone**. Press **Kudos** to thank a colleague with a short message and the values it shows. Kudos add points to Educator awards; you can't send one to yourself.

Some courses end with a quiz: press **Take the quiz** and pass it to finish the course and get your certificate. You see how many you got right. If you run out of tries, ask your manager for another try.

Directors and admins also see **Cover while I'm away**: choose who gets your time-off approvals and emails while you are away. It ends by itself after the last day.

## Health and safety

Press **Report a hazard** to tell your director about something unsafe; you can add a photo straight from your phone camera. **My H&S duties** shows a duty you hold, such as first aider or fire warden, and whether the certificate it needs is still valid. **H&S tasks** lists safety jobs given to you: add a photo or file as evidence, then press **Close task**. **Safety data sheets** opens the sheet for each product kept at your centre. **Safe work procedures** lists the procedures to read and sign; you are asked again when one changes.

## Shifts to accept and clock-in fixes

If your centre asks staff to accept their shifts, each new shift waits in **Shifts to accept**. Press **Accept**, or **I can't do this shift** and say why: your director is told. The shift stays yours until they change it. In **Fix my clock-in**, press **Ask for a fix** next to a clock-in that is wrong, or **A clock-in is missing** if you forgot to clock in. Enter the right times and what went wrong. Your director checks it; you can withdraw it while it waits. A clock-in in a timesheet that is already locked for payroll cannot be changed.

## Quick requests (＋)

On a phone, the **＋** at the top of the screen, beside the ⚙️, opens quick requests: **Request time off**, **Off sick** (with a sick note or a photo), **Log overtime**, **New claim**, **Report a hazard**, **Report an injury** and **My HR to-do**. Each one opens the usual form. On a computer, press **＋ New request** at the top of **My HR**.

## Your to-do

**To do** on **My HR** lists everything waiting for you in one place: onboarding steps, courses and quizzes, your part of a review or a review to sign, company documents to read or accept, letters and tax forms to sign, policies and safe work procedures, shifts to accept, a missing clock-out, H&S tasks and certificates that are running out. The oldest and overdue come first. Tap one to go straight to it.

## Getting set up

The **Getting set up in HR** card lists a few first steps for your role, such as your emergency contact, your policies and your certificates. Each one has **Go** to open the right screen and **?** to explain it. It ticks itself as you go. Press **Hide** to fold it away and **Show** to bring it back.

## Help on every screen

Press **?** on My HR, and on every Human Resources tab, to read the guide for that screen without leaving it. In **Help & guides**, each topic can show **Guides**, **Videos** or **FAQs**. FAQs show where to click.

## Questions

??? Where do I ask for time off? | **My HR > ＋ New request > Request time off**, or **Time off > Request time off**. On a phone, use the **＋** at the top.
??? How do I send a sick note? | **＋ > Off sick**: the request opens with sick leave chosen. Add a photo or a file under the note, and pick the days it covers.
??? Something is on my to-do that I already did. | Open it from **My HR > To do**. Most items tick themselves when the job is done (for example signing the policy). A document asks you to press **I have read and accept it**.
??? I forgot to clock out yesterday. | **My HR > Fix my clock-in > Ask for a fix**. It shows on your to-do as **Clock-out missing** until you ask.
??? Who can see my to-do? | Only you. Your director sees their own team lists, not your list.
