# My HR

**My HR** is your own corner of KiddieTrac HR: your to-do list, onboarding, courses, reviews, shifts, documents, claims, overtime and health and safety, all on one page. Everyone on staff has one, including directors and agency admins. It appears when your agency uses the KiddieTrac HR add-on.

[[open: #my-hr | Open My HR]]

## Finding your way around

1. Open **My HR** from the menu (directors and admins: **Human Resources > My HR**).
2. Look at the tiles at the top: **My time off**, **My shifts & open shifts**, **Shifts to accept**, **Fix my clock-in**, **My documents**, **Claims**, **Overtime**, **Courses**, **Reviews**, **Team directory**, **Kudos**, **Report a hazard**, **Safe work procedures**, **My H&S duties**, **H&S tasks** and **Safety data sheets**.
3. Tap a tile to jump to that part of the page. **My time off** and **Report a hazard** open their own screens.
4. If a tile has nothing for you yet, a note says "Nothing here for you yet."

## Working through your to-do

**To do** lists everything waiting for you in one place: onboarding steps, courses and quizzes, your part of a review or a review to sign, company documents to read or accept, letters and tax forms to sign, policies and safe work procedures, shifts to accept, a missing clock-out, H&S tasks and certificates that are running out. Overdue items come first.

1. Read the count at the top ("3 waiting · 1 overdue").
2. Tap a row. You go straight to the place where it gets done.
3. Do the job (sign, upload, accept…). The row disappears on its own once it is done.
4. Press **Show all** if there are more than eight.

## Getting set up

New staff see a starter card such as **Getting set up in HR** (directors: **Your first week as a director**; admins: **Your first week in HR**).

1. Press **Go** on a step to open the right screen.
2. Press the **?** beside a step to read how to do it.
3. Press **Hide** to fold the card away, or **Show** to bring it back. When every step is ticked it says "All done."

## Making a quick request (＋)

1. On a computer, press **＋ New request** at the top of My HR. On a phone, tap the **＋** at the top of the screen, beside ⚙️.
2. Choose **Request time off**, **Off sick**, **Log overtime**, **New claim**, **Report a hazard**, **Report an injury** or **My HR to-do**.
3. Fill in the form that opens and send it.

> 💡 **Off sick** opens a time-off request with sick leave already chosen. Press **📎 Add a note or photo** to add a sick note taken with your phone camera, and pick the days it covers. Sick notes are seen only by you, your director and your agency admins.

## Onboarding, courses and reviews

1. Under **Your onboarding**, press **Upload** to add a document a task asks for, or **Mark done** for anything else. Signing a policy, adding a certificate or finishing a course ticks its task by itself.
2. Under **Your courses**, press **Start the course**. When you finish, press **Mark complete** (type your name if asked). Some courses end with **Take the quiz**: pass it to finish. Then press **Download certificate**.
3. Under **Your reviews**, press **Open review**, give a rating and a comment for each section, then **Save draft** to keep it or **Submit** to send it to your reviewer.
4. Once your reviewer submits theirs, open the review again to read it and sign by typing your name.
5. Under **Your goals**, press **Goals and one-to-ones**, then **Check in** on a goal to say how it is going.

> ℹ️ One-to-one notes are private to you, your manager, your agency admins and your centre's director. A quiz shows how many you got right, not which ones; if you run out of tries, ask your manager for another try.

## Your shifts

1. Under **My shifts and availability**, press **Weekly availability** to set when you can work, or **I can't work a date** for a one-off.
2. Next to a shift, press **Give away or swap** to offer it to a colleague. Press **Take shift** or **Accept swap** on one a colleague offers you. Your director makes the final decision.
3. Under **Open shifts you can pick up**, press the ℹ️ on a row to see the shift and ask for it.
4. If your centre asks staff to accept their shifts, each new shift waits under **Shifts to accept**. Press **Accept**, or **I can't do this shift** and say why. Your director is told; the shift stays yours until they change it.

## Fixing a clock-in

1. Under **Fix my clock-in**, press **Ask for a fix** next to the wrong clock-in, or **A clock-in is missing** if you forgot.
2. Enter the right **Started** and **Finished** times and **What went wrong**.
3. Press **Send to my director**. The row shows **Fix asked**; press **Withdraw** while it waits if you change your mind.

> ⚠️ A clock-in inside a timesheet that is **Locked for payroll** can't be changed. Ask your director.

## Documents, claims, overtime and pay

1. Under **My documents**, press ⋮ on a document to **Preview** or **Download** it. If it asks, read it and press **I have read and accept it**.
2. Under **Expense and mileage claims**, press **New claim**, add the receipt (a phone photo works) and send it.
3. Under **Overtime and time in lieu**, press **Log overtime** (if your director logs it for you, the screen says so). Your banked hours show beside it.
4. If your agency runs payroll in KiddieTrac, **My payslips** and **Tax forms** appear too; see [My payslips and tax forms](my-payslips).

If your pay deposit details change you get an email. If it wasn't you, tell your director straight away.

## Team directory, kudos and cover

1. Press **Team directory** to see colleagues, and **Org chart** for who reports to whom. Your email and phone stay hidden unless you switch on **my email** or **my phone**.
2. Under **Kudos**, press **🙌 Give kudos**, pick a colleague, write a few words and the values it shows, then **Send kudos**. Kudos add Educator awards points; you can't send one to yourself.
3. Directors and admins: under **Cover while I'm away**, press **Set cover**, choose who gets your time-off approvals and emails, and the dates. It ends by itself after the last day, or press **End now**.

## Health and safety

1. Press **Report a hazard** to tell your director about something unsafe; add a photo from your camera.
2. Under **Injury reports**, press **Report an injury or near miss**.
3. **My H&S duties** shows a duty you hold (first aider, fire warden…) and whether its certificate is still valid.
4. Under **My H&S tasks**, add a photo or file as evidence, then close the task.
5. **Safety data sheets** opens the sheet for each product kept at your centre (**Open SDS**). **Safe work procedures** lists what to read and sign; press **Open Policies to review**.

## Who can do what

- **Educators and home visitors**: everything above except **Cover while I'm away**.
- **Centre directors and agency admins**: the same for themselves, plus cover. They manage other people's files under **Human Resources**, never in My HR.
- Nobody approves their own time off, claims, overtime or clock-in fixes.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? I don't see My HR. | Your agency needs the KiddieTrac HR add-on. Ask your director.
??? Something is on my to-do that I already did. | Open it from **To do**. Most items tick themselves when done; a document needs **I have read and accept it**.
??? I forgot to clock out yesterday. | **Fix my clock-in > Ask for a fix**. It stays on your to-do as a missing clock-out until you ask.
??? It says "You are not placed at a centre yet. Ask your director." | You need a centre on your account before you can ask for a fix. Your director adds it.
??? Who can see my to-do? | Only you. Your director sees their team lists, not your list.
??? Will I get emails? | You get a bell (and email, if you chose it) before a task or course is due, when a review waits, and when your shifts change.
