# Texts and phone calls — choosing what you get

Your agency can reach you by text message and, for urgent notices, by an automated phone
call. You decide which kinds you want.

[[show: #settings @ Profile > Texts and phone calls | Show me my text and call settings]]

## Where to find it

- **Everyone:** **Settings → Profile**, the **Texts and phone calls** card.
- **Agency admins** can also open **My profile** and choose the **💬 Texts & calls** tab.

Both show the same settings, so a change in one appears in the other.

## Texts

First, texts need your yes. If you have not agreed yet, the card says so, with an
**Agree to receive texts** button. It shows exactly what you are agreeing to; press
**Yes** and a confirmation text arrives on your phone.

- [ ] Make sure your **mobile number** is on your profile.
- [ ] Press **Agree to receive texts** and confirm.
- [ ] Switch off any kinds of text you do not want.

Then each kind of text has its own switch:

| Kind | What it is |
|---|---|
| Sign in and sign out | When your child is signed in or out, and who by |
| Sign-in reminders | A child was not signed in, or was never signed out |
| Announcements | Announcements from your agency, when they are also sent by text |
| Text broadcasts | Messages the office sends to a group, a room or everyone |
| Replies from staff | A reply to a text you sent to the agency number |

You only see the kinds your agency actually sends.

## Phone calls

Announcement calls ring your phone and read a short message out loud — for closures,
evacuations, lockdowns and other urgent news. Each reason your agency calls for has its
own switch, so you could, say, keep closures and emergencies but turn off non-urgent
calls.

**Don't phone me at all** overrides everything above it, **emergencies included**. Texts
and email are not affected. If your agency does not place calls at the moment, the card
says so and keeps your choices for when it does.

![Phone calls: a switch per reason, and "Don't phone me at all" to stop every call](https://api.kiddietrac.com/help-img/my-texts-and-calls.png)

## Replying to a text

- Reply normally, and your message reaches the staff at your centre, who can answer you
  by text.
- Reply **STOP** to stop all texts from your agency to that phone. **START** turns them
  back on. **HELP** tells you who is texting and how to reach them.

## If you share a phone

If two accounts use one number — two parents on one phone, for example:

- the phone gets **one** copy of a text or call, not one per account;
- **STOP** or **Don't phone me at all** from that phone applies to **every** account on it
  at your agency.

## Questions

??? I turned a kind of text on but I still don't get it. | Your agency may have switched that kind off for everyone, or you have not agreed to receive texts yet. Check for the Agree to receive texts button on the card.
??? Will turning off calls stop emergency calls? | Turning off one reason stops only that reason. "Don't phone me at all" stops every call, emergencies included.
??? I replied STOP by mistake. | Text START to the same number and texts resume for every account on that phone.
