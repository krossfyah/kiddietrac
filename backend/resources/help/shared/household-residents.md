# Household residents in a home child care

**🏠 Household residents** lists the adults who live in a home where child care is provided, with their background checks. Licensing rules in many provinces and states require every adult living in the home to have a current check, so this screen is where the home keeps that record. You see the homes you work in or visit.

[[open: #household | Open Household residents]]

## Getting there

1. On a computer, open the menu on the left and go to **Administration**, then **Household residents**.
2. On a phone, tap the **Open Household residents** button at the top of this guide. The screen works the same on a phone.

The menu entry only shows when your agency's rules cover household residents.

## Reading the screen

Each home has its own card with its name at the top. Under it, every adult shows their full name, relationship, date of birth and the date they moved in. Under each adult is the list of their checks, each with a status chip (**Current**, **Due**, **Expired** or **Not on file**), the date done, the renew-by date and the reference number.

If an adult has *No background checks on file.* in orange, a check is missing.

## Adding an adult who lives in the home

1. Scroll to the bottom of the home's card.
2. Type their **Full name** (required).
3. Type the **Relationship** (for example, spouse, adult child, tenant).
4. Set the **Date of birth** and the **Moved in** date.
5. Tap **Add adult**. They appear on the card straight away.

## Adding a background check

1. Find the adult on the home's card.
2. In the row under their name, pick the type of check.
3. Type the **Reference** number from the certificate or letter.
4. Set the date the check was done and the renew-by date. Leave renew-by blank and it is filled in from your rules.
5. Tap **Add check**. The check appears with its status.

[[show: #household @ Add check | Show me]]

> 💡 Add a new check when one is renewed, rather than removing the old one: the history shows the home was always covered.

## Removing an adult or a check

1. To take off an adult who moved out, tap **Remove** beside their name and confirm. They are kept in the record as removed.
2. To take off a check entered by mistake, tap the **✕** beside it and confirm **Remove**.

> ⚠️ A new adult moving in (including a teenager turning 18) usually needs a check before or soon after they move in. Tell your director the same day.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Home child care provider / educator | Add adults and checks for their own home |
| Home visitor | See and update the homes they visit |
| Centre director / agency admin | Every home in their scope |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? I can't find Household residents in the menu. | It only appears when your agency's province or state rules cover adults living in a home child care.
??? The screen is empty. | No home is linked to you yet. Ask your director to check that you are assigned to the home.
??? It says "Could not save". | A required box is empty (the **Full name**) or the connection dropped. Check and tap the button again.
??? Do I upload the check itself here? | No. This screen records the check's type, reference and dates. Give the original to your director for the file.
??? Is anyone told when a check expires? | The status turns **Due** and then **Expired**, and your director sees it on the compliance screens.
