# Sales calendar

**Sales › Calendar** shows the whole sales team's time in one place, like Outlook: website bookings, meetings you
set up, time off and blocks, working hours, and where everyone is. All times are in Eastern Time. Use it to find a
free slot before you offer a prospect a meeting.

[[open: #sales-calendar | Open Calendar]]

## Finding a free time

1. Open **Sales › Calendar**.
2. Switch the view (day, week or **Month**) and use the arrows to move; press **Today** to come back.
3. Each person has their own colour. Use **Hide** / **Show** in the bar to show only the people you need.
4. Look for a gap inside everyone's working hours.

## Adding a meeting

1. Press **New meeting**.
2. Choose the lead, the start and end time, and who to invite. A Teams link is added automatically.
3. Save. The invitation goes out from the sales mailbox and the meeting shows on the calendar and under **Sales › Bookings**.

## Blocking out time off

1. Press **Time off / block**.
2. Choose the day(s) or the hours, and add a short note.
3. Save. The time shows as busy, so nobody books you then.

## Setting your working hours

1. Press **Working hours**.
2. Set the hours you work each day.
3. Save. The calendar uses them to show when you are free.

## Opening an entry

1. Click an entry. A panel opens beside the calendar (the calendar does not move).
2. Read the time, the lead and who is invited; press **Join Teams** or **Open in Outlook** for a meeting.
3. Change it and press **Save**, or **Delete** it. Press Escape or **Close** to shut the panel.

> 💡 The **Whereabouts** row shows who is out of the office or travelling on each day.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Sales rep | See the whole team's calendar; add meetings and their own time off and working hours. |
| Platform admin | The same, for the whole sales team. |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? A meeting is at the wrong time. | The calendar is in Eastern Time. Check the time zone you entered when you booked it.
??? A website booking is not on the calendar. | It shows only once it has been accepted under **Sales › Bookings**.
??? Does deleting a meeting tell the prospect? | Cancel it from **Sales › Bookings** with **Email them (recommended)** ticked so they are told.
??? A no-show is marked on a meeting. | The meeting was marked as missed; follow up from the lead page.
