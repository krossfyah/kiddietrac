# Reminders before meetings and tasks

While KiddieTrac is open, a reminder pops up in the bottom-right corner before your meetings, the events in your connected Outlook or Google calendar, and your tasks. It works like Outlook: it stays on screen until you deal with it.

## What you get a reminder for

- **KiddieTrac meetings** you organise or were invited to (not ones you declined).
- **Your own calendar** events, if you connected Outlook or Google in My settings.
- **Tasks** assigned to you: at the reminder time you set, or at 9:00 on the day they are due.
- **Sales** bookings, calls and follow-ups (sales team only).
- Parents get a reminder only for a meeting they were invited to.

## What you can do with it

- **Join** opens the video call (when the meeting has one).
- **Open** takes you to the meeting, task or event.
- **Snooze** brings it back in 5, 10 or 15 minutes, or when the meeting starts.
- **Dismiss** clears it for good. A dismissed reminder never comes back on another tab, after a reload or on another device. If the meeting is moved to a new time, you get a new reminder.

When several are due at once they are listed together, with **Dismiss all**. The **–** button shrinks the window to a small button you can click to open it again.

## When it reminds you

The default is **15 minutes before**. Change it in **My settings → Reminders** (or the ⚙ in the My day panel): at start time, 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes, 1 hour before, or **Off**.

The sound is in **Settings → Notifications → Sounds & vibration → Meeting & task reminders**. If you already allowed browser notifications, you also get one while KiddieTrac is in a background tab. Reminders only appear while KiddieTrac is open: they never send an email, text or phone notification.
