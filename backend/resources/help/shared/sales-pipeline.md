# Sales pipeline

**Sales › Pipeline** is the sales team's board: every open prospect sits in a column for its stage, from **New** to
**Won** or **Lost**, with the money each stage is worth. Open it each morning to see what is moving, what is stuck and
which follow-ups are due today.

[[open: #sales | Open Pipeline]]

## Reading the board

1. Open **Sales › Pipeline**. The heading reads **Sales pipeline** — "Track prospects from first contact to won."
2. Read the tiles across the top: **Open leads**, **Pipeline value / yr**, **Weighted forecast / yr**, **Follow-ups due** and **My open**.
3. Read the columns: **New**, **Contacted**, **Qualified**, **Proposal Sent**, **Negotiation**, **Won**, **Lost**. Each heading shows how many leads it holds.
4. Under a heading, the green figure is the total estimated yearly revenue in that stage, and "… % →" shows what it counts for at that stage's chance of closing. The weighted figures add up to **Weighted forecast / yr**.
5. On each card read the company, contact, yearly value (from the latest proposal, or entered by hand) and owner. A card whose follow-up date is today or past is flagged.

## Moving a deal forward

1. Tap a card to open the lead.
2. Change its **Stage** at the top of the lead page. A **Stage updated** toast appears.
3. Log what you did in **Activity & history** and set the **Next follow-up** date.
4. Go back to **Pipeline**: the card is in its new column.

> 💡 A lead moves to **Won** by itself when a proposal is signed by both sides, so you rarely need to mark wins by hand.

## Checking your win rate

Under **Win/loss analytics** you see your **Win rate**, **Won revenue**, **Avg won deal** and **Closed deals**. Only
leads marked **Won** or **Lost** count, so close out dead deals as **Lost** rather than deleting them.

## Adding a lead from here

1. Press **+ New lead**.
2. Fill in at least the **Contact name**, then the company, contact details, source and estimated revenue.
3. Press **Create lead**. The card appears in **New**.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Sales rep | See the whole team's pipeline and work any lead. **My open** counts your own. |
| Platform admin | The same, plus sales settings such as plans and pricing. |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? "Could not load leads" | Refresh the page. If it keeps happening, raise a ticket from Help.
??? The value on a card looks wrong. | It comes from the latest proposal; if there is none, it is the figure entered by hand on the lead. Edit **Est. annual revenue** on the lead to change it.
??? Can I drag a card to another column? | No. Open the lead and change its **Stage**.
??? Does anything here email the prospect? | No. The pipeline only shows and logs your work.
