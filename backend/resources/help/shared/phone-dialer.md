# Phone — calling families, staff and leads from the portal

**📞 Phone** lets you call a parent, a member of staff or a sales lead from your computer or the app, without using
your own phone number. The person you call sees your agency's line (or the KiddieTrac line) as the caller ID, never
your personal number. The screen also keeps a list of calls made and, for the people who look after the line, missed
calls, voicemails and texts. Open it from **Operations › Phone** or the 📞 icon in the top bar.

[[open: #phone | Open Phone]]

The banner says whether calling is on for you: "Call families, staff and leads from your browser. They see …" means
you can call. "Calling from the portal is not switched on for you here." means an admin has not given your role
access yet.

## Making a call from the dial pad

1. Open **Phone**.
2. In the dial pad on the left, type the number in **Enter a number**, or press **👥** to pick from your phone's
   contacts (in the app). Use a Canadian or US number with its area code.
3. Optionally type the person's name in **Who (optional)** so the call is easy to find later.
4. Press the green **Call** button.
5. Your browser (or the app) rings first and connects by itself. Then the person you are calling rings. Keep this tab
   open for the whole call.
6. Hang up with the red button. The call appears at the top of **Calls made**.

> ⚠️ The first time, your browser asks to use the microphone. Allow it. If you refused, you see **Microphone needed**
> — "Allow the microphone, then call again."

To call the last number again, press **Redial** (the arrow beside **Call**).

## Calling someone from their record

Every phone number shown in the portal — on a family, a staff record, a lead or a website chat — is a link.

1. Tap or click the phone number.
2. Choose **📞 Call with KiddieTrac** at the top of the little menu (it shows **● Recorded** if your agency records calls), or **📱 Use this device** to call from your own phone.
3. The call starts the same way as from the dial pad.

You can also open the **Contacts** tab on the Phone screen, search your KiddieTrac contacts and leads, and call from
there.

## Finding a past call

1. Open the **Calls made** tab.
2. Type a name or number in **Search calls** to filter the list.
3. Each row shows who you called, when and how it ended (**Talked**, **No answer**, **Busy**, **They hung up**, **You
   hung up** …).
4. If the call was recorded, press **▶ Recording** to play it.

Admins can switch the drop-down from **My calls** to **Everyone's** to see every call made on the line.

## Missed calls, voicemails and texts

The **Missed**, **Voicemail** and **Texts** tabs appear only for the people your agency chose to look after the line.
A red count shows how many are new.

1. Open **Voicemail**.
2. Press **▶ Play** on a message to listen to it.
3. Press the handset button (**Call back**) on the row to ring the caller back.
4. Sales reps can press **➕ Lead** to turn the voicemail into a sales lead: the recording is attached to the new lead.
5. To remove a voicemail for everyone, press the bin button (**Delete this voicemail**) and confirm. This cannot be undone.

In **Texts**, you can reply to numbers that have texted the line. Before texting someone who has never texted the
line and is not a contact, you are asked **Do you have this person's permission to text them?**. A number that texted
STOP cannot be texted until it sends START.

Press **🔔 Alerts** to choose whether you are emailed about **Missed calls** and **Voicemails**.

## Turning calling on (admins)

1. Press **⚙ Settings** under the banner.
2. Tick **Calling from the portal is on**.
3. Under **Who may call**, tick the roles: **Agency admins**, **Centre directors**, **Educators** (and, for the
   KiddieTrac line, **Platform admins** and **Sales reps**).
4. Tick **Record calls** if you want calls recorded. The person called hears "This call may be recorded" first.
5. Press **Save**.

Calling from your agency's own number needs your Telnyx account and a voice number first (**Settings → Phone &
carriers**). See [Carrier settings](carrier-settings).

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Agency admin** | Call (if allowed), see everyone's calls, open **⚙ Settings**, choose who may call and recording |
| **Centre director**, **educator** | Call when the admin has ticked their role. See their own calls |
| **Sales rep**, **platform admin** | Call from the KiddieTrac line, answer its voicemails and texts, turn voicemails into leads |

Parents and home visitors do not have this screen.

## Questions and problems

??? The Call button is greyed out. | Calling is not switched on for your role. Ask your agency admin to tick your role under ⚙ Settings › Who may call.
??? "The portal phone is not connected". | The phone part of the page did not start. Reload the page and try again.
??? "Not a number we can call". | Use a 10-digit Canadian or US number with its area code.
??? "You are already on a call". | Hang up first, then start the next call.
??? The person says my call came from an unknown number. | They see your agency's line, not your own phone. Tell them the number, or add it to the family's notes.
??? I don't see Missed, Voicemail or Texts. | Those tabs are for the people chosen to look after the line. Ask your agency admin.
??? Is the call recorded? | Only if your agency ticked Record calls. The banner and the phone-number menu say "recorded", and the person hears a notice first.
