# Forms to sign — reading, filling and signing your forms

**Forms to sign** lists the forms and policies your agency has asked you to read and sign: a code of conduct, a
safety policy, a training sign-off, a fillable form. You read each one, fill it in if it has boxes, and sign it on
the screen. Open it when the menu or your dashboard shows forms waiting, or when your director asks you to sign
something.

[[open: #my-forms | Open Forms to sign]]

The screen has three tabs: **To sign** (waiting for you), **Drafts** (forms you started and saved) and
**Submitted** (forms you have signed).

## Signing a form you only need to read

1. Open **Forms to sign** and stay on **To sign**.
2. On the form's card, press **📄 Read the form**. It opens in a viewer inside KiddieTrac. Read it to the end.
3. Close the viewer and press **✍️ Sign** on the same card.
4. Draw your signature in the box with your finger or mouse ("Draw your signature below to confirm you have read this
   form").
5. Press **Submit signature**. **Thank you — your signature has been recorded.** appears and the card leaves **To
   sign**.

## Filling in and signing a fillable form

1. On the form's card, press **📝 Fill & sign**. The form opens with boxes to fill.
2. Tap a highlighted box and type. Work through every box.
3. To finish later, press **Save draft**. The form waits in **Drafts** with **Last saved** and the time.
4. When every box is filled, press **Sign & submit**, draw your signature and confirm.
5. **Form submitted** appears. The form moves to **Submitted**.

To pick up a draft, open **Drafts** and press **Continue** on its card.

> ⚠️ Closing a form without saving asks "Close without submitting? Anything you typed will be lost." Press **Save
> draft** first if you want to keep what you typed.

## Fixing a form sent back for correction

1. A returned form is back in **To sign** with an amber **Sent back for correction** box and the office's note.
2. Read the note.
3. Press **📝 Fill again** or **✍️ Sign again**, correct what was asked, and submit.

The old copy is kept, marked **🔁 Replaced by a corrected copy**.

## Seeing what you have signed

1. Open the **Submitted** tab.
2. Each card shows **Signed** and the date, and how many answers it holds.
3. Press **View my copy** to open your signed copy.

Some forms are **Reusable** (a form you may need again, such as a weekly checklist). After you sign one, it moves to
**Already signed — fill again only if you need to**, below the list. Fill it again only if you need a new copy.

Your signed copies are also kept in **My documents**, with the office's counter-signature status.

## Who sees what

- **Educators and home visitors** see the forms their agency assigned to their role.
- **Directors and agency admins** create and assign forms in **Forms Manager** and see who has signed. They counter-sign
  in **Signed documents** when a form needs it.
- Parents have their own Forms to sign screen with the forms for families.

## Questions and problems

??? The screen says "You're all caught up — nothing to sign right now." | Nothing is waiting for you. Your signed copies are in My documents.
??? I signed a form, but it's back in To sign. | The office sent it back for correction (read the amber note), or it is a reusable form being offered again.
??? "The form filler is not available." or "Signature pad is not available." | Part of the page did not load. Refresh the page and try again.
??? "Could not submit — please try again." | Your connection dropped while sending. Your draft is kept if you saved it; try again in a moment.
??? I signed the wrong form or made a mistake. | You cannot undo a signature yourself. Tell your director: they can send it back to you for correction.
??? Do I get a copy by email? | No. The signed copy, with the date, time and signature stamp, is kept in My documents.
