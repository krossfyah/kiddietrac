# Getting help: raise a ticket

Something not working, a question about how to do something, or a request for your agency?
Raise a ticket. It goes to the right people, and you can follow it until it is answered.

[[open: #tickets | Open Support tickets]]

## Where to find it

- **Parents and educators:** the **🛟 Support** tile on your home screen.
- **Directors and admins:** **Administration → Support tickets** in the menu.
- Then press **+ Raise ticket** at the top of the screen.

## Raise a ticket

1. Press **+ Raise ticket**. You see **What can we help you with?**
2. Pick the card that fits. The cards are in two groups:
   - **Get help with KiddieTrac** — answered by the KiddieTrac team: **Report a problem**,
     **How do I…?** and **Suggest an improvement**.
   - **Ask your agency** — answered by your agency's administrators: **Account & sign-in**,
     **Billing & payments**, **Enrollment & families**, **Maintenance / repair**,
     **Policy & compliance** and **Something else**. Maintenance and policy are for staff.
3. Answer the questions for that card. Each card asks only what helps: for a problem,
   **What were you trying to do?** and **What happened instead?**; for a repair, **Which home
   or centre?** and **Could someone get hurt?**
4. Under **How is this affecting you?**, choose the line that is true, from **Just a question
   or idea** to **A child’s safety or care is affected**.
5. Add **Screenshots or files** if you have them: drop them on the box, paste a screenshot
   with **Ctrl+V**, or choose files. Images, PDFs, logs, CSV or zip, up to 10 MB each.
6. Check **Best way to reach you**, then press **Send request**.

You see **Request #… sent**, and you are emailed when someone replies.

> **Tip:** For a problem, copy any message on the screen exactly as it read. It is the fastest way to a fix.

## What is sent with it

Open **Sent automatically with your request** to see it: the screen you were on, your device
and browser, the window size and your time zone.

## Following your ticket

Your tickets are listed under **Open**, **Resolved** and **All**. Tick **Raised by me** to see
only yours, or use **Search tickets…**. Open a ticket to read the replies, answer, add files or
confirm it is fixed.

??? Who answers my ticket? | Each card says so: "Answered by the KiddieTrac team" or "Answered by your agency’s administrators".
??? Can I add a screenshot later? | Yes. Open the ticket and add files to it at any time.
