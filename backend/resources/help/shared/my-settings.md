# My settings — your profile, notifications and sign-in

**My settings** holds everything about *your own* account: your name and photo, the language you read KiddieTrac
in, what you are notified about, your saved signature, your password and the devices you are signed in on. What you
change here applies only to you, never to anyone else in your agency. Open it from **Settings › My settings**.

[[open: #settings | Open My settings]]

The screen has a row of tabs across the top: **Profile**, **Notifications**, **Documents**, **Payroll**,
**Calendar**, **Signature**, **Security** and **About**. You only see the tabs that apply to your role (see *Who sees
what* below). The tab you last opened is remembered until you sign out.

## Updating your profile

1. Open the **Profile** tab.
2. To change your picture, press **Change photo** and choose a clear photo of your face. It is checked on your device
   before it uploads.
3. Correct **First name**, **Last name**, **Phone**, **Date of birth** or **Email**.
4. If one email address is shared by more than one account, set a **Username (optional)** to sign in with instead.
5. Press **Save profile**. **✓ Saved** shows under the button.

> ℹ️ Changing your email changes the address you sign in with and where KiddieTrac emails you.

Further down the **Profile** tab:

- **Your KiddieTrac ID** — your reference code. Quote it when you contact the office or support.
- **Your bio** (home child care providers) — a few sentences about you that families see on your provider card and
  in their welcome email. Press **Save bio**.
- **Language** — pick English, Français, Español or हिंदी. It is saved for your next sign-in on any device.
- **My day panel** (directors, admins, sales) — how far ahead the right-hand panel's *Coming up* list and *Meetings*
  look: from **Today** to **1 month**. It saves as soon as you pick.
- **Reminders** — how long before a meeting, calendar event or task a reminder pops up in KiddieTrac.
- **Keep KiddieTrac ready** (the app) — keeps the app warm so it opens quickly.

## Choosing what you are notified about

1. Open the **Notifications** tab. It has four sub-tabs.
2. In **🔔 Notify me about**, switch each topic on or off, for the app and for email. Your choices save as you
   switch them.
3. In **📱 Texts & calls**, choose which text messages and phone calls you want, or **Don't phone me at all**.
4. In **📲 Push & alerts**, allow notifications on this device. Press **Send test notification** to check that one
   arrives. Staff can also send themselves a **🚨 Test urgent alert**.
5. In **🔊 Sounds & vibration**, pick the sound and vibration for each kind of notice.

> ⚠️ A topic showing **🔒 Required by … for your role** has been made required by your agency. You cannot switch it
> off; ask your agency admin if you think it should not apply to you.

## Telling your agency how you are paid

Educators, home visitors, centre directors and agency admins have a **Payroll** tab.

1. Open the **Payroll** tab.
2. Under **How you are paid**, choose **🏦 Direct deposit** or **Interac e-Transfer**.
3. For direct deposit, enter the **Name on the account**, **Transit number**, **Institution number** and
   **Account number**. They are on a void cheque or your bank's direct-deposit form.
4. Press **Save**. The tab then shows **On file:** with only the last digits.

Only you can change these details. They are stored encrypted, and only your administrator and centre director can
see them.

## Your documents and calendar

- **Documents** — the same list as **My documents**: files your agency shared with you and forms you have signed.
- **Calendar** — **Add to my calendar** gives you a private link that puts your approved time off, closures and
  holidays, dated announcements and field trips in Apple, Google or Outlook calendar. Press **Copy link** and add it
  to your calendar app.

## Saving your signature

1. Open the **Signature** tab.
2. Under **My signature**, press **Draw and save**, sign with your finger or mouse, and confirm.
3. Do the same under **My initials**.

Your saved signature is never put on a document by itself. When a form asks you to sign, you tap **By tapping, I sign
this document with my saved signature** — or draw a fresh one.

## Keeping your sign-in safe

1. Open the **Security** tab.
2. To change your password, enter your **Current password**, then your **New password** twice under **Confirm new
   password**, and press **Change password**.
3. Under **Two-factor authentication**, set up a code from an authenticator app. Some roles show **Required for your
   role**: you must keep it on.
4. Under **🔑 Passkeys**, press **Add a passkey** to sign in with your fingerprint, face or device PIN instead of a
   password.
5. Under **📱 Signed-in devices**, press **Manage devices** to see every phone, tablet and browser signed in to your
   account and sign one out, for example a lost phone.

On the app you also see **Fingerprint / Face sign-in** and a **Quick-unlock PIN** for this device.

> ⚠️ **Delete my account** at the bottom of **Security** closes your account at once: you are signed out on every
> device and cannot sign in again. Records your agency must keep by law stay with the agency. Staff should talk to
> their director first.

The **About** tab shows the app and web build (support may ask for it) and the **Diagnostics overlay** switch, which
you only turn on when support asks.

## Who sees what

| Tab | Who sees it |
|---|---|
| **Profile**, **Notifications**, **Documents**, **Signature**, **Security**, **About** | Everyone |
| **Payroll** | Educators, home visitors, centre directors and agency admins (not resellers, auditors or sales) |
| **Calendar** | Paid staff, the KiddieTrac team and sales |
| **My day panel** card | Agency admins, centre directors, platform admins and sales |

Parents have their own version of this screen with **Payments** and **Photos** tabs: see
[Updating your profile and contacts](updating-your-profile-and-contacts).

## Questions and problems

??? I saved my profile but my name hasn't changed everywhere. | Other screens pick it up the next time they load. Refresh the page. Your name on documents you have already signed does not change.
??? "Could not save: …" when I press Save profile. | Usually the email is already used by another account, or a field is not valid. Correct it and save again; if it keeps failing, ask your agency admin.
??? I don't get any notifications on my phone. | Open Notifications › 📲 Push & alerts, allow notifications, then press Send test notification. If the test does not arrive, check that notifications for KiddieTrac are allowed in your phone's settings.
??? Why can't I switch a notification off? | Your agency made that topic required for your role (🔒 Required by …). Only your agency admin can change that.
??? I don't see the Payroll tab. | It shows for educators, home visitors, centre directors and agency admins only. Sales, resellers and auditors are not paid through KiddieTrac payroll.
??? I lost my phone. | Open Security › 📱 Signed-in devices › Manage devices and sign the lost phone out. Then change your password.
??? Does changing the language change it for others? | No. Language is your own choice, saved to your account.
