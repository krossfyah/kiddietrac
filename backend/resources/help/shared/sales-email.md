# Sales email (the shared sales mailbox)

**Sales › Sales email** is the email client the sales team shares. It shows the team's mailboxes (sales@, info@ and any
other KiddieTrac mailbox that was added), so everyone reads the same inbox, answers prospects from the same address and
sees what a colleague already replied. It works like Outlook: mailboxes and folders on the left, the message list in
the middle and the open email on the right. On a phone you see one pane at a time, with **Back** to return.

[[open: #sales-email | Open Sales email]]

## Adding a team mailbox

1. Press **＋ Add account** at the bottom of the left pane.
2. Type the mailbox address (for example info@) and press **Next →**, or pick the provider tile.
3. For Microsoft 365 press **Sign in with Microsoft**. If the mailbox has its own sign-in (like sales@), sign in as that address. If it is a shared mailbox with no password of its own, sign in as **yourself** (you need Full Access to it in Microsoft 365).
4. Allow the permissions Microsoft asks for. The mailbox appears in the left pane for the whole sales team.

> ℹ️ A mailbox added here belongs to the sales team, not to you: every rep and superadmin sees it. Your own
> agency mailbox (if you have one) stays in the separate **Email** screen.

## Reading and answering prospects

1. Click a mailbox's **Inbox** (or another folder) in the left pane.
2. Use **Search this folder** and **All** / **Unread** to find the message, then click it.
3. Press **Reply**, **Reply all** or **Forward**. The reply starts from the mailbox the email came to and already has that mailbox's signature.
4. Add people in **To**, and press **Cc** or **Bcc** if you need those lines.
5. Press **📎 Attach** for files (up to 10 files and 25 MB per email), and **👁 Preview** to see exactly what goes out.
6. Press **➤** (Send), or **Ctrl+Enter**.

Use the toolbar on an open email for **Mark unread**, **Flag**, **Move**, **Archive** and **Delete**.

## Letting the AI help with a reply

1. In a reply or a new email, press **✨ AI**.
2. Choose **💡 Suggest replies** (on a reply), or one of **📝 Turn my notes into an email**, **✍️ Make it clearer**, **✂️ Make it shorter**, **😊 Make it friendlier**, **👔 Make it more formal** or **🔤 Fix spelling & grammar**.
3. Read the text it puts in the box ("✨ AI suggestion in place - read it before sending."). Change it as you like, or press **↩ Undo** to put back what you had.

> ⚠️ The AI only suggests. Nothing is sent until you press Send, and the signature and the quoted email are never changed.

## Signature, out-of-office and new-mail alerts

1. Press **⚙️ Manage accounts**, then **Settings** beside the mailbox.
2. Set the mailbox's **Signature** and **Automatic replies (out of office)**. These are shared: they change the mailbox for the whole team.
3. Tick **Alert me when new mail arrives in this inbox (bell, phone and sound)** and pick a sound. This choice is only for you; everyone else on the sales team keeps their own.
4. Press **Save settings**.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Sales rep | Read, reply, send and file mail in every team mailbox; add a mailbox; set their own alerts. |
| Platform admin (superadmin) | The same, from **Sales › Sales email** in the platform menu. |
| Anyone else | Sees "🔒 The sales mailbox is for the sales team and superadmins." |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? A red banner says the mailbox needs to sign in again. | Microsoft stopped trusting the old sign-in (a password change or removed access). Press **🔌 Reconnect** and sign in again. Mail and settings are kept.
??? Sending fails or Reply is greyed out. | "Sending works from mailboxes signed in with Microsoft or Google." A mailbox added another way is read-only here: reconnect it with Microsoft, or answer from its own mail app.
??? "The AI did not answer just now. Please try again." | The AI service was busy. Try again in a moment, or write the reply yourself.
??? I don't hear new mail. | Tick the alert box in that mailbox's **Settings** (it is per person), and check your notification sounds in **My settings**.
??? The list is empty. | No mailbox is connected yet, or the folder is empty. Press **＋ Add account** or pick another folder.
