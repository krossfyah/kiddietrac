# Welcome to Kiddietrac

Kiddietrac is a modern childcare management platform built for Ontario centres. Whether you're a director running operations, an educator caring for children, or a parent staying connected, Kiddietrac brings everything into one simple app.

## What Kiddietrac does

For **directors**, Kiddietrac handles enrollment, billing, ratio compliance, staff certifications, incident reporting, and CCEYA-style compliance exports.

For **educators**, the tablet view lets you quickly log meals, naps, diaper changes, activities, and observations. Photos taken on the tablet are instantly shared with parents. Daily ratios are tracked in real time.

For **parents**, the app provides a live timeline of your child's day, daily AI-summarized digests, photos shared by educators, a messaging thread with the room, and your billing details.

## Three things to know

1. **Your role determines what you see.** When you log in, Kiddietrac shows you the right view automatically.
2. **Data flows in real time.** When an educator logs a meal, it appears in the parent's app within seconds.
3. **Your data is yours.** Centres own their own data and can export it at any time.
