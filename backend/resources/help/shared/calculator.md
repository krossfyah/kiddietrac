# The calculator

A small calculator pops up over whatever screen you're on, so you can work out a fee, a prorated amount or a refund without leaving the portal.

## Opening it

- Click **🧮** in the top bar, or
- press **`Alt + C`** (Windows / Linux) or **`⌥C`** (Mac) from anywhere in the portal, even while you're typing in a form.

Press the same shortcut, **Esc**, or **✕** to close it. Drag it by its title to move it; it opens in the same spot next time.

## Using it

Click the keys or type on your keyboard while the calculator is selected:

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| `0–9` `.` | Numbers |
| `+` `-` `*` `/` | Add, subtract, multiply, divide (`x` also multiplies) |
| `( )` | Brackets |
| `%` | Percent: `200 × 15%` = 30, `200 + 15%` = 230, `200 − 15%` = 170 |
| **Enter** or `=` | Work it out |
| **Backspace** | Delete the last character |
| **Delete** | Clear |

The answer shows as you type. Your last 12 sums are listed under the display; click one to use its answer again. The list is cleared when you close the browser tab.

## Putting the answer into a form

Click into the field you want to fill (an amount, a rate, hours), open the calculator, work it out and press **Insert ↵**. The answer goes straight into that field. **Copy** puts it on your clipboard instead.
