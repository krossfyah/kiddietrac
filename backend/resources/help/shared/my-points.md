# My points and rewards

You earn **points** every month for using KiddieTrac: observations, lesson plans, walks,
incident reports, the photos and videos you share, and your daily logs. Open **⭐ My points**
to see them.

- **Your total** for the month, and **what earned it**.
- **How to earn points**: what each activity is worth and the most that count in one day.
- **Your centre this month**: a friendly leaderboard of points. Nobody sees what anyone was
  paid.
- **Your rewards**: bonuses, payouts and gift cards from your director or admin.

## Gift cards

When you receive a gift card you get a notification and an email, but the card itself stays
safely in the app. In **Your rewards**, click **Show my gift card** to see the code, or
**Open your gift card** for a Giftbit or Tango Card card (a Giftbit card may let you choose the brand). A Tango Card card may show a card number and PIN instead, with instructions for using it.

A card marked **Being prepared** appears a few minutes later.

Use the month picker at the top to look back at earlier months.
