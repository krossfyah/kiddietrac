# My points and rewards

**My points** shows the points you earn each month for the work you record in KiddieTrac: observations, lesson plans,
walks, incident reports, the photos and videos you share, daily logs and (with KiddieTrac HR) kudos from colleagues.
At the end of the month your director or agency admin can turn points into a bonus, a payout or a gift card. Educators,
home visitors and centre directors earn points.

[[open: #my-points | Open My points]]

## Checking your points this month

1. Open **⭐ My points** from the menu (or the **Home** tiles).
2. Read the big number on the left: your total for the month, with the month name above it.
3. Look at **What earned them**: one box per activity with the points it gave you and how many you logged. A box
   that says *daily limit reached on some days* means some entries that day did not count (see the next section).
4. Scroll down to **🏆 Your centre this month** to see the friendly leaderboard. Your own row is highlighted and says
   *(you)*. It shows points only, never what anyone was paid.

> ℹ️ Points are worked out from the records your work leaves behind, so there is nothing to claim or submit. If you
> delete an observation or a log, its points go with it.

## Seeing how to earn more

1. Find the **💡 How to earn points** card.
2. Each line shows an activity, what one entry is worth, and the most that count in one day (*up to 10/day*, for
   example).
3. Spread your entries over the day: once you reach an activity's daily limit, more entries still save normally but
   earn no extra points that day.

> 💡 Your agency sets the values and limits, so they can differ from another agency's. The card always shows yours.

## Looking back at an earlier month

1. Press the month box at the top right of the screen.
2. Pick the month and year.
3. The totals, the leaderboard and your rewards redraw for that month.

## Opening a reward or gift card

1. Go to **🎁 Your rewards**. Each reward shows the amount and type, the month, the points and who gave it, plus a
   note if they wrote one.
2. Check the coloured status: **Waiting for pay run** (a bonus added to your next pay), **Paid**, **Sent**,
   **Being prepared**, **Redeemed**, **Expired**, **Failed** or **Cancelled**.
3. For a gift card, press **Show my gift card**. The code appears with a **Copy** button, and **Open your gift card ↗**
   opens the card's own page when there is one (some let you pick the brand). Some cards also show how to use them.
4. Press **Hide** to cover the code again.

> ⚠️ Keep your gift card code private. It is shown only to you, inside the app, never in the email.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do here |
|---|---|
| Educator, home visitor | See their own points, the centre leaderboard and their own rewards. |
| Centre director | Earns points too, and sees the same screen. Awards points to staff under **Educator rewards**. |
| Agency admin | Does not earn points. Gives rewards in **Educator rewards** and sets values in **Settings → Educator awards**. |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? The screen says "Points are switched off for your agency right now." | Your agency has turned points off. Ask your director or agency admin if you think it should be on.
??? I don't see the leaderboard. | Your agency can hide it. Your own points and rewards still show.
??? My points look too low. | Check the daily limits in **How to earn points**: entries past the limit don't add points. Only entries in the month shown count, and deleted entries lose their points.
??? I don't see Kudos from colleagues. | Kudos only count when your agency has KiddieTrac HR.
??? My gift card says Being prepared. | The card is being bought. It usually appears within a few minutes. Open the screen again later.
??? My reward says Failed. | Something went wrong buying the card. Your director or agency admin can see why and send it again.
??? Will I be told when I get a reward? | Yes. You get a notification and an email saying a reward is waiting. The gift card code itself stays in the app.
??? The screen shows an error instead of my points. | Refresh the page. If it keeps happening, raise a ticket from **Help**.
