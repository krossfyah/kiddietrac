# Proposals (every proposal at a glance)

**Sales › Proposals** lists every proposal the sales team has made, across all leads: where each one stands, what it
is worth and what needs you next (for example a client signature waiting for your countersignature). Proposals are
built and sent from the lead's **📄 Proposals** tab — this screen is where you track them all together.

[[open: #sales-quotes | Open Proposals]]

## Reading the numbers

The tiles at the top count **Proposals** (with the drafts under it), **Open pipeline** (waiting on the client),
**Won**, **Win rate**, **Open rate** (how many sent proposals were opened), **Average proposal** (before tax) and
**To countersign**. Under them, four charts: **📈 Proposals by month**, **🍩 Where they stand**,
**🪜 From sent to customer** (Sent › Opened › Signed › Customer) and **💰 Value won by month**.

## Finding a proposal

1. Pick a filter above the table: **All**, **Open**, **Signed & won**, **To countersign**, **Drafts**, **Declined** or **Expired**. Each shows how many it holds (empty filters are hidden).
2. Type in **🔍 Search client, number, title…** to narrow by client, proposal number, title or rep.
3. Click a column heading (**Proposal**, **Client**, **Status**, **Value**, **Sent**, **Valid until**, **By**) to sort; click again to reverse.
4. Read **Progress** for how far it got, and "opened 3×" under it for how often the client opened it. A red **Valid until** date means a sent proposal has run out.

The footer shows how many proposals match and their **Total**.

## Acting on a proposal

1. Click the row, or choose **⋮ > 👁 Open**, to open the lead on that proposal. Edit, email, copy the signing link or countersign it there.
2. Choose **⋮ > 📄 Download PDF** to save the branded PDF.
3. For a proposal that set up a customer, **⋮ > 🔑 Licences** opens that customer's licences.
4. To remove a test or mistaken proposal, choose **⋮ > 🗑 Delete** and confirm **Delete**. The lead's history keeps a note that it was deleted.

> ℹ️ The statuses are **Draft**, **Sent**, **Opened**, **Client signed** (your turn to countersign), **Signed**,
> **Customer**, **Declined** and **Expired**. A 📎 beside the status means a signed contract is attached.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Sales rep | See every proposal, open, download and delete them. |
| Platform admin (superadmin) | The same, plus **🔑 Licences** for customers it set up. |
| Anyone else | Sees "Proposals are for the sales team and superadmins." |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? There is no Delete on a proposal. | A proposal that already set up a customer cannot be deleted (it shows **🔑 Licences** instead).
??? "No proposals match." | The filter or search hides them. Choose **All** and clear the search box.
??? "Could not load proposals: …" | The list did not load. Refresh the page; if it keeps happening, raise a ticket.
??? How do I make a new proposal? | Open the lead and press **+ New** on its **📄 Proposals** tab. The builder starts from the defaults in **Plans & pricing › 📄 Proposal terms**.
??? Does deleting email the client? | No. Nothing is sent to the client.
