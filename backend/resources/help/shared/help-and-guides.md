# Help & guides — finding the answer you need

**Help & guides** is KiddieTrac's library of step-by-step guides. Everyone has it, and each person sees the guides
for their own role: a parent sees parent guides, an educator sees educator guides, and so on. Open it when you want
to learn a feature, look up how something works, or find the answer to a question before asking someone.

[[open: #help | Open Help & guides]]

## Getting help for the screen you are on

You do not have to come to this screen to get help. Every screen has a round **?** in the top-right corner of its
banner.

1. On any screen, press the **?** in the banner's top-right corner (its tooltip reads **Help for this page**).
2. A help panel slides in from the side with the guide for that screen.
3. Read the guide. Press **Open** or **Show me** buttons inside it to jump to a screen or have the button you need
   ringed for you. The panel gets out of the way so you can see the screen.
4. Under **More on this page**, pick another guide about the same screen if the first one is not what you need.
5. Press **Open full article in Help & Guide** to read it here with its table of contents, or **✕** to close the panel.

> 💡 The **?** is the fastest way to help. The guide it opens is chosen for that screen and for your role.

## Browsing guides by topic

1. Open **Help & guides** from the menu (**Settings › Help & guides**).
2. On the home page, look under **📚 Browse topics**. Each tile is a topic, with the number of guides in it.
3. Press a topic. Its guides show as cards.
4. Use the chips at the top to switch between **📘 Guides** (every guide in the topic), **🎬 Videos** (the guides that
   have a short video) and **❓ FAQs** (every question and answer in that topic).
5. Press a card to read the guide. Press **‹ All topics** to go back.

The home page also shows **⭐ Featured** guides, **🕘 Recently viewed** (the guides you opened last, on this device)
and **🔥 Popular this month**. New to KiddieTrac? The **New to KiddieTrac?** box has **Roles & responsibilities →**
(who can do what) and **Getting started guides**.

On a phone, the topic list becomes a **Pick a category…** drop-down above the guide.

## Searching for a guide

1. Click in the **🔍 Search topics…** box at the top of the topic list.
2. Type at least two letters, for example `invoice` or `sign out`.
3. Matching guides show as a list, with their topic above each title. Press one to read it.
4. Nothing fits? Press **✨ Ask the AI about "…" instead** under the results.

## Reading a guide

- **Numbered steps** are done in order, one action each. Names in bold boxes are the exact buttons, tabs and fields
  you will see on the screen.
- **Green** boxes are tips, **blue** boxes are notes and **amber** boxes are cautions: read the amber ones before you
  act.
- Questions with an arrow open when you press them and show the answer.
- A checklist (boxes you can tick) remembers your ticks in this browser.
- On a long guide, **On this page** lists its sections; press one to jump to it.
- **🖨️** prints the guide and **🔗** copies a link to it, to send to a colleague.
- At the end, answer **Was this article helpful?** with **👍 Yes** or **👎 No**. If you press No, you can say what was
  missing and press **Send**. Your answer helps the guides get better.

## Asking the AI assistant

1. Press **✨ Ask AI** under the banner.
2. Type your question in **Ask anything about KiddieTrac…**, or tap one of the suggested questions.
3. Press **➤**. The answer comes from the help guides and links the guide it used.
4. Press **New chat** to start over.

> ⚠️ AI can make mistakes. Check the linked guide before you change anything important.

## Other buttons on this screen

- **🎬 Take the tour** replays the short welcome tour of the portal.
- **📚 Knowledge base** opens your own agency's guides and policies (what *your* centre does), next to KiddieTrac's
  guides (how KiddieTrac works).
- The **📖 Guides | 🎓 Training** switch at the top opens **Training**: short video tutorials for your part of
  KiddieTrac.

## Who sees what

| Role | Sees |
|---|---|
| Parents | Parent guides: forms, billing, messages, attendance and your account |
| Educators and home visitors | Guides for the classroom or home visits, plus your own account and HR |
| Centre directors and agency admins | Every guide for running a centre or agency. They also see **How the guides are doing**: views, the helpful rate, the most read guides and what people said was missing |
| Sales, resellers and auditors | Guides for their own screens only |

Guides change as KiddieTrac changes, and they show in your portal language (English, French, Spanish or Hindi).

## Questions and problems

??? I can't find a guide for what I'm doing. | Press the **?** on the screen you are using: it opens the guide made for that screen. Or search with a different word, or press ✨ Ask AI.
??? The help panel says "This guide could not be opened just now". | Your connection dropped for a moment. Close the panel and press the ? again, or open Help & guides from the menu.
??? A guide mentions a button I don't have. | Some buttons depend on your role, your centre or an add-on your agency has. Ask your director or agency admin.
??? "Could not find … on this screen" after Show me. | The button may be on another tab, or need a permission you do not have. Read the steps above it to find the tab.
??? Ask AI doesn't answer. | Your agency may have AI features switched off. The guides and search still work.
??? Who do I ask when the guides don't help? | Parents: your centre's office or the Message Centre. Staff: your director. Directors and admins: raise a support ticket.
??? Does pressing 👎 tell anyone who I am? | It records which guide was not helpful and what you typed, so the guide can be fixed. Nobody is emailed.
