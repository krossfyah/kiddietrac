# Launch demo

**Sales › Launch demo** opens KiddieTrac's demo environment (the Test Agency) in a new browser tab, already signed in
as a demo administrator. Use it on a call or in a meeting to show a prospect the real product — attendance and
check-in, billing, the parent app, messaging and reports — without typing a password and without showing anyone's real
data.

[[open: #sales-demo | Open Launch demo]]

## Starting a demo

1. Open **Sales › Launch demo**.
2. Press **🚀 Open demo in a new tab**. The button reads **Preparing demo…** for a moment.
3. A toast says **Demo opening** ("A new tab is launching the Test Agency.") and the demo opens in a new tab.
4. Share that tab on your call. Your own sales session stays signed in, in the first tab.

> ℹ️ The demo session lasts up to 8 hours. If it signs out in the middle of a meeting, come back to this tab and
> press **🚀 Open demo in a new tab** again.

## Showing the product

1. Start on the demo's dashboard so the prospect sees a day at a glance.
2. Go straight to the problem they told you about (for example billing for an agency still on spreadsheets).
3. Show the parent side from the same tab when it helps (check-in, photos, invoices).
4. Close the demo tab when you are done.

> ⚠️ The demo is a shared environment. Anything you add or change there is seen by the next person who runs a demo:
> do not type a prospect's real details into it, and tidy up anything you created.

## After the demo

1. Go back to the lead in **Sales › Leads** or the **Pipeline**.
2. Log what you showed and agreed in the lead's notes.
3. While it is fresh, build a proposal on the lead's **📄 Proposals** tab, or send an NDA first if they asked for one.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Sales rep | Launch the demo as often as needed. |
| Platform admin (superadmin) | The same, from **Sales › Launch demo** in the platform menu. |
| Agency staff | Do not see this screen. |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? Nothing opened when I pressed the button. | Your browser blocked the new tab. Allow pop-ups for the portal and press the button again.
??? "Could not start demo" | The demo sign-in could not be made. With "No demo account is set up for the Test Agency." a platform admin must restore the demo administrator account; for any other message, try again in a minute.
??? Does the prospect see real families? | No. The demo holds demo data only; no real family's information is shown.
??? Is the demo sent to the prospect? | No. It opens in your browser only; nothing is emailed or texted.
