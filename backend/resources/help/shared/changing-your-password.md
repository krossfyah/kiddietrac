# Changing your password

**Settings → Security.** Where you change your password. KiddieTrac also asks everyone
to choose a new one every **90 days**.

## Your first password

When your centre invites you, the invitation email contains a **link to set your own
password**. You choose it yourself on the **Set your password** page. Nobody else ever
sees it. There is no temporary password to change afterwards.

If the link has expired or no longer works, ask your centre to send the invitation again.

> **Note:** If an administrator reset your password and emailed you a **temporary** one, it only works once. After you sign in with it, you must choose a new password before anything else in the portal will open.

## How to change your password

1. Open **Settings** and go to the **Security** tab.
2. Enter your **Current password**.
3. Enter your **New password**, then type it again in **Confirm new password**.
4. Press **Change password**. When it saves, you will see **✓ Password changed**.

[[show: #settings @ Security > Change password | Show me the Change password button]]

Use the new password the next time you sign in.

## What a password must be

The same rules apply everywhere a password is set (the invitation page, a reset, and
this screen):

- at least **8 characters**
- **upper and lower case** letters
- at least one **number**
- at least one **symbol**
- **not** your current password, and not one you have used in the **last 6 months**

As you type, a checklist under the box shows which rules you have met. The button refuses
a password that does not meet them all yet.

## Every 90 days

Passwords expire **90 days** after they were last changed. This is your agency's security
policy, and it is common in childcare because licensing audits and insurers ask for it.

- **Before it expires, you get an email.** The warnings go out 14 days, 7 days and 1 day
  before, and once on the day it expires. You receive each one once, not every day.
- **The email has one button, "Change your password"**, which opens KiddieTrac and asks
  you to sign in as usual.
- **If you do nothing,** nothing is lost and nobody removes your access. The next time
  you sign in after the date, you see **Choose a new password**, and you are taken to
  **Settings → Security**. Everything else waits until you have chosen one. Once it saves,
  the portal works as normal again.

> **Important:** KiddieTrac never emails you a password, or a link that sets one, as part of this reminder. If an email about an expiring password asks for anything else, it did not come from KiddieTrac. Forward it to info@kiddietrac.com.

## If you forget your password

You can reset it yourself.

1. On the sign-in page, press **Forgot?** next to the password box.
2. Enter your **Email**. If more than one account uses that address, also fill in
   **Username**, so the reset goes to the right account.
3. Open the email and follow the link to **Set a new password**.

The reset link **works once and lasts 60 minutes**. If it has expired, request another
one from the sign-in page.

## Too many wrong attempts

After **5 wrong passwords within 15 minutes**, sign-in is paused for **5 minutes**. The
pause lifts by itself. An administrator can also unlock the account straight away. This
protects your account from somebody guessing at it, and it cannot lock you out for good.

## Tips for a strong password

- Longer is stronger. Aim for 12 characters or more.
- Don't reuse a password from another site.
- A passphrase like "OakTree-RainsAt-3am" is easy to remember and hard to guess.
- The **✨ Suggest a strong password** button on the set-password and reset pages will
  make one up for you.

??? I changed my password and I'm still being asked to change it. | Use **Settings → Security → Change password** while signed in. That is the change that starts a new 90-day period.
??? My invitation link says it is invalid. | Links expire, and a newer invitation replaces an older one. Ask your centre to send it again.
