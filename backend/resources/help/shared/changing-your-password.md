# Changing your password

If your centre director just invited you, you received a temporary password in your welcome email. **Please change it immediately after your first login.**

## How to change your password

1. Click your name or avatar in the top-right corner of any page
2. Click **Account settings**
3. Click **Change password**
4. Enter your current password (the temporary one from your welcome email)
5. Enter your new password — at least 8 characters, mix of letters and numbers recommended
6. Click **Save**

You'll need to use your new password the next time you log in.

## What if I forget my password?

If you can't log in, contact your centre director. They can reset your password through their admin dashboard. Self-service password reset is coming in a future update.

## Tips for a strong password

- At least 12 characters
- Mix uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and a symbol
- Don't reuse passwords from other sites
- Consider a passphrase like "OakTree-RainsAt-3am" — easy to remember, hard to guess
