# Notifications — your inbox of alerts and updates

**Notifications** is your personal inbox of everything KiddieTrac told you about: a new message, an announcement, a
form to sign, a time-off decision, a clock reminder, a Safe Arrival alert, a task. The 🔔 bell in the top bar shows
how many are unread and opens the same list. Open this screen to catch up after a shift, find something you
dismissed on your phone, or clear old notices.

[[open: #notifications | Open Notifications]]

Every notification here was also sent to your phone as a push notification if you allowed them. Opening it here
marks it read on every device.

## Reading and opening a notification

1. Open **Notifications** from the menu, or press the 🔔 bell.
2. Unread notifications are highlighted. Each shows what kind it is (**Message**, **Announcement**, **Form**,
   **Task** …), the title, and when it arrived, in your agency's time zone.
3. Press a notification. It is marked read and takes you to what it is about, for example the chat conversation or
   the form to sign.

Notices of the same kind are grouped: press **＋ … earlier like this** under one to see the older ones, and
**－ Hide the earlier ones** to fold them away. The list shows 12 at a time; use the page buttons underneath.

## Filtering and marking read

1. Use the **All**, **Unread** and **Read** buttons above the list to choose what you see.
2. Press **✓ Read all** to mark every notification read at once.

> 💡 "Inbox zero. 🎉" means you have nothing unread.

## Deleting notifications

To delete one:

1. Open the **⋮** menu on its row.
2. Choose **Delete notification**.
3. Confirm "Delete this notification? This cannot be undone."

To delete several:

1. Press **☑️ Select**. A tick box appears on each row.
2. Tick the ones you want, or press **Select all**.
3. Press **🗑 Delete** and confirm. Press **Cancel** or **Done** to leave select mode.

Deleting a notification only removes it from your inbox. The message, announcement or form it pointed to is not
deleted.

## Choosing what you are notified about

Press **🔊 Sounds** on this screen to choose the tone and vibration for each kind of notification. To choose *which*
notices you get, and whether they also come by email:

1. Open **My settings** and the **Notifications** tab.
2. In **🔔 Notify me about**, switch each topic on or off for the app and for email.
3. In **📲 Push & alerts**, allow push notifications on this device and press **Send test notification**.

Some topics may show **🔒 Required by … for your role**: your agency made them required, so they cannot be switched
off.

[[show: #settings @ Notifications > Notify me about | Show me]]

## Who sees what

- **Educators and home visitors** see notices about their children and rooms (or home visits), messages,
  announcements, their forms, time off, timesheets and clock reminders, and safety alerts sent to staff.
- **Sales reps** see notices about their leads, meetings, website chats and the KiddieTrac line.
- Everyone sees only their *own* notifications. Nobody else can read your inbox.

## Questions and problems

??? I got a push on my phone but can't find it here. | Check the Read filter: opening it on your phone already marked it read. If you deleted it, it is gone from the list.
??? Pressing a notification does nothing. | A few older notices have nothing to open. The thing it pointed to may also have been removed or closed.
??? "Could not load your notifications. Check your connection and try again." | Your connection dropped. Refresh the page.
??? I get too many notifications. | Switch off the topics you don't need in My settings › Notifications › 🔔 Notify me about. Required topics stay on.
??? I don't get any notifications on my phone. | In My settings › Notifications › 📲 Push & alerts, allow notifications and send a test. Also check your phone allows notifications for KiddieTrac.
??? Does a chat notification show the message? | No. For privacy, the notice says who wrote to you, not what they wrote. Open it to read the message in the Message Centre.
