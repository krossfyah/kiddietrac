# Sales customers

**Sales › Customers** lists everyone who said yes: each won agency, what it bought, what it is worth each month and
year, whether its KiddieTrac account is set up yet, and what it owes. Use it to check a customer before a renewal
call, to see your monthly and yearly revenue grow, and to spot money that is overdue.

[[open: #sales-customers | Open Customers]]

## Reading your numbers

1. Open **Sales › Customers**. The heading reads "Everyone who said yes: what they bought, what it is worth…".
2. Read the charts: **Customers won** each month, **Monthly revenue** and **Yearly revenue** at the end of each month.
3. Read the rings: **Average customer**, **One-time fees**, **Onboarding** (no KiddieTrac account yet), **No services yet**, **Owed to us**, **Overdue** and **Collected**.
4. Under **Accounts receivable**, see what is owed but not due yet, current and overdue.

## Finding a customer

1. Use **Search customer, ID, contact, service**, or filter by status (**Active**, **Paused**, **Cancelled**).
2. Read the table: **Customer ID**, **Customer**, **Status**, **Services**, **Monthly** and more.
3. Open a row to see the customer's page.

## Reading a customer's page

1. **Agency**, **Contact** and **Owner** show who they are and who on your team looks after them. **Agency contact** and "Invoices go to …" say where bills are sent.
2. **KiddieTrac account** shows **Customer since**, **Next invoice**, **Centres** and **Time zone** — or "Not set up yet: use Approve & set up customer on the proposal."
3. **Services and pricing** lists each **Service** with **Price**, **Discount** and **Total**, then the totals **Before discounts**, **After discounts** and **Total with tax**, split **Monthly**, **Yearly** and **One-time**.
4. **Account (AR)** lists their invoices and payments once their account is live.

> ℹ️ A lead becomes a customer when it is marked **Won**, usually by a proposal signed on both sides. If it shows "No services yet", no proposal has been accepted or signed; its services fill in when one is.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? A won lead shows "Onboarding". | Its KiddieTrac account has not been created yet. Open its proposal and use **Approve & set up customer**.
??? The monthly figure is 0. | The customer has no accepted or signed proposal on file, so there are no services to add up.
??? "Could not load customers" | Refresh. If it keeps happening, raise a ticket from Help.
??? Can I change a customer's price here? | No. Prices come from the proposal; send a new proposal to change them.
