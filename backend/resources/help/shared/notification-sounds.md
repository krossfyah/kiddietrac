# Notification sounds and vibration

Choose the tone (and, on Android, the vibration) for each kind of notification: chats,
announcements, Safe Arrival alerts, sign-ins, invoices and more. Everyone can set their own.

[[open: #settings?tab=notifications&sub=sounds | Open Sounds & vibration]]

## Where to find it

Every role gets there the same three ways:

- **My settings → Notifications → 🔊 Sounds & vibration**.
- The menu under your name at the top: **🔊 Notification sounds**.
- The 🔔 notifications panel: **🔊 Sounds & settings**.

## On a phone (the KiddieTrac app)

1. For each kind of notification, pick a tone from the list. Press **▶** to hear it here
   (an Android phone also vibrates).
2. Pick the vibration for it (Android).
3. To check it for real, use **Show a real notification on this phone with this tone and vibration**.

**🚨 Emergency messages** always ring repeatedly until answered (up to 2 minutes). You can
choose the tone, not whether it rings.

On an **iPhone** the tone you choose is used, but Apple does not let apps set their own
vibration, so iPhones vibrate the standard way.

> **Tip:** If you see "Update the KiddieTrac app to hear these", your app plays one standard
> chime for everything. Your choices are saved and start working when the app is updated.

## On a computer (in a web browser)

While KiddieTrac is open, it plays the same tones in the browser. Under **On this computer,
while KiddieTrac is open**:

- **Play sounds for chats, announcements and other notifications** — on or off.
- **🔉 Volume**.
- **Mute while I'm typing in chat** — no sound while you are writing a message.

When KiddieTrac is closed, notifications come from your browser and use your computer's own
notification sound. Browsers do not let a website choose that sound.

These computer settings belong to that computer and browser. Each phone keeps its own choices.

## Starting again

**Reset all to defaults** puts every tone and vibration back.

??? I hear nothing on my computer. | A browser only plays sound after you have clicked somewhere on the page. Check that the sound switch is on, the volume is up, and the browser tab is not muted.
??? Does this change my texts or emails? | No. Texts and phone calls are set under **Notifications → 📱 Texts & calls**.
