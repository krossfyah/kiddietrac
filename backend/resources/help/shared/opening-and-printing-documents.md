# Opening and printing documents

Anything you open in KiddieTrac — a PDF, a photo of a card, an incident report, an
emergency card, a payslip — opens **inside the portal**, in a panel over whatever screen
you were on.

It used to open a new browser tab. That works badly in the phone app, where there is no
tab bar to come back from, and it is why a document sometimes arrived as a blank window.

## What you get

A dark header across the top of the panel with the document's name, and two controls:

- **🖨 Print**
- **✕** to close and go straight back to where you were

The document fills the rest. Scroll it as normal. Closing the panel leaves the screen
behind it exactly as it was — nothing reloads and nothing loses its place.

## Printing

Press **Print**. On a computer the print dialog opens over the panel and prints the
document, not the page behind it.

**In the phone app** the button reads **🖨 Print in browser** and hands the document to
your phone's browser, which is where a real print dialog lives. The app itself has no
printer support, so this is the difference between printing and not printing at all. The
link it creates is short-lived and single-purpose: it is minted only when you actually
press Print, expires in a few minutes, and is recorded at both ends.

## PDFs in the phone app

Android's app window cannot display a PDF on its own — which is what the blank window
was. The portal now draws PDF pages itself, so they show in the panel like everything
else. A page or two may take a moment on an older phone.

If a document ever fails to appear, the panel says so and offers Print rather than
sitting blank.

## Worth knowing

- **Downloading is unchanged.** Wherever a screen offers Download, it still saves the file.
- If you were expecting a new tab and did not get one, this is why — check the panel over
  the current screen.
- The panel is a viewer. Editing, signing and re-uploading still happen on the screen the
  document came from.
