# Signing in with a passkey

A passkey lets you sign in with your **face, fingerprint or device PIN** instead of typing
a password. It is stored by your phone or computer (and, with iCloud Keychain or Google
Password Manager, synced to your other devices). Nobody can phish it, because it only
works on the real KiddieTrac site.

Your password keeps working alongside it, and you can remove a passkey any time.

## Adding one

- [ ] Sign in with your password as usual.
- [ ] When KiddieTrac offers **Add a passkey**, press it — or go to **Settings → Security** and press **Add a passkey**.
- [ ] Confirm with your face, fingerprint or PIN when your device asks.
- [ ] Look for **✓ Passkey added**.

[[show: #settings @ Security > Add a passkey | Show me where to add a passkey]]

![Settings → Security → Passkeys: add one, and see each device you use](https://api.kiddietrac.com/help-img/passkeys-security.png)

Agency admins: open **My profile**, then **🔐 Account & pay** — the same security card is
there.

If you press **Not now** on the offer, it will not ask again for 30 days.

## Signing in with it

On the sign-in page, press **Sign in with a passkey** and confirm with your face,
fingerprint or PIN. On many phones and browsers your passkey is also offered straight
from the email box — tap the suggestion and you are in.

A passkey sign-in does not ask for a two-factor code: the passkey already proves both
that you have the device and that you are you.

## Managing your passkeys

**Settings → Security** lists each passkey with the device it was made on (for example
"iPhone · Safari" or "Windows · Chrome") and when it was last used. Remove one you no
longer use — your password and any other passkeys are unaffected.

## Where passkeys do not work

The **KiddieTrac Android app** cannot use passkeys; the screen says so. Add and use your
passkey in Chrome or Safari, or on a computer. In the Android app, keep using your
password and the fingerprint unlock.

## Questions

??? Do I still need my password? | Yes. It is your way back in if you lose your device, and it still has to be changed when it expires.
??? I lost my phone. | Sign in with your password and remove that passkey under Settings → Security. If your passkey was synced (iCloud or Google), it is also on your other devices.
??? Is a passkey the same as the fingerprint unlock in the app? | No. The fingerprint unlock reopens the app you are already signed in to; a passkey signs you in from scratch.
