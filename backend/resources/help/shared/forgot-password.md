# Forgot your password?

You can reset your own password from the sign-in page. KiddieTrac sends you a **6-digit code** by email, text message or phone call, and you use it to choose a new password. You do not need to contact your centre.

## Get a code

1. On the sign-in page, press **Forgot?** next to the password box.
2. In **Email, username or mobile number**, type any one of them.
3. Under **Send my code by**, choose **✉️ Email**, **💬 Text** or **📞 Call**.
4. Press **Send code**.

Where the code goes:

- **Email** goes to the address on your account. The email also has a reset link you can click instead of typing the code.
- **Text** goes to the mobile number on your account.
- **Call** rings the mobile number on your account, and an automated voice reads the code out twice.

You see the same message whether or not an account was found: "If an account matches, we've sent a 6-digit code." This protects your account from people who are guessing.

## Enter the code

1. Type the 6-digit code into **6-digit code**. When all six digits are in, it checks the code by itself. You can also press **Continue**.
2. If more than one account uses that email or phone number, choose **which account you want to reset**.
3. The **Set a new password** page opens. Choose your new password and save it.

The code works for **10 minutes**. After **5 wrong tries** it stops working, and you need a new one.

> **Tip:** Never share your code. KiddieTrac will never ask you for it.

## If the code does not arrive

- Wait 30 seconds, then press **Send a new code**.
- Press **Try another way** to pick email, text or a call instead.
- For email, check your spam or junk folder.
- Already have a code from earlier? On the first step, press **I already have a code**.

A text or call only reaches you if your centre sends texts and calls, and your mobile number is on your account. If not, choose **Email**.

## More than one account on one email

Some people share an email address with another account, for example a parent who also works at the centre. Open **More than one account on this email?** and type your **Username** if you know it. It is optional. If you leave it blank, you choose the account after you enter the code.

## Signing in on a poor connection

If your phone or Wi-Fi drops the connection while you sign in, KiddieTrac quietly tries again before showing an error. If it still cannot get through, you see a plain message, such as:

- **You're offline.** Connect to Wi-Fi or mobile data, then try again.
- **We couldn't reach KiddieTrac.** Check your internet connection, turn off any VPN or data saver, then try again.

These mean the request never reached KiddieTrac. Your password was not the problem.

??? I typed my mobile number but nothing came. | Check that it is the mobile number your centre has on file. If your centre does not send texts or calls, choose Email instead.
??? The code says it didn't work or has expired. | Codes last 10 minutes and allow 5 tries. Press Send a new code and use the newest one.
??? Does the old reset link still work? | Yes. The email still has a link you can click. It works once and lasts 60 minutes.
??? I see "Too many tries — wait a minute". | Wait a minute, then try again. This limit protects your account.
