# Sales expenses and suppliers

**Sales › Expenses** is where the sales team logs what it spends — travel, meals, third-party services and running
costs — with the receipt, and where a superadmin approves it and marks staff paid back. The **Suppliers** tab keeps
the list of vendors you buy from. Totals and charts show where the money goes each month.

[[open: #sales-expenses | Open Expenses]]

## Logging an expense

1. Open **Sales › Expenses** and press **Add expense**.
2. Choose the **Category** and the **Vendor** (**Select a supplier**, or **Add new supplier** if it is not listed).
3. Write **What it was for**, and anything else useful (who you met, which prospect).
4. Enter the **Amount (before tax)**, the tax, the **Currency** and who it was **Paid by**.
5. Attach the receipt (a photo or PDF).
6. Save. The expense shows in the list as waiting for approval.

> 💡 A superadmin logging company spend can tick **Log as approved** so it skips the approval step.

## Approving and paying back

1. Filter the list by status, or look at **Waiting approval** in the tiles.
2. Open the row and check the receipt with **Download receipt**.
3. Press **Approve**.
4. Once the person has been paid back, press **Mark reimbursed**. **Owed to staff** goes down.

## Reading the totals

The tiles show **This month**, **In range**, **Average a month**, **Waiting approval**, **Owed to staff**,
**Recurring a month** and **Tax paid**. Below them: **Spent (CAD)** by month, **By category** and **Top vendors**. Use
the category menu and **Search vendor, notes, who** to narrow the table (**Category**, **Vendor / what**, **Amount**,
**Paid by**, **Status**). **Recurring costs** lists the subscriptions that come back every month.

## Keeping the supplier list

1. Open the **Suppliers** tab and press **Add supplier**.
2. Enter the name and contact details, then **Save supplier** ("Supplier saved").
3. To retire one, press **Archive**: it leaves the Vendor list, but expenses already logged keep it. **Show archived** and **Restore** bring it back.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Sales rep | Log their own expenses, add suppliers, see the team's spending. |
| Platform admin (superadmin) | Everything, including approving and marking reimbursed. |

Other roles see "Expenses are for the sales team and superadmins."

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? "No expenses in this range yet." | Widen the date range, or log the first one with **Add expense**.
??? "Give the supplier a name" | A supplier needs a name before it can be saved.
??? I attached the wrong receipt. | Open the expense and use **Replace receipt**.
??? Can I delete an expense? | Use the row's **Delete**. Keep approved and reimbursed ones for the books.
