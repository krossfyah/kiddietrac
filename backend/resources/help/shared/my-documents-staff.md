# My documents — your signed forms and files

**My documents** is where KiddieTrac keeps *your own* copies: every form and policy you have signed, the terms you
agreed to when you first signed in, and files your agency has filed on your record (a contract, a certificate, an ID
document). Open it when you need a copy of something you signed, want to check whether the office has counter-signed
it, or need to print or send a document.

[[open: #my-documents | Open My documents]]

The menu entry shows a small count: how many documents are on file for you.

## Finding a document

1. Open **My documents** from the menu.
2. The table lists your documents, newest first, with four columns: **Document**, **Signed**, **Counter-signed** and
   the buttons.
3. Under each title, a grey line says what kind of document it is, for example **Signed form**, **Terms, Privacy &
   NDA**, **Contract**, **Certificate** or **ID document**, and its size.
4. The **Signed** column shows the date and time you signed it (in your agency's time zone), or "filed" and the date
   the office added it.
5. Long lists are split into pages of 25. Use the page buttons under the table.

## Opening, downloading and printing

1. Find the document's row.
2. Press **👁 View** to read it in a viewer inside KiddieTrac. Nothing opens in a new tab.
3. Press **⬇ Download** to save the file to your device, or **🖨 Print** to print it.

On a phone, the three buttons are folded into the **⋮** menu at the end of the row.

> 💡 A signed form shows its signature stamp: the date and time it was signed and from where. That is the copy to keep
> or hand over if anyone asks for proof.

## Checking whether the office counter-signed

Some forms also need a director or admin to sign. The **Counter-signed** column tells you where yours is:

| You see | It means |
|---|---|
| **✅ Counter-signed** | The office signed it too. The line under it says who (Director or Admin) and when. |
| **⏳ Not counter-signed yet** | It is waiting for the office. You do not need to do anything. |
| **↩️ Sent back for correction** | The office returned it. Their note is shown in quotes. Open **Forms to sign**, fix it and sign again. |
| **🔁 Replaced by a corrected copy** | You signed a corrected copy; this older one is kept for the record. |
| **No counter-signature needed** | The form only needed your signature. |

## Who sees what

- **Educators and home visitors** see the documents filed on their own staff record: forms and policies they signed,
  the terms they agreed to, and files the office filed for them.
- If you are also a parent at your centre, the documents for your own family (incident reports, forms you signed as
  a parent, forms shared with you) appear here too.
- Directors and agency admins see staff documents in the person's HR file, not here. Nobody else can open your copies
  from this screen.

## Questions and problems

??? The list says "Nothing filed yet". | You have not signed a form in KiddieTrac yet, and the office has not filed anything for you. Once you sign one in Forms to sign, a copy appears here.
??? "These could not be loaded just now. Please try again in a moment." | Your connection dropped or the server was busy. Refresh the page.
??? "That document could not be opened." | The file is missing or was replaced. Ask your director to file it again.
??? A form I need to sign isn't here. | This screen holds copies of what you already signed. Forms waiting for your signature are in Forms to sign.
??? My form says Sent back for correction. What do I do? | Read the office's note, then open Forms to sign: the form is back in To sign. Fill or sign it again and submit.
??? Can I delete a document? | No. Signed documents are records your agency must keep. If something is wrong, tell your director.
??? Who is told when I sign? | The office sees your signed form in its review list. You are not emailed a copy; it is kept here.
