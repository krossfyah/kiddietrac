# Ontario workforce funding (WEG and CWELCC)

For Ontario agencies that receive the Wage Enhancement Grant (WEG) and CWELCC workforce compensation (WCF). KiddieTrac works out each person's amounts, puts them on every payslip as separate lines, checks the 32-day rule and builds the year-end reconciliation. It keeps records only. It never pays anyone.

## Switch it on
**Settings → Payroll → Ontario workforce funding.** Tick **Use Ontario workforce funding in payroll**. The 2026 values come filled in from the 2026 guidelines:

- Wage floor: $25.86 an hour for RECE program staff, $26.86 for RECE supervisors and home child care visitors
- WCF annual increase: up to $4.00 an hour, until the wage reaches $28 (program staff) or $31 (supervisors)
- WEG: up to $2.00 an hour, until the wage reaches $33.81

When your service manager publishes next year's values, pick the year and enter them. Write your **distribution policy** in the same card, or press **Add to the Policy library** to upload the signed copy and track its yearly review.

## Each person's wage
Open **Finance → Workforce funding → Staff wages** and press **Wages** on a person's row. Enter their **base wage**, plus any 2024 GOF enhancement you still pay. KiddieTrac then works out the rest in the guideline's order: WEG, then the WCF increase, then the wage-floor top-up.

- Eligibility comes from the person's role and an active **RECE** certification on their staff record. A director without an RECE, or an educator approved by the director, counts as **non-RECE**: they can get WEG, but not WCF. You can override the eligibility.
- Leave a funding amount blank to use the calculation, or type an override. If an override goes over a ceiling or the maximum, you see a warning.
- When someone's pay changes, add a **new record** from the date it takes effect. The old record ends the day before. To fix a mistake, use **Correct**. Every change is recorded in the audit log.

## Payroll runs
When you choose **Generate payroll**, each funded person shows their WEG, WCF and floor rates. Ticking someone adds a separate line for each component (hours × rate) and includes it in their pay. The payslip PDF shows the base wage, WEG and workforce compensation on separate lines. Funding is paid for every hour worked, including overtime, at the same hourly amount.

**Pay period** shows hours from the clock × each component for any period. It also shows whether that period's payslips carry the funding lines. You can download the lines as a CSV.

## Funding received and the 32-day rule
On **Funding received**, record each payment from your service manager: the date, amount, stream and period covered. Each payment must reach staff pay within 32 days. Each one is marked **Met** (with the first pay date), **Pending** (with the days left) or **MISSED**. A wrong entry is voided, not deleted.

## Year-end reconciliation
**Year-end report** totals each calendar year by person and by position: hours, FTE, base wages, WEG, WCF increase, wage floor and the 17.5% benefit allocation. Non-RECE director-approved staff appear in a separate table, and funding received is compared with what you paid out. Download it as CSV or PDF; each download is recorded in the audit log. Keep these records for seven years. KiddieTrac never deletes them.
