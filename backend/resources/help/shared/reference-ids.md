# Your KiddieTrac ID

Every child, family, staff member and parent at your centre has a short reference ID, like **ILH-C-0042**. It is the quickest way for the office to find the right record when you call or email.

## What the ID means

An ID has three parts:

- **The letters at the front** belong to your agency, for example **ILH**.
- **One letter for the type of record:**
  - **C** — a child
  - **F** — a family
  - **S** — a staff member
  - **P** — a parent or guardian
- **A number** that counts up within your agency.

So **ILH-C-0042** is a child, and **ILH-F-0017** is a family. If you work at the centre and also have a child there, you have two IDs: one as staff and one as a parent.

An ID stays with the record. It is never given to anybody else, and it is never renumbered.

## Finding your ID

1. Open **Settings** and stay on the **Profile** tab.
2. Look for the **Your KiddieTrac ID** card.

[[open: #settings | Open Settings]]

The card lists your own ID, your family's ID and each of your children's IDs.

You also see IDs elsewhere in the portal:

- **Parents:** your child's page shows a **Child ID**. The family details on that page, and **Family & pick-ups**, show your **Family ID**.
- **Staff:** a child's page shows its **Child ID**.

## Copying an ID

Each ID sits in a small grey box with a copy button (**⧉**) beside it. Tap the button, and the ID is copied, ready to paste into an email or a message. The box turns green for a moment to show it worked.

## Quoting your ID to the centre

When you contact the office about an invoice, a form or your child, give them your ID. The staff can type it into their search and go straight to your record, even if your name is spelled differently somewhere or two families share a surname.

> **Tip:** Your ID is not a password. It is safe to read out over the phone or put in an email.

## Where else you will see it

Your ID is printed on many documents from the centre, such as invoices, receipts, account statements, report cards, the emergency card and signed forms. Staff also see their ID on their payslips. If the office asks for your "account ID" or "reference", this is it.

??? My centre changed its letters. Is my ID different now? | The letters at the front can change if your agency updates them. Your number and type stay the same, and an ID printed with the old letters still finds your record.
??? I don't see the Your KiddieTrac ID card. | The card only appears when you have an ID at the agency you are signed in to. If you belong to more than one agency, switch to the right one first.
