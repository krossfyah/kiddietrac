# Pickup authorizations

Who may collect a child is one list: the family's **emergency contacts**, with each person marked as allowed to pick up or as an emergency contact only. Parents keep it in their app, staff can edit it too, and everyone sees the same list: the child's record, the educator's record, the emergency card and the sign-out check.

## Guardians

Parents and guardians on the family are authorised by their own **Authorized for pickup** setting (on by default). Only staff can add or change guardians.

## How parents keep the list

In their app, parents open **Family & pick-ups** (the **Pickup people** tile opens the same screen) and can:

- **＋ Add contact** — name and phone (required), relationship, a second phone with its type and extension, email, notes, and **Allowed to pick up the children**.
- **Edit** a contact, including unticking **Allowed to pick up the children**.
- **Remove** a contact.

A family can have up to 20 contacts. Pick-up logins can't change the list; only the family's parents and guardians can.

## Who is told

Every addition, change and removal made by a parent:

- notifies the child's room educators, the centre's directors and your agency's admins (**🚗 Pick-up list addition / change / removal**), with what changed;
- emails the directors and admins;
- notifies the family's other parents;
- is written to the audit log with the before and after.

Tapping the notification opens the child's record.

## How staff change it

- Families → the family → **Edit** → **Emergency** tab. **+ Add emergency contact**, or change a contact and tick **Authorized for pickup**.
- The **Emergency** step of the New family wizard.

The current list shows on the child's record → **Overview** → **Pick-ups & emergency contacts**.

## At pickup

- Check the person against the child's record. If they're not on it, don't release the child; call the director or a guardian.
- Ask a new pick-up person for photo ID the first time.

## Pick-up logins

A contact allowed to pick up can also have their own login to check the children in and out, without billing. See [Pick-up logins](pick-up-logins).

## Best practice

- Grandparents and regular helpers: add them with a phone number and tick allowed to pick up.
- Someone who should only be called: leave allowed to pick up unticked.
- When a helper stops, remove them, or untick allowed to pick up.
