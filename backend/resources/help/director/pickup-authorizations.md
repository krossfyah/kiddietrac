# Pickup authorizations

Authorize non-guardian people to pick up your child. Staff verify ID at pickup.

## Adding a person (parent)

1. Sidebar → **Pickup people**.
2. Pick the child.
3. Tap **+ Add authorised person**.
4. Enter their full name (required), relationship (grandparent, aunt, nanny…), phone, and optional expiry date.
5. Optionally paste a photo-ID URL for staff to compare against.
6. Save.

## At pickup

- Person arrives → staff checks the kiosk screen for that child's authorised pickups
- Staff verify ID against the saved name/photo
- Authorisation can be permanent or have an expiry date (e.g. "summer camp counsellor — expires Aug 31")

## Removing

Tap **Remove** on any authorisation. It's soft-removed (active=0) so the audit trail persists.

## Default authorisations

Family guardians (marked on the `guardians` table) are automatically authorised — no need to add them. This page is for **additional** people beyond the guardians.

## Best practice

- Add the grandparents as a permanent authorisation
- Add the nanny / babysitter with their phone number
- Expire the summer-camp-counsellor row after the program ends
