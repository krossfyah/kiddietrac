# Parent issues

Parents can report a problem from their app with **Report an issue**: they choose the child, a category and a reason, and write what happened. Every report lands on the **Parent issues** screen, where you reply to the family, keep internal notes, set the status and assign it to someone.

[[open: #parent-issues | Open Parent issues]]

## Where it is

Sidebar → **Operations** → **Parent issues**. Educators have **Parent issues** in their menu too.

Who sees what:

- **Agency admins** see every centre in the agency.
- **Centre directors** see their own centres.
- **Educators** see reports about the children in their own rooms. They never see a **Concern about a staff member**.

## When a report comes in

- The centre's **directors** and the agency's **admins** get a notification, a push and an email with the family, the child, the category, the reason and what the parent wrote.
- The child's **room educators** get a notification and a push (no email).
- A **Concern about a staff member** goes to directors and admins only, and shows a **⚠️** and a red border on the list.

Tap the notification to open that report straight away.

## The screen

Four counts across the top: **Open**, **In progress**, **Resolved** and **Closed**. Under them:

- **Status** — starts on **Open & in progress**. Choose one status, or **All**.
- **Category** — narrow to one kind of report.
- **Centre** — shown when you have more than one.
- **↻ Refresh**.

Each row shows the category and reason, who reported it, the family, the child, when, who it's assigned to and how many messages it has.

## Handling a report

1. Tap the report. It opens with everything the parent wrote, the parent's phone and email, and the conversation so far.
2. To answer the family, type in **Reply** and press **Save**. The parent gets a notification and an email, and sees it in their app.
3. To leave a note for staff only, tick **🔒 Internal note — the family will not see it** before you save.
4. Change **Status** as you go.
5. Directors and admins can set **Assigned to**. The person assigned gets a notification.

The first reply to the family moves a report from **Open** to **In progress** by itself. A reply and a status change saved together reach the family as one email, not two.

> **Tip:** When you mark a report **Resolved**, write a short reply at the same time so the family knows what was done.

## Statuses

- **Open** — new, not yet answered.
- **In progress** — someone is on it.
- **Resolved** — dealt with.
- **Closed** — finished.

If the family writes again on a **Resolved** or **Closed** report, it **reopens** and you're told. Follow-ups go to the person assigned and the centre's directors and admins (or, if nobody is assigned, to them and the room educators).

## Who can be assigned

The centre's directors, the agency's admins and, for a report about a child, that child's room educators. Educators can't be assigned a concern about a staff member.

## The digest

Reports still **Open** or **In progress** appear in the director and admin digest email under **🗣️ Parent issues waiting**, oldest first, until they are resolved or closed.

## The record

Every report, reply, internal note, status change and assignment is written to the audit log.

## Questions

??? Can an educator assign a report or see staff concerns? | No. Educators can reply and change the status on reports about children in their rooms. Assigning, and anything about a staff member, is for directors and admins.
??? A parent can't choose Billing. | Pick-up logins don't see billing, so that category is hidden for them.
??? Can I delete a report? | No. Close it instead. The history stays on the record.
