# Setting up your centre — your first 30 minutes

Welcome to Kiddietrac. This guide walks you through your first 30 minutes setting up the platform. By the end, you'll have one room, one family, one child enrolled, and one staff member invited.

## Before you start

You'll need:

- Your centre's licence number (CCEYA licence)
- A list of your rooms with capacity and age ranges
- At least one family's information (parent email, address)
- Your staff's emails

## Step 1 — Add your rooms (5 minutes)

In the left sidebar, find **QUICK ADD** and click **🏫 New room**.

For each room, you'll set:

- **Room name** — what your staff call it (e.g. "Sunshine Room", "Tigers")
- **Age group** — infant / toddler / preschool / kindergarten / school age
- **Min/max age in months** — used for ratio calculation
- **Capacity** — the licensed maximum number of children for this room
- **Ratio** — the Ontario standard is 1 educator per 3 infants, 1:5 toddlers, 1:8 preschool, 1:13 kindergarten
- **Color** — pick a colour to identify the room visually throughout the app

Repeat for every room in your centre. Most Ontario CCEYA centres have 3-5 rooms.

## Step 2 — Add families (10 minutes)

A "family" is a group of related people — typically one or two parents with one or more children. Each child belongs to exactly one family.

Click **👪 New family** in QUICK ADD.

For each family, enter:

- **Family name** — e.g. "The Williams Family" or "The Smith-Patel Family"
- **Primary email and phone** — used for billing if no specific contact is set later
- **Address** — used for the family's mailing address

Don't worry about adding the parents yet — that comes in Step 4.

## Step 3 — Enroll children (10 minutes)

Click **👶 New child** in QUICK ADD.

For each child, enter:

- Name, preferred name (nickname), date of birth, gender
- The family they belong to (from your Step 2 list)
- The room they're enrolled in (from your Step 1 list)
- **Start date** — usually today, but can be future-dated
- **Monthly fee** — what the family pays per month at full rate
- **CWELCC eligible** — most children under 6 are eligible for Ontario's CWELCC subsidy
- **Medical notes** — allergies, conditions, EpiPen location, etc.
- **Dietary notes** — vegetarian, kosher, no dairy, etc.

This is also when you can flag severe allergies (peanut, etc.) — those will show as red warnings on the educator's tablet.

## Step 4 — Invite parents (5 minutes)

Click **💌 Invite parent** in QUICK ADD.

Pick the family you want to invite a parent to. Then enter:

- The parent's first name, last name, email
- Their relationship (mother / father / guardian / etc.)
- **Primary contact** — is this person the main billing & communication contact?
- **Can pick up** — can this person sign the child out at the end of the day?
- **Receives billing emails** — should this person get the monthly invoice?

When you click **Send invitation**, Kiddietrac sends them a welcome email with a temporary password. They can log in immediately and see their child's daily activity.

For families with two parents, you'll invite each separately. Both will see the same child but have their own login.

## Step 5 — Invite your staff

Click **👨‍🏫 Invite staff** in QUICK ADD.

For each educator and any co-directors:

- Name and email
- Role: **Educator** (tablet view, room-focused) or **Director** (full admin)
- Send welcome email — they'll get login details

After they log in for the first time and change their password, they're ready to start logging activities.

## What's next?

Now your centre is set up. Day-to-day operations work like this:

- **Educators** use the tablet view to check children in, log meals/naps/activities, take photos
- **Parents** see all of this in their app
- **You** review the dashboard for ratios, generate monthly invoices, manage incidents

See **Generating monthly invoices** to learn how billing works.
