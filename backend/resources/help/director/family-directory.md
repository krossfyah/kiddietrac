# Family directory

Connect with other families at your centre. Strictly opt-in.

## Opting in

1. Sidebar → **Family directory**.
2. Choose what to share:
   - **Share my email** — other families can email you
   - **Share my phone** — other families can call/text
   - **Share my address (city only)** — never your street address, just your city
   - **Show my children's first names** — helps families recognise each other
3. Tap **Save preferences**.

You can toggle any of these off at any time — changes propagate instantly.

## Browsing the directory

Same screen scrolls down to show all other opt-in families at your centre. Cards show what each family chose to share.

## What's NEVER shared

- Street address (city only, if you opt in)
- Phone if you didn't opt in
- Email if you didn't opt in
- Child last names
- Child date of birth
- Any billing / payment info
- Anything you didn't explicitly enable

## Use cases

- Carpool coordination
- Playdate scheduling
- Class parent meetings
- Community-building events

## Privacy reminder

Other families can see what you've shared — but they can't see what you haven't. The opt-in is per-family, not per-other-family. There's no fine-grained "share with this family but not that one" — you share with everyone at your centre, or with no one.
