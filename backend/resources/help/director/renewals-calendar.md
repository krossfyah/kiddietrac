# Renewals calendar

The **Renewals calendar** puts every staff background check and staff certificate (CPR, first aid and the others you track) that expires in the next six months on one timeline, so you can ask for renewals before anything lapses. It is a read-only list: you look here to see what is coming, then renew the record on its own screen. Open it from **Compliance > Renewals calendar**.

[[open: #renewals | Open Renewals calendar]]

## Reading the calendar

1. Open **Compliance > Renewals calendar**.
2. Start with **⛔ Expired**. These have already run out and need action today.
3. Move to **⚠ Next 30 days**. These are the renewals to ask for now.
4. Look over **Next 90 days** and **Later** (up to six months away) to plan ahead.
5. In each row, read **Staff member** (the person) and **What** (the check or certificate by name, for example *Vulnerable sector check* or *Standard First Aid*). The small tag beside it says whether it is a **Background check** or a **Certificate**.
6. Read the **Expires** column: the date, and how many days are left or how many days ago it ran out.

Each section header shows how many items it holds, for example **⚠ Next 30 days (3)**. A section with nothing in it says **None.**

When a certificate or check has been renewed, only the newest one counts: the old record does not stay on the list as expired.

> 💡 Check the calendar at the start of every month. Anything in **⚠ Next 30 days** is your to-do list for the month.

## Renewing something on the list

The calendar does not change records. Renew the item where it lives.

**A background check:**

1. Open **Human Resources > Documents & letters > Background checks** (or **Background checks** in the menu when the HR add-on is off).
2. Find the person's check and choose **✏️ Edit**.
3. Enter the new **Expires** date and reference, then press **Save changes**.
4. Press **View** and upload the new document.

[[show: #background-checks @ + Add check | Show me Background checks]]

**A certificate (CPR, first aid and others):**

1. Open **Operations > Certifications**.
2. Add the new certificate for the person with its issue and expiry dates, and upload the document.

Come back to the **Renewals calendar**. The item has moved to a later section or left the list.

## What is on the calendar

- **Background checks**: vulnerable sector, criminal record, driver and the other check types you track.
- **Staff certificates**: CPR, first aid, food handler and the other certificate types your province or state uses.

It lists items that are expired or expire within 180 days. A certificate or check with no expiry date is never on it.

Only people who work for you now are listed. Someone whose account was deactivated or suspended, whose HR file says they have left, or whose only centre has closed is not on the calendar.

## Who can see it

| Role | What they see |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | The checks and certificates of everyone in the agency. |
| Centre director | Only the staff placed at their own centres. Staff of the agency's other centres, and agency-level admins, are not shown. |
| Staff | Not this screen. They get a reminder about their own check or certificate before it expires. |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? The page says "The renewals calendar didn't load." | The list could not be loaded, often because the connection dropped. Press **Try again**. If it keeps happening, report it from **Help** with the time it happened.
??? Every section says None. | Nothing expires in the next six months, or no checks or certificates with expiry dates are on file yet. Add them on **Background checks** or **Certifications**.
??? A person I expected is missing. | They may not be placed at one of your centres (directors see their own centres only), their account may be deactivated, or their HR file may say they have left. A record with no expiry date is not listed either.
??? I renewed a certificate but the old one still shows as expired. | The new one needs the same certificate type and a later expiry date. Check the type and the date on **Certifications**.
??? Does anyone get an email? | The calendar itself sends nothing. Staff and managers get reminders for checks and certificates as they near expiry, as set in your agency's reminder settings.
