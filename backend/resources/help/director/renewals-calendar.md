# Renewals calendar

The **Renewals calendar** puts every staff background check that expires in the next six months on one timeline, so you can ask for renewals before anything lapses. It is a read-only list: you look here to see what is coming, then renew the record on its own screen. Open it from **Compliance > Renewals calendar**.

[[open: #renewals | Open Renewals calendar]]

## Reading the calendar

1. Open **Compliance > Renewals calendar**. The page is titled **Renewal calendar**.
2. Start with **⛔ Expired**. These have already run out and need action today.
3. Move to **⚠ Next 30 days**. These are the renewals to ask for now.
4. Look over **Next 90 days** and **Later** (up to six months away) to plan ahead.
5. In each row, read the **Name / certification** column: the person, the type of check, and, in brackets, the record it comes from.
6. Read the **Expires** column: the date, and how many days are left or how many days ago it ran out.

Each section header shows how many items it holds, for example **⚠ Next 30 days (3)**. A section with nothing in it says **None.**

> 💡 Check the calendar at the start of every month. Anything in **⚠ Next 30 days** is your to-do list for the month.

## Renewing something on the list

The calendar does not change records. To renew a check:

1. Note the person and the check type on the calendar.
2. Open **Human Resources > Documents & letters > Background checks** (or **Background checks** in the menu when the HR add-on is off).
3. Find the person's check and choose **✏️ Edit**.
4. Enter the new **Expires** date and reference, then press **Save changes**.
5. Press **View** and upload the new document.
6. Come back to the **Renewals calendar**. The item has moved to a later section or left the list.

## What is on the calendar today

The calendar reads **background checks** (vulnerable sector, criminal record, driver and the other check types you track). It lists checks that are expired or expire within 180 days.

> ℹ️ CPR, first aid and other staff certificates are not on this calendar. Use **Operations > Certifications**, where the **Expired** and **Expiring in 30 days** counts show the same information for certificates.

## Who can see it

| Role | What they see |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | The checks of the whole agency. |
| Centre director | The calendar from their Compliance menu; they renew the checks of their own centres' staff on **Background checks**. |
| Staff | Not this screen. They get a bell reminder about their own check before it expires. |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? The page stays on "Loading…". | The list could not be loaded. Refresh the page; if it keeps happening, report it from **Help** with the time it happened.
??? Every section says None. | No background check expires in the next six months, or none are on file yet. Add checks on **Background checks**.
??? A CPR certificate that expires soon is missing. | Certificates are not on this calendar. Look under **Certifications**.
??? I renewed a check but it is still under Expired. | The expiry date was not changed on the check. Edit the check and enter the new expiry date.
??? Does anyone get an email? | The calendar itself sends nothing. Staff get bell reminders for checks expiring within 60 days, at most once a week per check.
