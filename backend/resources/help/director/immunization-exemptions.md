# Immunization exemptions

**Immunizations → a child → What is due.** Record that a child is exempt from a dose on
your schedule — for a religious or conscientious belief, or a medical reason — so it stops
counting as overdue and the family is not chased for it.

There are two ways to do it: one dose at a time from the schedule, or every dose an
exemption form covers in one action, straight off the filed form.

[[open: #immunizations | Open Immunizations]]

## Exempting one dose

1. Open the child's immunizations — from a row on **Immunizations**, or the child's record
   → **Immunization** tab.
2. On **💉 What is due**, find the dose. Anything not overdue or due soon is under
   **Show the rest of the schedule**.
3. Press **Exempt** at the end of its row.
4. Choose the **Reason** — *Religious or conscientious belief*, *Medical reason
   (documented by a physician)* or *Other* — and add a note if you need to.
5. **Record exemption**.

The row now reads **Exempt**, with the reason printed under the chip, so the next person to
look does not have to ask why.

## Exempting everything an exemption form covers

A family with an exemption usually brings one form that covers the whole required
schedule. Exempting fourteen doses one at a time is how one gets missed, so do it from the
form instead.

1. File the form like any other record: **＋ Upload a record**. See
   [Filing an immunization record](filing-an-immunization-record).
2. On **📄 Records on file** (or **Immunizations → Records received**), the form shows
   **Details pending**. Press **➕ Add details**.
3. Choose **Exemption form** at the top, instead of **Doses given**.
4. Pick the **Reason on the form**, and add a note if you need to.
5. Tick every dose the form exempts. **Tick all required** ticks every dose your schedule
   marks **REQUIRED** in one go.
6. **Record exemptions**.

Every exemption recorded this way is joined to the form it was read off, so the reason
says what was claimed and the form shows what was produced.

## The rules worth knowing

- **A reason is required.** An exemption is refused without one. Licensing asks for a
  *documented* exemption; without a reason it is just a missing dose with a better label.
- **A dose already recorded as given is never turned into an exemption.** If a card
  already put a date against it, the exemption form leaves it alone and tells you it was
  skipped, by name. The count that comes back is what was actually written.
- **Exempting twice does not make two records.** It updates the one that is there.
- **Removing an exemption asks first.** **Remove exemption** puts the dose back on the
  schedule, where it counts as due or overdue again and the family can be reminded about
  it. If the dose only existed to carry the exemption, it is removed completely; if it
  also held real details, only the exemption comes off.

## What an exemption changes

- The dose is never counted as overdue.
- It is left out of the immunization reminders sent to families. See
  [Immunization reminders](immunization-reminders).
- Parents see **Exempt** and the reason on their own **What is due** tab. They cannot
  set or remove one.
- Every exemption set or removed writes an audit row naming the child, the vaccine, the
  dose and the reason. Recording them off a form names every dose.

> **Note:** On the [Immunization report](immunization-report), a child with any exempt dose shows the status **Exempt**, with the reason in the **Exemption** column.

## Who can do this

Agency admins and centre directors, for children at their centres. Educators do not see the
**Exempt** button. Whether a child is exempt is a licensing position, not a note taken at
the door.

## Common questions

??? The Exempt button is missing. | You are signed in as an educator, or you are looking at a parent's view. Only directors and agency admins can exempt a dose.
??? The form says a dose was skipped. | That dose was already recorded as given, with a date. An exemption form does not overwrite a recorded dose.
??? Can a parent exempt their own child? | No. A parent can send the form in; somebody at the centre records the exemption from it.
