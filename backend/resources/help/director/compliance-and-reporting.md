# Compliance and reporting

Kiddietrac tracks the things CCEYA inspectors care about, so you can show compliance at any time.

## What Kiddietrac tracks

- **Educator-to-child ratios** — real-time, per room (see **Understanding ratios**)
- **Staff certifications** — RECE, First Aid, CPR, Vulnerable Sector Check, expiry dates
- **Immunization records** — what's been collected, what's missing
- **Incident reports** — submitted, reviewed, resolved

## The Compliance section

In the sidebar, click **Compliance**. You'll see:

- All rooms with their current ratio status
- Number of children missing immunization records
- Staff certifications: total, expired, expiring soon (30 days / 90 days)

## Exporting a compliance snapshot

For an inspector visit or your own records:

1. Go to **Compliance → Export**
2. Kiddietrac generates a printable HTML report
3. In your browser, press **Ctrl+P** (or Cmd+P on Mac) and choose **Save as PDF**

The report includes:

- Centre information (name, licence, capacity, address)
- Each room with current children, educators present, ratio status
- All staff with their certifications and expiry dates
- Generated timestamp

This is a real-time snapshot, not an official Ministry document. Use it for your own preparation; for official reports, use the Ministry's forms.

## Best practices for CCEYA inspections

- **Run the compliance export the day before** so you know what your snapshot looks like
- **Address any expired certifications** — these are the most common findings
- **Make sure immunization records are on file** for every child
- **Check that ratios have been compliant** all week (use the attendance report)

## Tracking certifications

For each staff member, click their profile → **Certifications** to:

- See all current certifications
- Add a new one with expiry date
- See which are expiring soon

Set a calendar reminder 60 days before any certification expires. Most are valid 1-3 years.

## Incident reporting

When an incident happens (injury, accident, behavioural concern):

1. The educator on duty creates the incident on the tablet
2. It appears in your **Compliance → Incidents** queue as "Submitted"
3. You review it, add any notes, mark as "Reviewed"
4. For serious incidents, follow your centre's standard CCEYA serious occurrence reporting

Kiddietrac doesn't replace your CCEYA serious occurrence reporting — it's a record-keeping tool. Serious occurrences must still be reported to the Ministry per CCEYA requirements.

## Attendance reports

For payroll or Ministry funding records:

1. Go to **Compliance → Attendance report**
2. Set date range
3. See total child-days and average per day
4. Export to PDF

Useful for monthly funding submissions or quarterly reviews.
