# OCCMS reporting

Ontario centres contracted for child care fee subsidy enter a **Record of Attendance (ROA)** in OCCMS every month: a code for each day for every child with a fee subsidy, plus licensed capacity, operating capacity, enrolment and vacancies by age group. York Region's deadline is the **8th of the following month**. OCCMS has no connection KiddieTrac can send to, so KiddieTrac prepares the ROA for you to copy in, and keeps a record of who checked it and when it was entered. Open **Compliance → OCCMS reporting** (Ontario agencies only; directors see their own centres, agency admins see every centre).

## The days

Pick the centre and the month. Each child with a fee subsidy that month (from **Subsidies → Provincial subsidy**) gets a row, with one code per day, worked out from:

- **P** Present: the child was signed in.
- **S** Sick, **V** Vacation, **A** Absent: an absence reported by the family or staff (sick → S, holiday → V, anything else → A).
- **A** with a red outline: absent on a scheduled day and nothing was reported.
- **H** Statutory holiday: a closure of type *Holiday*. **N** Non-paid day: any other closure (PD day, emergency, renovation).
- **W** Withdrawal: on the child's withdrawal date.
- **O** Other: only after you have checked with your municipality.
- Blank: no care that day (weekend, a day the child is not scheduled). A grey dot means no fee subsidy that day.

Tap any day to change its code. A changed day has an orange underline, and every change (old code, new code, who, when) is listed under **History**.

At the end of each row: days present, absences this month, absences used this calendar year against the allowance, and how many are left. **Needs attention** lists children who used 80% or all of their allowance, and any child absent **5 days in a row without notice** — tell the family's caseworker (CSR).

## Capacity and vacancies

Licensed capacity is the total of your rooms' capacities for each age group. Enter the **operating capacity** (the spaces you can fill with the staff you have; never more than licensed) and save. It carries forward to the next month. Enrolled and vacancies are worked out for the last day of the month.

## Review, sign-off and OCCMS

1. Check the days and the capacity.
2. **Mark reviewed** (opens on the 1st of the next month).
3. A **different person** presses **Sign off**. The grid is then frozen as signed: later attendance changes do not alter it (you will see how many days changed since).
4. Enter it in OCCMS, then press **Record OCCMS submission** with the date (and a reference if you have one). The month is locked.

Changing a day after review sends the month back to draft. **Reopen** (with a reason) unlocks a signed-off month; **Amend** (with a reason) unlocks a submitted one so you can correct it and enter the correction in OCCMS.

**ROA as CSV** and **Printable ROA (PDF)** give you the grid laid out like the OCCMS ROA, with the capacity table and the sign-off names.

## CWELCC summary

The second tab counts the children enrolled in the centre's rooms by age group, split into CWELCC-eligible and not, next to licensed and operating capacity, with a CSV. A child is eligible under 6; one who turns 6 between January 1 and June 30 stays eligible until June 30 (preschool, kindergarten or family age group), otherwise until the end of the month they turn 6; in a school-age room, until the end of their birth month. It also lists children leaving CWELCC in the next two months (families need 2 months' notice) and families whose CWELCC enrolment does not match the child's age.

## Settings and reminders (agency admins)

Under **Settings**, choose your municipality: **York Region** (48 absence days a year, 3 fewer for each month after January the subsidy starts), **City of Toronto** (35 a year, at most 20 in a row), **Region of Peel** (enter the allowance from Peel's Service Provider Handbook) or **Other** (York's rules until you set your own). You can change any number and the due day.

On the 1st, directors and admins get a bell and an email that last month's ROA is ready to review, and two days before the due day (the 6th) another if it is not signed off yet. Switch these off under Settings.
