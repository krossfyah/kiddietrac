# Marketing campaigns

Compose rich newsletters, open-house invites and promotions, then send them to
the families you choose. Sidebar → **Marketing → Campaigns** (this group used to be called Growth).

![Click + New campaign on the Campaigns screen](https://api.kiddietrac.com/help-img/campaigns.png)

## Composing

1. Click **+ New campaign**.
2. Fill in:
   - **Campaign title** (internal) and **Email subject**.
   - **Audience** — all families / currently enrolled / waitlist / prospects /
     staff.
   - **Channel** — in-portal (a News post), email, or both (the email plus a News post).
   - **Hero image** (optional) — upload a banner shown at the top of the message.
   - **Body** — use the rich-text editor (fonts, colours, headings, lists,
     links, images).
   - **Schedule** (optional) — leave blank to send manually.

## Greeting each family by name

Type **{first_name}** anywhere in the body or the email subject, or press **👤 First name** in the editor
toolbar. Each family's email then uses their own first name: "Hi {first_name}," arrives as "Hi Sarah,".
Where there is no single reader (the News post every family sees) or no first name on file, it reads
"there" ("Hi there,"). Preview and View show it with a sample name.

## White-label header

White-label branding is **part of your plan** — it is not a per-campaign
choice. When your agency is on a package that includes white-label, your
**agency logo and details** (name, support email, business address) appear at
the top of every campaign automatically, pulled from **Settings → Branding**.
The composer shows a banner telling you whether it's active. Agencies without
the add-on get a clean standard header. (White-label is available on the Growth
plan and above; it's switched on when the agency is enrolled in that package.)

## Preview before sending

Click **👁 Preview** to see the campaign exactly as recipients will get it — the
white-label header, your hero image and body, and the footer. Always preview
before you send.

## Viewing a campaign

In the campaigns list, open a row's **⋮** and choose **View**. A popup shows the email exactly as
families get it (with a sample first name), plus its status, audience, channel, subject, preview text,
schedule and, once sent, the delivery counts. View never sends anything.

## Who receives a campaign

A family campaign goes to every parent or guardian of a family still with you, **including parents who
have not accepted their app invite yet** (since 27 November 2026; before that they were held back). It does
not go to families that have left, closed or suspended accounts, test agencies, or anyone with no email on
file, and each address gets one copy.

## Recipients & stats

Once a campaign has gone out, open its row's **⋮** and choose **Recipients & stats**. You see who it went
to and what happened to each person: **Sent** (our mail service accepted it and nothing came back),
**Opened**, **Bounced** (their mail server sent it back, with the reason in plain words), **Failed**,
**Held back** (not sent because of the email rules, for example a closed account or email switched off)
and **Not known**. Filter by status, search, and export to CSV, Excel or PDF from the bar under the
table. **View the email they got** shows the copy that went to that person.

What it cannot tell you: mail services send no delivery receipts, so "Sent" is the closest there is to
"delivered". Opens are counted when the email's images load, from our own server with no third-party
tracker, so someone who blocks images shows as not opened. Link clicks are not tracked. A campaign that
also posted to News shows how many of the people it was for have opened it there, and the in-app notice
shows how many have read it. Campaigns sent before 27 November 2026 are rebuilt from the email log, so a
few people may show as "Not known". Directors see only their own centres' campaigns.

## Resend to bounced

When some emails bounced, **Recipients & stats** shows how many bounced and how many of those people now
have a corrected address. Correct the person's email on their record first, then press **↻ Resend to
bounced** (above the status filter, or in the campaign's **⋮**). The list shows each person's name, the
address it bounced at, their current email and what can be done: **Address corrected — ready to resend**,
**Same address — fix it first** (with a button to open their record), **No email on file** or **Account
deactivated**. Only ready people can be ticked, and they are all ticked to start with. You are asked to
confirm how many before anything goes out. The campaign goes to their current address, never twice to an
address that already got it, at most 80 at a time, and the usual email rules still apply. Once it is sent
the person shows as sent, with the address that bounced in the details, and the audit log names every
address it went to.

## The footer

Every campaign ends with a **"Powered by Kiddietrac"** footer. It also shows
your agency's **Privacy policy** and **Terms & conditions** links — set those
once under **Settings → Branding** (they're part of your agency setup) and they
appear on every campaign automatically.

## Sending

Use **Send now** (or schedule it). In-portal campaigns post to families' feeds
immediately; emailed campaigns are queued for delivery. Sent campaigns are
read-only.

> Who can use this: **Centre director** (their centre), **Agency admin** (whole
> agency).
