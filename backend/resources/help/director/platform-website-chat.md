# Website chat

Visitors on **kiddietrac.com** can chat with us from the bubble in the corner of every page. **Sales › Website chat**
is where the team answers them, live. When nobody is available, **Maya**, the virtual assistant, answers — and she
always says she is the virtual assistant, never a person. Open this screen whenever you are on duty for the website.

[[open: #sales-webchat | Open Website chat]]

> ℹ️ This screen is for the KiddieTrac platform and sales team. Agency admins do not have it.

## Going online

1. Open **Sales › Website chat**.
2. Tick **Available for website chat** at the top of the screen.
3. Keep the portal open. While the box is ticked and the portal is open you count as online, and visitors are told a person is there.
4. Untick it (or close the portal) when you stop. You go offline within a couple of minutes.

[[show: #sales-webchat @ Available for website chat | Show me the availability switch]]

When a visitor writes, **Website chat** in the menu shows a badge and the portal pops up an alert with a chime, even
on another screen. The banner reads "Nobody online — Maya is answering" when no one is available.

## Answering a visitor

1. Pick the conversation from the list on the left (**Open** is shown first).
2. Press **Join**. The chat is now yours and Maya stops answering. If a colleague already holds it, the button reads **Take over**.
3. Type in the reply box and press **Send** (or **Enter**). The visitor sees you typing. Typing a reply also takes the chat from Maya.
4. If the visitor has gone quiet, press **👋 Nudge** ("They will see it in the chat on the website.").
5. If they are a real prospect, press **🎯 Add as lead** (or **🎯 Open lead** if they already are one). "The transcript is on the lead as a note."
6. Press **Close chat** when the conversation is finished.

## Stepping away

1. Press **Hand back to Maya** before you leave a chat you hold.
2. Maya picks up the conversation, including anything the visitor asked that you had not answered.

Maya also covers for you: when **nobody is online** she answers at once, and when people are online but nobody picks
up within about 45 seconds she answers so the visitor is not left waiting. She never answers while a person holds the
chat.

## What the list tells you

- **Wants a person** — the visitor asked for a human.
- **With Maya** / **Maya is answering** — nobody has joined yet.
- **On the website now** / **Left the page** — whether the visitor is still there.
- **Closed** — "writing here reopens it if they are still on the page".

Switch between **Open** and **Closed** at the top to find earlier conversations.

## Transcripts and alerts

A visitor who arrives while nobody is online sends a push alert to the team. A "visitors are waiting" email and
finished transcripts go to the address a platform admin sets in **Website › Chat transcripts to**; if that is blank,
alerts are push only. Superadmins also see website chats in **Message Centre › 🌐 Website**.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Sales rep | Go online, join, reply, nudge, add leads and close chats. |
| Platform admin (superadmin) | The same, plus the **🌐 Website** tab in Message Centre and the transcript address. |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? Does the visitor know when they are talking to Maya? | Yes. Maya always introduces herself as the virtual assistant, and a person joining is announced in the chat.
??? I joined a chat and need to leave. | Press **Hand back to Maya**. She takes over and answers anything still unanswered.
??? "No open website chats. New ones appear here the moment a visitor writes." | Nobody is chatting right now. Keep the screen open; new chats appear and chime.
??? "Could not join" | A colleague may have just taken the chat, or the connection dropped. Refresh the list and try again.
??? Can I work a chat from my phone? | Yes. On a phone the conversation opens full-screen; use **Back** to return to the list.
