# Report cards

AI-written narrative report cards for each child, organised by the areas of your learning framework. Directors review, edit and send back; **report cards are now approved by agency admins**.

[[open: #report-cards | Open Report cards]]

## Generating a draft

1. Open **Administration › Report cards** and choose the **✨ Generate with AI** tab.
2. Under **Child**, pick the child (**Select a child…**).
3. Under **Term**, pick the term, or choose to type your own (for example "Summer 2027").
4. Press **✨ Generate with AI**. If AI is off for your agency, the button reads **📝 Draft from observations** and the draft is written from the child's logged observations instead.
5. After 15 to 30 seconds the draft appears: one narrative for each area of your learning framework — for How Does Learning Happen? that is **Belonging**, **Well-being**, **Engagement** and **Expression** — plus a short **Next steps** list.
6. Read and edit each text. The AI gets you most of the way; you make it sound like you.
7. Sign and submit it, or sign and approve it — see **Who approves** below.

The draft uses the child's framework-tagged observations and milestone records, so it is specific and uses the child's name. A child with few observations gets a thinner draft — log a few more first.

## All report cards

Under the approvals on the **Report cards** screen, **All report cards** lists every card you can see, newest first: the child, the term, who made it, its status, the centre and when it was last changed.

- Agency admins see every card in the agency. Directors see the cards for their own centres.
- **Who approves:** the educator or provider signs and submits (*Prepared by*). One of your agency's own **agency admins** signs and approves (*Approved by*; platform staff can't), which sends the card to the family. Directors review, edit and send back cards for their centres, but don't approve them. Cards a director approved before this change keep that approval.
- Tap **Draft**, **Awaiting approval** or **Sent** to show just those. Each chip shows how many there are.
- Search for a child, or narrow the list by term, by who made it, or by centre.
- Tap a card to open it in a popup, or use **⋮** on its row: **View**, **Edit**, **Download PDF**, **Print**, **Email to family**, **Review & approve**, **Sign & approve**, **Send back** and **Delete draft**. You only see the ones you can use.
- **Several at once:** tick the boxes at the start of the rows (the box in the header ticks every row shown, then offers **Select all … matching**). For agency admins, the bar above the list shows **Sign & approve** and **Delete drafts**. You sign once and confirm. Each card is still checked on its own: a card that is already approved, or at a centre you don't direct, is skipped, and the list at the end says which ones and why. Deleting drafts cannot be undone.
- **Editing a sent card** makes a new version (v2, v3…). The new version is approved again before it reaches the family. The earlier one stays, marked **Replaced**, and both PDFs are kept under **History & versions**.
- **Email to family** lists the child's guardians who have an email address. Nobody is ticked for you. Add a note if you like, preview the email, then send. Each person gets a secure link to the PDF that works for 14 days. Who sent it, to whom and when is kept in the card's history.
- **Delete draft** is for agency admins, and only for drafts that were never sent.

Cards for children who left and centres that closed are not listed for directors and educators. **Archived is gone:** agency admins see those cards in the normal lists (All, Draft, Awaiting approval, Sent) under their real status, marked **Child left** or **Centre closed**, with the full **⋮** menu and the bulk actions. No new card can be made for a child who has left.

## The PDF

The PDF is laid out as a progress report. Your agency's logo and name sit at the top, with the term and date. A ruled grid shows the child, the card's reference, days absent, days late and late pick-ups for the term (from attendance; blank when the term has no dates), the room and educator, your agency and centre addresses, every director and admin of your agency (**Director / Administrator**; platform staff are never listed), and both phone numbers. Each learning area of your framework has its own grey panel, followed by **Strengths / Next Steps for Growth** and the signatures of the educator and the approver. A reference number and page number are on every page. Drafts are stamped *DRAFT – not approved*. Print uses the same layout.

Agency admins can choose whether the educator's RECE registration number is printed: **Report card settings** at the bottom of the Report cards tab. It is on unless you turn it off.

## Statuses

- **Draft** — generated or edited, not yet submitted.
- **Awaiting approval** — signed and submitted, waiting for an agency admin to approve it.
- **Sent** — approved and sent to the family.
- **Replaced** — an earlier version of a card that was edited after it was sent.

To save a PDF, open the card's **⋮** menu and choose **Download PDF**.

## When to generate

- Quarterly (Sept / Dec / Mar / Jun)
- Year-end (June)
- Transition (when child moves rooms or graduates)

## Reusing observations

The same observations feed Year-end **Portfolios** (different feature — chronological evidence) and Report cards (narrative summary). Both share the same framework-tagged observations.
