# Report cards

AI-generated narrative reports per child, organised by HDLH domain. Director reviews + edits + sends.

## Generating

1. Sidebar → **Report cards**.
2. Enter child ID + term (e.g. "2026 Year-End", "Fall 2026").
3. Open the **✨ Generate with AI** tab on the Report cards screen, pick the child and term, and tap **Generate with AI**. (If AI is off for your agency, the button says **Draft from observations** and writes the draft from logged observations.)
4. 15-30 seconds later, four narratives appear:
   - **Belonging** — 4-5 sentences on sense of community + relationships
   - **Well-being** — physical + emotional development
   - **Engagement** — exploration + curiosity + play
   - **Expression** — communication + creativity + language
5. Plus a **Next steps** suggestion list (3 items).

The AI uses the child's HDLH-tagged observations + milestone records to write specific, warm, name-using narratives.

## Reviewing + editing

Each domain text is editable. Tweak as needed — the AI gets you 90% of the way, you tighten the language.

## All report cards

Under the approvals on the **Report cards** screen, **All report cards** lists every card you can see, newest first: the child, the term, who made it, its status, the centre and when it was last changed.

- Agency admins see every card in the agency. Directors see the cards for their own centres.
- Tap **Draft**, **Awaiting approval** or **Sent** to show just those. Each chip shows how many there are.
- Search for a child, or narrow the list by term, by who made it, or by centre.
- Tap a card to open it below the list, or use **⋮** on its row: **View**, **Edit**, **Download PDF**, **Print**, **Email to family**, **Review & approve**, **Send back** and **Delete draft**. You only see the ones you can use.
- **Editing a sent card** makes a new version (v2, v3…). The new version is approved again before it reaches the family. The earlier one stays, marked **Replaced**, and both PDFs are kept under **History & versions**.
- **Email to family** lists the child's guardians who have an email address. Nobody is ticked for you. Add a note if you like, preview the email, then send. Each person gets a secure link to the PDF that works for 14 days. Who sent it, to whom and when is kept in the card's history.
- **Delete draft** is for agency admins, and only for drafts that were never sent.

Cards for withdrawn children and closed centres are not listed there. Agency admins get one more chip, **Archived**: those cards, each marked *Archived — child withdrawn* or *Archived — centre closed*. Agency admins have the full **⋮** menu on them, including **Edit** and **Email to family**. If the child has left, guardians still on file can still be emailed. Directors and educators do not see archived cards.

## The PDF

The PDF carries your agency's logo, name, centre address, phone, email and licence number, the child's room and term, and a **Prepared by** box for the educator. It ends with the educator's signature beside the approver's signature, title and approval date, and a reference number on every page. Drafts are stamped *DRAFT – not approved*.

Agency admins can choose whether the educator's RECE registration number is printed: **Report card settings** at the bottom of the Report cards tab. It is on unless you turn it off.

## Statuses

- **Draft** — generated, not yet sent
- **Reviewed** — director has edited + signed off
- **Sent** — emailed to the family + guardians notified

## PDF export

Tap **⤓ Download PDF** on any report card. Cleanly formatted with the agency logo + child name + term + four domain blocks + next-steps.

## When to generate

- Quarterly (Sept / Dec / Mar / Jun)
- Year-end (June)
- Transition (when child moves rooms or graduates)

## Reusing observations

The same observations feed Year-end **Portfolios** (different feature — chronological evidence) and Report cards (narrative summary). Both share the underlying HDLH-tagged observation data.
