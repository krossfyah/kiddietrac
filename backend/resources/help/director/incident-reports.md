# Incident reports

**Incidents** records injuries, illnesses, behavioural incidents, medication errors and serious occurrences. A report is written by the educator, signed, reviewed by a director, shared with the family to acknowledge, and closed with the director's signature, with every step kept on the record.

**Where it is:** sidebar → **Operations** → **Incidents**. The list is headed **⚠️ Incident Reports**.

[[open: #incidents | Open Incidents]]

## How a report moves

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **DRAFT** | Still being written. Only staff see it. |
| **AWAITING REVIEW** | Signed and submitted to a director. |
| **REVIEWED** | A director has reviewed it and may have added notes. |
| **AWAITING PARENT** | Sent to the child's parents to acknowledge. |
| **ACKNOWLEDGED** | A parent has signed it. |
| **CLOSED** | A director has signed it off. It can still be read, but not changed. |

A serious occurrence is badged **SO** in the list, using your province's or state's wording.

## Filing a new report

1. Press **+ New report**. The **New incident report** form opens.
2. Choose the **Child \*** and set **When did it happen? \***.
3. Choose the **Type \*** (General, Injury, Illness, Behavioural, Medication error, your serious-occurrence type, or Other) and the **Severity** (Low, Medium or High).
4. Add the **Location**, for example "Acorn Room, outdoor playground".
5. Describe **What happened? \*** factually, then fill in **Action taken** and **Follow-up required**.
6. Press **Save & submit** and sign in the box that opens, then press **Sign & submit**. Or press **Save as draft** to finish it later (a draft is not signed).
7. A green **Submitted for review** (or **Saved as draft**) appears and the report opens.

## Finding a report

1. Use the status dropdown (**All statuses**, **Draft**, **Awaiting review**, **Reviewed**, **Awaiting parent**, **Acknowledged**, **Closed**) to narrow the list.
2. The table shows **When**, **Child**, **Type**, **Severity**, **Status**, **Last update** and **Recorded by**.
3. Click a row to open the report and act on it, or use the row's **⋮**: **View** (read only, nothing can be changed by mistake), **View report** (the printable PDF) or **Edit** (the **Manage incident** box).

## Reviewing a submitted report

1. Open a report that is **AWAITING REVIEW**.
2. Press **🔍 Review incident**. The **Review incident** box shows what you are reviewing.
3. Type **Your review notes**: what you checked and what happens next.
4. Leave **Notify the family now** ticked to send it to the parents straight away, or untick it if you need to speak to them first.
5. Press **Record review**. You see **Reviewed, and the family has been notified.** (or **Review recorded.**).

If you reviewed without notifying, press **Notify parent** later and confirm. The parents get a notification and an email, and the status becomes **AWAITING PARENT**.

## Adding notes and logging contact

Under **Notes & details** on the report, type in **Add a note or extra detail…** and press **Add note**. Notes are staff-only and record who wrote them and when; parents never see them.

The **Manage incident** box (row **⋮ > Edit**) has four tabs:

- **Status**: choose **Move it to**, say **Why is it moving?**, press **Change status**.
- **Note**: a staff-only note; press **Add note**.
- **Log contact**: record **Who did you speak to?**, **Their name**, **How?** and **What was discussed?**, then **Log this contact**. Logged contacts print on the report as evidence.
- **Report**: **📄 Open the report** for the printable PDF on your branding.

## Emailing the report

1. On a report that is not a draft, press **📧 Email report**.
2. Tick **Parent / guardian**, **Centre director** and/or **Agency admin**, or type an address in **Also send to (optional email)**.
3. Press **Send**. You see **✓ Sent to 2 recipients.**

## Closing a report

1. Press **🔒 Close incident**.
2. In **Close this incident?**, write **How this was resolved**; this appears on the report.
3. Press **Continue to sign**, sign, and press **Sign & close**. You see **Incident closed and signed off**.

## What parents see

Parents see a report once it is shared. They type their full name, draw a signature and press **Acknowledge**. Under **Your response** they can add comments and tick **I would like to meet to discuss this**; your centre is told.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Educator | File reports, save drafts, submit and sign, email a submitted report. |
| Centre director | Everything above for their centres, plus review, notify parents, manage status, log contact and close. |
| Agency admin | The same as a director, across the agency. |
| Parent | Reads shared reports about their own child, acknowledges and responds. |

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? I see "Not submitted — the report needs your signature." | You closed the signature box. The form is still filled in: sign, or press **Save as draft**.
??? I see "Not closed — the sign-off needs your signature." | Closing needs your signature. Press **🔒 Close incident** again and sign.
??? I see "Pick at least one recipient." | Tick at least one box, or type an email address, before pressing **Send**.
??? I see "It is already at that status." | In **Manage incident**, pick a different status under **Move it to**.
??? The list says "No incident reports". | None match the status you picked. Choose **All statuses**.
??? There is no Review button on my report. | **🔍 Review incident** only shows while the report is **AWAITING REVIEW**. A director's own draft can be moved on from **Manage incident > Status**.
??? I see "Could not record the review". | The review was not saved and the box stays open with your notes. Copy your notes, then use **⋮ > Edit > Status** to move the report to **Reviewed by a director** with your notes under **Why is it moving?**, and press **Notify parent**. Raise a support ticket so the review button can be checked.
??? Can a report be deleted? | No. Reports stay on the child's record. Close it with a note explaining what happened.
