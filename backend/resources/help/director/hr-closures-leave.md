# HR: closures taken from leave, leave to review, part-time pro-rata

Part of the KiddieTrac HR add-on. Everything here is in **Human Resources > Time off > Closures & checks**. A balance is never changed by itself: every change is a new line in the person's balance history, with the reason.

## Balance after all pending requests

When you approve time off, the request shows the balance now, the balance after this request and, when the same person has other requests of that type waiting, the balance **after all pending requests**. It turns red when that goes below zero. The **Balances** tab shows the same figure under each pending amount.

## Take a closure from leave (for example a winter shutdown)

Closures come from **Settings > Closures**. A holiday, emergency or other closure can take the closed working days off the staff's leave. A statutory holiday never does.

1. Find the closure under **Closures: take the days off leave?** and choose **Take from leave…** in its ⋮ menu.
2. Pick the leave type (Vacation is chosen for you). The preview lists everyone who works at that centre, the working days the closure shuts for them, how much it takes, and their balance before and after.
3. Untick anyone to leave out. Weekends, statutory holidays, days before someone started, and days already taken by another closure are never counted. Someone who works at two centres only loses a day when both are shut.
4. Press **Take from leave**.

Or choose **Don't take from leave** to record that the closure is not deducted.

Nothing is ever taken automatically, and a closure that is already over asks you to tick a box first. **Reverse** gives everything back (with a reason); you can then choose again. Only agency admins decide. Directors see their centres' closures and who they affect.

If a closure that was already taken from leave changes dates, it shows **Dates changed - review** and appears under **Leave to review**.

## Leave to review

Adding, moving or removing a closure or a statutory holiday changes how many working days booked time off covers. KiddieTrac does not re-deduct anything. Each booking whose amount would now come out differently is listed under **Leave to review**, with what it was booked at, what it counts now, and why. The directors and admins of that centre get a bell.

- **Use the new amount**: one new line goes in the balance history for the difference (or, for a request still waiting, it will take the new amount when approved).
- **Keep as booked**: nothing changes.

Nobody reviews their own time off. The request also shows "Amount may be wrong" in **Requests**, and the **Approve** popup lets you use the new amount as you approve. Admins can press **Check booked leave now** to check every booking from the last year against today's calendar.

## Part-time pro-rata (agency admins)

Turn on **Pro-rata new grants for part-time staff**, set the company's full-time week (40 hours to start), and choose the leave types and who counts as part-time (part-time, casual, student). From then on, each NEW grant (a new leave year, or a new pay period) for a part-time person is their hours a week out of the full-time week: 20 of 40 hours gets half.

- The hours come from the person's HR profile (**People > View HR profile > Pay and role history**, hours a week). Anyone with no hours on their file gets the full amount.
- Grants already in the balance history never change.
- **Preview** shows each part-time person's yearly entitlement with and without pro-rata before you save.
