# Customizing your dashboard

Your **Agency overview** dashboard shows "Business insights" widgets you choose and arrange.

## Add, remove & arrange widgets

1. Click **+ Add widget** to open **Customize widgets**.
2. Under **On your dashboard**, **drag the tiles** to set the order they appear.
3. Click **×** on a tile to remove it, or **＋** on an **Available widget** to add it.

Changes apply immediately.

## Available widgets

Attendance today, Staff on floor, Capacity utilisation, Outstanding receivables, Centre occupancy, Compliance snapshot, Pending approvals, Activity feed, Recurring/Annualised revenue, Users by role, Enrollment & revenue, and more.

## The top bar

The bar across the top greets you by name and shows a live clock, local weather, and quick shortcuts — **Quick add**, **Messages**, **Announcements**, and the **AI assistant**. Click your name to open your profile.
