# Agencies (reseller)

**Agencies** is the plain list of the agencies that carry your brand. Use it to check, at a glance, which agencies belong to your reseller. For live numbers, flags, admins and opening an agency, use **All my agencies** instead.

[[open: #reseller-agencies | Open Agencies]]

## Checking which agencies are yours

1. Open **Agencies** from the **Reseller** section of the menu.
2. Read the banner: it shows your reseller's name and how many agencies you have, for example "The agencies that carry your brand (4)."
3. Read the list under **Your agencies**. Each row (🏢) is one agency that shows your brand.
4. To work inside one of them, open **All my agencies** and press **Open agency →** on its card.

## Adding or moving an agency

1. To add a brand-new agency, open **Onboard an agency** and fill in the form. It appears here as soon as it is added.
2. To move an agency that already uses KiddieTrac under your brand, raise a ticket with KiddieTrac ([[raise-a-ticket]]). Only the platform team can tick an existing agency to your reseller.
3. Refresh this screen to see the change.

> ℹ️ An agency belongs to one reseller only. An agency that is with another reseller cannot be moved to you without that being arranged with KiddieTrac.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Reseller | Sees the names of their own agencies. Adds new ones on **Onboard an agency**. |
| Platform admin | Assigns agencies to any reseller on **Resellers › Agencies**. |
| Agency admins | Do not see this list. |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? It says "No agencies yet. KiddieTrac assigns agencies to your account." | No agency belongs to your reseller yet. Add one on **Onboard an agency**, or ask KiddieTrac to move an existing one to you.
??? An agency I onboarded is missing. | Refresh the page. If it is still missing, check **All my agencies**; if it is not there either, raise a ticket.
??? "Could not load your reseller." appears. | Your reseller access is not active, or your session ended. Refresh the page and sign in again.
??? Can I remove an agency from my brand here? | No. This list is read-only. Ask KiddieTrac to move it.
