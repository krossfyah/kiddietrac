# Payment plans

Split a large balance into manageable weekly, biweekly, or monthly installments for a single family.

## Creating a plan (director / admin)

1. Sidebar → **Payment plans**.
2. Tap **+ New payment plan**.
3. Fill in: Family ID, total amount, # of installments (2-24), first due date, cadence (weekly / biweekly / monthly), optional notes.
4. The system pre-calculates equal installments (last one absorbs any rounding).
5. Notifications fan out to family guardians on creation.

## Viewing as a parent

- Sidebar → **Payment plans** under Account.
- See each installment with its due date + amount + status (pending / paid / cancelled).

## Cancelling

Director or agency admin can press **Cancel plan** on any active plan. All pending installments switch to `cancelled`. Already-paid installments stay paid.

## Useful for

- Spreading a large enrolment deposit over 4 months
- Tuition catch-up after a missed period
- Family in financial hardship who needs a custom schedule

## Different from auto-pay

Auto-pay charges full invoices on their due date. Payment plans split one balance across multiple due dates with separate invoices.
