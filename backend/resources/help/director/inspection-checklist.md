# Inspection prep checklist

**Compliance › Inspection checklist** is a ready-made list of what provincial and municipal licensing inspectors
usually look at, grouped by topic. Walk through it before an inspection, mark each item, and you can see at a glance
how ready you are. The first time anyone opens it, KiddieTrac fills it with a standard set of items for your agency.

[[open: #inspection | Open the Inspection checklist]]

## Getting ready for an inspection

1. Open **Compliance › Inspection checklist**. The heading reads **Inspection prep checklist**, with "… marked complete (…%)" and a green progress bar.
2. Work down one topic at a time. Each table has a **Checklist item** column and a **Status** column.
3. Check the item in your centre (the poster is up, the drill log is signed, the first-aid kit is stocked …).
4. In **Status**, choose **✓ Pass**, **✗ Fail** or **N/A** (does not apply to you). Leave **Pending** for anything not checked yet.
5. The row turns green for pass, red for fail and grey for N/A. It saves as soon as you choose — there is no Save button.
6. Fix each red item, then come back and change it to **✓ Pass**.

> 💡 Only **✓ Pass** counts towards the percentage. Mark items that do not apply as **N/A** so the list shows what is really left.

## Starting fresh for the next inspection

The statuses stay as you left them. Before the next inspection, set each item back to **Pending** and walk the list
again, so last year's answers are not mistaken for this year's.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | See and mark every item for the agency. |
| Centre director | See and mark the agency's items, and items for their own centre(s). |
| Platform admin | The agency chosen in the agency switcher. |

> ℹ️ The standard items are shared by the whole agency: when one director marks an item, every director in the agency sees the new status.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? Can I add my own items or change the wording? | Not from this screen. Ask your agency admin to raise a ticket from Help if your province needs different items.
??? My change did not stick. | Refresh the page. If the status went back, you may not have access to that centre's item; ask your agency admin.
??? Does marking an item notify anyone? | No. The checklist sends no emails, texts or notices.
??? Is this the same as inspection forms? | No. Inspection forms are filled-in forms you keep as records; this is a readiness checklist.
