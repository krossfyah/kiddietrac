# Daily Overview: one provider, one day

**Daily Overview** is a quick look at one provider (a home or a centre) on one day: which children came and went, who worked, what was eaten, planned and logged, and how the day went. It opens on today, and any past day works too. Agency admins open it from **Overview > Daily Overview**; anyone with the **Agency overview** or **Dashboard** can also press **📅 Review day** on a centre's card.

[[open: #provider-day | Open Daily Overview]]

## Choosing the provider and the day

1. Open **Daily Overview**. If no provider is chosen yet, the screen says "Select a provider to see its day."
2. Pick one from the **Provider** list (**Select a provider…**).
3. Use **‹** (**Previous day**) and **›** (**Next day**) beside the **Date** to step through the week, or pick a date. The day's name ("Today", "Yesterday", or the weekday) shows beside the **Date** label.
4. Press **Today** to come back to today. **›** is greyed out on today, because there is no future day to show.

## Checking the children

The **Children — clock in / out & care** table lists every child expected that day, with the totals at the top: at Centre, not at Centre, absent and not scheduled.

1. Read each row: **Child**, **In**, **Out**, **Care today** (meals, naps and care logged), **Via** (One-Tap or manual) and **Status**.
2. Look for small badges on the times: **automatic** means nobody signed the child out so the time was filled in; **entered by parent** or **entered by educator** means the real time came from the automatic sign-out email; **corrected** means staff fixed it later.
3. A child marked **Not booked** is not scheduled to attend that day.

## Signing a child in or out today

1. On today's date, find the child and press **Sign in** (or **Sign out**) in the **Action** column.
2. Confirm in the yellow **Not using One-Tap** warning. The child is recorded as of now and their family is notified.
3. For a child not here yet whose family will be late, press **⏰ Late drop-off**; for a child who is here, **⏰ Pickup**. Enter the time and save.

## Fixing a missed or wrong time

1. Press **⏱ Fix** on the child's row. It is there today and on past days.
2. In **Add a missed sign in or out**, fill in **Arrived**, **Left** or both, and choose **Why is this being added late?**.
3. Press **Save**. "Attendance recorded." appears, the row shows **corrected**, and the family is emailed that the record was corrected.

## Checking the team

**Provider & educators — clock in / out** lists everyone assigned there, with their clock-in and clock-out times. A green dot means they are active in the app now. Someone not yet in shows **Not clocked in** (today), **Did not clock in** (a past day) or **Not scheduled**; someone on a break shows "on lunch now" or "on a break now".

## The rest of the day

- **This week's meal plan**: the week's menu, with today highlighted, or "No menu published for the week of …".
- **📚 Planned for this day**: the lesson plan activities.
- **🧮 Last headcount — by educator**: **✓ All accounted for**, or **⚠** how many were not accounted for, plus missed headcounts.
- **Check-in method**: how many check-ins came through One-Tap and how many were manual.
- **🚶 Walk Watch**: walks and outings, with **● LIVE** and a **Live map** for one in progress.
- **📊 Provider performance**: attended, meals and snacks, naps, activities, incidents and moments, plus ⭐ educator points for the month.
- **👀 Observations recorded**, **📸 Photos & videos captured** and **💬 Educator ↔ parent chat**.
- **📝 Daily forms submitted**: the forms due that day. A **one-time** form shows only until it is signed.
- **Activities & daily logs**: every log of the day; use **All children** to filter to one child.

## Who can do what

- **Agency admin** and **platform admin**: every provider in the agency, with sign in/out, late times and **⏱ Fix**.
- **Centre director**: their own centres, opened with **📅 Review day** on their **Dashboard**, including **⏱ Fix**.
- **Educators, parents and auditors**: do not see this screen.

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? A child shows as Absent but was there. | Use **⏱ Fix** on their row to record the real arrival and departure times, with a reason.
??? Why is there no Sign in button on a past day? | Sign in and Sign out record "now", so they only appear on today. For an earlier day use **⏱ Fix**.
??? A section is empty. | Nothing was recorded for it that day. The card says so, for example "No observations recorded on this day."
??? The screen says "Could not load: …". | The day could not be fetched. Choose the provider again or reload the page.
??? It says "This provider's day could not be displayed." | The information loaded but could not be drawn. Reload the page; if it keeps happening, tell KiddieTrac support which provider and date.
??? It says "No providers found." | Your agency has no open centres yet, or none in your scope. Add one under **Centres**.
