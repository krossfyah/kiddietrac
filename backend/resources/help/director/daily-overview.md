# Daily Overview: one provider, one day

**Daily Overview** is a quick glance at one provider (home or centre) on any day: who came,
who is working, what was eaten and done, and every daily log. It opens on today; any past day
works too.

[[open: #provider-day | Open Daily Overview]]

> Who can use this: **Agency admin**, **Centre director**, **Platform admin**.

## Getting there

- In the menu: **Agency overview → 🗓️ Daily Overview**.
- From the **Agency overview** screen: a provider's **📅 Review day** link opens that provider's day.

Pick the provider at the top. Use **‹ ›** (**Previous day** / **Next day**) to step through the
week; the day's name is shown so you always know where you are.

## The team: provider and educators

**Provider & educators — clock in / out** lists everyone working there that day with their
clock-in and clock-out times. A green dot means they are active in the app now; amber means on
shift but quiet. On the Agency overview, a home with nobody clocked in says **No staff clocked
in**, and hovering it lists who is assigned there.

Everyone assigned to the home is listed, even before they clock in: **Not clocked in** (today),
**Did not clock in** (a past day) or **Not scheduled** (not on the schedule that day).

## The children

**Children — clock in / out & care** lists every child scheduled that day: when they arrived
and left, the check-in method (One-Tap or manual), and the care logged. A
departure marked **automatic** was filled in because nobody signed out; **entered by parent** or
**entered by educator** means the real time came from the automatic sign-out email. Children who are
**Still in**, **Went home** or are **Absent** are counted at the top.

Today, you can mark a child present or gone home from here. Their family is notified.

## The rest of the day

- **This week’s meal plan** — or a reminder to add one so parents can see it.
- **Activities & daily logs** — meals, naps, care and moments.
- **📚 Planned for this day**, **🧮 Last headcount — by educator**, **📸 Photos & videos
  captured**, **👀 Observations recorded**, **📝 Daily forms submitted**, **🚶 Walks &
  outings** and **💬 Educator ↔ parent chat**.
- **📊 Provider performance** — the day's numbers, such as moments per child.

> **Note:** **📝 Daily forms submitted** lists the reusable forms due every day. A one-time form (marked **one-time**) shows only until the provider signs it, then it leaves the list.

??? A child shows as Absent but was there. | Mark them present from Daily Overview (today), or correct the times in Attendance for a past day.
??? Why is a section empty? | Nothing was recorded for it that day. The section says so, for example "No observations recorded on this day."
