# Policies, sign-offs and the training matrix

Keep your policies and procedures in one place, see who has read and signed the current version, and keep the written record licensing asks for. In Ontario (O. Reg. 137/15 s.6.1) staff review each policy before working with children and whenever it changes, and the records are kept for 3 years. Directors and admins manage policies in **Compliance > Policies**; every staff member, directors included, signs theirs in **Compliance > Policies to review**.

[[open: #policies | Open Policies]]

The **Policies** screen has three tabs: **📚 Library**, **🧮 Training matrix** and **⚠️ Contraventions**.

## Adding a policy

1. Open **Compliance > Policies** on the **📚 Library** tab.
2. Press **+ Add policy**.
3. Give the policy a title and choose where it applies: **Agency-wide (every centre)** or one centre. Directors add policies for their own centres.
4. Tick **Required (counts in the training matrix)** if staff must sign it.
5. Press **Save**. The policy shows **No text yet**.
6. Press **Add text** on its row, then choose **Upload a document** (PDF or Word, up to 20 MB) or **Write or paste text**.
7. Write the **Summary (required)** that staff will see, then press **Publish**.

Everyone the policy applies to gets a bell and an email asking them to read and sign it.

> 💡 Ontario agency admins can press **🍁 Add the Ontario checklist** to list the policies Ontario licensing asks for, marked **Required** or **Recommended**. It adds titles only: KiddieTrac never writes the policy for you.

## Changing a policy

1. Press **Publish new version** on the policy's row.
2. Upload or write the new text.
3. Fill in **What changed (required)**: staff see it.
4. Press **Publish**. The version number goes up, and staff are asked to sign the new version.

If you reviewed the policy and nothing changed, press **Mark reviewed** instead. The review date moves on and existing sign-offs stand (use it for the program statement's yearly review).

## Following up sign-offs

1. Read the cards at the top of the Library: **Required policies**, **Sign-offs on current versions** and **Reviews overdue**.
2. Read each row's **Status** chip (current, review due, review overdue, no text yet) and its **Signed** count.
3. Press **Open** on a policy to see every version (with **View**), each staff member's status (signed, **Signed an older version**, **Not signed**), reviews, training and contraventions.

## Recording training or a contravention

1. Press **Record training** on the policy.
2. Tick who was trained and set the date, then press **Record**.
3. For a breach, press **Record contravention**, choose the centre, describe what happened and the **Action taken**, then press **Record**.

From January 1, 2027, Ontario expects training on every required policy (s.58(1.1)). Contraventions are listed together on the **⚠️ Contraventions** tab.

## Checking the training matrix

1. Open the **🧮 Training matrix** tab.
2. Read it: staff down the side, required policies across the top. ✓ signed, ! signed an older version, ✕ not signed, 🎓 trained.
3. Filter by centre, or tick **Include recommended policies**.
4. Press **⬇ Export CSV** for your licensing file.

## Signing a policy (every staff member)

1. Open **Compliance > Policies to review**. Policies waiting are under **To sign**.
2. Press **📖 Read policy** and read it. The card then shows **Opened ✓**.
3. Tick **I have read and understand this policy, and I will follow it.**
4. Type your full name.
5. Press **✍️ Sign**. The policy moves to **Signed**, with the date, time and where it was signed from.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Every policy, agency-wide or per centre; the Ontario checklist; weekly reminders on or off. |
| Centre director | Policies for their own centres; sign-offs, training and contraventions there; their own sign-offs. |
| Educator, home visitor | Read and sign their own policies in **Policies to review**. |

**Archive** takes a policy off the current list; tick **Archived** to see it again and **Restore** it. Nothing is ever deleted.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? "A short summary is required — staff see it." | Every new version needs a summary of what changed. Write one line and publish again.
??? "That file is over 20 MB" | Save the document smaller (for example as a PDF) and upload it again.
??? Staff say they cannot sign. | They must open the policy first (**📖 Read policy**), tick the box and type their name.
??? Who gets reminders? | On Mondays, staff with policies to sign get one email, and directors and admins get a summary with reviews due in 30 days. An agency admin can switch this off at the bottom of the Library.
??? Where are safe work procedures? | They are policies in the category Safe work procedure, shown under **Human Resources > Health & safety > Safe work procedures** when the HR add-on is on.
