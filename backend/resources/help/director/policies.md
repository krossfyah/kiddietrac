# Policies, sign-offs and the training matrix

Keep your policies and procedures in one place, see who has read and signed the current version, and keep the written record Ontario licensing asks for (O. Reg. 137/15 s.6.1: staff review each policy before working with children and whenever it changes; records kept for 3 years). Open **Compliance → Policies**.

## Library

Each policy shows its current **version**, its **review** status (current, due within 30 days, overdue, or no text yet) and how many of the staff it applies to have **signed** the current version.

- **Add the Ontario checklist** (agency admins, Ontario agencies) lists the policies Ontario licensing asks for, marked **Required** or **Recommended**, with titles only. KiddieTrac never writes the policy for you: you upload or write your own.
- **Add policy** adds one of your own. Choose **Agency-wide** or a single centre. Directors add policies for their own centre.
- **Add text / Publish new version**: upload a PDF or Word document, or write or paste the text. A short **summary of what changed** is required. Every staff member it applies to gets a bell and an email and is asked to sign the new version.
- **Mark reviewed**: you reviewed it and nothing changed. The review date moves on and existing sign-offs stand. Use this for the program statement's yearly review (s.46) when nothing changed.
- **Record training**: who was trained on the policy, and when. From January 1, 2027, Ontario expects training on every required policy (s.58(1.1)).
- **Record contravention**: what happened, at which centre, and the action taken.
- **Archive** takes a policy out of the current list. Nothing is ever deleted.

**Open** shows every version (with **View**), each staff member's status, reviews, training and contraventions.

## Training matrix

Staff down the side, required policies across the top: ✓ signed, ! signed an older version, ✕ not signed, 🎓 trained. Filter by centre, include recommended policies, and **Export CSV** for your licensing file.

## Reminders

On Mondays, staff with policies still to sign get one email listing them, and directors and admins get a summary of who still has to sign and which reviews are due within 30 days. Agency admins can switch this off at the bottom of the Library.
