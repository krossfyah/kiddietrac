# HR: documents, pay history and leaving

Keep the company handbook and forms, each person's papers, their pay and role changes, and everything about someone leaving in one place. It is part of the KiddieTrac HR add-on: company documents are under **Human Resources > Documents & letters**, and each person's documents, history and leaving pack are on their HR profile in **Human Resources > People**.

[[open: #hr-documents | Open Documents & letters]]

## Sharing a company document

1. Open **Human Resources > Documents & letters**. The **Company documents** tab opens first.
2. Press **New folder**, enter the **Folder name**, choose **Who can see it** (**Agency admins only**, **Everyone on staff** or **Chosen people**) and press **Save**.
3. In the folder, press **Upload**.
4. Choose the files, the **Type**, and if you like an **Expires on (optional)** or **Follow up on (optional)** date.
5. Tick **Ask staff to read and accept it** if everyone must confirm they read it.
6. Press **Upload**. The document shows in the folder with **Opened** and **Accepted** columns.

[[show: #hr-documents @ Company documents > New folder | Show me]]

Open a document's **⋮** menu for **Preview**, **Download**, **Who has read it**, **Edit details** or **Remove**. A new folder is seen by agency admins only until you change **Folder settings**. A document that must be accepted waits in each person's **My HR** until they press **Read and accept**.

## Adding documents to a person's file

1. Open **Human Resources > People**.
2. Open the person's **⋮** menu and press **View HR profile**.
3. On the **Documents** card, press **Documents**, then **Upload files**.
4. Choose up to 20 files (15 MB each), a **Type** (ID, contract, certificate, work permit and so on) and **Who can see it**: **Shared with them** (they see it in My HR) or **Managers only**.
5. Add an **Expires on (optional)** date and a **Follow up on (optional)** date if you want reminders, then press **Upload**.

You and the person's manager get a reminder 30 days and 7 days before a document expires, and on the day. Files are private: they open from a short-lived link and never sit on a public address.

> ℹ️ Sick notes and time-off notes that staff attach to a request are seen only by the person, their director and the agency admins, never by their file manager.

## Scheduling a raise or a role change

1. On the person's HR profile, find the **Pay and role history** card and press **History**. Every change to pay rate, pay type, job title, employment type and hours a week is listed, with who changed it, when and why.
2. Press **Schedule a change**.
3. Choose **Takes effect on**, then fill in what changes: **Job title**, **Employment**, **Hours a week**, **Pay rate**, **Pay type**.
4. Write **Why (kept with the change)** and press **Save change**.

A change dated today or earlier applies at once; a later one applies itself on that date. Pay figures are shown only to people allowed to see pay. Nobody can change their own pay.

## When someone leaves (agency admins)

1. On the person's HR profile, press **Start leaving** on the **Leaving** card. The **Leaving pack** opens.
2. Enter the **Last day worked**, **Notice given on** and **Reason (ROE code)**.
3. Work through the **Checklist**: keys, devices, uniform, final pay, the leaving letter and the ROE. Closing their access ticks itself once you press **Off-board from their user record**.
4. Check the **Leave payout**: each paid-out balance is valued at their pay rate. Change any amount if needed.
5. Press **ROE sheet** to see insurable hours and earnings by pay period, then **Print** or **Download CSV**.
6. Press **Complete leaving**, choose a new manager for anyone who reported to them, and press **Complete**.

Completing books the payout against their balances and marks their HR file Terminated on the last day. It does not close their account: off-board them from their user record. KiddieTrac does not file anything with Service Canada: enter the ROE figures in ROE Web or give them to your payroll provider.

### Undoing a leaving or rehiring someone

1. Open **People > Leavers** and press **Leaving pack** on the person.
2. Press **Undo leaving**, write why, and press **Undo leaving** again: their account reopens and the payout is reversed.
3. Or, when someone comes back later, press **Rehire**, enter the **New start date** and press **Rehire**. Their leave balances start at zero unless you untick that.

[[show: #hr-people?tab=leavers @ Leavers > Leaving pack | Show me]]

The **Leavers** tab lists everyone who has left. **Download all their data** gives a ZIP of their file, documents, leave, time and HR records (without their SIN or bank numbers) for an access request.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Company folders and documents, every person's documents and history, leaving, undo, rehire, data downloads. |
| Centre director | Documents and pay and role history of people at their own centres (pay only when allowed to see pay); reads the leaving pack and the ROE sheet. |
| Staff | See documents shared with them and company documents in **My HR**. |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? Staff can't see a company document. | Check the folder's Folder settings: a new folder is for agency admins only until you choose Everyone on staff or Chosen people.
??? A balance says "Not paid out". | Only leave types marked Paid out when someone leaves in Time off > Leave types are valued (by default vacation and the Overtime bank). Others stay as they are.
??? I see Leaving pack but not Start leaving. | Starting and completing a leaving is for agency admins.
??? Someone's bank details changed and they got an email. | KiddieTrac emails the person whenever their bank or e-Transfer details change, showing at most the last 3 digits, so they can tell you if it wasn't them.
??? Can I remove the folder? | Yes, from Folder settings, once it is empty.
