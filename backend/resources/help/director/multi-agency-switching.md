# Managing more than one agency

If you run more than one childcare agency on Kiddietrac, you can switch between them from a single sign-in without logging out and back in.

## The agency switcher

Look for the agency switcher pill at the bottom of the sidebar, just above your name. It shows a building icon and the current agency's name with a small chevron.

Click it to see every agency you have admin access to. The currently active one is highlighted with a checkmark. Pick a different agency and Kiddietrac reloads the dashboard scoped to that tenant — every page (Centres, Families, Children, Billing, etc.) now shows data for the new agency only.

## What stays separate per agency

Each agency keeps its own data fully isolated:

- Centres, rooms, and capacity
- Families, children, and enrollments
- Staff and role assignments
- Invoices and billing
- Compliance reports
- Custom roles and sibling-discount tiers
- White-label branding

Switching agencies does **not** copy or share anything between them. Each agency is a separate tenant; the switcher just changes which one your screens show.

## Adding access to another agency

To get admin access to a second agency, an existing admin of that agency invites you via their User management screen with the role "Agency admin". You'll see the new agency appear in your switcher on next reload.

If you're a **Platform admin**, you automatically have access to every agency on the platform — no invitation needed.

## Tips

- Your role pills (Centre director, Agency admin, etc.) reflect the **currently active** agency. Switching changes which permissions apply.
- Your sign-in session, MFA setup, and chat conversations are tied to YOUR user account, not to any one agency — they follow you across switches.
- If you change a setting (like sibling-discount tiers) while in Kiddietrac, that change applies only to Kiddietrac. Switch to iLearn first to change iLearn's settings.
