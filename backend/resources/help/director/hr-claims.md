# HR: expense and mileage claims

Staff claim back money they spent for work: a receipt for supplies, or the kilometres they drove. A director or agency admin approves the claim, and it is paid on the person's next payslip as a reimbursement. Claims are part of the KiddieTrac HR add-on and live on the **Claims & mileage** tab of **Human Resources > Payroll**.

[[open: #payroll?tab=claims | Open Claims & mileage]]

## Setting up claims (agency admins, once)

1. Open **Human Resources > Payroll** and choose the **Claims & mileage** tab.
2. Press **Claim settings**.
3. Enter the **Mileage rate ($ per km)**. With no rate, staff cannot file mileage claims and the tab says "No mileage rate is set yet".
4. Under **Categories staff choose from (one per line)**, type one category per line (for example Craft supplies, Snacks, Parking).
5. Leave **A receipt photo is required for every expense claim** ticked unless you really accept claims without a receipt.
6. Press **Save settings**. A **Claim settings saved.** toast appears.

> ℹ️ KiddieTrac never sets a mileage rate for you. Use the rate your agency has agreed to pay.

## Filing a claim (staff)

Staff file their own claims from **My HR**, so the people you approve for never need this screen.

1. Open **My HR** and find **Expense and mileage claims**.
2. Press **New claim**. The **New expense or mileage claim** form opens.
3. Under **Type**, choose **Expense (with a receipt)** or **Mileage**.
4. For an expense: choose the **Category**, enter the **Amount before tax** and the tax, describe **What it was for**, and add the **Receipt photo** (on a phone this opens the camera).
5. For mileage: enter **From**, **To** and the **Distance (km)**. The amount is the distance times your agency's rate.
6. Press **Send claim**. The claim shows as **Waiting for approval**.

A claim that is still waiting can be taken back with **Withdraw claim**.

## Approving or declining a claim

1. Open **Human Resources > Payroll > Claims & mileage**.
2. Keep the **To approve** filter on to see only claims waiting for a decision.
3. Press **View claim** on a row. The claim opens with the receipt, the amount and who filed it.
4. Open the receipt to check it matches the amount.
5. Press **Approve**, or press **Decline**.
6. If you decline, type the reason under **Why is it declined? (the person sees this)** and confirm with **Decline claim**.

The person gets a notice either way. An approved claim moves to **Approved: in the next pay**; a declined one shows **Declined** with your reason.

[[show: #payroll?tab=claims @ Claims & mileage > Claim settings | Show me Claim settings]]

## How approved claims are paid

1. An agency admin opens the **Payroll** tab and presses **＋ Generate payroll** for the period.
2. Each person's row in the run lists the approved claims to reimburse, inside their gross pay.
3. When the admin presses **Approve and create payslips**, the claims are written on the payslips and change to **Paid**.

A paid claim cannot be paid a second time. Claims are not added to payslips that arrive from an outside payroll system.

## Checking what was claimed

1. Press **Claims report**.
2. Choose the dates.
3. Switch between **By person**, **By category** and **By month**. Totals count approved and paid claims only.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Approve or decline every claim in the agency, change **Claim settings**, run the payroll that pays them. |
| Centre director | Approve or decline claims of staff at their own centres; read the report for those centres. |
| Educator, home visitor | File, follow and withdraw their own claims in **My HR**. |

Nobody approves their own claim, even an agency admin. Receipts are private files: only the person, their director and the agency admins can open them.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? The Claims & mileage tab is missing. | The tab is part of the KiddieTrac HR add-on. Without it, Payroll shows only its own tab. Ask your agency admin about the add-on.
??? Staff say they cannot choose Mileage. | No mileage rate is set. An agency admin adds one under **Claim settings**.
??? The list is empty. | The **To approve** filter only shows waiting claims. Choose **All** to see every claim, and check the people are at your centres.
??? I cannot approve a claim. | It is your own claim. Another director or an agency admin approves it.
??? The claim says "Add a photo of the receipt." | Receipts are required for expenses. The person adds a photo, or an admin unticks the receipt rule.
??? An approved claim did not appear on the payslip. | It is added only to payslips made with **＋ Generate payroll** after it was approved. Check the run's period and that the person was ticked.
