# HR: expense and mileage claims

Staff claim back money they spent for work, and the claim is paid on their next payslip. This is part of the KiddieTrac HR add-on.

## Filing a claim (staff)

Open **My HR**, find **Expense and mileage claims** and choose **New claim**.

- **Expense:** pick the category, enter the amount before tax and the tax, and add a photo of the receipt. On a phone, the receipt button opens the camera.
- **Mileage:** enter where you drove from and to, and the distance in km. The amount is the distance times your agency's rate per km.

Your claims show with their status: **Waiting for approval**, **Approved: in the next pay**, **Paid** or **Declined** (with the reason). You can withdraw a claim while it is still waiting.

## Approving claims (directors and admins)

Open **HR**, then the **Claims** tab. Directors see the claims of the people at their own centres; agency admins see the whole agency. Nobody approves their own claim.

Open a claim with **View claim**. You can look at the receipt, then choose **Approve** or **Decline**. A decline needs a reason, and the person sees it.

## How approved claims are paid

An approved claim is added to the person's next payslip as a reimbursement line when an admin makes the payroll run (**Payroll**, then **Run payroll**). Each person's row shows the reimbursements to include. Once that payslip is created, the claim shows as **Paid**, and it can't be paid twice.

## Reports and settings

- **Claims report** totals approved and paid claims by person, by category and by month, for the dates you choose.
- **Claim settings** (agency admins) sets the mileage rate per km, the categories staff choose from, and whether every expense needs a receipt. With no rate set, mileage claims are off.

Receipts are private files. Only the person, their director and the agency admins can open them.
