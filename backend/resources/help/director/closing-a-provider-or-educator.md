# Closing a provider, off-boarding an educator

Two guided flows for the same kind of event: somebody stops working with you, and a
number of loose ends stop being anybody's job unless they are dealt with now. Both work
the same way — you are shown a **plan** first, nothing changes until you confirm, and the
office is told what the departure leaves behind.

## Off-boarding an educator or home visitor

**Administration → User management**, open the person, **Off-board**.

### The plan

Before you decide anything, the dialog counts what is attached to them:

- rooms they are assigned to
- **unclosed shifts** — a punch left running
- open tasks
- upcoming scheduled shifts
- documents on file
- **hours worked since their last payslip**, which is the number that is easy to forget
  and hard to fix later

A warning appears in amber for any **room that has no other educator**. Those rooms lose
their only assigned person the moment this completes, and the children in them are still
there tomorrow.

### The decisions

- **Last day.**
- **Hand their rooms to** — another active educator or director at the same centres.
  Candidates are offered, not chosen for you: a handover is a judgement about people.
- **Close open shifts** — ends any punch still running so their hours stop.
- **Move open tasks** to whoever takes over.
- **Deal with upcoming shifts.**
- **Send a goodbye email** to the person themselves, covering their access and final pay.

Press **Check** first. It reports exactly what will happen — rooms handed over, shifts
closed, tasks moved — and changes nothing. Only **Off-board** commits.

### What the office is told

Directors and agency admins receive **"Staff off-boarded — [name]"**, one addressed
recipient and the rest in BCC. It leads with what matters operationally rather than with
the fact of the departure:

- rooms now left with **no other educator**, and how many children are in them
- the ratio effect at the centres involved
- unclosed shifts and unpaid hours still outstanding
- where their tasks and rooms went

## Closing a provider or centre

**Centres**, open the provider, **Close provider**.

### The plan

- how many **children need a destination**, across how many families
- how many **places are free elsewhere** in the agency — and, plainly, if there are not
  enough for everyone
- how many staff accounts are attached to this provider

An empty provider says so, and can simply be archived.

### A decision per family

Every family gets a row and must be given an answer — **transfer** to a named room
elsewhere, or **withdraw**. The flow will not proceed while any family is still
undecided. A closure that half-happens is worse than one that has not started.

You also pick the **last operating day**, and whether to close the staff accounts here and
archive the provider afterwards.

**Check** reports the transfers and withdrawals it would make and changes nothing.

### What the office is told

Directors and agency admins receive **"Provider closed — [name]"**, addressed to one and
BCC to the rest: who closed, from when, which children moved where, which were withdrawn,
who is **still enrolled afterwards**, and the rooms and ratios the agency is left with.

## Notices reach a closed account

Email to somebody whose account has just been closed is **not** suppressed. The goodbye
message, the final-pay note and anything else about the closure itself are exactly the
messages that must still arrive, and they are exempted from the gates that otherwise stop
mail to an inactive account.

## Worth knowing

- **Nothing happens until you confirm.** Both flows have a check step that reports the
  outcome and writes nothing.
- Closing a provider does not delete anything. Records are archived and remain readable —
  see [Archived records](archived-records).
- If the office email did not arrive, check **Settings → Email settings** for the
  agency-wide notifications switch before assuming it failed.
