# HR: letter templates and e-signature

Part of the KiddieTrac HR add-on. Open **HR > Letters**.

## The five letters

Your agency has five templates: **offer letter**, **employment contract**, **contractor agreement**, **warning letter** and **termination letter**. They start with general wording. That wording is not legal advice and does not cover the rules where you work.

## Review by your lawyer first

Until a template is reviewed, the editor shows **Draft – have an employment lawyer review before use**. The note shows only in the editor, never on a letter you send.

A template **cannot be sent** until an agency admin ticks **Reviewed by our lawyer on** and enters the date. If anyone changes the text later, the tick is cleared, so the new wording is reviewed before it goes out.

## Editing a template (agency admins)

Open the template from its ⋮ menu. Change the title and the text. Click a fill-in field such as `{{full_name}}` or `{{pay}}` to add it where your cursor is. When the letter is sent, each field is filled in from:

- the employee file: job title, employment type, centre;
- the person's profile: name, hire date, pay;
- your agency: its name and the date.

A blank line starts a new paragraph.

## Sending a letter

1. Click **Send a letter**.
2. Choose the letter, the person, and who signs for the agency. Only your own directors and admins can sign.
3. Add an effective date and details if the letter uses them.
4. Click **Preview** to see the letter exactly as it will be sent.
   - If a field used inside a sentence is empty (for example, no pay rate on file), the letter can't be sent until you fill it in.
   - A field that sits on a line of its own, such as `{{details}}`, is simply left out when it is empty.
5. Click **Send for signature**.

The person gets a notice and signs the letter in **Forms to sign**. A director or admin then countersigns it there, as with any other form. Directors can send letters only to people at their own centres. **Sent letters** shows each letter and whether it has been signed.
