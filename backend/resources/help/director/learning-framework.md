# Choosing your learning framework

Your learning framework is the curriculum framework your agency plans and reports against. KiddieTrac uses it in three places:

- **Observations**: when an educator uses **Structure with AI**, the observation is linked to your framework's areas.
- **Report cards**: each report card has one narrative for each area of your framework.
- **Framework gaps**: the gaps report shows, for every child, how many observations each area has, so you can see where your programme is light.

[[open: #learning-framework | Open Learning framework]]

## The frameworks you can choose

| Framework | Where it's used | Areas |
|---|---|---|
| How Does Learning Happen? (HDLH) | Ontario | Belonging, Well-being, Engagement, Expression |
| Early Learning for Every Child Today (ELECT) | Ontario continuum | Social, Emotional, Communication/language/literacy, Cognitive, Physical |
| British Columbia Early Learning Framework | British Columbia | 4 areas of early learning |
| Flight | Alberta | Well-being, Play and playfulness, Communication and literacies, Diversity and social responsibility |
| Accueillir la petite enfance | Québec | Physical and motor, Cognitive, Language, Social and emotional |
| Head Start ELOF | United States | 5 central domains |
| Early Years Foundation Stage (EYFS) | England | 3 prime and 4 specific areas |
| HighScope | International | 8 content areas |
| Our own framework | Your agency | 2 to 10 areas you name yourself |

If you haven't chosen one, KiddieTrac uses the framework for your province (HDLH in Ontario) and says so at the top of the screen.

## How to choose or change it

- [ ] Go to **Settings → Learning framework**.
- [ ] Click the framework you use. Each card lists its areas.
- [ ] For your own framework, name it and add 2 to 10 areas, each with a short description of what it covers. The description helps the AI and your team.
- [ ] Press **Save framework**.

> **Note:** Changing framework affects new observations and report cards only. A report card already written keeps the framework it was written in, and still prints with those sections.

??? Do older observations count toward the new framework? | Yes. An observation without framework links counts toward the area its domain matches (for example, a Language & literacy observation counts toward Expression in HDLH, or Communication and language in EYFS).
??? Why does the gaps report show an area as a gap for every child? | Some areas, such as EYFS Literacy or Mathematics, only fill up when observations are linked to them. Observations structured with AI are linked directly; manual ones count by their domain.
