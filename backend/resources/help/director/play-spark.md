# Play Spark

Play Spark gives your rooms a fresh play idea in one tap, pitched so every age in the room can join in, and holds the weekly lesson plan that families see. Directors and admins also use it to draft whole weeks with AI, look through the ideas your team has made, and print a monthly play report for each room.

[[open: #play-spark | Open Play Spark]]

The tabs you see depend on what your agency has switched on: **✨ Right now**, **📅 This week**, **🗓️ Plan the week**, **📚 Library** and **📊 Monthly report**.

## Sparking an idea right now

1. Open **Operations > Play Spark** and press the **✨ Right now** tab.
2. Choose the **Room**. Under it you see how many children are signed in now, this week's theme and the learning areas the room needs more of lately.
3. Under **Ages**, check the age groups that are joining in (**Infants**, **Toddlers**, **Preschool**, **School-age**).
4. Pick **Where** (**🏠 Indoor**, **🌳 Outdoor** or **Either**), the **Time** (10 to 45 min) and **Your involvement** (**Children lead**, **Guided** or **Hands-on**).
5. Check **Materials on hand**. Type a new one in **Add a material…** and press **Add**, or press **📷 Scan** to photograph the materials and add what it spots. Take the photo of materials only, with no people in it; the photo is not kept.
6. Optionally type a **Theme (optional)**, such as "dinosaurs", or tap an activity under **On today's plan · tap to spark it**.
7. Press **✨ Spark an idea**. The idea appears on the right, with a picture of the end result, what you need, how to set up, and steps for each age under **Every age joins in**.

## Running the idea and logging it

1. Read the **🛡️ Safety check** box (it says **⚠️ Safety check - read before you start** when extra care is needed).
2. Tick **I have read the safety check**. The **✅ We did it** button becomes available.
3. After the activity, press **✅ We did it**, tap the children who joined in, and add **A note (optional)**.
4. Press **Log it**. A toast says how many children it was logged for. KiddieTrac adds the activity to each child's daily record, which families see in their feed as usual, and drafts an observation for each child.

Other buttons on the idea: **☆ Save** (adds it to your favourites), **📚 Share to library** (shares it with your agency), **📅 Add to this week** (puts it on a day of the room's weekly plan, this week or next, then press **Add**), **🔄 Another idea** and **🖨️ Print**.

## Planning the week

1. Press the **📅 This week** tab to see the room's weekly lesson plan, day by day.
2. Add or change activities, or press **✨ Draft this week with AI** where it is offered.
3. Choose **Draft** (staff only) or **Published** (families can see it), then press **💾 Save week**.
4. To have AI draft a whole week, press the **🗓️ Plan the week** tab and follow the steps there.

> ℹ️ Families only see a week once it is **Published**. Drafts made from a curriculum unit start as drafts.

## Using the library and the report

1. Press **📚 Library** and choose **My ideas**, **★ Favourites**, **📚 Agency library**, **👥 Team ideas** or **💡 Activity ideas**.
2. Press an idea to open it. In **👥 Team ideas** you can read any idea made at your centres and save a copy to your own ideas.
3. Press **📊 Monthly report**, choose the room and the month, and read the Play Spark activities and how many children joined in. Press **🖨️ Print** to print it.

## Who can do what

- Agency admins, centre directors and platform admins see every tab their agency has switched on, including **🗓️ Plan the week**, **👥 Team ideas** and **💡 Activity ideas**.
- Educators see **✨ Right now**, **📅 This week**, **📚 Library** (their own ideas, favourites and the agency library) and **📊 Monthly report**.
- Parents see **Play at home** instead: what their child played at the centre and how to try it at home.
- **✨ Right now**, the library of ideas and the report need Play Spark in your agency's plan. **✨ Spark an idea**, **📷 Scan** and **🗓️ Plan the week** also need AI switched on for your agency. Without AI, saved ideas, pictures and **✅ We did it** still work.

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? I see "AI is switched off for this agency". | New ideas and material scans are paused. Your saved ideas, their pictures and **✅ We did it** are still in the **📚 Library**. Ask your agency admin about switching AI on.
??? I see "You are not placed in a room yet. Ask your director to add you to one." | Play Spark needs a room. Ask your agency admin to add you to a room.
??? The ✅ We did it button is greyed out. | Tick **I have read the safety check** first.
??? Scan says "That photo has a person in it." | Take a new photo of just the materials.
??? Scan says "No play materials spotted. Try a closer photo." | Move closer so the materials fill the photo, or type them in with **Add**.
??? A tab is missing. | Tabs only show when your agency has the matching feature, and some are for directors and admins only (see **Who can do what**).
??? I see "Nothing here is switched on for your agency." | None of Play Spark's features are on for your agency. Ask your agency admin.
