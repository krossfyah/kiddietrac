# The agency calendar

**Calendar** is the calendar for the whole agency. Staff shifts, closures, holidays, meetings, family vacations, field trips, conferences, child absences, staff time off and birthdays all land on the same grid, so you can see in one place who is working and what is happening. Open it from the sidebar: **Operations > Calendar**.

[[open: #staff-calendar | Open Calendar]]

## Moving around the calendar

1. Pick a view with **Day**, **Week** or **Month**. Day is an agenda for one day; Week runs Monday to Sunday; Month shows the whole month with "+ N more" when a day is busy. Your choice is remembered (phones start on Day).
2. Use **◀** and **▶** to go back or forward one day, week or month. The title in the middle shows where you are.
3. Press **Today** to jump back to today. Today is tinted and its date sits in a filled circle.
4. Narrow what you see with the filters: the centre list (**All centres — every staff member** or one centre, shown when you have more than one), **All roles** (Lead, Support, Floater, Volunteer) and **All staff** to see one person's shifts.

Shift colours follow the role: Lead purple, Support blue, Floater amber, Volunteer green.

## Adding a shift

1. Press **+ Add ▾** and choose **Shift**, or click a day and press **＋ Add shift** (in Day view, **+ Add a shift**).
2. In **New shift**, pick the **Staff member** and the **Room**.
3. Set the **Date**, **Start**, **End** and **Role**.
4. Press **Create shift**. The shift appears on the grid.

To add the same shift on several days, drag across those days in the Week or Month grid. The form then says "📅 Creating N shifts…" and one shift is made for each day.

## Changing or deleting a shift

1. Click the shift on the grid. **Edit shift** opens.
2. Change what you need and press **Save changes**.
3. To remove it, press **Delete** and confirm "Delete this shift?".

A shift that runs past midnight belongs to the day it starts.

## Adding other things to a day

Press **+ Add ▾** to open **Add to the calendar**. It lists only what your role can reach:

- **Meeting**: invite staff, families or anyone by email.
- **Closure**, **Vacation hold**, **Field trip** and **Conference slots**: open that screen's own form, with the date you were looking at already filled in.
- **Child start date**: takes you to the child's record, where the start date lives.
- **Holiday**: closes several providers at once (see below).

## Adding a holiday for several providers

1. Press **+ Add ▾ > Holiday**.
2. Set the **Date** and, for more than one day, **Until (optional)**.
3. Under **Who is off**, tick the staff and providers who close, or press **Select everyone**. Use the search to find someone.
4. Tick **Tell families and staff now** to send the usual closure notice, and **Pause billing for these days** if families should not be charged.
5. Press **Add holiday**. A toast confirms it, for example "Holiday added at 3 centres".

## Opening, editing or deleting a closure

1. Click any event on the grid. It opens with its dates, centre, reason, billing and who added it.
2. For a closure, press **Edit**. In **✏️ Edit closure**, change the **Type**, **First day closed**, **Last day closed (leave empty for a single day)**, **Reason** or **Pause billing on these days**, then press **Save changes**. "Closure updated · Everyone affected has been told it changed."
3. Or press **Delete** and confirm. Everyone who was told about the closure is told it has been removed.

Other events (time off, vacation holds, absences, birthdays) show an **Open …** button to the screen that owns them.

## Who can do what

- **Agency admin**: every centre, all filters and every **+ Add ▾** item their plan includes.
- **Centre director**: their own centres; **+ Add ▾** lists only the screens a director has.
- **Platform admin**: the agency they are viewing.
- **Educators, parents and auditors** do not see this screen.

What the calendar shows besides shifts (closures and holidays, birthdays, child absences, family vacations, staff time off, calendar sync) is chosen by an agency admin in **Settings > Calendar settings**.

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? Saving a shift says "Pick a staff member and a room". | Both are required. Choose them in **Staff member** and **Room**.
??? The Room list says "No rooms in this centre — add one in Centre setup first." | Add a room to that centre first, then come back.
??? An item is missing from + Add ▾. | Your role cannot open that screen (for example Conferences), so the shortcut is not offered.
??? The closure edit says "The last day cannot be before the first day." | Fix the dates so the last day is the same as or after the first day.
??? The calendar says "Could not load: …". | Reload the page. If it continues, check your connection or contact your agency admin.
??? Do families hear about a closure I change here? | Yes. Editing or deleting a closure tells the families and staff it affects.
