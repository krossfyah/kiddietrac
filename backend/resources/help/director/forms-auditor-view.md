# Custom forms for auditors

**Menu → Forms** shows the agency's custom forms: the online registration, consent, survey
and feedback forms the agency built for families and staff, and the answers that came back.
As an auditor you can look and download. You cannot change anything.

[[open: #forms | Open Forms]]

> ℹ️ Signed PDF forms (waivers, consent forms, staff paperwork) are not here. They are in **Forms Manager**, on its **✅ Completed** tab.

## Finding a form

1. Open **Forms** from your menu.
2. Type part of the title in **🔍 Search forms…** to narrow the list.
3. Each row shows the form's title, how many questions it has, its audience and when it was last changed.
4. The label on the right gives its status: **DRAFT** (not shown to anyone yet), **LIVE** (open to its audience) or **ARCHIVED** (hidden, kept for records).
5. Use the page buttons at the bottom when there are more than eight forms.

## Reading the answers to a form

1. Press the **responses** pill on the form's row, for example **12 responses**.
2. Each answer card shows who sent it and when (in the agency's time), then each answer beside its question.
3. Signatures show as a picture. A payment shows the amount and **authorised**.
4. Press **⤓ Download CSV** to save every response as a spreadsheet, one column per question, for your working papers.
5. Press **×** to close.

## Seeing the form as families saw it

1. Press the form's **⋮** button.
2. Choose **👁 Preview**. The form opens exactly as a respondent sees it, with the agency's logo and colours.
3. Press **×** to close the preview. Nothing is submitted from a preview.

## What you can and cannot do

| You can | You cannot |
|---|---|
| List and search forms | Create, edit or delete a form |
| Read every response and download the CSV | Change a form's status or audience |
| Preview a form | Email a form to parents |

The builder's buttons (**+ New form**, **⋮ > Edit form**, **⋮ > Email to parents**,
**⋮ > Delete form**) may still show, but the server refuses them for an auditor account with
"Auditor accounts are read-only." Nothing is changed and nothing is sent.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? The screen says "Could not load". | Your auditor account may not have been given the custom forms list yet. Ask the agency admin who invited you for the CSV of the responses you need (they get it from Custom forms, ⋮ > View responses, ⤓ Download CSV), and tell them the Forms screen did not load.
??? The list says "No forms yet." | The agency has not built any custom forms. Its paperwork may all be PDFs: check Forms Manager.
??? I pressed a button and got "Auditor accounts are read-only." | That button changes data, which auditor accounts are never allowed to do. Nothing was changed.
??? Are the times in my time zone? | Times are shown in the agency's time zone, the same zone the agency's records use.
??? Can I see who opened a form but did not answer? | No. Only submitted answers are kept.
??? Is my activity recorded? | Yes. Like every account, what you open and download is recorded in the agency's audit log.
