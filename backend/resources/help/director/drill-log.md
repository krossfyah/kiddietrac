# Drill log

Record every fire, severe-weather, lockdown, vehicle and shelter-in-place drill, and see at a glance which drills are still owed under your province's or state's rules.

## Where it is

Sidebar → **Drill log** (beside Incidents). Admins see every centre; directors and educators see their own centres.

## What it checks

The rules come from your agency's **province / state** (Agency overview → Edit agency → Country & compliance):

| Province / state | Drills owed |
| --- | --- |
| Ontario, Alberta, British Columbia, Nova Scotia | Fire drill every month |
| Ohio | Fire drill every month; severe-weather drill every month March–September; lockdown drill every quarter; vehicle evacuation drill every month if you transport children |
| Texas | Fire drill every month; severe-weather and lockdown drills 4 times a year |
| New York | Evacuation drill every month; shelter-in-place drill twice a year |

**Due and missed** shows, for each centre and each drill: *done* for this period, *due* this period, or *missed* last period.

## Recording a drill

1. Choose the centre (and the room, if the drill was for one room only), the type and when it happened.
2. Add the evacuation time, how many children were present, which staff took part, and anything to fix (a blocked exit, a slow room).
3. Press **Record drill**.

Drills can't be dated in the future, and a recorded drill can't be edited. Check the details before you press **Record drill**.

## Removing a drill

Drill records are kept for **12 months**. They are your evidence for inspections.

- The person who recorded a drill, a director or an admin can remove it **within 24 hours of entering it**. Use this to undo a mistake, such as the wrong centre or date, then record it again correctly.
- After 24 hours, the drill stays in the log until it is a year old. If you try to remove it, the portal tells you the date from which it can be removed.

Every recording and removal goes in the audit log.

??? I entered a drill with the wrong date yesterday. Can I fix it? | Only within 24 hours of entering it: remove it and record it again. After that the record stays for 12 months.

## Reminders

Admins can switch on an email to directors on weekday mornings when a drill is still owed or was missed. It is off unless you turn it on.

## Ontario: missing-child drills and the drill schedule

From **January 1, 2027** Ontario centres must run a **missing-child drill at least every 3 months** (O. Reg. 137/15 s.68.2) and keep the records for at least 12 months. Fire drills stay monthly (s.68).

- **Missing child** is a drill type for Ontario agencies. Choosing it adds: the room(s) or area, the scenario, who led it, how long until the child was located (seconds), whether a 911 call and a parent call were simulated, and what to improve.
- The **Drill schedule** card replaces *Due and missed* for Ontario. For each centre it shows the last missing-child drill and when the next is due (3 months later), and the same for fire drills — **On track**, **Due**, **Due soon** or **Overdue**.
- A school-based program can switch a centre's fire drills from **Monthly** to **3 per school term** (directors for their own centre, or an admin).
- Drill records are never deleted by a clean-up job. Removing one from the log hides it but keeps it on file.

**Schedule reminders** (Ontario, on by default): each morning, a centre's directors get an in-app notice and an email when its missing-child drill is due within 14 days or overdue, or its fire drill is overdue. Agency admins also hear about anything overdue. Each change is sent once, not every day. An admin can switch them off at the bottom of the Drill log.
