# Drill log

Record every fire, severe-weather, lockdown, vehicle, shelter-in-place and missing-child drill, and see at a glance which drills are still owed under your province's or state's rules. Open it from the sidebar: **Operations** → **Drill log**.

[[open: #drills | Open Drill log]]

The screen has four tabs: **🚒 Drills** (what is owed and what was done), **🗺️ Fire plan** (the evacuation plan for each centre or room), **👩‍🏫 Educators** (each person's fire drill training, schedule and drill map) and **⚙️ Settings**.

## What the Drills tab checks

The rules come from your agency's **province / state** (Agency overview → Edit agency → Country & compliance):

| Province / state | Drills owed |
| --- | --- |
| Ontario | Missing-child drill at least every 3 months (from January 1, 2027); fire drill every month |
| Alberta, British Columbia, Nova Scotia | Fire drill every month |
| Ohio | Fire drill every month; severe-weather drill every month March–September; lockdown drill every quarter; vehicle evacuation drill every month if you transport children |
| Texas | Fire drill every month; severe-weather and lockdown drills 4 times a year |
| New York | Evacuation drill every month; shelter-in-place drill twice a year |

Each centre is a card. Each drill it owes has a row with the last one recorded, when the next is due and a status: **Done**, **Due**, **Not due**, **Missed** — or, for Ontario, **On track**, **Due soon**, **Due** or **Overdue**. Under each row are the drills recorded in the month you picked. Use the month box and **All my centres** at the top to change what you see. A chip beside the centre's name shows its fire plan, for example **🗺️ Fire plan not reviewed in 12 months**; tap it to open the **🗺️ Fire plan** tab.

## Recording a drill

1. Press **＋ Record drill** at the top, or **＋ Record** on the drill's own row (the centre and type are then filled in). The **🚒 Record a drill** window opens.
2. Choose the **Centre**, the **Room (optional)** — leave **Whole centre** for an all-centre drill — and the **Type**.
3. Check **When**. It starts at the time now, in your agency's time zone.
4. Enter the **Evacuation time (seconds)**, the **Children present** (for a fire drill: **Children evacuated**), the **Staff present** and any **Notes**, such as the exit used.
5. For a fire drill, choose the **Plan used**, the **Route used** (**Primary route**, **Alternate route** or **Other**), an optional **Route note**, the **Attendance (children here today)** and any **Issues found**. **✏️ Draw or update the plan** opens the fire plan editor without losing the form.
6. For a missing-child drill, fill in the **Room(s) / area**, **Led by**, **Time until child located (seconds)**, the **Scenario**, **What to improve**, and tick **911 call simulated** and **Parent call simulated** if you did.
7. Press **Record drill**. A **Drill recorded** message appears and the drill shows under its row.

Drills can't be dated in the future, and a recorded drill can't be edited. Check the details before you press **Record drill**.

## Viewing or removing a drill

1. Tap a recorded drill, or press its **⋮** and choose **👁 View details**, to see everything recorded for it. From there you can open the **🗺️ Fire plan** it followed or the **🗺️ Drill map**.
2. To remove a drill entered by mistake, press **⋮** > **🗑 Remove drill** (or **🗑 Remove** in the details) and confirm. A **Drill removed** message appears.

Drill records are kept for **12 months** as your evidence for inspections. The person who recorded a drill, a director or an admin can remove it **within 24 hours of entering it**. After that, the menu shows **🔒 Kept until** a date a year after the drill. Every recording and removal goes in the audit log, and a removed drill is kept on file.

## Marking a drill not required at a centre

1. On the drill's row, press **⋮** > **🚫 Not required here…**.
2. Type **Why is this drill not required here?** — for example "No children in care during evening hours".
3. Press **Mark not required**. The row turns grey and its reminders and missed-drill notices stop.
4. To undo it, press **⋮** > **✅ Make required again** and **Make required**.

## Educators: training, schedule and drill map

On the **👩‍🏫 Educators** tab, directors and admins set, for each person:

1. **📅 Set training dates** — **Renewed on**, **Expires on** and a **Note (optional)**, then **Save dates**. The **Status** column shows when training is **Current**, **Expires soon** or **Expired**.
2. **🔥 Set fire drill schedule** — **Repeats** (weekly, monthly, every 2 or 3 months, once, or **No schedule**), then the **Week of the month**, **Day of the week**, date and **Time**. Press **Save schedule**.
3. **⬆️ Add a map** (or **🔄 Replace map**, **🗑 Remove map**) — a picture or PDF up to 15 MB. **🗺️ View map** opens it.

Educators see their own row only.

## Settings

On **⚙️ Settings**, Ontario centres choose their **🔥 Centre fire drill rule**: **Monthly**, or 3 per school term for a school-based program. Agency admins also choose the **🔔 Notifications** — who hears about drills, whether each educator is reminded before their scheduled fire drill, whether directors and admins are told on the morning of each scheduled drill and when one was not recorded, and the weekday-morning email about drills still owed or missed. Ontario agencies also have the schedule reminders (on by default). Each switch saves as soon as you change it.

## Who can do what

- **Agency admins** see every centre, record and remove drills, mark drills not required, set each educator's training, schedule and map, and change every setting.
- **Centre directors** do the same for their own centres, and set their centres' fire drill rule. They do not see the **🔔 Notifications** settings.
- **Educators** see their own centres, record drills, and see their own training dates, schedule and map.
- Parents do not see the Drill log.

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? I entered a drill with the wrong date yesterday. Can I fix it? | Only within 24 hours of entering it: remove it and record it again. After that the record stays for 12 months.
??? The Remove option is greyed out and says "Kept until". | The 24 hours have passed. Drill records are evidence and are kept for 12 months.
??? A centre shows "No drill rules for this jurisdiction yet." | Your agency's province or state has no drill rules in KiddieTrac yet. Check Country & compliance on the agency, and you can still record drills.
??? The Plan used list says "Fire plans could not be loaded". | Close the window and try again. If it keeps happening, record the drill without a plan and raise a ticket.
??? It says "The map must be 15 MB or smaller." | Make the file smaller, for example a photo instead of a large scan, and add it again.
??? Who gets the drill emails? | Only the people chosen under ⚙️ Settings → Notifications. Reminders are off unless an admin turns them on, except Ontario schedule reminders, which are on by default. Each change is sent once, not every day.
