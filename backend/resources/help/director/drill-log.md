# Drill log

Record every fire, severe-weather, lockdown, vehicle and shelter-in-place drill, and see at a glance which drills are still owed under your province's or state's rules.

## Where it is

Sidebar → **Drill log** (beside Incidents). Admins see every centre; directors and educators see their own centres.

## What it checks

The rules come from your agency's **province / state** (Agency overview → Edit agency → Country & compliance):

| Province / state | Drills owed |
| --- | --- |
| Ontario, Alberta, British Columbia, Nova Scotia | Fire drill every month |
| Ohio | Fire drill every month; severe-weather drill every month March–September; lockdown drill every quarter; vehicle evacuation drill every month if you transport children |
| Texas | Fire drill every month; severe-weather and lockdown drills 4 times a year |
| New York | Evacuation drill every month; shelter-in-place drill twice a year |

**Due and missed** shows, for each centre and each drill: *done* for this period, *due* this period, or *missed* last period.

## Recording a drill

1. Choose the centre (and the room, if the drill was for one room only), the type and when it happened.
2. Add the evacuation time, how many children were present, which staff took part, and anything to fix (a blocked exit, a slow room).
3. Press **Record drill**.

Drills can't be dated in the future. The person who recorded a drill, a director or an admin can remove it. Every recording and removal goes in the audit log.

## Reminders

Admins can switch on an email to directors on weekday mornings when a drill is still owed or was missed. It is off unless you turn it on.
