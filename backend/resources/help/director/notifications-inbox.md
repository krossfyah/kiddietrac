# Notifications inbox and the bell

The 🔔 in the top bar and the **Notifications** inbox are where KiddieTrac tells you what happened: your own safety
alerts, messages, announcements, forms, invoices and payments, and — for agency admins and directors — what your
team did and submitted today. Open the inbox to catch up, find something you dismissed, or clear old notices.

[[open: #notifications | Open the inbox]]

## Checking the bell

For agency admins and centre directors, the 🔔 on a computer opens **one list, newest first**, mixing three kinds of
item by the time they happened:

- **Your own safety alerts**: Safe Arrival, ratio, late pickup, children still signed in, fever, lockdown, fire,
  hazards, weather, missed headcounts and similar. Each has its own icon and a small amber **Alert** chip; an unread
  alert has a light orange background.
- **Agency activity**: check-ins, clock-ins, new families, sign-ins, inspections, home visits, new staff, time off
  and incidents.
- **What your team submits** (see below), each with the person's first name.

1. Press the 🔔 in the top bar. New items since you last looked have a light blue background.
2. Click a row to open its record or screen. An alert is marked read when you open it.
3. When your own inbox has unread notifications, press the **Your inbox · N new** line at the bottom to open it.
4. Press **🔊 Sounds** at the top to choose your notification sounds.

Nothing is pinned to the top, so an alert from this morning sits below everything that happened since. Directors see
only their own centres; agency admins see the whole agency they are working in. On a phone, the 🔔 opens the inbox.

## Reading the inbox

1. Open **Notifications** from the menu (**Operations › Notifications**).
2. Use **All**, **Unread** and **Read** above the list to choose what you see.
3. Click a row. It is marked read on the way to whatever it points at — the chat thread, the invoice, the form.
4. Under a group of the same notice, press **＋ … earlier like this** to see the older ones.
5. Use the page buttons under the list: it shows 12 at a time.

A **blue dot** and a light blue background mean unread. Each kind has its own icon: 💬 chat, 🧾 invoice, ✅ payment,
📢 announcement, 📅 digest, 📣 marketing, 🚪 tour, 📝 form.

## Marking read or unread

1. Open the **⋮** menu on a row.
2. Choose **📖 Mark read** or **📩 Mark unread**.
3. To mark everything read, press **✓ Read all** and confirm.

Mark something **unread** if you opened it by accident, so it stays in the unread list and the bell count.

## Deleting notifications

1. For one row, open its **⋮** menu and choose **Delete notification**, then confirm. A deleted notification cannot be
   brought back.
2. For several, press **☑️ Select**, tick the rows (or **Select all**), press **🗑 Delete** and confirm.
3. Press **Done** to leave select mode.

Deleting only removes the notice. The message, invoice or form it pointed to stays.

## What your team submits

For agency admins and directors, the bell and the inbox also list what staff send in:

- Forms and checklists signed or filled in by staff; policies acknowledged; certificates uploaded
- Fire drills, and fire plans sent for review; hazards reported
- Medication doses given; field trips; shared notes; report cards sent for approval
- HR: onboarding tasks done, courses completed, self reviews, injury and near-miss reports, expense and mileage
  claims, and shift swaps waiting for your approval

You never see your own submissions. Only open centres, and children still enrolled, are listed. These rows only open
their record: they cannot be marked read or deleted. They never ring your phone or send email.

To hide them:

1. Open **My settings** and the **Notifications** tab.
2. In **🔔 Notify me about**, switch off **Staff submissions**.

## Who sees what

| Role | Sees |
|---|---|
| **Agency admin** | Own notifications and alerts, plus activity and staff submissions for the whole agency |
| **Centre director** | Own notifications and alerts, plus activity and submissions for their own centres only |
| **Auditor** | Only the notifications sent to them. No agency activity feed or staff submissions |
| **Platform admin** | Own notifications, and the agency feed for the agency being viewed |

## Questions and problems

??? Why does a Safe Arrival alert no longer stay at the top of my bell? | The bell is one list in time order. Alerts keep their amber Alert chip and orange background until you open them, but newer items sit above them.
??? A director says they cannot see a drill from another centre. | Directors see only their own centres in the bell and the inbox. Agency admins see the whole agency.
??? Can I stop staff submissions ringing my phone? | They never ring your phone. They are only listed. To hide them, switch off Staff submissions in My settings › Notifications › 🔔 Notify me about.
??? "Could not load your notifications. Check your connection and try again." | The connection dropped. Refresh the page.
??? Does a chat notification show the message? | No. For privacy, chat notices and their push and email copies say who wrote, not what they wrote. Open it to read the message.
??? I deleted a notification by mistake. | It cannot be brought back, but the thing it pointed to is still there: open the screen it was about.
