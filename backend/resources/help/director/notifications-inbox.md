# Notifications inbox and the bell

The 🔔 in the top bar and the **Notifications** inbox are where KiddieTrac tells you what happened.

[[open: #notifications | Open the inbox]]

## The bell for agency admins and directors

For agency admins and centre directors, the 🔔 on a computer opens **one list, newest first**. It mixes three kinds of item by the time they happened. Nothing is pinned to the top, so an alert from this morning sits below everything that happened since.

- **Your own safety alerts**: Safe Arrival, ratio, late pickup, children still signed in, fever, lockdown, fire, hazards, weather, missed headcounts and similar. Each one has its own icon and a small amber **Alert** chip. An unread alert has a light orange background. Click it to mark it read and open its screen.
- **Agency activity**: check-ins, clock-ins, new families, sign-ins, inspections, home visits, new staff, time off and incidents.
- **What your team submits**: see the next section.

New items since you last opened the bell have a light blue background. When your own inbox has unread notifications, a **Your inbox · N new** line at the bottom opens it. **🔊 Sounds** at the top opens your sound settings.

Directors see only their own centres. Agency admins see the whole agency they are working in.

## What your team submits

The bell also lists what staff send in, each with the person's first name. Click a row to open the record or its screen.

- Forms and checklists signed or filled in by staff
- Fire drills, and fire plans sent for review
- Hazards reported
- Medication doses given
- Field trips
- Shared notes
- Report cards sent for approval
- Policies acknowledged
- Certificates uploaded
- HR: onboarding tasks done, courses completed, self reviews, injury and near-miss reports, expense and mileage claims, and shift swaps waiting for your approval

You never see your own submissions. Directors see only the people and records of their own centres. Only open centres, and children still enrolled, are listed.

These items are a list only. They do not ring your phone and do not send email.

To turn them off, go to **Settings → Notifications → 🔔 Notify me about** and switch off **Staff submissions**.

## The inbox

The **Notifications** inbox lists every notification sent to you, newest first. On a phone, the 🔔 opens it.

For agency admins and directors, the inbox also lists what your team submits, mixed in by time, the same as the bell on a computer. These rows only open their record. They cannot be marked read, marked unread or deleted, and the **Staff submissions** switch hides them here too.

### Filter

Three choices at the top: **All**, **Unread only** and **Read only**.

### Marking read, and unread

- **Click any row**: it is marked read on the way to whatever it points at.
- **The ⋮ menu on the row**: **Mark read** or **Mark unread**, plus **Delete**.
- **✓ Read all**: asks first, then marks every unread notification read.

Mark something **unread** if you opened it by accident, so it stays in the unread list and the bell count.

### Deleting

Use **Delete** in the row's ⋮ menu. It asks first, because a deleted notification cannot be brought back. To clear several at once, press **☑️ Select** and tick the rows.

### What you see

- A **blue dot** and a light blue background mean unread.
- Each kind has its own icon: 💬 chat, 🧾 invoice, ✅ payment, 📢 announcement, 📅 digest, 📣 marketing, 🚪 tour, 📝 form.
