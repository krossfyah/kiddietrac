# Message Centre — who educators can message

You decide who **educators** and **home visitors** can message in the Message Centre.
Directors and admins can always message everyone, and parents can always reply to the
people messaging them.

**Where:** Settings → Notifications → **Message Centre — who staff can message**
(agency admins and super admins).

## The settings

Educators and home visitors each have their own:

| Setting | Choices | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Families they can message | Families in their rooms · All families at their centre | Families in their rooms |
| Staff they can message | Agency admins · Directors · Super admin · Other educators · Home visitors | Admins, directors and super admin |

"Families in their rooms" means the families of children in the rooms they are assigned
to. Someone who has not been put in any room yet falls back to their centre's families.

**Conversations these rules no longer allow:** *Keep as read-only history* (the default)
or *Hide them*. A read-only conversation can still be read, but nothing new can be sent
in it; the message box is replaced by a line explaining why. A group conversation that
includes a director or an admin stays open.

Changes apply straight away and are recorded in the audit log.
