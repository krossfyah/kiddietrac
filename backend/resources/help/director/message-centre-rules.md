# Message Centre — messaging families and staff

The **💬 Message Centre** is KiddieTrac's private chat. Directors and agency admins use it to message one family,
one colleague or a group of colleagues, to send one message to a whole audience, and to read replies. Every message is
kept with the family's or staff member's record, so nothing is lost in personal phones. Open it from
**Operations › Message Centre**.

[[open: #chat | Open the Message Centre]]

## Reading your conversations

1. Open the **Message Centre**. The **💬 Inbox** tab lists your conversations, newest first, with a count of unread
   messages.
2. Type in **🔍 Filter…** to find a family, colleague or group by name.
3. Click a conversation to open it. The thread shows each message with its time and, for your own messages, where it
   is: **Sent — not read yet**, **Delivered**, **Read**, **Opened by the family** or **Replied**.
4. Inside a thread, use **🔍 Search messages…** to find a word.

If your agency texts families or runs the website chat, a **Texts** and a website tab appear next to **💬 Inbox**.

## Starting a conversation

1. Press **+ New chat**. The **✉️ New message** window opens.
2. Under **To**, pick a family or a colleague. Each person shows their role (Admin, Director, Educator, Parent …).
3. Optionally fill **Subject (optional)**, for example "Pickup change today".
4. Write your **Message**. Add an **Emoji**, a **Photo** or a **Voice note** if you like.
5. Press **Send message**. The conversation opens and appears at the top of your inbox.

To talk with several colleagues at once, press **New group** at the top of the window, give the group a name (for
example "Toddler Room Team"), tick the people, and press **Create group**.

> 💡 In a thread, the **＋** beside the message box adds a photo, a file, a voice note or your location, starts a
> **Call** inside KiddieTrac, or sends a **👋 Nudge** that rings the other person's phone when something cannot wait.

## Messaging a whole audience

1. Press **📣 Message everyone**.
2. Under **Send to**, pick **All parents**, **All educators**, **All staff**, **All contractors**, **All admins and
   directors** or **Everyone**.
3. Add a **Subject (optional)** and write the **Message**.
4. Press **Check who this reaches** to see the list of people before sending.
5. Press **Send**.

Each person gets the message in their own thread, so anyone who replies is replying to you privately, not to the
whole group. For news that everyone should see in one place, use **Announcements** instead.

## Tidying the inbox

1. Open the **⋮** menu on a conversation row.
2. Choose **Archive** to move it to **📥 Archived**. Open **📥 Archived** and choose **Restore** to bring it back.
3. In a group you no longer need, choose **Leave group**.
4. To remove a conversation for everyone, open it and choose **Delete**. You are asked first, and it cannot be undone.

## Choosing who educators and home visitors can message

Agency admins decide who **educators** and **home visitors** can message. Directors and admins can always message
everyone, and parents can always reply to the people messaging them.

1. Open **Settings › Agency notifications** and the **💬 Message Centre** tab.
2. For **Educators** and for **Home visitors**, choose **Families they can message**: **Families in their rooms** (the
   default) or **All families at their centre**.
3. Tick the staff they can message: **Agency admins**, **Directors**, **Super admin**, **Other educators**, **Home
   visitors**. The default is admins, directors and super admin.
4. Under **Conversations these rules no longer allow**, choose **Keep as read-only history** (the default) or **Hide
   them**.
5. Set the **Nudges** and **Calls** switches (**Families can nudge staff**, **Time between nudges**, **Ring through Do
   Not Disturb**, **Families can call staff**).
6. Press **Save**. Changes apply straight away and are recorded in the audit log.

"Families in their rooms" means the families of children in the rooms the person is assigned to. Someone not yet put
in any room falls back to their centre's families. A read-only conversation can still be read, but nothing new can be
sent in it: the message box is replaced by "This conversation is read-only." A group that includes a director or an
admin stays open.

## Privacy and encryption

- Message text is **sealed (encrypted) when it is stored**. Only people in the conversation can read it in
  KiddieTrac.
- **Search still works**: KiddieTrac unseals messages to match your search, for conversations you are allowed to see.
- **Push notifications and missed-message emails say who wrote, not what they wrote** ("New message from …"), so
  nothing private shows on a lock screen or in an inbox.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| **Agency admin** | Message anyone in the agency, use **📣 Message everyone**, set the Message Centre rules |
| **Centre director** | Message families and staff of their own centres, use **📣 Message everyone** for their centres |
| **Educator**, **home visitor** | Message the families and colleagues the rules allow, one thread at a time |
| **Parent** | Message and reply to their centre's staff |

## Questions and problems

??? An educator says they can't find a family in New message. | The rules limit them to families in their rooms (or their centre). Check their room assignment, or widen Families they can message in the Message Centre rules.
??? A conversation says "This conversation is read-only." | The rules changed and no longer allow it. It stays as history. Change the rules, or start a new conversation that is allowed.
??? Why doesn't the push say what the parent wrote? | For privacy, chat notices say who wrote, not what. Open the Message Centre to read it.
??? "Could not send. Please try again." | The connection dropped. Your text is still in the box; press send again.
??? I don't see 📣 Message everyone. | It is for directors and agency admins only.
??? Do families get an email? | They get an app notification. If they miss it, they may get an email saying they have a new message, without the message text. When you pick a family, New message shows whether they Can be emailed or Can be texted.
