# One-Tap (QR check-in)

**One-Tap** is KiddieTrac's QR contactless check-in and check-out. Print a One-Tap
poster for a centre door: families scan it with their phone camera to sign children
in or out, with no tablet required.

## Turning it on

1. Sidebar → **Settings → Centres** (or *Manage this centre* from Agency overview).
2. Open a centre and find **Kiosk mode**.
3. Tick **Enable kiosk for this centre**. A kiosk URL and a **One-Tap (QR check-in)**
   card appear.

## Printing the poster

1. In the One-Tap card, click **🖨️ Print One-Tap poster**.
2. A print-ready poster opens (centre name, One-Tap QR code, and the 3 steps
   families follow). Print it and post it at your entrance.

The One-Tap QR code is generated **in your browser** — the kiosk link is never sent to
any third party. If you rotate the kiosk token, reprint the poster.

## How families use it

1. Scan the code with the phone camera.
2. Tap the child's name.
3. Enter the 4–6 digit kiosk PIN to sign in or out. (Set per-guardian PINs from
   the family detail.)

> Who can set this up: **Centre director**, **Agency admin**.
