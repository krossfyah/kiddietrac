# QR code check-in

Print a scannable QR poster for a centre door. Families scan it with their phone
camera to sign children in or out — no tablet required.

## Turning it on

1. Sidebar → **Settings → Centres** (or *Manage this centre* from Agency overview).
2. Open a centre and find **Kiosk mode**.
3. Tick **Enable kiosk for this centre**. A kiosk URL and a **QR check-in** card
   appear.

## Printing the poster

1. In the QR check-in card, click **🖨️ Print QR poster**.
2. A print-ready poster opens (centre name, QR code, and the 3 steps families
   follow). Print it and post it at your entrance.

The QR code is generated **in your browser** — the kiosk link is never sent to
any third party. If you rotate the kiosk token, reprint the poster.

## How families use it

1. Scan the code with the phone camera.
2. Tap the child's name.
3. Enter the 4–6 digit kiosk PIN to sign in or out. (Set per-guardian PINs from
   the family detail.)

> Who can set this up: **Centre director**, **Agency admin**.
