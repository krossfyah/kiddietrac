# Support desk (reseller)

The **Support desk** brings every support ticket raised in your agencies into one list. Tickets about an agency's own matters are yours to answer. App problems, questions and ideas go to the KiddieTrac platform team: you can follow their status here, and you can hand any ticket on to the platform team when you need their help.

[[open: #reseller-support | Open Support desk]]

## Finding a ticket

1. Open **Support desk** from the **Reseller** section of the menu.
2. Pick which tickets to show in the first box: **Open**, **Resolved & closed** or **All**. It opens on **Open**.
3. To see one agency only, pick it in the second box (it starts on **All agencies**).
4. Read the table: **#**, **Subject** (with who raised it), **Agency**, **Status**, **Priority** and **Updated**. A ticket marked **With the platform team** belongs to KiddieTrac; one marked **Escalated** has been handed on.

## Answering a ticket

1. Click the ticket's row. A popup opens with the original message and every reply so far.
2. Type your answer in **Write a reply…**.
3. Press **Send reply**. "Reply sent." appears under the buttons, the ticket moves to **Waiting on them**, and the person who raised it is notified.
4. To change the status, pick **Open**, **Waiting on them**, **Resolved** or **Closed** in the status box.
5. Press **Update status**. "Status updated." appears.
6. Close the popup with **✕** (or press Esc). The list refreshes.

> 💡 Use **Waiting on them** when you have asked the agency a question, and **Resolved** once it is fixed, so your **Open** list stays short.

## Handing a ticket to the platform team

1. Open the ticket.
2. Press **Escalate to the platform team**.
3. In **What should they know? (optional)**, say what you have already tried.
4. Press **Escalate**. "Sent to the platform team. It stays open here until it is resolved." appears, and the ticket is marked **Escalated**.

The KiddieTrac team is emailed and alerted straight away.

## Tickets that are with the platform team

Click one to see its status only: "This ticket is with the platform team." with **Status** and when it was resolved. You cannot reply to or change these; the platform team answers the person who raised it directly.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Reseller | Sees tickets from their own agencies; answers agency tickets, changes their status, escalates. |
| Platform admin | Sees the same desk for any reseller (**Viewing reseller** on **Reseller overview**); answers platform tickets in **Support tickets**. |
| Agency admins and staff | Raise and follow their tickets under **Support tickets** in their own menu. |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? The list says "No tickets here." | Nothing matches the boxes at the top. Pick **All** and **All agencies** to see every ticket.
??? I see "You do not manage a reseller." | Your account has no active reseller role. Raise a ticket with KiddieTrac ([[raise-a-ticket]]).
??? There is no reply box on a ticket. | It is with the platform team, so you see its status only.
??? "Could not open the ticket." appears. | The ticket may have moved to another agency or your session ended. Refresh the page and try again.
??? Who is told when I reply? | The person who raised the ticket gets a notification in KiddieTrac and an email. Nobody else in the agency is contacted.
??? Can I undo an escalation? | No, but the ticket stays on your desk. Add a reply when it is fixed and set it to **Resolved**.
