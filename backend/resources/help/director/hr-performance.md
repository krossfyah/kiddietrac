# HR: reviews, goals and courses

**Performance & learning** is where you run probation and annual reviews, set goals, keep one-to-one notes, and give your team courses with an optional quiz and a certificate. It is part of the KiddieTrac HR add-on, under **Human Resources** in the menu. It has two tabs: **Reviews & goals** and **Courses**.

[[open: #hr-performance | Open Performance & learning]]

## Starting a review

1. Open **Human Resources > Performance & learning**. The **Reviews & goals** tab opens first.
2. Press **Start a review**.
3. Choose the **Person** and the **Reviewer**. The reviewer must be someone else: nobody reviews themselves.
4. Choose the **Cycle** (Probation, Annual or Custom) and the **Template**.
5. Add a **Title (optional)**, the **Due** date and the **Period from** / **Period to** it covers.
6. Press **Start the review**. The person and the reviewer are told, and the review shows in the list.

[[show: #hr-performance @ Reviews & goals > Start a review | Show me]]

## Writing and signing a review

1. Open the review's **⋮** menu and press **Open review**.
2. The person writes the **Self review** and the reviewer writes the **Manager review**, each section with a rating and comments.
3. Press **Save draft** to come back later, or **Submit** when it is finished.
4. Once both parts are submitted, each of you types your full name under **Sign-off** and presses **Sign**.

The person reads the manager review only once it is submitted. Each signature shows the date, time and place it was signed. To stop a review, press **Cancel review** in its **⋮** menu.

> 💡 With the AI Bundle, **✨ Draft my comments** offers a starting point from the person's own records, and **✨ Suggest goals** offers goals you can tick and add. Always read it and make it yours.

## Setting goals and keeping one-to-one notes

1. In an open review, under **Goals**, press **Add a goal**, write it and press **Save goal**.
2. To log progress, press **Check in** on the goal, choose its status (Not started, On track, At risk, Done or Dropped) and press **Save check-in**.
3. For all of a person's goals and one-to-ones, open **Human Resources > People**, open their **⋮** menu, press **View HR profile**, then press **Goals and one-to-ones** on the **Goals** card.
4. Press **Add a one-to-one note**, write what you talked about and the next steps, and press **Save note**.

One-to-one notes are private: only the two of you, your agency admins and your centre's director see them.

## Review templates (agency admins)

1. Under **Review templates**, press **New review template**.
2. Enter the **Template name**, the **Rating scale, lowest first (one per line)** and the **Sections** (one per line; add " | a prompt" for a question under it).
3. Press **Save template**.

Without a template, reviews use the standard one: four childcare sections rated 1 to 5.

## Adding a course (agency admins)

1. Open the **Courses** tab and press **New course**.
2. Enter the **Course title** and choose the **Content**: a link, a video link, an uploaded file or a KiddieTrac training video.
3. Tick **Counts toward PD hours** and enter the **PD hours** if it should.
4. Tick **They type their name to confirm they understood it** if you want a signed confirmation.
5. Press **Save course**.

[[show: #hr-performance?tab=courses @ Courses > New course | Show me]]

### Adding a quiz

1. Open the course's **⋮** menu and press **Add a quiz**.
2. Set the **Pass mark (%)** and the **Tries allowed**.
3. Press **+ Add a question**, write it, choose whether it has one or several right answers, add the answers and tick the right one(s).
4. Press **Save quiz**.

With a quiz, people finish the course only by passing it. They see how many they got right, never which.

## Assigning a course and following it

1. Open the course's **⋮** menu and press **Assign**.
2. Set the **Due** date.
3. Tick **Everyone in a role**, or search and tick people under **Or choose people**.
4. Press **Assign**.
5. Press **See who has it** to follow progress. From there: **Send a reminder**, **Remind everyone not done**, **Allow another try** (after a failed quiz), **Download certificate**, **Remove**, or **Stop for new people** to end a role rule.

A course given to a role also reaches anyone who joins that role later. When someone finishes, they get a certificate in your agency's name, and approved PD hours if the course counts.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Everything, for every centre: templates, courses, quizzes, reviews, assignments. |
| Centre director | Reviews, goals and one-to-ones for people at their own centres; assigns courses and follows them. Does not add or edit courses or templates. |
| Educator, home visitor | Do not see this screen. They write their self review, take courses and quizzes in **My HR**. |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? I can't choose myself as the reviewer. | That is on purpose: nobody reviews themselves. Pick another manager or an agency admin.
??? The ✨ buttons are missing. | They need the AI Bundle add-on, and AI must not be switched off for your agency.
??? Someone failed the quiz and has no tries left. | Open See who has it and press Allow another try. You can't do that for yourself.
??? Why can't I mark a quiz course complete? | A course with a quiz is finished only by passing the quiz; the certificate is issued then.
??? I removed someone from a course; will they get it again? | No. Someone you remove does not get it back from a role rule.
??? Is New course missing? | Courses and templates are added by agency admins.
