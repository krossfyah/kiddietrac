# Home visitor inspection forms

Inspection forms are the two standard forms home visitors complete when they inspect a home child care provider: the **Monthly** monitoring and inspection report, and the **Quarterly** Ministry checklist (O. Reg. 137/15). This screen is the reviewer's view: every form your home visitors have filled in, in one table you can search, sort, open, correct and download as a PDF. You can also fill in an inspection yourself.

[[open: #hcc-forms | Open Inspection forms]]

**Where it is:** sidebar → **Home Visitors** → **Inspection forms**. The entry only appears when inspection forms are turned on for your agency.

## Finding a form

1. Open **Home Visitors** → **Inspection forms**. The table lists every form, newest visit first.
2. Type in **🔍 Filter by provider, centre or home visitor…** to narrow the list as you type.
3. Use the first drop-down to choose **All forms**, **Monthly** or **Quarterly**.
4. Use the second drop-down to choose **All statuses**, **Draft**, **Submitted** or **Edited**.
5. Press a column heading (**Form**, **Status**, **Provider visited**, **Centre**, **Date**, **Home visitor**, **Quarter**) to sort by it. Press it again to reverse the order.

The bar under the table shows how many forms are listed, and how many are submitted, edited and still drafts.

> ℹ️ The search box and the two filters show on a computer or tablet. On a phone you see the full list.

## Reading and downloading a form

1. On the form's row, press **ℹ️ View**. The filled form opens as a PDF inside the page.
2. If the form was changed after it was submitted, **🕓 Edit history** above the PDF lists who changed it, when, their reason, and each old value → new value.
3. Press **⬇ Download PDF** to save a copy, or press **📄 Download PDF** straight from the row in the table.
4. Press **← Back** to return to the list.

## Correcting a submitted form

1. On the row, press **✏️ Edit / correct** (or **✎ Edit / correct** on the form's view).
2. A yellow note says you are editing a submitted form and that your changes are tracked.
3. Make your changes.
4. Type why in **Reason for edit (saved to the audit trail)**, for example "Corrected the provider name".
5. Press **Save changes**. A **Changes saved** message appears and the form shows **Edited** in the list.

## Doing an inspection yourself

1. Press **➕ New inspection** at the top of the list. The **Inspection forms** page opens with a card for each form.
2. Press **Start form →** on the form you need.
3. Choose the **Centre** (optional) and check **Date of visit**.
4. Fill in the form, including the provider's name.
5. Press **Save as draft** to finish later, or **Submit form** when it is complete. A **Form submitted** message confirms it was sent to your directors and admins. The form is recorded under your name.

> 💡 Answers are also kept on the device as you type. If the page reloads, you see "We kept what you had already filled in on this device." Press **Start fresh instead** to throw that copy away.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | See every form in the agency, view, download, correct, and fill in their own. |
| Centre director | The same as an agency admin. |
| Platform admin | The same, in the agency they are working in. |
| Home visitor | Fills in forms and sees their own; does not see this reviewer table. |
| Educators and parents | Do not see inspection forms. |

Inspection forms are part of the home visiting features of your plan.

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? I see "Home-visitor forms aren’t enabled for this agency." | Inspection forms are switched off for your agency, or your plan does not include home visiting. Ask KiddieTrac support to turn them on.
??? The list says "No forms match." | Your search or filters hide every form. Clear the search box and set both drop-downs back to **All forms** and **All statuses**.
??? I pressed Submit form and see "Please enter the provider name in the form before submitting." | The provider's name in the form is empty. Fill it in and press **Submit form** again.
??? It says "Please set the date of visit." | Pick a date in **Date of visit** and submit again.
??? It says "Could not save:" with a message. | Nothing was saved to the server, but your answers are still on screen and on the device. Check your connection and press the button again.
??? The PDF says "Could not load the PDF." or the download failed. | Reload the page and try **⬇ Download PDF** again.
??? Who is told when a form is submitted? | The completed form is emailed as a PDF to the agency's directors and admins, and stored here.
