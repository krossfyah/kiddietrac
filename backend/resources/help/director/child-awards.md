# Child awards and certificates

The Awards screen is where you celebrate a child's effort: "Star of the Week", "Helper of the Day", "Kindness Award" or anything you like, daily, weekly or monthly. Each award shows in the family's portal, and you can print a certificate to send home or put on the wall.

[[open: #awards | Open Awards]]

## Giving an award

1. Open **Operations > Awards** and press **+ New award**. The **🏆 New award** popup opens.
2. Choose the **Child** (required). The list shows the enrolled children in the rooms you can see.
3. Type the **Award** title (required), or pick a suggestion as you type, such as **Star of the Week**, **Super Reader**, **Team Player** or **Most Improved**.
4. Press a **Badge** to choose the picture that goes with it (🏆 is chosen to start with).
5. Choose the **Period**: **Daily**, **Weekly** (the default) or **Monthly**, or leave it blank.
6. Check the **Date**. Today is filled in.
7. Add a **Message (optional)**, a note for the child and the family, for example "For helping Maya tidy the block corner every day this week."
8. Press **Give award**. The popup closes and the award appears in the list.

> 💡 Your agency admins and centre directors get a notice in KiddieTrac whenever someone gives an award, so you can see recognition happening across your rooms.

## Printing a certificate

1. Find the award in the list.
2. Press **🖨️** (**Print certificate**). A preview of the certificate opens, with the child's name, the award, the date and your agency's name.
3. Press **🖨️ Print** to print it or save it as a PDF, or **✕ Close** to go back.

Families can open and print the same certificate from their own Awards screen.

## Deleting an award

1. Find the award in the list.
2. Press **🗑️** (**Delete**).
3. Confirm **Delete this award?**. The award is removed from the list and from the family's portal.

## What you see in the list

Each award card shows the badge, the award title with its period (for example **Weekly**), the child's name, the date, who gave it, and the message in quotes.

## Who can do what

- Agency admins, centre directors and platform admins give, print and delete awards for the children they can see.
- Educators give, print and delete awards for the children in their rooms, from the same screen.
- Parents see their own children's awards in the portal and can print the certificate. They cannot give or delete awards.
- The screen needs the Awards feature in your agency's plan.

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? The list says "No awards yet". | Nobody has given an award yet. Press **+ New award** to give the first one.
??? The popup says "No children found in your rooms." | You are not linked to a room with enrolled children. Ask your agency admin to check your room or centre assignment.
??? I pressed Give award and saw "Pick a child." or "Enter an award title." | Both the child and the award title are needed. Fill in the one that is missing and press **Give award** again.
??? The list shows "Could not load awards". | The awards did not load. Refresh the page. If it keeps happening, contact support.
??? Is the family emailed about the award? | The award appears on the family's Awards screen in their portal. Nothing is printed or sent home unless you print the certificate.
??? Can I change an award after I give it? | No. Delete it and give it again with the right details.
