# Voice messages

Send a recorded audio message instead of typing.

## How to record

1. Open any chat conversation (sidebar → **Messages**).
2. Look for the 🎤 microphone icon in the message input area.
3. Tap and hold to record. Release to send, or swipe to cancel.
4. Recording shows a live waveform + elapsed timer.
5. Max length 5 minutes per message.

## Recipient experience

- Voice messages appear in the chat with a play button + duration label.
- Tap play to listen. Tap again to pause.
- Speed buttons available (1×, 1.5×, 2×) for faster playback.

## When voice helps

- Quicker than typing a long status update
- Better tone — friendlier than text for sensitive topics
- Hands-free while holding a child

## Storage

Audio files live in `/storage/voice-messages/YYYY/MM/`. Max 5MB per file (about 5 minutes of mono speech). Files persist as long as the message conversation exists.
