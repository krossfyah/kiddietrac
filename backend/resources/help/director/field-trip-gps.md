# Walk Watch

Two screens look after children who leave the centre. **Field trips** (Programs → Field
trips) is where you plan an outing and collect the parents' permission slips. **Walk
Watch** (Administration → Walk Watch) is KiddieTrac's live GPS: an educator starts a walk
on their phone, parents are told straight away and can follow on a live map, and you see
every group that is out — who has them, and where they are.

[[open: #trip-gps | Open Walk Watch]]

## Planning a field trip

1. Open **Programs** → **Field trips** and press **+ Plan trip**. The **New field trip** window opens.
2. Choose the **Centre**, then enter the **Title**, the **Destination** and the **Trip date**.
3. Enter the **Cost per child ($)**, or leave 0 for a free trip.
4. Under **Children invited**, tick each child who is going.
5. Press **Create + send permission slips**. The trip appears in the list and the parents of each invited child are asked to sign a permission slip.

The circles above the list show **Upcoming trips**, **Slips signed**, **Awaiting parents**, **Past trips**, **Walks** and **Children who went**, with charts of trips and walks by month and how children travel.

## Following a trip and its permission slips

1. Tap a trip's name, or press its **⋮** > **View details**. The details show the destination, date and times, tiles for **Children**, **How long**, **Distance**, **Steps (est.)**, **Transport** and **Cost**, the GPS route on a map, the **🪜 Trip steps** and the **🧒 Children on this trip** table.
2. Read each child's **Permission** (**Approved**, **Declined** or **Waiting**), **Answered by**, **When** and **Signed**.
3. Press **🔑 Permission slips** (or **⋮** > **Permission slips** on the list) for the **APPROVED**, **DENIED** and **PENDING** counts. Open **✍️ View signature** under a child to see the parent's signature with its date, time and approximate location.
4. To remove a trip, press **⋮** > **Delete trip** and confirm. This removes the trip and its permission slips.

## Starting a walk (educator)

1. On the phone, open **Walk Watch** (the 🚶 Walk Watch tile) and fill in **Where to?** (and, optionally, what it is).
2. Tick the children who are coming. Only children who are **checked in right now** can be added.
3. Tap **▶ Start walk & share location** and allow location when the phone asks.
4. A floating **Live** bar stays on screen while the walk is shared: **Map**, **👀 Count** for a headcount of the children on the walk, and **Stop**.

The phone sends its location about every 12 seconds while the walk is live. Tap **👀 Count** and tick each child as you see them; if someone is not accounted for, the screen offers to alert the director straight away.

## Watching walks and outings (directors and admins)

1. Open **Administration** → **Walk Watch**. Today's walks and outings are listed, each with a status chip such as **Out now**, **Back** or **Planned**.
2. Each card shows the centre and destination, when the group left and came back, the distance and GPS points, **With** (the educator and a tap-to-call number), **Set off from**, **Right now** (or **Last seen**) and the **Children on this outing**.
3. To see another day, use **‹ Previous**, **Today**, **Next ›** or the **Day** box. Past walks show their route map.
4. Press **↻ Refresh** for the latest position. Today's view also refreshes every 30 seconds.

## What parents get

- When the walk starts: a bell in the app, a push to the KiddieTrac app and an email — *"Ava is out on a walk"*, where to and with whom.
- A **live walk** banner in the app opens the map, refreshed about every 12 seconds, with distance, steps and time so far.
- When the walk ends: *"Ava is back from the walk"* by bell, push and email.
- The walk goes on the child's daily log, and the evening summary shows where they went with the route map.

## Ending a walk

- The educator taps **Stop** (or **End walk**). Parents are told the children are back.
- With the KiddieTrac app open and location allowed, the app can notice the group is back at the centre and ask **🏠 Back at …?** — one tap ends the walk. Detection in the background is an agency setting (Room Controls → **🚶 Walks**) and is off unless you switch it on.
- A walk nobody ended is closed automatically once the day is over (no parent notification is sent then).

## Who can do what

- **Agency admins** see every centre's trips and walks, plan and delete trips, and switch on background walk detection.
- **Centre directors** plan trips and see their own centres' trips and walks. Walk Watch is in the agency admin's **Administration** menu.
- **Educators** start, count and end walks on their phone for children who are checked in.
- **Parents** sign permission slips and follow only the walks their own child is on, for the last 30 days. Location is shared only while a walk is live.

Starting and ending a walk are in the audit log.

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? A child is missing from the walk list on the phone. | Only children checked in right now can be added. Sign the child in first.
??? Walk Watch says "Nothing on this day". | No walks or outings were logged that day. Try another day with ‹ Previous or Next ›.
??? The trip details say "No GPS route recorded for this trip yet." | The route appears once staff start the trip on their phone with location allowed.
??? Plan trip says "Pick at least one child". | Tick at least one child under Children invited, then press Create + send permission slips again.
??? Parents weren't told the walk ended. | A walk closed automatically at the end of the day sends no notice. Ask educators to tap Stop when they are back.
??? Can a parent see other children on the walk? | No. Parents see only their own child's walks.
