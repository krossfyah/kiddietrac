# Ohio publicly funded child care (PFCC)

For Ohio agencies. Keep each child's PFCC authorization, the family's weekly copay and absent days in one place, and check each week's attendance before you submit it in TAP.

## Where it is

Sidebar → **Ohio PFCC**. Directors see their own centres; admins see every centre. It only shows for agencies in Ohio, or where an agency admin has switched it on in **Ohio PFCC → Limits & alerts**.

## Authorizations

1. Open the **Authorizations** tab and press **Add authorization**.
2. Choose the child, then enter what the county authorized:
   - the case number;
   - the start date, and the end date if there is one;
   - the care unit: hourly (under 10 hours a week), part-time (10–33) or full-time (33+);
   - the units per week;
   - your rate per unit;
   - the family's weekly copay.
3. Upload the **signed copay agreement** with **Upload**. **View** opens it again later.

KiddieTrac does not hold Ohio's rate table. Enter the rate shown on the child's authorization. When an authorization ends, press **End** and give the last day of care.

## Copays

The **Copays** tab lists every week (Sunday to Saturday) of an authorization with a copay. Press **Record** to enter what the family paid, or to mark a week waived. Copays are kept separately from invoices, and **families are never messaged about them**.

When a family has missed the number of weeks set under Limits & alerts (2 by default), the centre's directors and the agency admins get a bell notice. Ohio expects non-payment past 2 weeks to be reported to the county within 3 weeks.

## Absent days

Each authorization shows the child's absent days for the current half-year (January–June or July–December) against your limit. Directors and admins get a bell notice when a child is 2 days from the limit, and again if the child goes over it.

The limit starts at **10**. Ohio sources give different numbers (10 or 20) for different cases, so **check it against your authorizations** and change it under Limits & alerts.

## TAP reconciliation

Ohio's TAP system has no connection KiddieTrac can use, so this report lines your KiddieTrac attendance up for you to match in TAP by hand. Choose a week (weeks run Sunday to Saturday) and check any child flagged for:

- care given with **no authorization**;
- a check-in **without a matching check-out** (or the other way round);
- **hours over** what the care unit allows;
- an authorized child with **no attendance** that week.

Absent days are shown too. **Download CSV** gives you the same list as a spreadsheet. A week more than 3 weeks old carries a warning: TAP sweeps unsubmitted matched attendance after 4 weeks.

## Splitting an invoice between the state and the family

On the **Invoice split** tab, open a **draft** invoice and press **Preview**. KiddieTrac works out the state's share for the invoice period:

> authorized units × rate − weekly copay, pro-rated by week

If it looks right, press **Apply to this draft**. The state's share goes on the invoice as a subsidy, and you can undo it until the invoice is sent. Nothing else changes:

- invoices that have been sent are never touched;
- an invoice that already carries another subsidy is not split;
- children without an authorization are not affected.

If your tuition is higher than the authorized amount, the difference stays on the family's invoice along with the copay. Check that this fits your agreement with the family.
