# The child record

Open any child from **Children** (or a family) to see their full record. The
header shows actions — **Edit**, **Emergency card**, **Archive**, and
**Compliance report** — and the record is organised into tabs.

## The emergency card

**Emergency card** in the header opens the child's card **in a panel over the portal**,
with **Print** and **✕** in its header. It used to replace the whole page in a new tab,
which took the navigation, the theme and any way back with it.

**Print** works on a computer. In the phone app the button reads **Print in browser** and
hands the card to your phone's browser, which is where a real print dialog lives — the app
has no printer support of its own. The link it mints lasts a few minutes and is recorded.

See [Opening and printing documents](opening-and-printing-documents).

## At a glance

The header shows the child's photo, their name and preferred name, age and date of
birth, and their **usual drop-off and pick-up times**. Guardians are listed with their
photos too, so you can match a face at the door.

If the usual times have not been set the header says so — set them with **Edit**. They
are wall-clock times: 8:15 means 8:15 on the room's clock, and they never shift with
anyone's timezone.

## Tabs

- **Overview** — guardians and active health flags.
- **Enrolment** — the child's current placement (**agency, centre, room**),
  status, enrolled-since date and monthly fee; **school details** (for
  school-age children); and the full **enrolment history**.
- **Daily reports** — recent check-ins and day events for the child.
- **Live feed** — photos/media tagged to the child plus observations.
- **Attachments** — documents on file for the child. **Attach a file** with an
  optional title, category and expiry date; **Open** it in the portal viewer,
  **Download** a copy, or remove it. Signed agreements are a legal record and show a
  padlock instead of a delete button.
- **🗄️ Full history** — every record held about this child, from across the whole
  system: attendance, care logs, observations, incidents, medications, immunisations,
  photos, chats, documents, report cards, awards, invoices, and the lesson and meal
  plans for the rooms they were in. Sections start collapsed with a count beside each;
  open one to page through it 25 at a time. This works for archived children too — see
  [Archived records and retention](archived-records).

## Children list

The **Children** list shows **every child across all your centres** (agency
admins). Use the **centre**, **status**, and **search** filters at the top to
narrow down, and toggle **Cards / Table** view.

> Who can use this: **Centre director**, **Agency admin** (educators see children
> in their own centre).
