# Payroll documents

**Accounting → Payroll → Documents issued.** Every payslip and payroll document ever
issued, as a record rather than a calculation.

## Why it is a record

A payslip used to be worked out afresh each time it was opened, so a change to a rate
or a correction to hours silently rewrote what somebody had already been paid and told
about. Issued documents are now stored: what it said when it was issued is what it says
today.

## What is in it

- The pay period, the payee, the units and rate, overtime, and the total.
- Its status — issued, paid, cancelled.
- A PDF, rendered through the active payslip **document template**.

## Payroll from iLearn

Agencies running iLearn have their pay periods synced across, history included, and new
ones follow automatically. A pay period is the record; the invoice iLearn also produces
for it is the same money said twice, so it is not double-counted here.

## Worth knowing

- **Backfill** builds documents from work already recorded, and skips anyone who
  already has one for that period.
- Educators see their own under **My pay** — never anybody else's.
- Overtime hours, multiplier and amount carry through to the payslip when recorded.
