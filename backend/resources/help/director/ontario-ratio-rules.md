# Ontario ratio rules for licensed child care centres

At a location set up as an Ontario **licensed child care centre**, KiddieTrac checks each room against O. Reg. 137/15 instead of the room's own ratio. Home child care locations, and any location with no licence type set, keep the room's own ratio exactly as before.

## Turn it on

1. Open **Room ratios** and press **⚙ Ratio settings** on the location.
2. Set **This location is a** to *Licensed child care centre*.
3. Check the program hours, the reduced-ratio windows and your outdoor play times.
4. Pick each room's **licensed age group** (infant, toddler, preschool, kindergarten, primary/junior school age, junior school age, or family age group).

## What is checked

| Age group | Ratio | Max group | Reduced ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| Infant (under 18 months) | 3 : 10 | 10 | never |
| Toddler (18–30 months) | 1 : 5 | 15 | 1 : 8 |
| Preschool (30 months–6 years) | 1 : 8 | 24 | 1 : 12 |
| Kindergarten (44 months–7 years) | 1 : 13 | 26 | 1 : 20 |
| Primary/junior school age (68 months–13 years) | 1 : 15 | 30 | 1 : 23 |
| Junior school age (9–13 years) | 1 : 20 | 20 | never |

- **Reduced ratios** apply only inside your arrival window (up to 90 minutes after opening, 30 for programs under 6 hours), departure window (up to 60 minutes before closing, or 30), and rest period (up to 2 hours, toddler and preschool groups only). Never during outdoor play. The room shows the window it is using, for example *Reduced ratio — arrival window, until 8:30 AM*.
- **Mixed ages.** Up to 20% of a toddler or preschool group's licensed capacity may be younger children (25% for kindergarten and school-age groups). Over that, the youngest child's age group sets the ratio and group size. Mixed-age grouping needs your program adviser's approval.
- **Supervisor.** The centre director counts fully toward ratio when the whole centre needs 1–4 staff, as half when it needs 5–6, and not at all at 7 or more.
- **Two adults** must be at the centre once 4 or more children in infant groups, or 6 or more other children, are signed in.
- **Qualified staff.** Every group needs at least one RECE on the floor (two for a preschool group of 17 or more). KiddieTrac uses the **RECE** record on each person's Certifications; an expired record does not count.
- **Family age groups:** no more than 15 children and no more than 6 under 24 months; staff by age (1 : 3 under 12 months, 1 : 4 to 24 months, 1 : 8 older), at least 2 staff over 6 children and 3 over 10 with a baby under 12 months.

A room that is in ratio but has one adult on site or no RECE shows **CHECK STAFFING**. The same result appears on the director dashboard, the compliance overview and the educator's ratio bar on Today.

## Good to know

- Staff are counted the way your agency already counts them: active shifts in the room first, otherwise people clocked in at the centre, shared across its rooms.
- A staff member approved by a director instead of being an RECE is not recorded in KiddieTrac yet; add a note on their Certifications and ask support.
