# Onboard an agency (reseller)

**Onboard an agency** is where a reseller adds a new child care agency under their own brand. One form creates the agency, its first centre and its first admin, who is emailed an invite to set a password. The new agency carries your brand straight away and appears in **All my agencies**.

[[open: #reseller-onboard | Open Onboard an agency]]

## Before you start

- [ ] The agency's legal or trading name
- [ ] A contact email and phone number for the agency
- [ ] Its street address, city, province or state, and postal code
- [ ] The name, email and phone number of the person who will be its first admin (an email nobody uses on KiddieTrac yet)

## Adding the agency

1. Open **Onboard an agency** from the **Reseller** section of the menu, or press **+ Onboard an agency** on **All my agencies**.
2. Under **The agency**, fill in **Agency name**, **Contact email** and **Contact phone**.
3. Pick the **Plan** (**Starter**, **Growth** or **Enterprise**). The price is the platform's list price; a reseller cannot change it.
4. Pick the agency's **Time zone** (Eastern, Central, Mountain, Pacific, Atlantic or Newfoundland). Every time the agency sees is shown in this zone, so choose carefully.
5. Pick the **Language** the agency starts in (English, French or Spanish).
6. Under **Address**, fill in **Street address**, **Unit / suite** (optional), **City**, **Province / state**, **Postal code** and **Country**.
7. Under **Their first admin**, fill in **First name**, **Last name**, **Email** and **Phone**.
8. Press **Add agency**. The button shows "Adding…" while it works.

A green **Agency added** toast appears ("The admin has been emailed an invite to set a password") and you are taken back to **All my agencies**, where the new agency is listed.

> 💡 The agency starts on a trial billing status. Its first admin then sets up centres, rooms, staff and families from inside the agency.

## After the agency is added

1. On **All my agencies**, find the new agency and press **Admins** to check the invite went out (status **Invited**).
2. If the admin did not get the email, press **Send new link** beside them.
3. Press **Open agency →** if you want to help set it up yourself; you work there as its agency admin.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Reseller | Adds agencies under their own reseller only. |
| Platform admin | Picks the reseller first in **Viewing reseller** on **Reseller overview**, then adds the agency under that reseller. Platform admins also add agencies under **Platform › All agencies**. |
| Agency admin (not a reseller) | Cannot add agencies; the form answers "You do not manage a reseller." |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? "Someone already has an account with that email." | The first admin's email is already used on KiddieTrac. Use another email for them, then ask KiddieTrac to link their existing account if needed.
??? "Choose the reseller first." | You are a platform admin and no reseller is selected. Open **Reseller overview**, pick one in **Viewing reseller**, then come back.
??? The button is greyed out. | It is working ("Adding…"). If a red **Could not add the agency** toast appears, read its message, fix the field it names and press **Add agency** again.
??? Can I set a discount or a different price? | No. Plan prices are set by KiddieTrac. Ask KiddieTrac about your commission terms instead.
??? Who receives emails? | Only the first admin, who gets the invite to set a password. Families and staff are not contacted until the agency invites them.
??? I picked the wrong time zone. | Raise a ticket with KiddieTrac ([[raise-a-ticket]]) straight away, before children and staff are added, and say which agency and which zone it should be.
