# Curriculum units

The Curriculum screen is a library of ready-made, multi-week units for infant, toddler, preschool and kindergarten / school-age rooms, built on Ontario's *How Does Learning Happen?* and its four foundations: Belonging, Well-being, Engagement and Expression. Preview a unit, copy it to adapt it for your agency, and use it to make draft weekly lesson plans for a room in a few taps.

[[open: #curriculum | Open Curriculum]]

## Finding a unit

1. Open **Engagement > Curriculum** (centre directors: **Programs > Curriculum library**). The **📖 Ontario library** tab is open.
2. Type in **Search units, themes, activities…**, or narrow the list with **All ages**, **All foundations**, **All seasons** and **All themes**. The count on the right says how many units match.
3. Each card shows the age group, the number of weeks, the season, the foundations it covers and how many activities it has.
4. Press a card to preview the unit.

The **🏠 Our units** tab lists the units your agency has copied and adapted. The number in brackets says how many you have.

## Previewing a unit

1. In the preview, read **🧭 About this unit**, the **🎯 Goals** and the **Observation prompts**.
2. Under **🗓 Weekly plan**, press **Week 1**, **Week 2** and so on to see each week's activities, day by day.
3. Press an activity to see its **Materials**, **Steps** and safety note, then press **Close**.
4. Scroll down for **Family engagement**, **Inclusion and additional needs**, **🗣️ French and bilingual learners** and **Safety**.
5. Press **← All units** to go back to the list.

## Making draft lesson plans from a unit

1. In the unit's preview, press **📅 Use this unit**.
2. Choose the **Room or centre**. Pick a room, or a centre marked "(whole centre)".
3. Set **Start the week of (Monday)**. The next Monday is filled in.
4. If the unit has more than one week, tick the weeks you want under **Which weeks**. The line below shows the dates the plans will cover.
5. Press **Make draft plans**. KiddieTrac makes one draft weekly lesson plan per unit week.
6. The **✓ Draft lesson plans** popup lists the weeks it made and any it left alone because they already had activities. Press **Open lesson plans** to go to the plans, or **Stay here**.

> ℹ️ Drafts are for staff only. Families see a week only once someone publishes it on the **📅 This week** tab of Play Spark.

## Adapting a unit for your agency

1. In a unit from the Ontario library, press **⧉ Copy to edit**. A **Copied** toast appears and the copy opens in **✏️ Edit our copy**.
2. Change the **Title**, **Theme**, **Season**, **Summary**, **Goals** and the other sections. Each list takes one item per line.
3. Under **Activities**, change an activity, press **📌 Place on a day** to choose its days, press **Remove** to drop it, or press **+ Add an activity**.
4. Press **💾 Save unit**. A **Saved** toast appears.

Your copy is listed in **🏠 Our units** with a **🏠 Our copy** tag. Open it and press **✏️ Edit** to change it again, or **Delete** to remove it. Lesson plans already made from it stay as they are, and the KiddieTrac library unit is not affected.

## Who can do what

- Agency admins and centre directors browse, use, copy, edit and delete units, and see the **💡 Activity ideas** tab, which opens the activity ideas in Play Spark's Library.
- Platform admins do the same for the agency they are working in.
- Educators can browse and use units but cannot copy, edit or delete them.
- Parents do not see this screen. The screen needs lesson plans to be part of your agency's plan.

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? I pressed Make draft plans and saw "Select a room or centre first." | Choose a room or centre in **Room or centre**, then press **Make draft plans** again.
??? I see "Choose at least one week." | Tick at least one week under **Which weeks**.
??? A week was "Left as they were". | That week already had activities in its lesson plan, so KiddieTrac did not overwrite it. Open the plan and add the activities yourself, or pick another start week.
??? I see "No rooms or centres were found for you to plan for." | You are not linked to a room or centre. Ask your agency admin to add you to one.
??? Saving my copy shows "Title needed". | Give the unit a title at the top of **About** and press **💾 Save unit** again.
??? Why can't I edit a library unit? | Library units belong to KiddieTrac and are read-only. Press **⧉ Copy to edit** to make your own copy.
