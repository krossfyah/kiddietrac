# Standing hours

Standing hours are the hours each person on a home's or centre's team works **every week**.
Set them once and the schedule fills itself; change one day on the calendar when it differs.

[[open: #schedule | Open Schedule]]

## Set someone's standing hours

1. Open **Schedule** and pick the home or centre at the top.
2. Press **👥 Standing hours**. Everyone assigned there is listed.
3. For an educator, tick **Keep on the schedule every week**. For the provider, tick **Use their
   own hours** — otherwise the provider keeps the centre's opening hours.
4. Set the start and end time, the role (lead, support, floater or volunteer) and the days.
5. Leave **Also update their upcoming shifts from tomorrow** ticked to rewrite the shifts
   already on the calendar from tomorrow on. Untick it to change only the pattern.
6. Press **Save** on that person's row. Press **Done** when you have finished.

> **Tip:** A day off or a late start is still done on the calendar: click that shift and change it.

## How the schedule keeps up

- If the **nightly fill** is on for your agency, it keeps everyone with standing hours on the
  schedule four weeks ahead. The window says so at the top.
- If it is off, shifts are written only when you save with **Also update their upcoming
  shifts from tomorrow** ticked.

To take someone off their standing hours, untick **Keep on the schedule every week** and press **Save**.

## Related buttons on Schedule

- **⚡ Autofill week** fills the week on screen from the centre's opening hours. Closure days
  and days already rostered are left alone.
- **+ New Shift** adds a single shift.
