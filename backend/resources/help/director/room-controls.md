# Room Controls

**Room Controls** is the one place for the settings that shape a room's day: who works
where, how often children are counted, sleep checks, daily records reminders, the
nightly auto sign-off and walk detection. Agency admins and centre directors open it from
the sidebar: **Settings** → **Room Controls**.

[[open: #educator-rooms | Open Room Controls]]

## The six tabs

| Tab | What it sets | Who can change it |
|---|---|---|
| **🚪 Room assignments** | Which rooms each person works in | Directors and agency admins |
| **👀 Headcounts** | Routine headcount reminders, and who gets them | Agency admins |
| **😴 Sleep checks** | Your sleep policy: check intervals and alerts | Agency admins |
| **📝 Daily records** | The evening reminder to educators about missing records | Agency admins |
| **⏱️ Auto sign-off** | Closing shifts and children's days nobody closed | Directors and agency admins |
| **🚶 Walks** | Walk Watch detection in the background on Android | Agency admins |

Where only agency admins can make changes, directors still see the settings, greyed out,
or a note saying **Only agency administrators can change this.**

## Assigning rooms to a person

1. Open the **🚪 Room assignments** tab.
2. Choose the person in the **Educator** list. Their rooms are shown, read-only.
3. Press **✏️ Edit assignments**. An **✏️ Editing** bar appears: "Tick a room to assign it. Nothing is saved until you press Save."
4. Tick the rooms they work in, and untick any they no longer work in.
5. Press **Save changes**, or **Cancel** to put everything back.

This decides whose children they see, and lets attendance and sign-out figures count
against a person instead of the whole centre. A banner counts people with no room and
rooms with nobody. See [Assigning educators to rooms](room-assignments).

## Turning on headcount reminders

1. Open the **👀 Headcounts** tab.
2. Tick **Remind educators after** and choose a time, from 15 to 120 minutes. The change saves as soon as you make it.
3. Under **Who gets head count reminders**, untick a centre or a person to stop their reminders. They can still take headcounts.

When a room with children signed in goes that long without a headcount, the educators
clocked in to that room get a bell and a push notification. Reminders are off by default.
They stop at the end of the educator's rostered shift that day, or at the centre's closing
time if there is no roster, so someone who stays clocked in is not reminded all evening.
See [Headcounts and the acting supervisor](headcounts-and-acting-supervisor).

## Sleep checks

1. Open the **😴 Sleep checks** tab.
2. Set how often sleeping children under 12 months and from 12 to 24 months are checked.
3. Choose whether the director is told about an overdue check, and the grace period before they are. Centres with their own policy can have their own intervals.
4. Press **Save**.

See [Sleep checks, policy and the inspector report](sleep-checks).

## Daily records

Each evening, educators who worked that day get an email about anything not recorded:
observations, daily logs, photos and videos, meals, and the week's lesson plan and menu.
On the **📝 Daily records** tab you set **What educators are expected to record each day**,
when it goes, and **Copies and escalation**, then press **Save**. **Reminders sent — last 14 days**
shows what went out. See [Daily records reminders](daily-records-reminders).

## Auto sign-off

1. Open the **⏱️ Auto sign-off** tab.
2. Switch on **Close staff shifts** and set the time.
3. Set **Give up on a shift after** (14 hours by default).
4. Switch on **Sign children out** and set that time.
5. Press **Save auto sign-off**.

This is the same setting as **Settings → Clock settings → Auto sign-off**. Changing it in
either place changes both. See [Auto sign-off](auto-sign-off).

## Walks

On the **🚶 Walks** tab, an agency admin can tick **Detect walks in the background (Android app)** and press **Save**. KiddieTrac always offers to start or end a walk while the app is open; this setting lets the Android app notice a walk with the phone in a pocket. Android asks each educator to allow location "all the time" and shows a **permanent location notification** while it runs. It is off by default, and a centre with no map location (check its address) is listed as off.

## Who can do what

- **Agency admins** can change every tab.
- **Centre directors** can change room assignments and auto sign-off for their centres, and see the other tabs read-only.
- **Educators** do not open Room Controls. They see the effect: their rooms' children, headcount reminders and the evening records email.

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? Where did the headcount reminder setting go? | It moved from the bottom of the Headcounts page to Settings → Room Controls → Headcounts. The Headcounts page shows whether reminders are on, with a **Change in Room Controls →** link here.
??? Where did the sleep check settings go? | They moved from Settings → Notifications to Settings → Room Controls → Sleep checks.
??? Is Room Controls the old Room assignments screen? | Yes. Room assignments is now the first tab of Room Controls, and it works the same way.
??? A tab only says "Only agency administrators can change this." | That setting belongs to the agency. Ask an agency admin to change it.
??? It asks whether to discard the unsaved changes when I pick another person. | You were editing someone's rooms and did not save. Choose to keep editing and press Save changes, or discard them.
??? It says "This agency has no rooms yet." | Add rooms to a centre first (Centres), then come back to assign people.
