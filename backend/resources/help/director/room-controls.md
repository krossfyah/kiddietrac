# Room Controls

**Room Controls** is the one place for the settings that shape a room's day: who works
where, how often children are counted, sleep checks, daily records reminders and the
nightly auto sign-off.

Find it in the sidebar under **Settings → Room Controls**. Agency admins and centre
directors can open it.

[[open: #educator-rooms | Open Room Controls]]

## The five tabs

| Tab | What it sets | Who can change it |
|---|---|---|
| **🚪 Room assignments** | Which rooms each person works in | Directors and agency admins |
| **👀 Headcounts** | Routine headcount reminders | Agency admins |
| **😴 Sleep checks** | Your sleep policy: check intervals and alerts | Agency admins |
| **📝 Daily records** | The evening reminder to educators about missing records | Agency admins |
| **⏱️ Auto sign-off** | Closing shifts and children's days nobody closed | Directors and agency admins |

Where only agency admins can make changes, directors still see the settings, greyed out,
with a note saying so.

## Room assignments

Pick a person, press **Edit**, tick their rooms and press **Save**. This decides whose
children they see, and lets attendance and sign-out figures count against a person
instead of the whole centre. See [Assigning educators to rooms](room-assignments).

## Headcounts

1. Tick **Remind educators after** and choose a time, from 15 to 120 minutes.
2. The change saves as soon as you make it.

When a room with children signed in goes that long without a headcount, the educators
clocked in to that room get a bell and a push notification. It applies to every centre in
the agency, and it is off by default.

Reminders stop at the end of the educator's rostered shift that day. With no roster, they
stop at the centre's closing time. Someone who stays clocked in after work is not
reminded all evening.

See [Headcounts and the acting supervisor](headcounts-and-acting-supervisor).

## Sleep checks

Set how often sleeping children under 12 months and from 12 to 24 months are checked,
whether the director is told about an overdue check, and the grace period before they
are. Centres with their own policy can have their own intervals. Press **Save**. See
[Sleep checks, policy and the inspector report](sleep-checks).

## Daily records

Each evening, educators who worked that day get an email about anything not recorded:
observations, daily logs, photos and videos, meals, and the week's lesson plan and menu.
Their director and the agency admins are copied. You set what is expected, when it goes
and when it escalates. See [Daily records reminders](daily-records-reminders).

## Auto sign-off

1. Switch on **Close staff shifts** and set the time.
2. Set **Give up on a shift after** (14 hours by default).
3. Switch on **Sign children out** and set that time.
4. Press **Save auto sign-off**.

This is the same setting as **Settings → Clock settings → Auto sign-off**. Changing it in
either place changes both. See [Auto sign-off](auto-sign-off).

> **Tip:** On the Headcounts page, agency admins see whether reminders are on and a **Change in Room Controls →** link that opens the Headcounts tab.

??? Where did the sleep check settings go? | They moved from Settings → Notifications to Settings → Room Controls → Sleep checks.
