# All contacts

**All contacts** is the agency's contact book: suppliers, trades, inspectors, insurers, landlords and anyone else the business deals with, plus the staff and families already in KiddieTrac, in one list instead of one person's phone. Open it from the sidebar: **Operations > All contacts**.

[[open: #contacts | Open All contacts]]

![All contacts: everyone in the system, plus anyone you add](https://api.kiddietrac.com/help-img/contacts.png)

## Finding someone

1. Open **All contacts**. The count beside the filters shows how many contacts match, for example "42 contact(s)".
2. Type a name, company or phone number in **Search name, company, number…**.
3. Press **Enter** or the search button. The search does not run while you type, so the list does not jump around.
4. To narrow by type, pick one from **All categories**, for example Supplier, Inspector or Parent.
5. Use the page controls under the table to move between pages or change how many rows show.

Contacts marked **EMERGENCY** are always pinned to the top of the list.

## Reading the table

| Column | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Contact** | Name, job title, and any tags |
| **Category** | What they are, for example Parent, Educator or Supplier |
| **Company** | Their organisation |
| **Phone** | Every number on file (a mobile is marked "mob") |
| **Email** | Their address, which you can click to write to them |
| **Address** | The full postal address |
| **Notes** | The start of any notes |

An empty cell says **Not available**: nobody has recorded it yet.

## Adding a contact

1. Press **+ Add contact**. The **Add contact** window opens.
2. If you have their business card, use the file button at the top to photograph or upload it (see below).
3. Fill in **First name** and **Last name**, or a **Company**. At least one of these is needed.
4. Choose **They are our…** (the category) and **Used by**: **The whole agency** or one centre.
5. Add **Phone**, **Mobile**, **Email**, **Website**, **Address**, **City**, **Province**, **Postal code**, **Our account no.**, **Licence / reg. no.**, **Hours** and **Notes** as needed.
6. Tick **Ring first in an emergency** to pin them to the top of the list.
7. Press **Add contact**. A "Contact added" toast appears.

## Scanning a business card

1. In **Add contact**, choose the card photo with the file button ("Have their card? Photograph it…").
2. Wait for "Reading the card…".
3. Check the result: "Card saved and 5 field(s) filled in. Check them before saving." Only empty fields are filled; anything you already typed is kept.
4. Correct anything misread and press **Add contact**.

If the card cannot be read, the image is still kept on the contact and the screen tells you to type the details in. Open it later with **🪪 Business card**.

## Editing or deleting a contact

1. Open the row's **⋮** menu.
2. Choose **✏️ Edit contact**, change the details on the **Details** tab and press **Save changes**. A **✉️ Emails** tab shows emails linked to the contact when your agency links emails to people.
3. To remove someone, choose **🗑 Delete contact** and confirm. The contact leaves the book but is kept on record, so it can be restored if it was a mistake.

Staff and parent rows come from their own records. Agency admins see **✏️ Edit staff record** or **✏️ Edit family record** to change them where they live; others see "from their staff record" or "from their family record".

## Who can do what

- **Agency admin** and **platform admin**: everything, including opening staff and family records from a row.
- **Centre director**: searches, adds, edits and deletes contact book entries; staff and family rows are read-only.
- **Educators and parents**: do not have this screen.

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? Saving says "Give the contact a name or a company." | Fill in a first or last name, or a company, then save again.
??? The list says "No contact matches that." | Nothing fits your search or category. Clear the search box or choose **All categories**.
??? The scan says "The card could not be uploaded." | The image did not reach KiddieTrac. Try a smaller photo or check your connection.
??? Why can't I edit a parent's phone number here? | It belongs to their family record, so there is only ever one copy. Agency admins use **✏️ Edit family record**; a director asks an agency admin or updates it in **Families**.
??? The Emails tab says "Linking emails to people is off for this agency." | An agency admin can turn it on in **Email settings**.
??? The screen says "Could not load contacts". | Reload the page; if it continues, contact KiddieTrac support.
