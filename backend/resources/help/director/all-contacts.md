# All contacts

One directory for everybody the agency deals with — the digital replacement for a drawer of business cards.

![All contacts: everyone in the system, plus anyone you add](https://api.kiddietrac.com/help-img/contacts.png)

## What is in it

Three sources in one list:

- **Everyone already in KiddieTrac** — parents, educators, directors, home visitors. Nothing to import; they are there.
- **Contacts you add** — a plumber, an inspector, a supplier, a landlord.
- **Scanned business cards**.

## The table

| Column | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **Contact** | Name, and job title where there is one |
| **Category** | What they are — Parent, Educator, Supplier |
| **Company** | Their organisation. A parent shows their family name |
| **Phone** | Every number on file |
| **Email** | Every address on file |
| **Address** | The full address, not just a town |
| **Notes** | Anything recorded |

An empty cell says **"Not available"** rather than a dash. A dash reads as "none", "unknown" or "not applicable" depending on who is looking; the honest answer is that nobody has recorded one.

The search waits for **Enter** or the search button. It does not filter as you type.

## Adding a contact

**+ New contact** takes a name, a category and whatever else you have — several phone numbers and addresses, a company, a job title, notes.

## Scanning a business card

**Scan card** takes a photo or an uploaded image and fills the form from it. Check what it read before saving; a card with an unusual layout will get some fields wrong.

**The card image is always kept**, even when the scan reads nothing useful. A photo of the card you can look at beats a blank form, and the failure is stated rather than left as an empty screen.
