# Parent-teacher conferences

**Conferences** lets your centre publish a block of time slots for parent-teacher meetings, and lets families book one themselves. You choose the centre, the evening and the length of each meeting; KiddieTrac cuts the time into slots, and each family picks one for their child from their own portal.

**Where it is:** sidebar → **Engagement** → **Conferences**. The banner reads **🗣 Parent-teacher conferences** and counts the slots in your agency.

[[open: #conferences | Open Conferences]]

## Publishing conference slots

1. Press **+ Create slots** in the banner. The **Create conference slots** box opens.
2. Choose the **Centre**. Only families at that centre will see these slots.
3. Pick the **Date**.
4. Set **Start** and **End**. They start at 15:00 and 19:00; change them to your evening.
5. Set **Length (min)**, the length of one meeting, from 10 to 120 minutes. The default is 20.
6. Press **Generate slots**. One slot is made for each length of time from the start until the end, for example 15:00, 15:20, 15:40 and so on to 18:40.
7. The new slots appear in the list with the status **open**.

> 💡 Run **+ Create slots** once per evening. For two evenings, or two centres, create each block separately.

You can also start from the agency calendar: **+ Add**, then **🗣 Conference slots** brings you here.

## Reading the slot list

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **When** | The date, then the time and length of the slot. |
| **Teacher** | **Any teacher** — slots are not tied to one teacher. |
| **Status** | **open** (free to book) or **booked**. |
| **Booked by** | The parent who booked it, with the child's name in brackets. |

The list shows slots from the last seven days onward, for the centres you work at.

## How families book

1. The parent opens **Conferences** in the family portal and sees the open, upcoming slots at their family's centre.
2. They press **Book** on a slot.
3. If they have more than one child, they are asked **Which child is this booking for?** and pick the child.
4. The slot changes to **booked** on your list, with their name and the child's under **Booked by**.

## Cancelling a booking

1. Find the booked slot in the list.
2. Press **Cancel** on its row.
3. Confirm **Cancel this conference?**. The slot goes back to **open** and another family can book it.

> ⚠️ Cancelling does not tell the family. Send them a message in Message Centre so they know, and offer another time.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Create slots at any of the agency's centres, see every booking, cancel any booking. |
| Centre director | The same, for their own centres. |
| Platform admin | The same, inside the agency they have switched into. |
| Parent | Sees open slots at their own centre and books one per child. |

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? The list says "No slots scheduled." | No slots exist for your centres from the last week onward. Press **+ Create slots**.
??? I see "That centre is not one of yours." | You can only publish slots at a centre you work at. Pick one of your own centres, or ask an agency admin.
??? How do I delete slots nobody booked? | There is no delete button. Unbooked slots simply stay **open**, and drop off the list a week after their date.
??? A parent wants to cancel their booking. | After booking, the slot no longer shows in the parent's list, so ask them to contact the centre and cancel it for them with **Cancel**.
??? A parent says "Slot no longer available". | Another family booked that slot first. They can pick another open slot.
??? Does anyone get a reminder email? | No. KiddieTrac does not send booking confirmations or reminders for conferences. Send an announcement or message to families with the dates, and remind them yourself the day before.
??? Can I assign a slot to a particular teacher? | Not on this screen. Every slot shows **Any teacher**; tell families who they will meet in your message.
