# Parent-teacher conferences

**Conferences** lets your centre publish a block of time slots for parent-teacher meetings, and lets families book one themselves. You choose the centre, the teacher, the evening and the length of each meeting; KiddieTrac cuts the time into slots, and each family picks one for their child from their own portal.

**Where it is:** sidebar → **Engagement** → **Conferences**. The banner reads **🗣 Parent-teacher conferences** and counts the slots in your agency.

[[open: #conferences | Open Conferences]]

## Publishing conference slots

1. Press **+ Create slots** in the banner. The **Create conference slots** box opens.
2. Choose the **Centre**. Only families at that centre will see these slots.
3. Choose the **Teacher**. The list holds the educators and directors who work at that centre. They are told when a family books. Leave **Any teacher** and the centre's director and agency admins are told instead.
4. Pick the **Date**.
5. Set **Start** and **End**. They start at 15:00 and 19:00; change them to your evening. The times are the centre's clock (your agency's time zone), whichever device you type them on.
6. Set **Length (min)**, the length of one meeting, from 10 to 120 minutes. The default is 20.
7. Press **Generate slots**. One slot is made for each length of time from the start until the end, for example 15:00, 15:20, 15:40 and so on to 18:40. A toast says how many were made, for example "9 slot(s) created."
8. The new slots appear in the list with the status **Open**.

> 💡 Run **+ Create slots** once per evening and teacher. For two evenings, two teachers or two centres, create each block separately.

You can also start from the agency calendar: **+ Add**, then **🗣 Conference slots** brings you here.

## Reading the slot list

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **When** | The date, then the time and length of the slot, in your agency's time zone. |
| **Teacher** | The teacher chosen for the slot, or **Any teacher**. |
| **Status** | **Open** (free to book) or **Booked**. |
| **Booked by** | The parent who booked it, with the child's name in brackets. |

The list shows slots from the last seven days onward, for the centres you work at. Each row has **Delete slot**, and a booked row also has **Cancel booking**.

## How families book

1. The parent opens **Conferences** in the family portal and sees the open, upcoming slots at their family's centre, plus their own family's bookings.
2. They press **Book** on a slot.
3. If they have more than one child, they are asked **Which child is this booking for?** and pick the child from the **Child** list.
4. A toast says "Booked. You can cancel it here if plans change." The slot changes to **Booked** on your list, with their name and the child's under **Booked by**.
5. The slot's teacher gets a **Conference booked:** notice with the child's first name and the time. With **Any teacher**, the centre's director and agency admins get it.

A family's own booking stays on their list, marked **For** and the child's first name, with a **Cancel booking** button. Any parent of that family can cancel it: the slot opens again, and the teacher (or, with **Any teacher**, the centre's director and agency admins) gets **Conference cancelled by the family**. Families never see another family's booking.

## Cancelling a booking

1. Find the booked slot in the list.
2. Press **Cancel booking** on its row.
3. Confirm **Cancel this booking? The time opens up for other families.** The slot goes back to **Open** and another family can book it.

> ⚠️ Cancelling a booking from your list does not tell the family. Send them a message in Message Centre so they know, and offer another time. To remove the time altogether and have the family told, use **Delete slot** instead.

## Deleting a slot

1. Find the slot in the list.
2. Press **Delete slot** on its row.
3. Confirm. For a free slot the question is **Delete this slot?**; for a booked one it warns **Delete this slot? It is booked: the family will be told the centre cancelled it.**
4. A toast says "Slot deleted." and the slot leaves the list.

When the slot was booked, the parent who booked it gets **Conference cancelled by the centre** with the date and time, asking them to book another time.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Create slots at any of the agency's centres, see every booking, cancel any booking, delete any slot. |
| Centre director | The same, for their own centres. |
| Platform admin | The same, inside the agency they have switched into. |
| Parent | Sees open slots at their own centre and their own family's bookings, books one per child, and cancels their family's bookings. |

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? The list says "No slots scheduled." | No slots exist for your centres from the last week onward. Press **+ Create slots**.
??? I see "That centre is not one of yours." | You can only publish slots at a centre you work at. Pick one of your own centres, or ask an agency admin.
??? I see "The end time has to be after the start time." | **End** is the same as or earlier than **Start**. Fix the times and press **Generate slots** again.
??? How do I delete slots nobody booked? | Press **Delete slot** on each one. Unbooked slots you leave alone stay **Open** and drop off the list a week after their date.
??? A parent wants to cancel their booking. | They can do it themselves: the booking stays on their **Conferences** list with **Cancel booking**. The teacher (or the director) is told and the time opens up again.
??? A parent says "Slot no longer available". | Another family booked that slot first. They can pick another open slot.
??? Does anyone get a reminder email? | No. KiddieTrac does not send reminders for conferences. Staff get an in-app notice when a family books or cancels, and families get one when the centre deletes their booked slot. Send an announcement or message to families with the dates, and remind them yourself the day before.
??? Can I assign a slot to a particular teacher? | Yes. Choose them under **Teacher** when you create the slots. Only educators and directors who work at that centre are offered; anyone else is refused with "Choose a teacher who works at this centre."
??? The time on a slot looks an hour or more off. | Slot times are saved in your agency's time zone and shown in it on every screen. If a time is wrong, delete the slot and create it again with the right time.
