# HR: directory, org chart, kudos, quizzes and who approves time off

Part of the KiddieTrac HR add-on. Agency admins see the whole agency; directors see the staff at their own centres.

## Directory and org chart

Open **Human Resources > People > Directory**. Everyone on staff is listed with their role, centres and manager. Email and phone show only when the person chose to share them under **My HR > Team directory**. This applies to admins too. Press ⋮ to view a person or open their HR file.

**Human Resources > People > Org chart** draws a diagram from the manager on each person's HR file. Each box shows the person's photo, name, job title, centre, employment type (for example Full-time or Contract), when they started and who they report to. Email and phone appear only if the person chose to share them. Press a box to see the person's details. Press ▾ under a box to fold that team away, and use −, **Fit** and + to zoom. Pick a centre to see only that centre; the managers above its staff stay on the chart in dashed boxes. Anyone without a manager is listed under **No manager set**, so you can fix it on their file.

Press **🖨 Print / Save as PDF** for a landscape chart with your agency's logo, name, address, phone and email, the centre you picked and today's date, with page numbers. To keep a PDF, choose **Save as PDF** in the print window. A large chart is split by team across pages, and the box where a team continues says which page it is on. Staff can open the same chart from **My HR > Team directory > Org chart**, showing the people at their own centres.

## Recognition (kudos)

Open **Human Resources > Educator awards > Recognition**. Anyone on staff can thank a colleague with a short message and up to four values. Everyone at the recipient's centres sees the team feed. There is no leaderboard and there are no prizes. Each kudos adds points to that person's Educator awards total, with a daily cap, so it counts toward the awards you already run. Nobody can send kudos to themselves.

The sender, or a manager of the person who got it, can press ⋮ > **Take it down**. It leaves the feed and stops counting.

Agency admins can press **Recognition settings** to switch kudos off, change the list of values, or limit how many kudos each person can give in a month.

## Course quizzes

In **Human Resources > Performance & learning > Courses**, press ⋮ > **Add a quiz** on a course. Add questions with one or several right answers, a pass mark and how many tries are allowed. Once a course has a quiz, people finish it by passing; the certificate is issued only then. They see how many they got right, not which ones. If someone runs out of tries, open the course's assignments and press ⋮ > **Allow another try**. You can't do this for yourself.

## Who approves time off

Time off is approved by an agency admin or a director of the person's centre. Agency admins can add more in **Human Resources > HR settings > Approvers**:

- **Add an approver for named people**: let a director or admin approve chosen people's time off, even at another centre. You can limit it to some leave types.
- **Set cover for a director**: while a director is away, another director or admin gets that director's time-off approvals and emails, for that director's centres only. Cover lasts up to 92 days and ends by itself after the last day.

A director can also set their own cover under **My HR > Cover while I'm away**. Nobody ever approves their own request. Every grant, cover and decision made through one is in the audit log, and ⋮ > **End now** stops it early.
