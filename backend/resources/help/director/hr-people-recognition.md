# HR: directory, org chart, kudos and who approves time off

Four parts of the KiddieTrac HR add-on that are about how your team fits together: the staff **Directory** and **Org chart** (under **Human Resources > People**), **Recognition** (thank-you kudos, under **Human Resources > Educator awards**) and **Approvers** (who approves whose time off, under **Human Resources > HR settings**). Agency admins see the whole agency; directors see the staff at their own centres.

[[open: #hr-people?tab=directory | Open the Directory]]

## Looking someone up in the directory

1. Open **Human Resources > People** and press the **Directory** tab.
2. Find the person in the list: **Name**, **Role**, **Centres**, **Manager** and **Contact**.
3. Press **⋮ > View** to see their centres, manager, **Reports to them**, email and phone.
4. Press **⋮ > Open HR file** to go to their employee file, or **🙌 Give kudos** in the View window to thank them.

> ℹ️ Email and phone show only when the person chose to share them under **My HR > Team directory**. Otherwise you see **Not shared**. This applies to admins too.

## Reading the org chart

1. Press the **Org chart** tab. The chart is drawn from the **Manager** on each person's HR file.
2. Each box shows photo, name, job title, centre, employment type, when they started and who they report to.
3. Press ▾ under a box to fold that team away, and ▸ to open it again.
4. Use **−**, **Fit** and **+** to zoom, and drag the background to move around.
5. Pick a centre to see only that centre. Managers above its staff stay on the chart in dashed boxes, so the line upward is never cut.
6. Anyone without a manager is grouped under **No manager set**. Set it with **People > ⋮ > Edit file > Manager**.

[[show: #hr-people?tab=orgchart @ Fit | Show me the zoom buttons]]

## Printing the org chart

1. Pick the centre you want (or all centres).
2. Press **🖨 Print / Save as PDF**.
3. In the print window, choose your printer, or **Save as PDF** to keep a file.

The chart prints in landscape with your agency's logo, name, address, phone and email, the centre you picked, today's date and page numbers. A large chart is split by team across pages; each box where a team continues says which page it is on.

## Giving kudos

1. Open **Human Resources > Educator awards** and press the **Recognition** tab.
2. Press **🙌 Give kudos**.
3. Choose **Who** to thank.
4. Tick up to four **Values it shows**.
5. Write **Your message** and press **Send kudos**. A "Kudos sent" toast appears; the person sees it on the team feed and in their bell.

Everyone at the person's centres sees the feed. There is no leaderboard and there are no prizes. Each kudos adds points to that person's Educator awards total, with a daily cap. Nobody can send kudos to themselves.

## Taking a kudos down

1. Find the kudos in the feed.
2. Press **⋮ > Take it down**.
3. Press **Take down** to confirm. It leaves the feed and stops counting toward points.

Only the sender, or a manager of the person thanked, can do this.

## Changing the recognition settings (agency admins)

1. On the **Recognition** tab, press **Recognition settings**.
2. Untick **Staff can give each other kudos** to switch kudos off.
3. Edit the **Values (one per line)**.
4. Set **Kudos each person can give a month (0 = no limit)**.
5. Press **Save**. The points per kudos are set in **Educator awards > Award settings**.

## Choosing who approves time off (agency admins)

Time off is approved by an agency admin or a director of the person's centre. To add more:

1. Open **Human Resources > HR settings** and press the **Approvers** tab.
2. Press **Add an approver for named people**.
3. Choose **Who approves** (a director or admin).
4. Tick the people under **Whose time off** (use **Search names**).
5. Tick **Leave types** to limit it, or tick none for every type.
6. Press **Save**. They can now approve those people's time off, even at another centre.

## Setting cover while a director is away

1. On the **Approvers** tab, press **Set cover for a director**.
2. Choose the **Director who is away** and **Who covers**.
3. Set the **First day** and **Last day** (up to 92 days).
4. Press **Save cover**. The cover gets that director's time-off approvals and emails, for that director's centres only, and it ends by itself after the last day.

A director can set their own cover in **My HR > Cover while I'm away > Set cover**. To stop a grant or cover early, press **End now** on its row.

## Course quizzes

Quizzes on courses are set up in **Human Resources > Performance & learning > Courses**. See [[hr-performance]].

## Who can do what

| | Agency admin | Centre director | Staff |
|---|---|---|---|
| Directory and org chart | Whole agency | Own centres | Own centres, from **My HR > Team directory** |
| Give kudos | Yes | Yes | Yes, from **My HR** |
| Take a kudos down | As manager or sender | As manager or sender | Own kudos only |
| Recognition settings | Yes | No | No |
| Approvers and cover | Yes | Own cover only | No |

Nobody ever approves their own time off. Every grant, cover and decision made through one is in the audit log.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? Someone's email and phone say "Not shared". | They have not chosen to share them. Only the person can change that, in **My HR > Team directory**.
??? Someone is in the wrong place on the org chart. | The chart follows the **Manager** on each HR file. Fix it with **People > ⋮ > Edit file**.
??? "Recognition is switched off for your agency." | An agency admin turned kudos off. They can turn it back on in **Recognition settings**.
??? "None left this month". | Your agency limits how many kudos each person gives a month. The count resets next month.
??? The covering director is not getting emails. | Check the cover's dates on the **Approvers** tab. Cover starts on the first day and covers only the absent director's centres.
