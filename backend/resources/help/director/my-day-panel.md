# My day - the right panel

On a computer, agency admins, centre directors and super admins have a panel on the right edge of every screen, like the one in Outlook. It keeps your calendar, meetings, tasks and today's numbers one click away while you work.

## Open and close it

- The thin bar on the right has four icons: **Calendar**, **Meetings**, **Tasks** and **Today at the centre**. Click one to open the panel on that section.
- Click the same icon again, or the **›** arrow at the top of the panel, to fold it back to the thin bar.
- KiddieTrac remembers whether you like it open or closed, and which section you used last.
- On a wide screen the panel sits beside the screen and the screen makes room for it. On a smaller laptop screen it opens over the right side of the screen and closes when you click anywhere else.
- It isn't shown on phones or narrow windows - the bottom bar is there instead.

## Calendar

A small month calendar. Dots mark days with meetings (teal), centre dates such as birthdays and absences (amber) and task due dates (purple). Click a day to see its agenda underneath. **New meeting** books a meeting on that day, and **Open calendar** goes to the full Calendar.

## Meetings

Your meetings for the next 45 days, grouped by day. Click one to see the details and reply; **Join** opens the video link. **New meeting** opens the same meeting form as the Calendar.

## Tasks

- **Assigned to me** and **Assigned by me** list the open tasks.
- Click the circle to mark a task done.
- Type in **Add a task…**, pick who it's for and an optional due date, then **Assign task** - the same as assigning from Staff → Tasks.
- **All tasks** opens the Tasks screen.

## Today at the centre

Children **At Centre** (of those enrolled), **Staff on floor**, Safe Arrival children **Not arrived** yet, and **Ratio alerts**. A centre director sees their centre; an agency admin sees every centre. Click a number to open the screen behind it. It refreshes on its own every couple of minutes while it's open; the **↻** button refreshes it now.

Everything in the panel follows what you can already see - your agency, and for a director, your centres.
