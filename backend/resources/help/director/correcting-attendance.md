# Correcting a missed sign-in or sign-out

**Sign in** and **Sign out** always record the time you press them. That's right at the door, and wrong when an educator forgot to sign a child out at 4:15 and you notice at 5:30. **Fix** records what really happened, at the time it happened, with a reason.

Only centre directors and agency admins can correct attendance.

## Where it is

- **Daily Overview** (agency admins: sidebar → **Overview** → **Daily Overview**) — every child's row has **⏱ Fix**, for today and for earlier days.
- **Today** — on each child in a room card, **⏱ Fix a missed entry**.

[[open: #provider-day | Open Daily Overview]]

## Making a correction

1. Press **⏱ Fix** next to the child.
2. **Which day** — today or an earlier day, up to 30 days back. Under it you'll see what's **Already recorded** for that day.
3. Fill in **Arrived**, **Left**, or both — whichever is missing.
4. **Why is this being added late?** Choose a reason:
   - Educator forgot to sign the child in
   - Educator forgot to sign the child out
   - Parent / guardian forgot to sign in or out
   - QR code, kiosk or app was not working
   - Wrong time was recorded
   - Child left early / arrived late and it was not recorded
   - Signed in or out on the wrong child
   - Other (explain in the notes)
5. **Notes** — optional, but **required for Other**. Say who noticed and when, or anything that helps.
6. Press **Save**.

The reason and notes go on the permanent record with your name.

## Removing a wrong entry

Under **Already recorded**, press **Remove** next to the wrong time and confirm. Then add the right one. Removals are in the audit log too.

## What KiddieTrac won't accept

- A time in the future.
- A day more than 30 days ago.
- A day that doesn't make sense, such as a sign-out before the sign-in, or two arrivals with no departure between them.

## Who is told

- **The family.** Each parent gets an email and an in-app notice: *"Maya's attendance record was updated"*, with the day and the times entered. It's worded as a correction to a day that has passed, never as "has arrived", and it says there's nothing they need to do. Your reason is **not** shown to them. If a wrong entry was removed, they're told which time was taken off. Pick-up logins aren't emailed.
- **Staff.** The child's centre directors and your agency's admins get an email: **Attendance corrected** or **Attendance entry removed**, naming who made the change.

## On the record

A corrected entry is flagged **(added later)**, carries the name of the person who entered it, and is audited with both the time it happened and the time it was entered. It is never a quiet edit.

??? Can educators fix a time? | No. They sign in and out at the door. If a time is wrong, they tell the director.
??? A parent says the times are wrong. | The email invites them to reply or speak to the centre. Check **Already recorded** for that day, remove the wrong entry and add the right one.
