# Allergy and dietary alerts

The Allergy alerts screen lists every child who has an allergy, a dietary need or a health alert on file, so you can check them at a glance, print them for the kitchen and fix anything that is missing. Whatever you record here is the same information the child's record holds: educators see it straight away on their roster, the day brief and the weekly menu.

[[open: #allergy-alerts | Open Allergy alerts]]

## Reading the screen

1. Open **Programs > Allergy alerts** in the sidebar. The page opens with the heading **⚠ Allergy & dietary alerts**.
2. Read the four tiles across the top: **Anaphylactic** (children who need an EpiPen, marked "🚨 EpiPen required"), **Total allergies**, **Dietary** and **Children affected**.
3. Scroll the list of cards. Each card shows one child and a tag for every item on file, such as **ALLERGY** Peanuts, **DIETARY** No dairy or **HEALTH** Asthma. An anaphylactic allergy starts with 🚨.
4. When there are more than ten children, use the page buttons under the cards to move through the list.

> ℹ️ Only children with something recorded appear here. A child who is missing from the list has nothing on file, which is not the same as having no allergies. Confirm with each family and record the answer.

## Adding a record for a child

1. Press **+ Add a record** (right side, above the list).
2. In the **Which child?** popup, type in **Search by name…** and press the child's name. Every enrolled child in your centres is listed, with their room.
3. The child's health popup opens with three sections: **🥜 Allergies**, **🍽️ Dietary needs** and **🩺 Health alerts**. Press **＋ Add** beside the section you need.
4. For an allergy, type the **Allergen** (for example "Peanuts") and choose a **Severity**: **Mild**, **Moderate** or **Anaphylactic**.
5. Fill in **Reaction** (for example "Hives, swelling") and **Action plan** (what to do first).
6. If the child carries an EpiPen, tick **EpiPen required** and type where it is kept, for example "Room 2 cupboard".
7. For a dietary need, type the **Restriction** (for example "No dairy") and any **Details** the kitchen needs. For a health alert, type the **Alert** (for example "Asthma") and the **Detail** an educator should watch for.
8. Add anything else in **Medical notes** or **Dietary notes** at the bottom.
9. Press **Save**. A **Saved** toast says the record is updated and that educators see it straight away. The list refreshes.

> ⚠️ If an anaphylactic allergy needs an EpiPen but the location is blank, KiddieTrac asks "Save anyway?" once. Add the location if you can: educators are told to look for it.

## Changing or removing an item

1. Find the child's card and press **✏️** (**Edit allergies & dietary needs**).
2. Change any field, or press **✕** (**Remove**) on the row you no longer need.
3. Press **Save**. When a child has nothing left on file, their card leaves the list.

To see the whole child record instead, press **👁️** (**View child record**) on the card.

## Who can do what

- Agency admins see and edit every child in the agency.
- Centre directors see and edit the children in their own centres.
- Platform admins see the agency they are working in.
- Educators do not open this screen. They see the same alerts on their roster, the day brief and the weekly menu.
- Parents do not see this screen.

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? The list says "No active alerts." Is that right? | It means nothing has been recorded yet, not that no child has an allergy. Press **+ Add a record** and record what each family tells you, even when the answer is none.
??? The Which child? popup says "Could not load children". | The list of children did not load. Close the popup, refresh the page and try again. If it keeps happening, contact support.
??? I pressed Save and saw "That could not be saved." | The change was not stored. Check your connection, press **Save** again, and if it still fails, contact support.
??? Is this the same as the allergies on the child's record? | Yes. Both use one editor and one set of information, so a change here shows on the child's record and the other way round.
??? Do families get an email when I add an allergy? | No. Saving updates what staff see. Families are not notified from this screen.
