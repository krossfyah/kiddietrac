# Closure reminder emails

Families and educators are told when a closure is **added**, and again as it
approaches, so nobody arrives at a locked door.


## Quick steps

1. Go to **Settings → Email settings → Reminders**.
2. Switch on the lead times you want: straight away, and 5, 3 or 1 days before.
3. Save. The switches are per agency.
4. Add a closure as usual — everyone affected is told immediately, then again at each
   lead time you chose.

Editing or deleting a closure sends a **change** notice rather than a fresh closure
notice, so nobody is told a centre is closing when the only news is that a date moved.

## Turning them on

**Settings → Email settings → Reminders.** Choose which lead times to send:

- straight away, when the closure is entered
- 5 days before
- 3 days before
- 1 day before

Each is a separate switch, and they are per agency.

## Who gets what

- **Parents** get the dates, which centre, and what it means for fees.
- **Educators** get the same dates worded for someone who works there.
- **Admins and directors** are copied, so you can see what went out.

## Fees

The parent wording does not promise what the invoice will say. It states that fees are
adjusted where the agreement between the family and the agency calls for it — because
whether a closure is billable is an agreement, not a system setting.

## Worth knowing

- Each reminder is sent once per closure per lead time; editing a closure does not
  re-send the ones already gone.
- Editing or deleting a closure sends a **change** notice rather than a fresh closure
  notice — telling somebody a centre is closing when the only news is that the date
  moved is how people learn to ignore the notices.
- Reminders respect the agency, centre and room email switches like every other email.
