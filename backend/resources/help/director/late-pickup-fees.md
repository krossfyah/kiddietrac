# Late pickups and fees

Everything about a late pickup (or a late arrival) lives in one place. Staff log it, the
fee is worked out for you, and a director or admin decides whether it is charged. Open it
from the sidebar: **Finance** → **Late pickups**.

[[open: #late-pickups | Open Late pickups]]

## How the fee is worked out

The suggested fee is a rate per minute past the centre's close time, after a few free
grace minutes — by default **$1.00 a minute after 5 minutes' grace**. The log form shows the
rate in use, for example "($1.00/min after 5 min grace)". Each centre's close time is filled
in as the default when you log. An agency admin sets the rate and the grace minutes in **Settings → Billing → ⚙️ Defaults & fees**,
in the **Late pickup fee** card. A new rate applies to pickups logged after it is saved.

## Logging a late pickup

1. Press **＋ Log late pickup**. The **Log a late pickup** window opens.
2. Under **What**, leave **Late pickup**, or choose **Late arrival**.
3. Choose the **Child**.
4. For a late pickup, set **Picked up at** and check **Centre closed at** (filled in from the child's centre). For a late arrival, enter the **Minutes late**.
5. Add any **Notes (optional)**. The form shows the minutes late and the **suggested fee**, or "within the grace period — no fee".
6. Press **Log for review**. It waits on the **Pending** tab for a director or admin, and they are emailed. This is the only option for educators.
7. Or, as a director or admin, press **Log & charge** to log it and charge the fee in one step. For a late arrival you are asked **How much to charge for this late arrival?** first.

You see **Logged — waiting for a director or admin to decide**, **Logged and charged …**, or
**Logged — within the grace period, no fee** (nothing to charge, so it is recorded as waived).

## Deciding on a pending late pickup

1. Open the **Pending** tab. The badge shows how many are waiting.
2. On the row, press **Approve & charge**. Check the **Fee** (filled in with the suggestion), add a **Note (optional)** and press **Charge fee**. The fee goes on the family's open invoice, or on their next invoice if none is open, and the parents are told.
3. Or press **Waive** — "It stays on record; nothing is charged." Add a note and press **Waive**.
4. Or press **Decline** — "Recorded as not chargeable — use this when it should not have been logged." Add a note and press **Decline**.

A record can only be decided once.

## The list

Use the tabs **Pending**, **Charged**, **Waived**, **Declined** and **All**. Each row shows the
**Child** (with the family and centre), **What**, **Picked up / arrived** (with the close time),
**Late by**, **Suggested**, **Status**, **Fee charged**, **Logged**, **Logged by**, **Decided**,
the **Invoice** it went on (or **not yet billed**) and **Notes**. Use the export buttons under
the table for Excel, CSV or PDF.

## Emails

The centre's directors and the agency's admins are emailed when something is logged for
review, and when a fee is logged and charged in one step. Parents are told when a fee is
charged; nothing is sent to them when a late pickup is logged, waived or declined.

## Who can do what

- **Agency admins** see every centre, log, charge, approve, waive and decline.
- **Centre directors** do the same for their own centres.
- **Educators** log late pickups and late arrivals for review only. They do not see **Log & charge** or the decision buttons.
- **Parents** see a charged fee on their invoice.

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? It says "The pickup time must be after the close time." | Picked up at must be later than Centre closed at. Check both, including the date.
??? It says "Enter a fee, or use Waive instead." | Approve & charge needs a fee above $0. To record it without charging, use Waive.
??? The fee was charged but the Invoice column says "not yet billed". | The family had no open invoice. The fee is recorded and goes on their next invoice.
??? I don't see Approve & charge. | Only directors and agency admins decide. Educators log for review.
??? The Pending tab was empty, so it showed All. | When nothing is waiting, the screen opens on All so you see your history.
