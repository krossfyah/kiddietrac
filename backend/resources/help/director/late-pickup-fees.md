# Late pickups and fees

Everything about a late pickup (or a late arrival) lives in one place: sidebar → **Late pickups**. Staff log it, the fee is worked out for you, and a director or admin decides whether it is charged.

## Fee settings

Sidebar → **Administration → Billing settings** (agency admin):

- **Late-pickup per minute** — dollars per minute past close (default $1.00).
- **Grace minutes** — free minutes before the fee starts (default 5).

Each centre's close time is used as the default close time when logging.

## Logging a late pickup

1. Sidebar → **Late pickups** → **＋ Log late pickup**.
2. Choose the child, the time they were picked up and the time the centre closed (filled in from the centre). For a late arrival, choose **Late arrival** and enter the minutes.
3. The form shows the minutes late and the **suggested fee** (per-minute rate after the grace period).
4. Choose:
   - **Log for review** — it waits in **Pending** for a director or admin (they are emailed). This is the only option for educators.
   - **Log & charge** (directors and admins) — logged and charged in one step.

## Deciding

On a pending row, a director or admin chooses:

- **Approve & charge** — the fee (prefilled with the suggestion, editable) is added to the family's open invoice, or recorded for their next invoice if none is open. The parents are notified of the fee.
- **Waive** — kept on record, nothing charged.
- **Decline** — recorded as not chargeable (it should not have been logged).

A record can only be decided once.

## The list

Filter by **Pending**, **Charged**, **Waived**, **Declined** or **All**. Each row shows the pickup time (as entered), minutes late, the suggested fee, the status, the fee charged, when and by whom it was logged, who decided and when, and the invoice it went on. Use the export buttons under the table for Excel, CSV or PDF.

## Emails

The centre's directors and the agency's admins are emailed when something is logged for review, and when a fee is logged and charged in one step.
