# Calendar settings

Calendar settings decide what your agency's calendar shows: closures and holidays, birthdays, who is away, and whether families and staff can sync your dates to their own phone calendar. Everything is on by default. Switch a layer off when it makes the calendar too busy to read. A layer you switch off is left out of the calendar completely, not just hidden, so nobody in your agency sees it.

[[open: #calendar-settings | Open Calendar settings]]

**Where it is:** sidebar → **Settings** → **Calendar settings**.

## Choosing what the calendar shows

1. Open **Settings** → **Calendar settings**. The card **📅 What the calendar shows** lists every layer with a tick box.
2. Under **Closures and holidays**, keep **Show closures and holidays** ticked to mark the days your centres are closed. A closed day is drawn above everything else on that day.
3. Under **Birthdays**, tick **Show birthdays** to turn birthdays on. This is the master switch for the two boxes below it.
4. Tick **Children’s birthdays**, **Staff birthdays**, or both. When **Show birthdays** is off, these two boxes are greyed out and cannot be changed. Staff birthdays only appear for people with a date of birth on their profile.
5. Under **Who is away**, tick the kinds of absence you want to see: **Child absences** (with the reason the parent or educator gave), **Family vacations** (booked holds on a child's place) and **Staff time off** (approved leave and vacation).
6. Tick **Include requests that are still pending** if you also want time off that has not been approved yet. This helps when you plan cover. Leave it off to see only settled leave.
7. Press **Save changes**. The button reads **Saving…** for a moment, then **✓ Saved** appears beside it and a **Calendar settings saved** message shows at the top.

> 💡 The calendar reads the records your agency already keeps (closures, the child health and attendance records, time-off requests). If a layer is on but empty, the records are missing, not the setting.

## Turning calendar sync on or off

Families and staff can add a private link to their Apple, Google or Outlook calendar from **Settings → Calendar** in their app. This switch controls those links for your whole agency.

1. Scroll to **Calendar sync** at the bottom of the card.
2. Untick **Allow calendar sync** to stop every link in your agency from showing your dates (closures, events, trips, absences and shifts).
3. Press **Save changes**.
4. To bring it back, tick **Allow calendar sync** again and press **Save changes**. Everyone's existing link starts working again; nobody has to add it a second time.

> ⚠️ Only an agency admin can change calendar sync. A centre director sees the switch greyed out with the note "Only an agency admin can change this."

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Change every layer and the **Allow calendar sync** switch. |
| Centre director | Change the layers. Sees **Allow calendar sync** but cannot change it. |
| Platform admin | The same as an agency admin, for the agency they are working in. |
| Educators and parents | Do not see this screen. They see the calendar the way you set it here. |

The settings apply to the whole agency, not one centre.

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? The screen says "This page is available to directors and agency administrators". | Your account is not an agency admin or centre director for this agency. Ask your agency admin to open the screen, or to check your role.
??? I unticked a layer but still see it on the calendar. | Press **Save changes** after changing a box, then reload the calendar. A layer only disappears once the change is saved.
??? The two birthday boxes are greyed out. | **Show birthdays** is off. Tick it first, then choose **Children’s birthdays** or **Staff birthdays**.
??? A staff member's birthday is missing. | Staff birthdays only show for people with a date of birth on their profile. Add it to their profile and it appears on the calendar.
??? I see "Save failed:" with a message. | The change was not saved. Check your connection and press **Save changes** again. If it keeps failing, the message says why; contact KiddieTrac support with that text.
??? Does switching off calendar sync delete anyone's link? | No. The links stop showing your dates while sync is off and start again when you turn it back on.
