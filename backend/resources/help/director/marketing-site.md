# Website control center

**Platform › 🌍 Website** runs KiddieTrac's own public website, www.kiddietrac.com: who subscribed, website chats, contact details, homepage text, search settings, the announcement bar, visitor numbers and the French and Spanish translations. It is for platform super-admins only; no agency sees it. Changes go live on the site within about a minute.

[[open: #marketing-site | Open Website]]

The screen has eight tabs: **Overview**, **Subscribers**, **Chat**, **Content**, **SEO**, **Announcement**, **Analytics** and **Translations**.

## Checking how the website is doing

1. Open **Platform › 🌍 Website**. It starts on **Overview**.
2. Read the tiles: **Subscribers / leads**, **Pageviews (14 days)**, **Pageviews (all-time)** and **Conversion** (leads divided by views over 14 days).
3. Check **Status**: whether the **Announcement bar** is **Live** or **Hidden**, whether **Google Analytics 4** is set, and whether **Custom homepage SEO** is **Set** or on **Site defaults**.
4. Scroll to the charts: **Visits and leads — last 30 days**, **Activity — last 8 weeks**, **Where visitors are**, **Where leads come from**, **Subscribers**, **Meeting requests** and **Most-read pages**.
5. Use **Open marketing site ↗** or **View sitemap ↗** to see the live site.

## Managing subscribers

1. Open the **Subscribers** tab. It has two sub-tabs, **Subscribed** and **Unsubscribed**, each with a count.
2. To add someone, press **+ Add subscriber**, type the email and press **Add subscriber**.
3. To stop someone getting marketing email, press **🚫** (**Unsubscribe**) on their row, or open **Unsubscribed** and press **+ Add to unsubscribed**. You can add a reason and choose **Email them a confirmation?**
4. To bring someone back, press **↩️** (**Re-subscribe**) on the **Unsubscribed** list.
5. To remove a row for good, press **🗑️** (**Delete**) and confirm.

> ℹ️ An unsubscribed address never gets marketing email until it is re-subscribed. Website subscribers are KiddieTrac's own list, separate from any agency's families.

## Reading and filing website chats

1. Open the **Chat** tab. **Active** and **Archived** show how many conversations each holds.
2. Press a conversation to open the whole transcript, including replies your team sent from the live inbox. Times are Toronto time.
3. Press its **⋮** for **⬇️ Download transcript**, **🗄️ Archive** (or **↩️ Move back to Active**) or **🗑️ Delete**.

## Editing the site's content

1. Open **Content**.
2. Change what you need in **Contact details** (**Contact email**, **Chat transcripts to**, **Phone**, **Address**), **Social links**, **Homepage hero statistics** or **Spam protection (reCAPTCHA)**.
3. Press **Save changes** under that card. Each card saves on its own.

Leave a field blank to use the site's built-in default. reCAPTCHA needs both keys and the **Require Google reCAPTCHA** box before it switches on.

## Search, announcement and analytics settings

1. **SEO**: set **Homepage title tag**, **Homepage meta description** and **Social share image (OG image) URL**, then **Save changes**.
2. **Announcement**: tick **Show the announcement bar at the top of the site**, fill **Badge text**, **Message**, **Button text** and **Button links to page**, then **Save changes**.
3. **Analytics**: read the first-party pageviews, **Top pages**, **Leads by source** and **Top countries**, and enter a **GA4 Measurement ID** if you use Google Analytics. Google Analytics loads only after a visitor accepts cookies.

## Reviewing translations

1. Open **Translations**. A note says whether automatic translation is on, paused or off.
2. Choose **To review — automatic**, **Edited** or **All**, and a language, or search with **Search English or translation**.
3. Correct the text if needed and press **Approve** (or **Save** on an edited row). Edited rows are never overwritten.
4. Press **Delete** to make the site ask for a fresh automatic translation next time a visitor sees that text.

## Who can do what

- **Platform super-admins**: everything on this screen.
- **Agency admins and every other role**: cannot use it. Anyone else who reaches it sees **Super-admin only**.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? I saved a change but the website still shows the old text. | Changes take about a minute to go live. Refresh the site after that; your browser may also be showing a cached copy.
??? "Could not load website data" appeared. | The connection dropped or your session ended. Refresh the screen.
??? "Enter a full email address." | The address you typed is not complete. Check for a missing @ or domain.
??? Translations say "Automatic translation is paused". | The translation service is out of credit or failed. New text shows in English to French and Spanish visitors until it is fixed.
??? Where do chat transcripts get emailed? | To **Chat transcripts to** on **Content**, or the **Contact email** when that is blank.
