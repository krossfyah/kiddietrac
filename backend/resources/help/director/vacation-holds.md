# Vacation holds

Pause tuition while a family is away on vacation. Parent requests, director approves with a credit amount.

## Parent: requesting

1. Sidebar → **Vacation hold**.
2. Tap **+ Request hold**.
3. Pick start + end dates and add a reason.
4. Submit. The centre director gets a notification.

## Director / admin: approving

1. Sidebar → **Vacation holds**.
2. Pending requests are highlighted.
3. Tap **Approve** or **Deny**.
4. For approval, enter a credit amount (or leave blank to auto-compute as `$30/day × days requested`).
5. The credit is recorded for application to the next invoice.

## How the credit shows up

- A `vacation_holds` row records the approved amount.
- On the next invoice generation, the credit applies as a negative line.
- Family sees the credit on their ledger immediately.

## Limits

No hard limits, but typical centre policies cap vacation holds at 2 weeks per year. Enforce via policy, not the system.
