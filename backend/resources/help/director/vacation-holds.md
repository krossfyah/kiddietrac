# Vacation holds

**Vacation holds** pauses a family's tuition while they are away. A family asks for a hold (or you add one for them), you approve or deny it, and an approved hold carries a credit for the family. Directors and agency admins open it when a family says they will be away, and to clear requests waiting for an answer.

[[open: #vacation-holds | Open Vacation holds]]

## Approving or denying a request

New requests show the status **requested**, with **Approve** and **Deny** on the row. Directors and agency admins get a notification when a family asks — "Vacation hold requested by" the family, with the dates.

1. Open **Administration › Vacation holds**.
2. Find the row with the status **requested**. Check **Family**, **Centre**, **Dates** and **Reason**.
3. Press **Approve**. The **Approve vacation hold** box opens.
4. Type a credit amount for the family, or leave the box blank to calculate it automatically.
5. Press **Approve**. The row changes to **approved** and the **Credit** column shows the amount.
6. To refuse instead, press **Deny** on the row. It changes to **denied** at once; there is no box to fill in.

Pressing **Cancel** in the approval box leaves the request as it was — nothing is approved.

> ⚠️ A blank amount is an estimate of **$30 a day** for every day of the hold, start and end days included (a 10-day hold = $300). If your daily rate is different, type the real amount.

## Adding a hold for a family

Use this when a family tells you by phone, email or at the door.

1. Press **+ Add hold** in the banner.
2. In **🏖 Add vacation hold**, pick the family under **Family** (**Select a family…**).
3. Set **From** and **To**. The end date can't be before the start date.
4. Add a **Reason (optional)**, such as "Family holiday".
5. Press **Add hold**. The hold joins the list as **requested**, and the family's guardians are told "A vacation hold was added to your account".
6. Approve it with the steps above to set the credit.

## What the family sees

- When you approve, every guardian gets a notification: "Vacation hold approved — Credit of $… will be applied."
- When you deny, they get "Vacation hold denied — Unable to approve at this time."
- The notification opens their **Billing** screen.

> ℹ️ The credit is recorded on the hold. Make sure it reaches the family's bill: check their next invoice in **Billing**, and add the credit there if it is not on it. See [[family-ledger]].

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | See every hold in the agency, add holds, approve or deny |
| Centre director | See, add, approve and deny holds for families in their own centres only; other centres' holds are not listed |
| Platform admin | The same as an agency admin, in the agency being viewed |
| Parent | Asks for a hold for their own family and is told the decision |

Educators do not see this screen.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? There is no Approve or Deny button on a row. | The buttons only show while a hold is **requested**. An approved or denied hold is final; add a new hold if the dates change.
??? "Family and both dates are required." | Pick a family and fill in both **From** and **To**, then press **Add hold** again.
??? "No families loaded — select an agency first." | The family list is empty because no agency is chosen. Choose your agency in the agency switcher and open the screen again.
??? I pressed Cancel by mistake. | Nothing changed — the hold is still **requested**. Press **Approve** again.
??? The credit was wrong. | A decided hold can't be edited here. Adjust the family's next invoice in **Billing**, or ask your agency admin.
??? The list says "No vacation holds." | No family has asked for a hold and none were added. Use **+ Add hold** to add one.
??? Is there a limit on how long a hold can be? | Not in KiddieTrac. Many centres allow up to two weeks a year — apply your own policy when you approve.
