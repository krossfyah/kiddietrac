# Announcements

**Operations → Announcements.** Post a notice to families, staff, or both, across the
agency or at one centre. People see it in the portal and, if you choose, by email, text
message and app notification.

[[open: #announcements | Open Announcements]]

## Posting an announcement

1. Open **Announcements** and press the **+** button (**New announcement**).
2. **Where**: **🏢 Everywhere — all centres**, or one centre. Only agency admins can send
   agency-wide. A centre director picks one of their centres.
3. **Who**: choose the audience (see below). The line under the box reads the two
   choices back as one sentence, for example "Every educator across the whole agency".
4. **Picture (optional)**: a JPEG, PNG or GIF, up to 5 MB.
5. Enter a **Title** and write the body. Use the toolbar for bold, italic, underline,
   lists and links. Formatting is kept in the emailed copy.
6. Choose how it goes out: **📧 Send email**, **📱 Send SMS**, **🔔 In-app notification**.
   Email and in-app are ticked by default.
7. To send it later, open **Schedule for later (optional)** and pick a date and time.
8. Press **Send**.

## Choosing who receives it

| Who | Reaches |
|---|---|
| **All parents** | Every parent with a child in the place you chose. This is the default. |
| **All educators** | Every educator there |
| **All contractors** | Everyone marked as a contractor |
| **All admins and directors** | Agency admins and centre directors |
| **Everyone** | Parents, educators, contractors, admins and directors |

When you send to one centre, staff attached to a different centre are left out. A notice
about one centre is not news at another. Staff attached to the agency as a whole, with no
centre of their own, still receive it.

> **Note:** Nobody is a contractor until you say so. There is no contractor role. On the **Staff** screen, switch on **Contractor** for each person the "All contractors" audience should reach.

## Pictures and text messages

The picture is sent with the text message. Large photos are resized automatically so
carriers will accept them. Where a phone cannot receive a picture message, the text
arrives with a link to the picture instead.

SMS sends as plain text, so keep it short. Texts only go to people with a mobile number
on file, and only when your agency's texting is set up. See
[Carrier settings](carrier-settings).

## How recipients get it

- **In-app**: a notification, plus the announcement in their feed (**News** for parents
  and educators).
- **Email**: the formatted version, sent from your agency's address when that is set up.
- **SMS**: the plain-text version, only if you ticked **📱 Send SMS**.

> Who can use this: **Centre director** (their centres), **Agency admin** (whole agency).
