# Announcements

**Operations → Announcements.** Post a notice to families, staff, or both, across the
agency or at one centre. People see it in the portal and, if you choose, by email, text
message and app notification.

[[open: #announcements | Open Announcements]]

## Posting an announcement

1. Open **Announcements** and press the **+** button (**New announcement**).
2. **Where**: **🏢 Everywhere — all centres**, or one centre. Only agency admins can send
   agency-wide. A centre director picks one of their centres.
3. **Who**: choose the audience (see below). The line under the box reads the two
   choices back as one sentence, for example "Every educator across the whole agency".
4. **Picture (optional)**: a JPEG, PNG or GIF, up to 5 MB.
5. Enter a **Title** and write the body. Use the toolbar for bold, italic, underline,
   lists and links. Formatting is kept in the emailed copy.
6. Choose how it goes out: **📧 Send email**, **📱 Send SMS**, **🔔 In-app notification**,
   and — for directors and agency admins — **📞 Voice call**. Email and in-app are ticked
   by default.
7. To send it later, open **Schedule for later (optional)** and pick a date and time, in
   your agency's time zone. It goes out within a minute of that time, by every channel
   you ticked — phone calls included — and shows **⏱ Scheduled** in the list until then.
8. Press **Send**.

## Choosing who receives it

| Who | Reaches |
|---|---|
| **All parents** | Every parent with a child in the place you chose. This is the default. |
| **All educators** | Every educator there |
| **All contractors** | Everyone marked as a contractor |
| **All admins and directors** | Agency admins and centre directors |
| **Everyone** | Parents, educators, contractors, admins and directors |

When you send to one centre, staff attached to a different centre are left out. A notice
about one centre is not news at another. Staff attached to the agency as a whole, with no
centre of their own, still receive it.

> **Note:** Nobody is a contractor until you say so. There is no contractor role. On the **Staff** screen, switch on **Contractor** for each person the "All contractors" audience should reach. With the HR add-on, the two stay in step: setting a person's employment type to **Contract** in their HR file switches **Contractor** on, and changing it to another type switches it off. Switching **Contractor** on here sets their HR type to Contract; switching it off clears a Contract type to "not set".

## Pictures and text messages

The picture is sent with the text message. Large photos are resized automatically so
carriers will accept them. Where a phone cannot receive a picture message, the text
arrives with a link to the picture instead.

SMS sends as plain text, so keep it short. Texts only go to people with a mobile number
on file, and only when your agency's texting is set up. See
[Carrier settings](carrier-settings).

## Sending it as a phone call

Tick **📞 Voice call** to also ring people's phones and read the announcement aloud —
"Announcement from *your centre*", then the title and message (up to 800 characters).

- [ ] Tick **📞 Voice call** and choose the **Reason for the call**.
- [ ] Keep the wording short and plain: it is read by a synthetic voice.
- [ ] Press **Send**, then **Place calls** to confirm. Calls cannot be taken back.

Only the reasons your agency allows are offered (**Settings → Carrier settings → Voice
calls**). If calls are switched off for your agency, the box says so and cannot be ticked.
The same rules as [Announcement calls](voice-announcements) apply to every person: anyone
who turned that reason off, or chose **Don't phone me at all**, is skipped; a
non-emergency only reaches people who agreed to be contacted; a shared phone rings once.
The result line reports how many are being called and how many were skipped.

> **Note:** A scheduled announcement with Voice call ticked rings people at the scheduled time, not when you press Send.

??? Why can't I see Voice call? | It is for centre directors and agency admins only, the same people who can place calls from SMS broadcast.
??? Why is Voice call greyed out? | Calls are switched off for your agency, or no call reasons are allowed. An agency admin sets both in Settings → Carrier settings → Voice calls.

## Asking people to do something by a date

1. In the new announcement, open **⏰ Asking for something? Due date & reminders (optional)**.
2. Set **Due by** to the date it is needed.
3. Under **Remind them (up to 2)**, pick **3 days before**, **1 day before**, **Morning of** or **Custom…**.
4. Under **Who gets the reminder**, choose only the people who have not tapped **✓ Done** yet, or **Everyone again**.
5. Press **Send**. Recipients see **⏰ Please do this by …** and a **✓ Mark as done** button.

To follow up, open the row's **⋮** and press **👁 View details**: **Who’s done?** lists each person or family and how
many of the total are done.

## Managing the list

Every row has a **⋮** menu: **👁 View details**, **🔁 Send again** (announcements you manage), **✓ Mark read** /
**● Mark unread** and **🗄 Archive** (your own list only), and **🗑 Delete** where you may remove it — deleting also
takes it out of every family's News. **🗄 Archived** at the top shows what you archived; **↩ Unarchive** brings it
back. Type in **🔍 Filter…** to find an announcement by its words.

## How recipients get it

- **In-app**: a notification, plus the announcement in their feed (**News** for parents
  and educators).
- **Email**: the formatted version, sent from your agency's address when that is set up.
- **SMS**: the plain-text version, only if you ticked **📱 Send SMS**.
- **Phone call**: the announcement read aloud, only if you ticked **📞 Voice call**.

> Who can use this: **Centre director** (their centres), **Agency admin** (whole agency).
