# Bus routes

For centres that pick up / drop off children at homes or schools.

## Setting up a route (admin)

1. Sidebar → **Bus routes**.
2. Tap **+ New route**.
3. Enter: route name (e.g. "Morning North Run"), assigned driver, vehicle label.
4. Save.

## Adding children to a route

1. Open the route.
2. Tap **+ Add child**.
3. Pick the child, pickup address, pickup time, drop-off time, and stop order on the route.
4. Save.

## At pickup time

The driver opens the route on their phone:
- See the ordered list of stops with addresses + times
- Mark each child "on board" as they're picked up
- GPS tracking (via field-trip GPS) can be enabled for parent live-view

## Notifications

Parents on the route get a notification when the bus is 5-10 minutes from their stop (manual trigger or based on GPS proximity if enabled).

## Editing

Routes can be paused (`active=0`) without losing the child roster. Useful for summer break or route restructuring.

## Common use cases

- Before-school pickup from neighbourhoods to the centre
- After-school dropoff back home
- Field-trip bus rosters (separate from regular routes)
- Multi-stop carpool coordination
