# Bus routes + transportation

For centres that pick children up from home or school, or drop them off. Each route
belongs to one centre and lists the children who ride it, in stop order, with their
pick-up address and times.

**Where it is:** sidebar → **Administration** → **Bus routes**. Agency admins see every
open centre. Centre directors see and manage only their own centres' routes.

[[open: #bus-routes | Open Bus routes]]

## Adding a route

1. Press **+ Add route**.
2. Enter a **Route name**, for example "Morning — North loop".
3. Pick the **Centre**.
4. Optionally pick a **Driver** (from your current staff) and enter the **Vehicle**, for example "Van 2 · ABCD 123".
5. Optionally add **Notes** for the route, such as "Use the side entrance on Maple St".
6. Under **Children on this route**, tick the children who ride it. Use **🔍 Find a child…** to search, or **Select all** and **Clear**. Only current children of the chosen centre are listed.
7. Press **Add route** (or **Save changes** when editing). The route appears in the list.

Children you tick are added to the end of the route, with their pick-up address filled
in from the family's address. You can then set each child's stop and times (see below).

> **Tip:** The list shows each route's centre, driver, vehicle, notes and how many children ride it.

## Setting each child's stop

1. On the route's row, press **🧒 Children**.
2. The table shows each child's **Stop**, **Pick-up address**, **Pick-up** and **Drop-off** times and **Notes**.
3. To add a child, fill in **➕ Add a child to this route** at the bottom: choose the **Child**, the **Stop #**, **Pick-up time**, **Drop-off time**, **Pick-up address** and any **Notes** (for example "Grandma meets the bus on Tuesdays" or "Needs a booster seat"). Press **Add to route**.
4. To change a child's stop, press **✏️ Edit** on their row, make your changes and press **Save changes**.
5. To take a child off the route, press **🗑 Remove**. They can be added again later.
6. Press **Done** when you are finished.

The pick-up address starts as the family's address. Change it if the child is picked up
somewhere else, such as a school.

## Editing a route

Press **✏️ Edit** on the route's row. You can change its name, centre, driver, vehicle,
notes and riders. Unticking a child takes them off the route. Children who were already
riding keep their stop, times, address and notes.

## Pausing or deleting a route

- **⏸ Pause** stops using a route for now, for example over the summer, and keeps its children and stops. Press **▶️ Resume** to start it again. You can also untick **Active (running)** when editing.
- **🗑 Delete** removes the route and takes every child off it. This cannot be undone, so use **Pause** if you may need the route again.

## Who can be on a route

- Only children currently enrolled at the route's centre. Withdrawn children drop off the list.
- The driver must be a current member of your staff.
- Routes can only be added to open centres. If you have none, the screen tells you.

??? Can parents see the bus route? | No. The Bus routes screen is for your staff; families do not see routes in their portal.
??? A child rides in the morning but not after school. How do I show that? | Leave the drop-off time empty and add a note on their stop, or set up separate morning and afternoon routes.
