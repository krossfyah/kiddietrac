# Bus routes + transportation

For centres that pick children up from home or school, or drop them off. Each route
belongs to one centre and lists the children who ride it, in stop order, with their
pick-up address and times. Open it from the sidebar: **Administration** → **Bus routes**.

[[open: #bus-routes | Open Bus routes]]

## Adding a route

1. Press **+ Add route**. The **Add a bus route** window opens.
2. Enter a **Route name**, for example "Morning — North loop".
3. Pick the **Centre**.
4. Optionally pick a **Driver** (from your current staff) and enter the **Vehicle**, for example "Van 2 · ABCD 123".
5. Optionally add **Notes** for the route, such as "Use the side entrance on Maple St".
6. Under **Children on this route**, tick the children who ride it. Use **🔍 Find a child…** to search, or **Select all** and **Clear**. Only current children of the chosen centre are listed.
7. Press **Add route**. A **Route added** message appears and the route shows in the list.

Children you tick are added to the end of the route, with their pick-up address filled
in from the family's address. You can then set each child's stop and times (see below).

> 💡 The list shows each route's **Centre**, **Driver**, **Vehicle**, **Riders** and **Status** (**Active** or **Paused**), with the route's notes under its name.

## Setting each child's stop

1. On the route's row, press **🧒 Children**. A window lists who rides the route.
2. The table shows each child's **Stop**, **Pick-up address**, **Pick-up** and **Drop-off** times and **Notes**.
3. To add a child, fill in **➕ Add a child to this route** at the bottom: choose the **Child**, the **Stop #**, **Pick-up time**, **Drop-off time**, **Pick-up address** and any **Notes** (for example "Grandma meets the bus on Tuesdays" or "Needs a booster seat").
4. Press **Add to route**. The child appears in the table.
5. To change a child's stop, press **✏️ Edit** on their row, make your changes and press **Save changes** (or **Cancel**).
6. To take a child off the route, press **🗑 Remove** and confirm. They can be added again later.
7. Press **Done** when you are finished.

The pick-up address starts as the family's address. Change it if the child is picked up
somewhere else, such as a school.

## Editing a route

1. Press **✏️ Edit** on the route's row. The **Edit route** window opens.
2. Change the name, centre, driver, vehicle, notes or riders. Unticking a child takes them off the route.
3. Press **Save changes**. Children who were already riding keep their stop, times, address and notes.

## Pausing or deleting a route

1. To stop using a route for now (for example over the summer), press **⏸ Pause**. Its children and stops are kept. You can also untick **Active (running)** when editing.
2. To start it again, press **▶️ Resume**.
3. To remove a route for good, press **🗑 Delete**, then **Delete route**. Every child is taken off it and this cannot be undone, so use **Pause** if you may need the route again.

## Who can do what

- **Agency admins** (and platform admins working in the agency) see every open centre and can add, change, pause and delete routes. Bus routes is in their **Administration** menu.
- **Centre directors** do not have Bus routes in their menu. If they open it from a link, they only see and manage their own centres' routes.
- **Educators and parents** do not see bus routes.
- Only children currently enrolled at the route's centre can ride it. Withdrawn children drop off the list.
- The driver must be a current member of your staff.

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? Can parents see the bus route? | No. The Bus routes screen is for your staff; families do not see routes in their portal.
??? A child rides in the morning but not after school. How do I show that? | Leave the drop-off time empty and add a note on their stop, or set up separate morning and afternoon routes.
??? It says "You have no open centres to add a route to." | Routes can only belong to an open centre. Open or add a centre first under Centres.
??? I pressed Add route and it says "Give the route a name." or "Choose the centre." | Both are required. Fill them in and press Add route again.
??? A child I want is not in the list. | Only current children of the chosen centre are listed. Check the centre at the top of the window, and that the child is enrolled and not withdrawn.
??? The child picker says "Every child at this centre is on the route". | Everyone at that centre already rides it. Use ✏️ Edit on a child's row to change their stop instead.
??? Is anyone notified when I change a route? | No. Adding, editing, pausing or deleting a route sends no messages to staff or families.
