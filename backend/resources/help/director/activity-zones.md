# Activity zones

Track which corner / centre / station each child engaged in during the day. Brightwheel-style activity logging.

## Setting up zones (director)

1. Sidebar → **Activity zones**.
2. Tap **+ New zone**.
3. Enter name (Art corner, Reading nook, Sensory bin, Outdoor play, etc.), emoji icon, and a colour.
4. Save. Repeat for each major activity area.

## Logging visits (educator)

1. Open **Activity zones**.
2. Each zone shows as a coloured tile with its emoji.
3. Tap **+ Log visit** on the relevant zone.
4. Enter the child ID.
5. Visit is recorded with timestamp.

Optional: enter a duration (in minutes). Without it, only the start time is logged.

## Daily report

Sidebar shows today's activity across all zones:
- Total visits + breakdown by zone
- Per-child timeline of which zones they visited and when

Useful at end-of-day to ensure each child engaged with diverse zones.

## Privacy

Zone visits are visible to staff at the centre and to guardians of the child. Other families can't see anyone else's visit history.

## Best practice

Start with 4-6 zones. Don't go overboard — you want educators to actually use the tracking, not feel overwhelmed.
