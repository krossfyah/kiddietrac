# Activity zones tracking

**Activity zones** track which play areas each child spends time in during the day: the art corner, the reading nook, the sensory bin, outdoor play. You set up the zones once for a room or for the whole centre, educators log a visit with two taps, and the screen shows today's visits per zone and per child so you can see who is getting a varied day.

[[open: #zones | Open Activity zones]]

The banner shows what you are looking at (the centre and room), today's date and the number of visits logged today.

## Choosing the centre and room

1. Open **Administration > Activity zones**.
2. If your agency has more than one centre, pick one in **Select a centre…**.
3. Use the centre list at the top to switch centre, and the room list to choose one room or **All rooms**.

Educators see only their own rooms. If they have more than one, they switch with the **All my rooms** chip and a chip for each room.

## Adding a zone

1. Press **+ New zone** in the banner. The **New activity zone** window opens.
2. Type a **Name**, for example "Art corner" or "Reading nook".
3. In **Where**, choose a room, or **Whole centre** for a space every room uses, such as the gym or playground.
4. Enter an **Icon (emoji)**, for example 📚 or 🧱. It starts as 🎨.
5. Pick a **Colour** for the zone's tile.
6. Press **Save**. A toast says "Zone added" and the tile appears under **Today's activity**.

Repeat for each area you want to track. Four to six zones per room is plenty: enough to show a pattern, few enough that educators actually log visits.

## Logging a visit

1. Under **Today's activity**, find the zone's tile. It shows the icon, name, room (or "Whole centre") and today's visit count.
2. Press **+ Log visit** on the tile. A window "Log a visit to" the zone opens with the room's children.
3. Type in **Search children…** to narrow the list if the room is large.
4. Tap the child, for example Maya. A toast says "Visit logged", the window closes and the zone's count goes up by one.

For a whole-centre zone, the list shows the children in the room you have chosen, or every room in view.

## Reading the day

**Per-child visits today** lists each child who visited a zone today, with a tag for every visit showing the zone and the time, for example "📚 Reading nook @ 10:15". Look down the list at the end of the day: a child with every visit in the same zone may need a nudge toward something new tomorrow.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Every centre and room in the agency; can add room zones and whole-centre zones. |
| Platform admin | The same, for the agency they have chosen. |
| Centre director | Their own centre, when the screen is open to them. |
| Educator | Only their own rooms: those rooms' zones, the centre's whole-centre zones and their own children. They can add zones for their rooms, not whole-centre zones. |

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? The screen says "No centres you can see." | Your account is not linked to a centre in this agency. Check the agency you have chosen, or ask an agency admin.
??? An educator sees "You are not assigned to a room yet — ask your director to add you to one." | Add them to a room on the staff or room settings. Zones follow the rooms a person works in.
??? It says "No zones here yet." | No zone has been set up for this centre or room. Press **+ New zone** to add one.
??? I get "Give the zone a name". | The **Name** is required. Type one and press **Save** again.
??? The child picker says "No children in this room." | No child is enrolled in that room today. Check the room list, or choose a different room.
??? I get "That child is not in one of your rooms." | Educators can only log visits for children in their own rooms. Ask the child's room educator, or a director, to log it.
??? Can I rename or delete a zone? | Not from this screen yet. Add a new zone with the right name and stop logging visits to the old one.
??? Do parents see zone visits? | No. Activity zones are for staff planning only.
