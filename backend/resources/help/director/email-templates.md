# Email templates — changing the wording

**Settings → Email templates** lets you rewrite the emails KiddieTrac sends on your
agency's behalf, in your own voice. Your logo, colours and layout are applied
automatically; you only change the words.

[[open: #email-templates | Open Email templates]]

## The templates you can edit

| Template | Who gets it, and when |
|---|---|
| Provider welcome | Families, introducing their provider or educator. Its **Our care** text is also your agency's care values in the staff welcome |
| Parent daily summary | Parents, each evening, summarising their child's day |
| Onboarding welcome | Anyone who finishes onboarding (except staff, who get the staff welcome) |
| Staff welcome (educators & providers) | A new educator, director or home visitor when they finish onboarding, with their director(s) and agency admins copied |
| Invite / set-password | Someone you invite, with their sign-in details |
| Announcement | A reusable announcement layout |

## Editing one

- [ ] Choose it from **Template to edit**.
- [ ] Change the wording in each box. Words in double braces, like `{{name}}` or `{{agency_name}}`, are filled in for each person — keep them.
- [ ] Press **👁️ Preview** to see it as it will arrive.
- [ ] Press **Send me a test** to receive it in your own inbox.
- [ ] Press **Save template**.

[[show: #email-templates @ Template to edit | Show me the template picker]]

![The staff welcome template, with its merge tags and editable boxes](https://api.kiddietrac.com/help-img/email-template-staff-welcome.png)

**Reset to default** puts back KiddieTrac's original wording if you want to start again.

## Staff welcome — what is automatic

You write the heading, welcome, first steps and sign-off. KiddieTrac adds the rest for
each person:

- your **care values**, taken from the Provider welcome's **Our care** text — edit them
  there and both emails change;
- a short tour of the portal features they will use;
- a link to **Help & guides**;
- who to ask — their director's name and contact details.

## Questions

??? I saved a change but an email still shows the old wording. | Emails already sent keep the words they were sent with. The next email uses your new wording.
??? Can each centre have its own wording? | No. Templates are per agency.
??? Where do I change the logo and colours? | On the **🎨 Branding** screen. The templates use them automatically.
