# Staff training, certificates and checks

Three screens keep staff qualifications in order. **Certifications** is the roster of every CPR, first aid and other certificate held by the staff you manage, with expiry dates and whether someone checked the card. **My certificates** is where each person (you included) adds their own. **My training** lists the deadlines and training hours your licensing rules ask of you, where your province or state sets them.

[[open: #certifications | Open Certifications]]

## Checking who needs to renew

1. Open **Operations > Certifications**. The **Staff certifications** roster opens.
2. Read the counts at the top: **On file**, **Expired**, **Expiring in 30 days** and **Not yet checked**.
3. Narrow the list with **All centres** and **All certificates**, or type in **Search staff or certificate**.
4. Read each row: **Staff**, **Certificate**, **Issued**, **Expires**, **Status** and **Checked**.
5. Open the red **⚠️ … staff have no current CPR** box when it shows: it names staff with no valid certificate of that type. Press **＋** and a name to add one for that person.

## Adding a certificate for someone

1. Press **＋ Add certificate**.
2. Choose the **Staff member**.
3. Pick the **Certificate** type. For **Other**, type the **Name of the certificate**.
4. Enter **Issued by**, the **Issue date** and the **Expiry date** (leave the expiry empty for a certificate that never expires).
5. Attach the **Certificate file**: a PDF or a photo of the card, up to 10 MB.
6. Press **Add certificate**. A **Certificate added** toast appears and the row joins the roster.

## Checking a certificate staff uploaded

1. Find a row with **Not yet checked** in the **Checked** column.
2. Press **📎 View** to open the attached file.
3. Compare the name, dates and issuer with what was typed.
4. Press **✅** (**Mark verified**). The row now shows **✓ Verified** and who checked it. Press **↩️** (**Mark not verified**) to undo it.

Use the **⋮** menu on a certificate card for **✏️ Edit**, **📎 View certificate** or **🗑️ Remove**.

## Adding your own certificate

1. Open **Compliance > My certificates**.
2. Press **＋ Add certificate**.
3. Fill in the type, dates and file as above, then press **Add certificate**.

Your own certificate is checked by another director or an agency admin, never by you.

## Training deadlines and hours (where your rules set them)

Where your licensing rules set staff deadlines (Ohio today), KiddieTrac counts them from each person's hire date: for example first aid and CPR within 90 days, orientation within 30 days, background checks before day one and every 5 years, and yearly training hours. In Ontario these parts do not appear.

1. Open **Compliance**. The **🎓 Staff training & checks** card lists each person, their **Hired** date, what **Needs attention** and their **Training hours**.
2. Press **Open** beside a person.
3. Enter the **Hire date** and registry number and press **Save**. Without a hire date the items show **Set hire date**: dates are never guessed.
4. Read each requirement's status: **Done**, **Due by** a date, **Overdue**, **Expired** or **Not on file**.
5. Under **Training hours**, press **Approve** or **Not approve** on hours staff logged, or **Add hours** for them.

Staff see their own list in **Compliance > My training** (**My training & checks**). Hours they add show **Waiting for review** until you approve them; hours a director adds count straight away.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Every certificate and training record in the agency. |
| Centre director | Staff at their own centres; their own certificates in **My certificates**. |
| Educator, home visitor | Their own certificates and training hours only. |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? My training is not in my menu. | Your agency's rules do not set staff deadlines (for example in Ontario), so the screen is hidden.
??? "The expiry date cannot be before the issue date." | Check the two dates; the expiry must be on or after the issue date.
??? "That file is over 10 MB." | Take a smaller photo or save the PDF smaller, then attach it again.
??? A person is missing from the roster. | They are not at your centres, or their account is closed. Agency admins see everyone.
??? Do staff get reminders? | Yes. Before a certificate expires, the holder, their centre's directors and the agency admins get a bell, a push and an email, on the steps your agency sets in **Settings > Notifications**. Training deadlines and short training hours send a bell to the person.
