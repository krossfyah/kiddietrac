# Staff training, checks and deadlines

Where your province or state sets deadlines for staff (Ohio today), KiddieTrac tracks them for you.
In Ontario nothing here changes — these sections only appear when your agency's jurisdiction has the rules.

## What is tracked

- **Deadlines counted from the hire date** — for example in Ohio: first aid and CPR within 90 days,
  child abuse prevention training within 60 days, orientation and staff health records (MMR, Tdap) within 30 days.
- **Required background checks** — in Ohio: BCI and FBI fingerprints, the Ohio and national sex offender
  registries and the child welfare system check, before day one and then every 5 years.
- **Registry number** — the Ohio Professional Registry number (OPIN).
- **Training hours** — the hours needed each training year (Ohio: 6 hours, July to June).

Each item shows **Done**, **Due by** a date, **Overdue**, **Expired**, **Not on file**, or **Set hire date**
when the person's hire date isn't recorded yet. Hire dates are never guessed.

## Where to find it

Open **Compliance**. The **Staff training & checks** card lists everyone, with what needs attention and
their training-hours progress. Press **Open** beside a person to:

- set their **hire date** and **registry number**;
- see each requirement and its status;
- see the training hours they logged (they count straight away), remove an entry that is wrong,
  or add hours for them.

Certificates and background checks are still recorded on the staff record (Certifications and
Background checks); the list updates from them.

Directors see the staff at their own centre(s); admins see everyone in the agency.

## Reminders

Staff get a weekly reminder in their bell when something is due within 14 days, overdue or not on file,
and when their training hours are short in the last 30 days of the training year. No emails are sent.
