# HR: availability, shift swaps and the rota plan

**Human Resources > Rota & shifts** is where directors build the week's shifts (**Schedule**) and check it (**Rota plan**): cost, ratio warnings, availability conflicts and the giveaways and swaps waiting for approval. Staff, in My HR, say when they can work and give away or swap shifts. The availability, swaps and rota plan are part of the KiddieTrac HR add-on; the Schedule works without it.

[[open: #schedule | Open Rota & shifts]]

## Adding shifts on the Schedule

1. Open **Human Resources > Rota & shifts**. The **Schedule** tab opens first.
2. Use **‹**, **›** and **Today** to pick the week, or switch to the month view.
3. Press **+ New Shift**, choose the person, the day, the times and the room, and press **Create**.
4. To fill the whole week from the centre's opening hours, press **⚡ Autofill week**, check the list and press **Fill schedule**.
5. To set the hours each person works every week, press **👥 Standing hours**.

Click a shift to change it (**Update**) or remove it (**Delete**). Autofill and standing hours leave out approved leave, can't-work dates and times outside a person's weekly availability.

## Checking the week on the Rota plan

1. Choose the **Rota plan** tab.
2. Pick the centre (if you have more than one) and the week with **‹**, **›** or **This week**.
3. Read the totals: **Hours**, **Cost** (if you can see pay), **Ratio warnings** and **Availability conflicts**.
4. Under **Ratio warnings**, see each time a room has fewer **Educators planned** than **Needed** for the **Children expected**. Press **Post an open shift** on a row to fill the gap.
5. Under **Shifts** and **By person**, check each shift; a shift that clashes with the person's leave or availability is highlighted.

Children are expected from their room and attendance pattern; a child with no pattern is expected Monday to Friday. Cost is hours times each person's hourly rate; salaried staff and people with no pay rate are not costed. Below, **Clock-ins this week** lists clock-ins away from the centre and people close to or into overtime (see [[hr-clock]]).

## Approving a giveaway or swap

1. On the **Rota plan**, find **Shift changes**.
2. Press **View change** on a row a colleague has accepted.
3. Add an optional comment and press **Approve** or **Decline**.

On approval you see **Shift change approved. The rota is updated.** and the shifts move to the new people. Nobody approves a change they are part of.

## Setting your availability (staff)

1. Open **My HR** and find **My shifts and availability**.
2. Press **Weekly availability**, tick the days you can work and the hours for each, and press **Save**. With every day unticked, you can be rostered any time the centre is open.
3. For a date you can't work, press **I can't work a date**, choose the date or range (or **Only part of the day**), add a reason if you like, and press **Save**.

## Giving away or swapping a shift (staff)

1. In **My shifts and availability**, press **Give away or swap** on an upcoming shift.
2. Under **What do you want to do?**, choose **Give this shift away** or **Swap it for one of a colleague's shifts**.
3. Choose **Anyone at the centre** or one **Colleague** (and **Their shift** for a swap).
4. Press **Offer it**.

You keep the shift until a director approves. Colleagues see **Take shift** or **Accept swap**; once accepted, a director decides. You can **Cancel offer** until then, and a colleague who accepted can **Let it go**.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Every centre: schedule, rota plan, approve changes, see cost. |
| Centre director | Their own centres; sees cost only when they can see pay. |
| Educator, home visitor | Their availability, can't-work dates, giveaways and swaps in My HR. |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? Cost is missing on the Rota plan. | Cost shows only to people who can see pay (directors and admins with the payroll feature on).
??? Autofill skipped someone. | They had approved leave, a can't-work date, or the time was outside their weekly availability.
??? There is no Approve button on a change. | The colleague has not accepted it yet, or you are part of the change.
??? "No shifts planned this week." | Add shifts on the **Schedule** tab first.
??? Does the person hear about rota changes? | Yes: one shift notice per person per batch of changes (see [[hr-open-shifts]]).
