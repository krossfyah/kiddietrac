# HR: availability, shift swaps and the rota plan

Staff say when they can work, give away or swap shifts, and directors see the planned week with its cost and ratio warnings. This is part of the KiddieTrac HR add-on and works with the existing Schedule screen.

## Your availability (staff)

Open **My HR** and find **My shifts and availability**.

- **Weekly availability**: tick the days you can work and the hours for each day. If you leave every day unticked, you can be rostered any time the centre is open.
- **I can't work a date**: add a date or a range of dates, for the whole day or part of it, with an optional reason.

Autofill and the standing rota leave out the days you are on approved leave, the dates you can't work, and the times outside your weekly availability.

## Giving away or swapping a shift (staff)

1. In **My shifts and availability**, choose **Give away or swap** on one of your upcoming shifts.
2. Give it to anyone at your centre, give it to one colleague, or swap it for one of a colleague's shifts.
3. Your colleague accepts it. Then a director or an agency admin approves it.

You keep the shift until it is approved. Your colleagues get a notice when you offer a shift. You can cancel your offer until it is approved. A colleague who accepted it can let it go.

## The rota plan (directors and admins)

Open **HR > Rota** and choose the week. Directors see their own centres. Admins see the whole agency.

- **Shift changes**: giveaways and swaps. Approve or decline the ones a colleague has accepted. Nobody approves a change they are part of. When you approve a change, the shifts move to the new people on the rota.
- **Ratio warnings**: the hours when a room has fewer educators planned than the ratio rules need for the children expected that day. Children are expected from their room and their attendance pattern. A child with no pattern is expected Monday to Friday.
- **Shifts**: each planned shift with its hours. A shift that clashes with the person's leave or availability is highlighted.
- **Cost**: hours times each person's hourly pay rate, per shift, per person and for the week. You only see cost if you can see pay, which means you are a director or admin and the payroll feature is on. Salaried staff and people without a pay rate are not costed.

Shifts themselves are still added and edited on the **Schedule** screen.
