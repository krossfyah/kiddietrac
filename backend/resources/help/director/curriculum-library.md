# Curriculum library

51 pre-built activity templates spanning infant / toddler / preschool / school-age, plus agency-authored templates.

## Browsing

Sidebar → **Curriculum**.

Cards show: title, age band, duration, HDLH domains tagged, author, use count, materials list.

## Using a template

Tap **Use template** on any card. The activities + materials + HDLH tagging get copied into a new lesson-plan draft. Edit / save / publish from there.

## Filtering

By age band: infant (0-18mo) / toddler (18-30mo) / preschool (30-72mo) / school-age (72+mo) / mixed.

By HDLH domain: Belonging / Well-being / Engagement / Expression.

By visibility: **platform** (KiddieTrac starter pack — visible to every agency) or **agency** (your own).

## Creating your own

Tap **+ New template**. Fill in title, description, age band, duration, materials (one per line), HDLH domains, activity steps (one per line). Pick visibility:

- **Private** — only you can see it
- **Centre** — visible to your centre's staff
- **Agency** — visible to every centre in your agency
- **Platform** — only the platform admin can publish (network-wide sharing)

## Starter pack contents

The 51 starter templates cover:
- 5 infant activities (Tummy Time, Sensory Bottles, Mirror Play …)
- 10 toddler activities (Water Play, Playdough, Block Towers …)
- 27 preschool activities (Story Sequencing, Nature Scavenger Hunt …)
- 6 school-age activities (Marshmallow Tower, Junior Yoga …)
- 3 mixed-age (Open Studio Painting, Show & Tell, Quiet Corner)

All tagged with the relevant HDLH domains + Canadian context.

## Use count

Each template tracks how many times it's been used. Lets you see what's working in your agency vs. ignored.
