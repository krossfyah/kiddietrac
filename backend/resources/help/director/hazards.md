# Hazards and telling parents

Keep a record of any **non-routine hazard** at a centre — a broken fence board, a gas smell, a flooded washroom, construction next door — what was done about it, and the notice parents received. In Ontario this is required from **January 1, 2027** (O. Reg. 137/15 s.24.3): parents must be told about the hazard and the steps taken. Open it from the sidebar: **Operations** → **Hazards**.

[[open: #hazards | Open Hazards]]

Directors and admins see the list of hazards with the tabs **Open**, **Resolved** and **All hazards**, and **All my centres** to pick a centre. Educators and home visitors see a **Report a hazard** form and the **Open hazards at your centre**.

## Recording a hazard

1. Press **Report a hazard**. (Educators fill in the form on the screen.)
2. Choose the **Centre**.
3. Enter the **Hazard** (for example "Broken fence board"), **Where** it is, the **Severity** and when it was **Identified**.
4. Describe **What is it**, and write the **Steps taken** to keep children safe — this is what parents will read.
5. Optionally add a **Photo (optional)**. On a phone this opens the camera.
6. Directors and admins: tick **Not routine — parents need to be told (Ontario s.24.3)** if it applies.
7. Press **Report hazard**. A **Hazard recorded** (or, for educators, **Hazard reported** — "Your director has been told.") message appears.

When someone reports a hazard, the centre's **directors and the agency admins** get an in-app notice and an email straight away. The person who reported it is not sent a copy of their own report.

## Notifying parents

1. On the hazard's row, press **⋮** > **Notify parents**.
2. Read the confirmation. It says how many parents in how many current families at the centre it reaches, in the app and by email — and that no text messages are sent.
3. Press **Send notice**. A **Parents notified** message says how many families got it in the app and by email, and how many staff got a copy.

Each parent gets an in-app notice and their own email: what the hazard is, where, when it was found, the steps taken, and how to reach the centre. Families who have left, and withdrawn children, are not included. The **Parents** column then shows when and how many families were told; emails held back by your email settings are reported rather than counted as sent. To send it again, choose **Notify parents again**.

## Staff get a copy

Every parent notice, and every "it is resolved" update, is also copied to the centre's **educators, home visitors and directors** and to the **agency admins** — an in-app notice and an email marked **Copy**, saying how many families it went to. Whoever is asked about it at pick-up has read exactly what parents read. Anyone who already got the parent email (a staff member who is also a parent at the centre) is not emailed twice.

## Resolving a hazard

1. Press **⋮** > **Mark resolved**.
2. Under **What was done**, write what fixed it, for example "Board replaced and checked".
3. Press **Mark resolved**. The hazard moves to **Resolved**.
4. If parents were told about it, press **⋮** > **Tell parents it is resolved**, then **Send update**. Staff are copied on this too.

A hazard resolved by mistake can be reopened with **⋮** > **Reopen**. Every report, change, notice and resolution is in the audit log.

## Photos

Whoever reports a hazard can add a photo straight from their phone camera, and add or replace it later with **⋮** > **Edit**. Reports with a photo show **📷 Photo attached**; press **⋮** > **See photo**. Photos are private files, seen only by people who can see the hazard. With the HR add-on, Hazards is also a tab in **Human Resources > Health & safety**, and you can give someone an H&S task to fix it.

## Who can do what

- **Agency admins** see every centre's hazards, edit them, notify parents, resolve and reopen.
- **Centre directors** do the same for their own centres.
- **Educators and home visitors** report hazards and see the open ones at their centre, with their photos. They cannot notify parents or resolve.
- **Parents** only receive the notices; they never see the Hazards screen.

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? Who is told when an educator reports a hazard? | The centre's directors and the agency admins, by in-app notice and email.
??? Do parents get a text? | No. Parent notices are in-app and email only.
??? Notify parents says "Record the steps taken first". | The notice tells parents what was done, so it can't go until Steps taken is filled in. The edit window opens: add the steps, press Save, then notify again.
??? It says "Nobody to notify". | The centre has no current families with a parent account, so there is nobody to send the notice to.
??? Report hazard says "Say what the hazard is." | The Hazard box is required. Type a short name for it and press Report hazard again.
??? I don't see Notify parents or Mark resolved. | Only directors of that centre and agency admins can. Educators and home visitors can report only.
