# Children (auditor view)

As an auditor you can look up any child in the agency you were invited to, open their record and print the papers an inspection asks for: the **Emergency card** and the **Compliance report**. Everything is read-only — you can look, filter and print, but nothing you do here changes a record.

[[open: #children | Open Children]]

## Finding a child

1. Open **Children** from the menu (🧒). The list starts on **Enrolled** children.
2. To look at one centre, pick it in the first box (it starts on **All centres**).
3. To include children who have left or are waiting for a space, change the status box to **All statuses**, **Waitlist**, **Withdrawn** or **Inactive**.
4. Type part of a first or last name in **🔍 Search by name…**. The list updates as you type.
5. On a computer, switch between **▦ Cards** and **☰ Table** at the top right of the list. On a phone the list is always a table.

Above the list, four rings count the children shown: **Children**, **At Centre now**, **With a photo** and **Family linked**. Each child shows a status badge: **AT CENTRE** (checked in right now), **ENROLLED**, **WAITLIST**, **WITHDRAWN** or **INACTIVE**.

> 💡 The table's **Last seen** column shows the child's last check-in. A date in grey italics means nobody has checked them in for two weeks or more — a quick way to spot files that may be out of date.

## Opening a child's record

1. Click the child's card or row, or open the row's **⋮** menu and choose **Open**.
2. The record opens in a popup over the list, so your filter and place in the list are kept.
3. Read the header: photo, name, age, date of birth, the **Child ID**, room, family, health-flag count and the usual drop-off and pick-up times.
4. Move between the tabs: **👤 Overview** (guardians, pick-ups and emergency contacts), **🏫 Enrollment**, **📊 Daily reports**, **🩹 Incidents**, **💉 Immunization**, **📸 Live feed**, **📎 Attachments**, **📝 Notes** and **🗄️ Full history**.
5. Close the popup with **✕** to go back to the list.

**🗄️ Full history** gathers every record held about the child — attendance, care logs, incidents, medications, immunizations, documents, report cards and more. Sections start closed with a count beside each; open one to page through it.

## Printing the emergency card and compliance report

1. Open the child's record.
2. Press **🚨 Emergency card** in the header. The card opens in a panel over the portal.
3. Press **Print** in the panel's header (in the phone app it reads **Print in browser** and hands the card to your phone's browser).
4. For the inspection summary, press **📋 Compliance report**. It lists the child, enrollment, doctor, health card (last four digits only) and active health flags.
5. Press **Print / Save as PDF** at the top of the report to print it or keep a PDF.

> ℹ️ The print link the phone app makes for **Print in browser** lasts a few minutes and is recorded in the audit log.

## Who can do what

| Role | On this screen |
|---|---|
| Auditor | Search, filter, open records, print the emergency card and compliance report. Cannot edit, archive, request supplies or message anyone. |
| Agency admin / centre director | Use **Administration › Children** instead — the same list with **✏️ Edit**, **🗄️ Archive** and the **Active / Archived** switch. |

An auditor's account is read-only everywhere in KiddieTrac: the server refuses any change, whatever button is pressed.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? I pressed Edit (or Archive) and got "Auditor accounts are read-only." | That is expected. Auditor accounts can view and print but never change a record. Ask the agency admin to make the change, then refresh.
??? The list says "Could not load:" followed by a message. | Your account could not read the children list in this agency. Make sure the right agency is chosen in the agency switcher, then refresh. If it still fails, ask the agency admin who invited you to check your auditor access.
??? The list says "No children match". | A filter is hiding them. Set the centre box back to **All centres**, the status box to **All statuses**, and clear the search box.
??? A child I expected is not listed. | The list starts on **Enrolled** only. Children who have left are under **Withdrawn**; children who were archived are kept as history; the **Archived** list is for agency admins, so ask the agency admin if you need one of those records.
??? The emergency card will not open. | Allow pop-ups for the portal if your browser blocked one, then press **🚨 Emergency card** again. In the phone app use **Print in browser**.
??? Does looking at a record notify the family? | No. Viewing and printing send no email, text or notification to families or staff.
