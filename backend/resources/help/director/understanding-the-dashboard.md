# Today's roster and your home screen

This guide covers two screens. Centre directors have **Today** (**Operations > Today**): a live roster of every room, where you can sign a child in or out, record a late drop-off and fix a missed entry. Auditors have **Home** (**Menu > Home**): a read-only launcher for the screens an auditor may open. Your **Dashboard** itself is explained in [[agency-overview]].

## For centre directors: the Today roster

[[open: #today | Open Today]]

### Reading the roster

1. Open **Operations > Today**. The heading reads **Today's roster** with today's date.
2. Each room has its own card. Beside the room name is its ratio badge: **COMPLIANT**, **TIGHT**, **BREACH**, **CHECK STAFFING** or **OVER GROUP SIZE**, and on the right "x children · y educators".
3. Each child card shows the child's name and age and where they are: **At Centre since** a time, **Not at Centre**, **Absent today**, or **Late drop-off · by** a time when the family said they will be late.

If a room says "No children enrolled in this room.", nobody is enrolled there yet.

### Signing a child in or out

1. Find the child's card and press **Sign in** (or **Sign out** if they are here).
2. A yellow warning, **Not using One-Tap**, reminds you that parents should normally check in themselves. Press **Check in anyway** (or **Check out anyway**) to go ahead, or **Cancel**.
3. A green toast confirms it, for example "Maya signed in". The child is recorded as of now and their family is notified.

### Recording a late drop-off or late pickup

1. For a child who has not arrived yet, press **⏰ Late drop-off**. For a child who is here, press **⏰ Late pickup**.
2. Enter the time the family gave (**Expected by** or **Picked up by**) and press **Save**. The button then shows that time. Safe Arrival waits until then before chasing the family.

### Fixing a missed sign-in or sign-out

1. On the child's card, press **⏱ Fix a missed entry**. The **Add a missed sign in or out** window opens.
2. Choose **Which day**, then fill in **Arrived**, **Left**, or both.
3. Pick a reason under **Why is this being added late?** and add **Notes** if needed (required for "Other").
4. Press **Save**. "Attendance recorded." appears. The entry is marked as a later correction with your name, the family is emailed that the record was corrected (without your reason), and admins and the director are told.

## For auditors: the Home screen

[[open: #home | Open Home]]

Home is a read-only launcher. The banner reads **Audit & compliance** and "Read-only · tap to open".

1. Open **Menu > Home** (or tap the logo at the top).
2. Under **Quick access**, tap a tile: **Compliance**, **Audit logs**, **Children**, **Forms** or **Help**.
3. The screen opens read-only. Come back with **Home** in the menu. **Forms Manager** is in the menu too.
4. When you are finished, tap **🏃 Sign out** at the bottom of Home.

Auditors cannot change records, sign children in or out, or send messages.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they see |
|---|---|
| Centre director | **Today** for their own centres, with sign in/out, late drop-off, late pickup and **⏱ Fix a missed entry** |
| Agency admin | Uses **Agency overview** and **Daily Overview** for the same jobs, across every centre |
| Auditor | **Home** and the read-only screens it opens; no **Today** |

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? Today says "Could not load roster". | The roster could not be fetched. Reload the page; if it continues, contact your agency admin.
??? A room is missing from Today. | A room whose roster cannot be loaded is skipped. Reload the page; if it is still missing, check the room in your centre's settings with your agency admin.
??? The fix window says "Give an arrival time, a departure time, or both." | Fill in **Arrived**, **Left** or both before saving.
??? It says "Choose why this is being added late — it goes on the record." | Pick a reason from the list. If you chose "Other", "Add a note explaining the reason." means the **Notes** box is needed too.
??? Does the family get a message when I sign a child in? | Yes. A manual sign-in or sign-out notifies the family, the same as an educator's.
??? As an auditor, a tile says "This section isn't available." | That screen is not open to auditors in your agency. Ask the agency admin who invited you.
