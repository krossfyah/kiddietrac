# Understanding the dashboard

Your dashboard is the first thing you see when you log in. It's designed to give you a real-time pulse of your centre in 10 seconds.

## The four stat tiles

At the top of the dashboard:

- **Enrolled** — total children currently enrolled across all rooms. Updates instantly when you enroll or unenroll a child.
- **Here right now** — children who've been checked in today and haven't been checked out yet. Should match what you see in the rooms.
- **Staff on floor** — staff who are clocked in right now. If this is 0 and you have children present, your rooms will show BREACH.
- **Receivables** — total outstanding invoice balance across all families. Updates when invoices are generated or payments are recorded.

## The room cards

Below the stats, you see one card per room. Each card shows:

- **Room name and colour**
- **Status badge** — COMPLIANT (green), TIGHT (orange), or BREACH (red). See **Understanding ratios** for what these mean.
- **Children present / educators present**
- **Capacity %**

If any room shows BREACH, you need to address it immediately. Either an educator needs to be clocked in to that room, or children need to be moved.

## Sidebar navigation

- **Dashboard** — this screen
- **Children** — your enrollment roster, filterable by room and status
- **Families** — list of all families with primary contact info
- **Staff** — your team, certifications, schedules
- **Live roster (Today)** — see who's present right now, room by room
- **Compliance** — ratio status, certification expiry warnings, immunization gaps

## What's NOT on the dashboard (but should be)

We deliberately keep the dashboard simple. Other things you can do but aren't on the dashboard:

- **Generate invoices** — Families → Invoices section
- **Review incidents** — Compliance → Incidents
- **Export compliance reports** — Compliance → Export

## What to check daily

- **Morning:** confirm Staff on Floor matches who's actually present. If you see 0, ask your educators to clock in.
- **Mid-day:** glance at the rooms — anything in TIGHT or BREACH?
- **End of day:** check that all children who were "Here right now" got properly checked out.
