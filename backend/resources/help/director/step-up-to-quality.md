# Step Up To Quality

Step Up To Quality is Ohio's quality rating system. This screen keeps the records the rating asks for: a timer for each child's screenings and assessments, the referrals you make, your continuous improvement plan, and an evidence binder that shows what is in place and what is missing. Directors and agency admins use it week to week, and again before a rating visit.

[[open: #sutq | Open Step Up To Quality]]

The banner shows your rating level (Bronze, Silver or Gold) and the curriculum framework your agency uses. The **All my centres** list under the banner narrows every tab to one centre.

## Recording a screening or assessment

The **Child timers** tab lists every enrolled child with a timer for each record, counted from the child's enrolment date:

- **Screening**: a developmental screening within 60 days of enrolment, then yearly. The results must be shared with the family within 30 days.
- **Family needs**: a family needs assessment within 30 days of enrolment, then yearly.
- **Assessment**: a child assessment at least twice a year. This column only shows from Silver up.

Each timer shows **Done**, **Due soon** (within 14 days) or **Overdue**, with the date it is due.

1. Open the **Child timers** tab.
2. Find the child, for example Maya, and press **Record** under the timer you completed.
3. Enter the **Completed on** date. It cannot be in the future.
4. Enter the **Tool** you used, for example ASQ-3 for a screening or COR Advantage for an assessment.
5. For a screening, fill in **Shared with family on** if you have already gone over the results with the family.
6. Add any **Notes** and press **Save**. The timer turns **Done** and shows the next due date.

## Marking screening results as shared

If you recorded a screening without a shared date, the timer shows "Shared with family: by" and a date.

1. Go over the results with the family.
2. On the **Child timers** tab, press **Mark shared** under the child's screening.
3. Today's date is saved as the shared date and the button goes away.

## Adding and following up a referral

1. Open the **Referrals** tab and press **Add referral**.
2. Choose the **Child**.
3. Check the **Referred on** date and enter who the child was **Referred to**, for example Help Me Grow.
4. Leave **Follow up by** empty to use 30 days after the referral, or pick your own date.
5. Enter a short **Reason** and press **Add**. The referral appears in the list with its follow-up date.
6. When you have followed up, press **Follow-up** on its row, enter the **Followed up on** date and the **Outcome**, and press **Save**. The follow-up turns **Done**.

A follow-up past its date shows in red as overdue.

## Building your improvement plan

1. Open the **Improvement plan** tab and press **Add goal**.
2. Pick the **Centre** the goal is for.
3. Enter the **Goal** from your self-assessment, the **Action** you will take and the **Owner**, for example "Priya".
4. Set the **Due** date and the **Status**: **Planned**, **In progress** or **Done**.
5. Press **Add**. The goal joins the plan.
6. To update it later, press **Edit** on its row, change the status or date, and press **Save**.

A goal past its due date that is not **Done** shows its date in red. **Remove** takes a goal off the plan after you confirm; the removal is recorded in the audit log.

## Checking the evidence binder

1. Open the **Evidence binder** tab. The top line says how many standards are in place, for example "5 of 7 in place".
2. Read each row: the **Area**, the **Standard**, its **Status** (**Done**, **Partly** or **Missing**), the **Evidence** KiddieTrac found and **How to fix** it.
3. Fix the gaps from the other tabs or screens, then come back to see the status change.

The binder covers the standards KiddieTrac holds records for: curriculum aligned to Ohio's Early Learning and Development Standards, lesson plans in use, screenings and sharing their results, family needs assessments, child assessments, referrals followed up on time, and the improvement plan.

> ℹ️ Your rating also looks at things kept outside KiddieTrac, such as staff qualifications, ratios and the self-assessment. Use the binder as a checklist, not your whole binder.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Everything on every centre, and switch the screen on and set the rating level in **Ohio PFCC > Limits & alerts**. |
| Centre director | Everything on this screen for their own centres. Cannot change the rating level. |
| Platform admin | The same as an agency admin, for the agency they have chosen. |
| Educators and parents | Do not see this screen. |

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? I can't find Step Up To Quality in the menu. | It shows for agencies in Ohio. For an agency outside Ohio, an agency admin can tick **Show Step Up To Quality for this agency** in **Ohio PFCC > Limits & alerts** and press **Save settings**.
??? The screen says "Step Up To Quality is shown for agencies in Ohio". | The feature is not switched on for this agency. Ask an agency admin to switch it on in **Ohio PFCC > Limits & alerts**.
??? Where is the Assessment column? | Child assessments twice a year start at Silver. At Bronze the column is hidden. An agency admin sets the level in **Ohio PFCC > Limits & alerts**.
??? A child is missing from the timers. | Only children currently enrolled show. Check the centre picked in **All my centres** and the child's enrolment.
??? Saving a referral says "Choose the child and who they were referred to." | Both **Child** and **Referred to** are required. Fill them in and press **Add** again.
??? Saving a goal says "Enter the goal." | The **Goal** field is required. Type the goal and press **Add**.
??? Are families told about referrals or screenings? | No. This screen only keeps your records. Nothing is emailed or texted to families from here.
??? Where do I pick the Ohio learning standards? | Ohio's Early Learning and Development Standards are under **Settings > Learning framework**, which an agency admin manages. New Ohio agencies use them by default.
