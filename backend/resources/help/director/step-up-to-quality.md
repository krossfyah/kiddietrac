# Step Up To Quality

Ohio's quality rating system. This screen keeps the records the rating asks for: per-child timers, referrals, your continuous improvement plan, and a binder showing what is in place and what is missing.

## Where it is

Sidebar → **Step Up To Quality**. It shows for agencies in Ohio, or where an agency admin has switched it on in **Ohio PFCC → Limits & alerts**. Set your rating level (Bronze, Silver or Gold) there too.

## Child timers

Each enrolled child has a timer for each of these, counted from the child's enrolment date:

- **Developmental screening**: within 60 days of enrolment, then yearly. The results must be shared with the family within 30 days of the screening. Press **Mark shared** once you have done that.
- **Family needs assessment**: within 30 days of enrolment, then yearly.
- **Child assessment**: at least twice a year, from Silver.

Press **Record** to enter the date and the tool used (for example ASQ-3). Each timer shows as **Done**, **Due soon** (within 14 days) or **Overdue**.

## Referrals

Record each referral: the child, the date, who it went to and why. The follow-up date defaults to 30 days later. Press **Follow-up** to record when you followed up and what came of it.

## Improvement plan

Add the goals from your self-assessment. Give each goal an action, an owner, a due date and a status: **Planned**, **In progress** or **Done**. A goal past its date that is not done shows as overdue.

## Evidence binder

The binder checks each standard KiddieTrac has records for and marks it **Done**, **Partly** or **Missing**, with the numbers behind it:

- curriculum aligned to Ohio's Early Learning and Development Standards;
- lesson plans in use;
- screenings, and sharing their results with families;
- family needs assessments;
- child assessments;
- referrals followed up on time;
- the improvement plan.

The rating also covers things kept outside KiddieTrac, such as staff qualifications, ratios and the self-assessment. Use the binder as a checklist, not your whole binder.

## Learning framework

**Ohio's Early Learning and Development Standards** are available under **Settings → Learning framework**. New Ohio agencies use them by default. The five domains and their strands are loaded. The detailed topics and age-band statements are not loaded yet.
