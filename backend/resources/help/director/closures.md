# Closure / holiday calendar

**Closures** lists the days a centre is closed: statutory holidays, PD days, emergencies and renovations. Adding one tells the centre's families and staff, can pause billing for those days, and shows the closure on the agency calendar, the staff schedule and the family's Today screen.

**Where it is:** sidebar → **Administration** → **Closures** (for centre directors, the same entry in their menu). The banner reads **🗓 Closures & holidays**.

[[open: #closures | Open Closures]]

## Adding a closure

1. Press **+ New closure** in the banner. The **New closure** box opens.
2. Choose the **Centre**.
3. Choose the **Type**: **Holiday**, **PD day**, **Emergency**, **Renovation** or **Other**.
4. Set **Date (start)**. For more than one day, also set **End date (optional, for multi-day)**.
5. Type a **Reason** families will read, for example "Family Day".
6. Leave **Pause billing on these days** ticked if families should not be billed for the closed days. Untick it if they pay as usual.
7. Press **Save**. The closure appears at the top of the list, with your name and the time under **Added**.

> 💡 You can also start from the agency calendar: press **+ Add**, then **🚫 Closure**. The New closure box opens here with the day you were looking at already filled in.

## Closing several centres for one holiday

The Closures screen closes one centre at a time. For a holiday that closes several providers at once:

1. Open the agency calendar.
2. Press **+ Add**, then **🎉 Holiday**.
3. In **🎉 Add a holiday**, tick the people who are off and set the dates. The box names the centres that will close.
4. Save. One closure is added for each centre, with the same notices as a single closure.

## Changing a closure

1. On the closure's row, open the **⋮** menu (or press the ✏️ icon) and choose **Edit**.
2. The **Edit closure** box opens with the closure filled in. The **Centre** is greyed out: moving a closure to another centre means adding a new one.
3. Change the type, dates, reason or billing and press **Save**.

Fixing a wrong date this way is better than deleting and re-adding, which would announce the closure to every family twice.

## Removing a closure

1. On the row, open the **⋮** menu (or press 🗑️) and choose **Remove**.
2. Confirm **Remove this closure?**. The row disappears.

## Who is told, and when

- **When you add a closure:** if your agency's closure reminders are on and set to announce straight away, every family and staff member at that centre gets a bell notice and an email. Agency admins and directors always get a bell notice.
- **When you edit or remove one:** agency admins and directors get a bell notice saying what changed and who changed it. Families are not emailed again, and notices already sent are not taken back.
- **Before the day:** reminder emails go out ahead of the closure; see [Closure reminder emails](closure-reminders).
- **In the app:** on a closed day, or up to two weeks before, a notice appears once a day when someone opens the app, worded for them: the provider whose centre it is ("You are closed today…"), other staff at that centre, or families ("We are closed today… Enjoy the day.").

## Closures at a glance

Above the list, tiles show the **Next closure** with a countdown, closed centre-days in the **Next 90 days**, days **Closed in** the year, days with **Billing paused** and **Centres affected**. The charts show **Up next**, **Closed days by month**, **What closes us** and **Days closed per centre**. Days are weekdays only: a long weekend's Saturday is not a lost care day. When closures span more than one year, year buttons at the top switch the year.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Add, edit and remove closures at any centre in the agency. |
| Centre director | The same, for their own centres only. |
| Platform admin | The same, inside the agency they have switched into. |
| Educator and parent | Cannot open this screen. They see closures on their calendar and Today screen. |

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? I pressed Save and saw "Pick a start date". | **Date (start)** is empty. Choose the first closed day.
??? The list says "No closures scheduled." | No closure has been added for your centres yet. Press **+ New closure**.
??? Families were not emailed when I added a closure. | Your agency's closure reminder settings decide whether families are told straight away. An agency admin can check them; see [Closure reminder emails](closure-reminders).
??? What does the Billing column mean? | **Paused** means families are not billed for those days. **No change** means billing runs as usual.
??? Does a closure change staff leave? | Booked leave over the closed dates is flagged for review. It is never re-deducted automatically; see [HR: closures taken from leave](hr-closures-leave).
??? Which holidays should an Ontario centre add? | New Year's Day, Family Day, Good Friday, Easter Monday, Victoria Day, Canada Day, Civic Holiday, Labour Day, Thanksgiving, Remembrance Day (if you close), Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year's Eve, plus your PD and training days.
