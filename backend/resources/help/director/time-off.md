# Time-off requests

**Staff → Time off requests.** Staff ask for time off; a centre director or agency admin
approves or declines it. A request can be whole days, or part of a single day.

[[open: #time-off | Open Time off]]

## Requesting time off

1. Open **Time off** and press **🌴 Request time off**.
2. Pick the **Type**: Vacation, Sick, Personal, Bereavement, Jury duty or Other.
3. Choose the **Start date** and **End date**.
4. For part of a day, switch **All day** off and set **From** and **To**. Part of a day
   has to be a single date, so the end date follows the start date.
5. Add a **Reason** and press **Submit**.

The agency's directors and admins get a notification and an email straight away. The
email has **Approve** and **Decline** buttons, so they can decide without opening the
portal. They can add a note for you on the next screen.

> **Tip:** A dentist appointment at 2pm is a part-day request, not a day off. That way the rota does not show you absent all day.

## Deciding (directors and admins)

Under **Team requests** there are two tabs:

- **Awaiting decision**: the requests to act on. Press **Approve** or **Deny**.
- **Approved & declined**: the record of what was decided, who decided it and when.

[[show: #time-off @ Awaiting decision | Show me the Awaiting decision tab]]

The person who asked is told your decision in the app and by email.

## When approved time off closes a centre

If the person is the **only** active staff member at their centre, like a home provider
working alone, approving a full-day request also marks the centre **closed** for those
dates. Families are then told through your agency's normal closure reminders.

A centre with other staff is not closed, because someone covers. **Part-day time off
never closes a centre.** The provider is still there for the rest of the day.

## Withdrawing a request

You can withdraw your own request while it is **pending**, and also after it has been
**approved**. Withdrawing an approved request puts the calendar back to normal and tells
the people who were told about it. If it had closed the centre, that closure is removed.
Parents hear about it only if they had already been told the centre was closing.

## On the calendar

Pending and approved time off shows on the staff calendar, so cover can be planned
before a decision is made. Declined requests do not appear.
