# Time-off requests

**Human Resources > Time off.** Staff ask for time off; a centre director or agency admin
approves or declines it. A request can be whole days, or part of a single day.

[[open: #time-off | Open Time off]]

## Requesting time off

1. Open **Time off** and press **🌴 Request time off**.
2. Pick the **Type**. Without the HR add-on the list is Vacation, Sick, Personal, Bereavement, Jury duty or Other; with HR it lists your agency's own leave types.
3. Choose the **Start date** and **End date**.
4. For part of a day, switch **All day** off and set **From** and **To**. Part of a day
   has to be a single date, so the end date follows the start date.
5. Add a **Reason** and press **Submit**.

The agency's directors and admins get a notification and an email straight away. The
email has **Approve** and **Decline** buttons, so they can decide without opening the
portal. They can add a note for you on the next screen.

> 💡 A dentist appointment at 2pm is a part-day request, not a day off. That way the rota does not show you absent all day.

## What the form adds with the HR add-on

1. Under **Type**, a line shows how much of that leave is available and how much is left after this request. It turns red when there is not enough left.
2. A box shows how many others at your centre are already off or asking for those days (never who).
3. Under **Note or photo (optional)**, press **📷 Take a photo** or **📎 Choose a file** to attach a sick note, then set the dates it covers. Only you, your director and your agency admin can see it.
4. A type that needs no approval (often sick days) says "No approval needed: recorded and taken off your balance straight away."

After sending, you can still add a note from **Your requests** with **📎 Add a note or photo**.

## Deciding (directors and admins)

Under **Team requests** there are two tabs:

- **Awaiting decision**: the requests to act on. Press **Approve** or **Deny**.
- **Approved & declined**: the record of what was decided, who decided it and when.

[[show: #time-off @ Awaiting decision | Show me the Awaiting decision tab]]

When you press **Approve**, a short **Approve time off** dialog opens (see below). You
can add a **Note back to them** there. The person who asked is told your decision in the
app and by email. With HR, the dialog also shows the balance now, after this request and
after all pending requests, and who else is away ("Also away at…").

> ⚠️ Nobody decides their own time off. Your own request sits in **Team requests** with only **Withdraw**; another director or an admin decides it.

## Reviewing leave that needed no approval

1. Find the request marked **To review** under **Awaiting decision**.
2. Check the dates and any note attached.
3. Press **Reviewed**. The request leaves the list; the balance is not changed (it was already taken off).

## Choosing whether the centre closes

When you approve, you decide whether the person's centre stays open or closes for those
dates. The **Approve time off** dialog shows the dates and the centre, and two choices:

- **Someone covers**: the centre stays open. Below it you see who else works at that centre, or "Nobody else works at this centre."
- **Close** *centre name* **for these dates**: the centre is marked closed. Parents of the children still enrolled there, and the agency's admins and directors, are emailed straight away. The dialog tells you how many parents will be emailed.

Press **Approve** to save your choice.

**Close** is ticked for you when the person is the **only** staff member at their centre
and the request is for whole days, like a home provider working alone. Otherwise
**Someone covers** is ticked. You can change either one. If the centre is already closed
for those dates, the dialog says so and nothing more is needed. The **Approve** link in
the email works the same way.

> 💡 Someone who works at several centres has no single centre to close, so the choice is not shown for them.

## Closing the centre after approving

1. Find the approved request.
2. Press **🔒 Mark** *centre name* **closed** (or **Mark centre closed** in the table).
3. Confirm. Parents, admins and directors are emailed at once, and the request shows that the centre is closed.

## Withdrawing a request

You can withdraw your own request while it is **pending**, and also after it has been
**approved**: press **Withdraw** and confirm. Withdrawing an approved request puts the calendar back to normal,
gives the leave back to your balance and tells the people who were told about it. If it
had closed the centre, that closure is removed. Parents hear about it only if they had
already been told the centre was closing.

## On the calendar

Pending and approved time off shows on the staff calendar, so cover can be planned
before a decision is made. Declined requests do not appear.

## Who can do what

- **Staff** (educators, home visitors) request and withdraw their own time off and see **Your requests**.
- **Centre directors** decide requests from staff at their own centres, plus any people an agency admin named for them on **HR settings > Approvers**, or a director they cover for.
- **Agency admins** decide every request in the agency.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? Submit does nothing or shows a message. | Read the red line above the buttons. The end date can't be before the start date, part of a day must be one date, and with HR a request may be refused when there is not enough balance left.
??? I can't approve my own request. | That is on purpose. Another director or an agency admin decides it.
??? "Amount may be wrong" shows on a request. | A closure or holiday changed after it was booked. See [[hr-closures-leave]] to use the new amount or keep it as booked.
??? The approval was refused because of minimum staff. | Your agency set a minimum number of staff per working day and chose to block approvals below it. See [[hr-attendance]].
??? Who gets an email? | Approvers when a request is made, the person when it is decided, and parents only when you close the centre.
