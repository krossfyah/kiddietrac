# Time-off requests

Staff submit; centre director / agency admin approves or denies. Approved time-off blocks shifts from being scheduled over those dates.

## Submitting (staff)

1. Sidebar → **My time off**.
2. Click **+ Request time off**.
3. Pick type (vacation / sick / personal / bereavement / jury / other), start + end date, and a reason.
4. Submit. Your direct supervisors get a notification immediately.

You can cancel a pending request. Once decided you can't cancel — talk to your director.

## Approving (directors / admins)

1. Sidebar → **Time off**.
2. Pending requests are highlighted at the top.
3. Click **Approve** or **Deny**. The requester gets a notification + email with your decision.

## How it interacts with the calendar

Approved time off auto-blocks the staff calendar for those dates. New shifts dragged across those days show a warning. (Conflict resolution lives in v22p52 — for now it warns, doesn't block.)
