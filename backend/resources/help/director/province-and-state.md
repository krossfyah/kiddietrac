# Province / state rules

KiddieTrac follows the rules of the province or state your agency operates in. Set it once and the right defaults follow.

## Setting it

Agency overview → **Edit agency** → **Country & compliance**: pick the country and the **Province / state**, then **Apply**. Picking a province or state in another country also switches the currency.

## What it changes

- **Drill log:** which drills are owed and how often.
- **Background checks and certifications:** the types offered. Ohio, for example, offers BCI and FBI fingerprints, the sex offender registries and the child welfare (SACWIS) check, all renewed every 5 years, plus first aid and CPR within 90 days of hire, child abuse training within 60 days, orientation within 30 days and the Ohio Professional Registry (OPIN).
- **Incident wording:** Ontario says *Serious occurrence*; Ohio says *Serious incident*, reported to DCY by the next business day.
- **Immunization schedule:** a new agency starts on Canada's (NACI) or the US (ACIP) schedule. Either can be loaded later from the schedule screen (**Load a standard schedule…**). Loading one replaces the current rows; the old rows are switched off, not deleted.
- **Data retention:** settings can't go below the legal minimum. In Ohio, for example, attendance records must be kept at least 12 months.
- **Timezone:** used only when your agency has no timezone set.

## Available today

Ontario, Alberta, British Columbia and Nova Scotia; Ohio, Texas and New York. Only rules we have verified are applied; anything not yet verified for a province or state is left as it was.

These are defaults to guide setup. Your agency remains responsible for checking its own licensing requirements.
