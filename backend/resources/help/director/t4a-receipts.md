# T4A annual tax receipts

Generate a downloadable PDF receipt that families submit with their Canadian T1 tax return to claim the childcare expense deduction.

## How to download

**Director / agency admin:**
1. Open **Families** → click the family.
2. In the family detail panel, click **⤓ T4A receipt** and pick the year.
3. The PDF downloads instantly.

**Parent / guardian (self-serve):**
1. Sidebar → **Billing**.
2. Scroll to **Tax receipts** and click the year you want.
3. PDF downloads.

## What's on the receipt

- Family name
- Children covered
- Each payment in the year (date · invoice # · amount)
- Total paid in the calendar year
- Agency name + support email (so CRA reviewers can verify)

## What if a payment is missing?

The receipt only includes payments with `paid_at` in the calendar year. If a payment was recorded incorrectly, fix the payment date — the next download will pick it up. No regeneration step needed.
