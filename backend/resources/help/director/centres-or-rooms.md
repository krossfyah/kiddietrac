# Centres or Rooms

Some agencies run multiple **centres**; others run a single site with several **rooms**. KiddieTrac lets each agency choose the word used throughout the portal.

## Change the term

1. Open **Edit agency** (from the Agency overview banner).
2. Under **Facilities are called**, choose **Centres** or **Rooms**.
3. It applies instantly — navigation, headings, buttons and reports switch to your chosen word.

Only the on-screen labels change; your data (addresses, names) is never altered. New agencies can set this when they first configure their agency.
