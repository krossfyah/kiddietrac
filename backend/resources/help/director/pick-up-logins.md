# Pick-up logins for emergency contacts

A grandparent, nanny or other trusted adult who collects a child can have their own **pick-up login**. They sign in like a parent: their own QR code to check the children in and out, daily reports, photos and messages. They never see billing — invoices, payments, statements, receipts and subsidies are refused for them by the system, not just hidden from the menu.

Agency admins and centre directors set these up. Parents can ask for one, but only staff can create it.

## Where to set one up

You can give a login in three places:

- **On the child's record** — **Overview** tab → **Pick-ups & emergency contacts**. Next to each contact: **🔑 Set up login**.
- **When adding a family** — the **Emergency** step of the New family wizard: tick **Give a pick-up login** and enter their email.
- **When editing a family** — Families → the family → **Edit** → **Emergency** tab: tick **Give a pick-up login** and enter **Email for the login**.

An email address is always needed: it's what they sign in with. Giving a login also ticks **Authorized for pickup**.

## Setting one up from the child's record

1. Open the child → **Overview**.
2. Under **Pick-ups & emergency contacts**, press **🔑 Set up login** next to the person.
3. If they have an email on file, confirm with **Set up login**. If not, enter one in the popup.

They're emailed straight away. Someone new to KiddieTrac gets a temporary password; someone who already has an account at your agency keeps theirs and is simply told they can now pick up. The contact then shows **🚸 Has login**.

## When a parent asks for one

Parents can press **🔑 Request pick-up login** next to a contact in their app. The centre's directors and your agency's admins get a notification and an email: **Pick-up login requested**.

On the child's record, the contact shows **⏳ … asked for a pick-up login → their email**, with two buttons:

- **✓ Approve** — sets up the login with the email the parent gave. The parent is told it's done.
- **Decline** — the parent is told the centre didn't set one up.

## Removing a login

- On the child's record, press **Remove** next to **🚸 Has login**, or
- In **Edit** → **Emergency**, untick **Has a pick-up login**.

They can no longer sign in for this family or use their QR code to pick up. Their contact details stay on file. A login also stops working if a parent removes the contact or unticks **Allowed to pick up the children**.

## Guardians who have never signed in

On the child's record, each guardian shows **✓ Signs in** or, if they've never signed in, **✉ Send login invite**. That sends a fresh temporary password to the guardian's email. A guardian who already signs in is never sent a new password. A guardian with no email needs one added in Families → **Edit** first.

## What you'll see

- On the child's record and in the family editor, pick-up logins are marked **🚸 pick-up login**.
- Every login set up, removed or declined is in the audit log.

??? Can a pick-up login see invoices? | No. Every billing page and request is refused for them. If the same person is also a paying parent on another family, they still see billing for that family.
??? Can educators set up logins? | No. Agency admins and centre directors only.
??? The person already uses KiddieTrac with us. | Use the same email. Their existing account is reused and their password doesn't change.
