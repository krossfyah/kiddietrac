# Quick notes

A notepad for the things you need to remember: jot a note in seconds and keep it just for you, or share it with your staff.

## Where it is

Press the **📃** button in the top bar for the notepad drawer, or open **Notes** in the sidebar (under Tasks) for the full screen. You can also add the **Quick notes** card to your dashboard: **+ Add widget → Quick notes**.

[[open: #notes | Open Notes]]

## Writing a note

1. Add a **Title** if you like, then write the note in the big box below it. On a computer press **Enter** to save (**Shift+Enter** starts a new line). On a phone or tablet, Enter starts a new line and the **Save** button saves.
2. The list shows each note's title only, on one line. A note with no title is listed by its first line. Tap a title to open the whole note.
3. Choose **🔒 Just me** or **👥 Agency** before you save, and a colour if you like.

## Pictures

Add up to 5 pictures to a note, such as a screenshot or a photo of a notice:

- On a computer, paste a screenshot into the note (**Ctrl+V**, or **Cmd+V** on a Mac), drag a picture onto it, or press **🖼 Add picture**.
- On a phone, **🖼 Add picture** opens your camera or your photos.
- You can add pictures while writing a new note or in **Edit note**. Tap a picture to see it full size; press **✕** on it to remove it.

Pictures are made smaller on your device and their location details are removed before they are sent. A picture is seen by exactly the people who can see its note, and is deleted with it. In the list, **🖼 3** means the note has 3 pictures. Each picture can be up to 10 MB.

## Who sees it

**Just me** notes are private: only you can ever see them, not your director, not an admin, not KiddieTrac support. **Agency** notes are for the staff of your agency. Any staff member who can see one can edit it, and every change is kept. A shared note can be for the whole agency or for one centre, and a shared note stays shared.

Admins see every agency note. Directors see agency-wide notes and the notes of their own centres. A director can delete any shared note at their centres; an admin can delete any shared note.

## Pin, colour, link and remind

- **📌 Pin** keeps a note at the top.
- **🎨 Colour** helps you sort notes at a glance.
- **✏️ Edit** lets you link a note to a child, family or staff member. It then shows on that record's page too, for the staff who can see that record. A shared note linked to a child or family belongs to that child's centre.
- Set a **reminder** date and the note shows in your calendar and Tasks in the right-hand panel on that day. A private note's reminder is only ever shown to you.

## History

Press **🕘** on a shared note to see who created it and who changed what, and when. Changes to shared notes are also kept in the audit log.

## Searching

Use **Search notes** to find a note by any word in its title or text. **My notes** and **Agency notes** switch between your private and shared notes.
