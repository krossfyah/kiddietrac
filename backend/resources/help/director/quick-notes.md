# Quick notes

A notepad for the things you need to remember: jot a note in seconds and keep it just for you, or share it with your staff. Press the **📃** button in the top bar for the notepad drawer, or open **Notes** in the sidebar (under Operations) for the full screen. You can also add the **Quick notes** card to your dashboard: **+ Add widget → Quick notes**.

[[open: #notes | Open Notes]]

## Writing a note

1. Add a title in **Title (optional)** if you like.
2. Write the note in the big box below it (**Write a quick note…**).
3. Choose **🔒 Just me** or **👥 Agency**. For an agency note at a multi-centre agency, pick **Agency-wide** or one centre.
4. Pick a colour dot if you like, and add pictures with **🖼 Add picture** (see below).
5. Press **Save**. On a computer, **Enter** also saves (**Shift+Enter** starts a new line). On a phone or tablet, Enter starts a new line and the **Save** button saves.

The list shows each note's title only, on one line. A note with no title is listed by its first line. Tap a title to open the whole note.

## Pictures

Add up to 5 pictures to a note, such as a screenshot or a photo of a notice:

- On a computer, paste a screenshot into the note (**Ctrl+V**, or **Cmd+V** on a Mac), drag a picture onto it, or press **🖼 Add picture**.
- On a phone, **🖼 Add picture** opens your camera or your photos.
- You can add pictures while writing a new note or in **Edit note**. Tap a picture to see it full size; press **✕** on it to remove it.

Pictures are made smaller on your device and their location details are removed before they are sent. A picture is seen by exactly the people who can see its note, and is deleted with it. In the list, **🖼 3** means the note has 3 pictures. Each picture can be up to 10 MB (PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF).

## Pin, colour, link and remind

1. Press **📌** (**Pin to top**) to keep a note at the top; press **📍** to unpin it.
2. Press **🎨** (**Colour**) to colour a note so you can sort notes at a glance.
3. Press **✏️** (**Edit**) to open **Edit note**. There you can change the text, link the note to a child, family or staff member under **Linked to**, and set a **Reminder** date. Press **Save**.

A linked note also shows on that record's page, for the staff who can see that record. A shared note linked to a child or family belongs to that child's centre. On the reminder day the note shows in your calendar and Tasks in the right-hand panel. A private note's reminder is only ever shown to you.

## Searching and switching lists

1. Use **🔒 My notes** and **👥 Agency notes** to switch between your private and shared notes.
2. Type in **Search notes** to find a note by any word in its title or text.
3. Press **🕘** (**History**) on a shared note to see who created it, who changed what, and when.

## Who can do what

- **Just me** notes are private: only you can ever see them — not your director, not an admin, not KiddieTrac support.
- **Agency** notes are for the staff of your agency. Any staff member who can see one can edit it, and every change is kept. A shared note stays shared.
- **Agency admins** see every agency note and can delete any shared note.
- **Centre directors** see agency-wide notes and the notes of their own centres, and can delete any shared note at their centres.
- **Educators and home visitors** see agency-wide notes and their centres' notes, and can write their own.
- Parents never see notes.

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? I can't make a shared note private again. | A shared note stays shared, because other staff may already rely on it. Write a new Just me note instead.
??? It says "Pick an agency to see and share agency notes." | You have more than one agency. Choose the agency in the top bar first.
??? It says "Write something first." | A note needs some text before it can be saved.
??? A picture won't add. | Only PNG, JPG, WebP or GIF pictures up to 10 MB can be added, up to 5 per note. A phone HEIC picture can't be read here: share it as a JPG, or take a screenshot of it.
??? I deleted a note by mistake. | A deleted note is gone for good from the list. For a shared note, its history stays in the audit log.
