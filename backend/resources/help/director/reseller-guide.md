# Reseller guide

As a reseller you sell KiddieTrac under your own brand. You own your agencies: you see how
each one is doing, onboard new ones, look after their admins, follow billing and your
commission, answer their tickets, and set the brand they all carry.

Your section of the menu has six screens:

| Screen | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **🤝 All my agencies** | Every agency and centre you brought to the platform, as of right now |
| **➕ Onboard an agency** | Add an agency under your brand and invite its first admin |
| **💳 Billing & commissions** | What the platform billed your agencies, what was collected, and your commission |
| **🎧 Support desk** | Tickets from all your agencies |
| **🎨 Branding** | The logos, colours and name your agencies show |
| **🏢 Agencies** | The agencies that carry your brand |

Inside an agency you work as its agency admin.

## All my agencies

Each agency shows **Enrolled / capacity**, **Here today**, **Staff on the floor**, **Open
tickets**, **Plan billing / month** and **Last activity**. Agencies that **Need attention** are
flagged. Sort by here today, last activity or open tickets, or use **Search agencies or
centres…**. **Centres (n)** opens the list of centres; **Open agency →** takes you into it.

## Onboard an agency

1. Open **➕ Onboard an agency**.
2. Under **The agency**, enter the **Agency name**, address, **Contact phone** and **Time zone**.
3. Under **Their first admin**, enter their name and email.
4. Press **Add agency**.

The agency carries your brand straight away. Its admin is emailed an invite to set a password,
and the agency appears in **All my agencies**.

## Admins

On an agency, press **Admins** to see its admins and when they last signed in. You can **Send
new link** (a fresh password link), **Turn off** or **Turn on** an admin, and **Invite another
admin**.

## Billing & commissions

Pick a month to see **Billed this month**, **Collected**, **Still owed** and **Your
commission**, which is a percentage of the money collected before tax. **Download statement
(CSV)** gives you the month line by line. If no commission terms are set yet, the screen says so.

## Support desk

Tickets from all your agencies arrive here. Agency tickets are yours to answer. App problems,
questions and ideas go to the platform team; you can follow their status. To hand a ticket on,
use **Escalate to the platform team** and say what they should know.

## Your brand and app

**🎨 Branding** sets the name and logos every agency under you shows: the **App & product
name** (what people see instead of "KiddieTrac"), the wordmark, the square logo, the favicon,
the boot screen, your colours and the **Page banner**. **Live preview** shows the result before
you save. Turn on **Let my agencies use their own logo and colours** if they may override it;
otherwise every agency shows your brand. Agencies see the new brand the next time they open a page.

**Hide KiddieTrac everywhere** is part of your licence and is set by KiddieTrac. Your own app
icon and app name in the app stores come with a white-label app build: ask KiddieTrac.
