# Grants (operating, wage-enhancement and one-time funding)

**Finance → Grants** tracks funding your agency or centre receives and must spend within a period: operating grants, wage enhancement, CWELCC cost-based funding, capital and one-time money. For each grant you see what was awarded, received and spent, and whether spending is keeping pace before the report is due. Open it when funds arrive, when you spend them and when the funder's report is coming up.

[[open: #grants | Open Grants]]

> ℹ️ Grants is for money the agency receives. Fee subsidies that come off a family's invoice are on **Compliance → Subsidies**.

## Adding a grant (agency admins)

[[show: #grants @ + Add a grant | Show me Add a grant]]

1. Open **Finance → Grants**.
2. Press **+ Add a grant**.
3. Type the **Grant name** (for example "2026 Wage Enhancement Grant") and the **Funder**.
4. Add the **Agreement / reference #** from the funding letter.
5. Choose the **Type**: General operating, Wage enhancement, CWELCC cost-based funding, Capital / repairs, One-time / special purpose or Other.
6. Choose the **Centre**, or leave **All centres (agency-wide)**.
7. Set **Period starts** and **Period ends**, the **Amount awarded** and the **Report due** date.
8. Write any conditions or eligible costs in **Notes (conditions, eligible costs)**.
9. Press **Save**. A **Grant saved** toast appears and the grant opens.

## Reading the grant list

1. Use the list filter to show **Active grants**, **Closed** or **All**.
2. Each card shows **Awarded**, **Received**, **Spent**, **Unspent** and **Still to receive**, the period and the report due date.
3. Read the bar: the fill is the share of the award spent, the line is the share of the period gone by.
4. Read the pace badge: **On track**, **Underspending** (spending is well behind the calendar; unspent funds may have to be returned), **Spending ahead** (check the award will last) or **Overspent**.
5. Click a card to open the grant.

## Recording money in and out

1. Open the grant.
2. Under **Transactions**, press the button for what happened:
   - **+ Funds received** when the funder pays an instalment;
   - **+ Spending** when you spend grant money;
   - **Funds returned** when you send unspent money back;
   - **Adjustment** for a correction (a negative amount reduces).
3. Set the **Date** and the **Amount**.
4. For spending, choose the **Category** (Wages, Benefits, Rent / occupancy, Program supplies, and so on) and, if the cost was a supplier bill, pick it under **Linked expense bill (optional)**.
5. Add a **Description** and a **Reference (cheque, EFT, payroll run)**.
6. Press **Save**. The totals, the bar and **By month** update straight away.

To undo an entry, press **Remove** on its row and confirm. The totals are recalculated and the removal is kept in the audit log.

## Reporting to the funder

1. Open the grant.
2. Check the pace note under the bar and the **By month** table.
3. Press **⬇ Export CSV**. The file lists every transaction with the totals (awarded, received, spent, returned, adjustments, unspent, still to be received) for your report.
4. When the period is over and the report is in, press **Edit**, set **Status** to **Closed** and press **Save**.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Adds and edits every grant, records transactions on all of them. |
| Centre director | Sees their own centres' grants and the agency-wide ones. Records and removes transactions on their own centres' grants. Cannot add or edit a grant, or change an agency-wide one. |
| Platform admin | The same as an agency admin, in the agency they are working in. |

Educators and parents do not see this screen.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? I don't see + Add a grant or Edit. | Only an agency admin can set up or edit a grant. A centre director can record transactions on their own centres' grants.
??? "Only an agency admin can change an agency-wide grant." | The grant covers all centres. Ask your agency admin to record the entry.
??? "Name, period and amount are required." | Fill in **Grant name**, **Period starts**, **Period ends** and **Amount awarded**.
??? "A date and an amount are required." or "The amount must be more than zero." | Every entry needs a date and an amount above zero. Only an **Adjustment** may be negative.
??? The list says "No grants yet." | No grant has been set up for your centres. An agency admin adds them with **+ Add a grant**. Check the filter is not on **Closed**.
??? My expense bill is not in the Linked expense bill list. | A centre grant offers that centre's bills and agency-wide bills only. Record the bill first on **Finance → Expenses**.
??? Does KiddieTrac send the report or any email? | No. Grants keeps your records; nothing is sent to the funder, families or staff. Every change is in the audit log.
