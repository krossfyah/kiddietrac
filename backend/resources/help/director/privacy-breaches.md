# Privacy breach register

**Administration › Privacy breaches** is your agency's register of privacy breaches: any time personal information
was lost, seen or sent where it should not have been (an email to the wrong family, a lost tablet, a list left on a
printer). Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) says every such breach must be recorded and the record kept for 24 months,
even when you decide it does not need reporting. Record it here as soon as you know, then fill in the rest as you go.

[[open: #privacy-breaches | Open Privacy breaches]]

## Recording a breach

1. Press **+ Record a breach**.
2. Under **What happened**, give it a title, the **Discovered on** date and a plain description. Add **Happened on** / **Happened until** and the **Centre** if you know them (or **Whole agency / not one centre**).
3. Under **Information and people involved**, describe the kind of information and enter the numbers in **People affected** and **Children affected**, and **Who received it or could see it**.
4. Under **Containment**, write what was done to stop it (recall the email, ask the recipients to delete it, change a password) and the **Contained on** date.
5. Press **Record breach**. A green **Breach recorded** toast appears with its reference, and the record reopens with its timeline.

> ⚠️ Counts only here. Keep the names of children and families out of this record.

A record needs a title, **Discovered on** and **What happened**; without them you see "A few things are needed".

## Deciding whether there is a real risk of significant harm

1. Open the breach with **Open …** on its row.
2. Under **Real risk of significant harm**, set **Sensitivity** and **Likelihood of misuse**.
3. Answer **Real risk of significant harm?** with **Yes** or **No**, and write your **Reasoning**.
4. Press **Save changes**.

If the answer is **Yes**, you must report to the Privacy Commissioner and tell the people affected as soon as you can.
Record it under **Reporting and notification**: **Reported to the Commissioner on**, **Commissioner file number**,
**People notified on**, **How they were notified** and **Others notified**. Then set **Status** to **Reported**, and to
**Closed** once everything is done.

## Logging each step

1. In **Steps and changes**, type what you did ("Asked the recipients to delete the email").
2. Press **Add step**. Every edit is also added to the timeline automatically, with who made it and when.

## Choosing who is alerted

1. Press **⚙ Alert settings**.
2. Untick anyone under **People (untick to leave someone out)** who should not get breach emails, and add any outside addresses under **Also email these addresses** (they get the summary email only and cannot open the register).
3. Choose the bell and phone notices, and the **Daily reminder while a breach is overdue** with its time.
4. Save. You see **Alert settings saved**.

**⤓ Download register** saves the whole register as a spreadsheet for an auditor or the Commissioner.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Record, edit, add steps, set alerts, download. |
| Centre director | Read the records for their own centres and agency-wide ones ("You can read this record. Your agency admins make the changes."). They get the alert emails for those. |
| Platform admin | The agency chosen in the switcher; KiddieTrac platform admins can also be alerted for every agency. |

Nothing in the register is ever deleted.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? The tiles show "Report or notify due". | A breach has **Yes** for real risk of harm but no reporting or notification dates yet. Open it and finish **Reporting and notification**.
??? An amber banner says a breach has no decision after a week. | Open it and answer **Real risk of significant harm?** with your reasoning.
??? Can I delete a record made by mistake? | No. Add a step explaining the mistake and set **Status** to **Closed**.
??? I can't see the + Record a breach button. | Only agency admins can record and change breaches.
