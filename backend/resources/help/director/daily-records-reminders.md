# Daily records reminders

Families count on the daily record: the observations, the meals and naps, the photo of
their child's day. Daily records reminders make sure gaps are noticed the same evening,
not at the next inspection.

Each evening, every educator who worked that day gets a short, professional email listing
anything not recorded. It counts how many days in a recent window had gaps, escalates
when that becomes a pattern, and ends with fresh tips. The centre's director and the
agency admins are copied.

The settings are in **Settings → Room Controls → Daily records**. Agency admins change
them. Directors can see them, and the reminders sent for their own centres.

[[open: #educator-rooms?tab=records | Open Daily records settings]]

## What is checked

For each educator, on each day the centre is open:

| Item | Checked when | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Observations** | Children were in care | At least 1 |
| **Daily log entries** (meals, naps, toileting, activities and notes together) | Children were in care | At least 3 |
| **Photos or videos** shared with families | Children were in care | At least 1 |
| **Meals and snacks logged** | Children were in care | At least one meal or snack |
| **Lesson plan** for the educator's room or centre this week | Every working day | On |
| **Menu** for the week, written and published | Every working day | On |

The daily numbers are the same ones the educator sees in their 7 p.m. "Your day at a
glance" email, so the two never disagree.

Only educators who worked that day are checked: a clock-in, a child they signed in, or
anything they logged. An educator on the clock with no children in the room is not asked
for observations of nobody. Days the centre is closed are skipped.

## Setting it up (agency admins)

1. Go to **Settings → Room Controls** and open the **📝 Daily records** tab.
2. Tick **Send daily records reminders**.
3. Set **Send at**, in your agency's time. The default is 6:30 p.m.
4. Under **What educators are expected to record each day**, set the minimums. Enter
   **0** to turn a check off.
5. Under **Scheduled for the week**, tick **Lesson plan** and **Menu** if you want them
   checked.
6. Under **Copies and escalation**, tick **Copy the director and agency admins** and set
   **Escalate after** (for example, 3 days in 30 days).
7. Press **Save**.

Reminders are **on by default**, with the defaults above, until an agency admin changes
them. To stop them, untick **Send daily records reminders** and press **Save**.

## Escalation

The email always says how many working days in the window had incomplete records, for
example "This is the 2nd working day in the last 30 days with incomplete records".

Once that count reaches your **Escalate after** number, the email changes:

- the subject starts with **Escalated:** and shows the count, such as "(4 days in 30)";
- it says the reminder has been escalated and shared with the director and administrator
  under your agency's documentation policy.

Before that, the email tells the educator how many such days lead to escalation.

## What the educator receives

- An email from your agency, with the director and agency admins on copy (if you ticked
  it). It lists each missing item, what was recorded against what was expected, and why
  it matters.
- Three tips: two for what they missed and one for busy days. The tips change every day
  and differ from one educator to the next.
- A notification bell in KiddieTrac that opens the **Daily log**.

Each educator gets at most one reminder a day. Nothing is sent to an educator whose
records were complete.

## Reminders sent — last 14 days

Below the settings, a table lists the reminders from the last 14 days: date, educator and
centre, what was not recorded, and how many days in the window had gaps. Escalated
reminders are marked **escalated**. A reminder that could not be emailed shows
**(not emailed)**.

Directors see only their own centres here.

??? Does it go to home visitors or directors? | No. Only people with the educator role at a centre get the reminder. Directors and agency admins are copied.
??? Can an educator still fix the record after the email? | Yes. The email asks them to add the entries that evening or first thing the next day.
??? Why did an educator not get a reminder? | They did not work that day, the centre was closed, everything was recorded, or their account is switched off.
