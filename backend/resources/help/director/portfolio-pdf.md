# Portfolio PDF export

A printable / shareable PDF of everything in a child's portfolio: milestones, observations, and recent daily activities.

## How to download

1. Open the child's **portfolio** screen (Children → click a child → **Portfolio** button).
2. Click **⤓ Download PDF** in the toolbar.
3. A multi-page PDF downloads — cover page, milestones grouped by status, all observations in reverse chronological order, then the last 60 daily-log entries.

## When to use it

- Year-end keepsake for the family
- Transition file when a child moves to school
- Compliance file for a centre inspection
- Print a copy for a parent-teacher conference

The PDF respects your white-label branding — agency logo on the cover, your support email in the footer.
