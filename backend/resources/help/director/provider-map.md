# Provider map

**Provider map** plots every open provider (centre) in your agency on a map, from the address saved on each centre. Use it to see where your providers are, to find the one nearest a family, or to spot a centre whose address is missing or wrong. Open it from the sidebar: **Overview > Provider map**.

[[open: #provider-map | Open Provider map]]

## Finding a provider on the map

1. Open **Provider map**. The banner reads "Every location in your agency, plotted by address."
2. Wait while the line under the banner says **Locating providers… (x/y)**. Addresses already found before appear at once; a new address takes about a second each.
3. When it finishes, the line reads, for example, "9 of 10 providers located · 1 missing/invalid address". Green means every provider was found.
4. Look at the list on the left (on a phone, under the map). Each provider shows its colour dot, name, address and a status: **Located**, **Could not locate this address** or **No address on file**.
5. Click a provider in the list. The map zooms to its pin and opens a card with the name, address, 📞 phone and **Capacity**.
6. You can also click any pin on the map, drag to move around and scroll or pinch to zoom.

Each provider keeps the same colour here as on the **Agency overview** and **Centres** screens, so a pin is easy to match to its card.

## Fixing a provider that cannot be found

1. Note the provider marked **Could not locate this address** or **No address on file** (shown in red).
2. Open **Centres**, find that centre, and choose **⋮ > Edit**.
3. Use **Find the address** to pick the right address, or correct **Address**, **City**, **Province / State** and **Postal / ZIP code** yourself.
4. Press **Save changes**.
5. Come back to **Provider map**. The provider is looked up again and gets its pin.

> 💡 A full street address with the postal code is found most reliably. A post office box or a town name alone often cannot be placed.

## Who can do what

- **Agency admin**: sees every open provider in the agency, and fixes addresses in **Centres**.
- **Platform admin**: the same, for the agency they are viewing.
- **Centre director, educators, parents**: do not have this screen.

Archived providers are not shown. Once a provider is found, its location is saved for everyone in the agency, so the map opens quickly next time.

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? The screen says "No providers to map yet." | Your agency has no open centres. Add one in **Centres**.
??? It says "Could not load the map: …". | The list of providers or the map itself could not load. Check your connection and reload the page. Some company networks block map tiles.
??? The map is grey or only half drawn. | Resize the window or reload the page; the map redraws to fit.
??? A pin is in the wrong place. | The address on the centre was matched to the wrong spot. Correct the address in **Centres**. If the pin still does not move after you save, contact KiddieTrac support to reset its saved location.
??? Can families see this map? | No. It is a reference for agency admins only.
