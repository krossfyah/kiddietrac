# Adding and managing leads

**Sales › Leads** lists every prospect — a childcare agency or centre you are selling KiddieTrac to — with its stage,
estimated yearly revenue, owner and next follow-up. From here you add leads, open one to work it, merge duplicates and
move each lead from first contact to **Won** or **Lost**. The whole sales team shares one list.

[[open: #sales-leads | Open Leads]]

## Adding a lead

1. Press **+ New lead** (on **Leads** or on the pipeline).
2. Under **Primary contact**, enter the **Contact name** (the only required field), **Job title**, **Email** and **Phone**. If someone else signs the deal, fill in the **Business owner / decision maker** fields.
3. Enter the **Company**, **Website** and what they are **Using today** (for example another app, or paper).
4. Choose the **Lead source**, the **Owner** (you, by default), the **Expected close** and **Next follow-up** dates, and the **Est. annual revenue** if you know it.
5. Press **Create lead**. The sales team is notified by email and you land on the new lead's page.

> 💡 The estimated revenue fills in by itself when you send a proposal. If you change it by hand, your figure stays until the next proposal.

## Working a lead

1. Open the lead from the list (tap a row or card).
2. Change the **Stage** at the top: **New → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal Sent → Negotiation → Won / Lost**. A **Stage updated** toast appears and the move is logged.
3. In **Activity & history**, choose a note, a **Meeting** or a **Follow-up task**, describe it ("Log a note, or describe the follow-up"), pick a due date for a task, and save it.
4. To keep a Teams call on file, press **Attach transcript / recording**; what was discussed is added to the lead's history.
5. Edit any detail and press **Save changes**. A **Saved** toast appears.

A lead moves to **Won** by itself when a proposal is signed by both sides; it then shows under **Sales › Customers**.

## Finding and merging duplicates

1. In the list, a lead that looks like another is flagged **Possible duplicate**, and **Possible duplicates above** lists the pairs with the reason.
2. Press **Review**, check which record to keep, then **Merge into the kept lead**. Its activity, proposals, bookings and files move across and empty details are filled in.
3. If they are different prospects, choose **Not the same** — they will not be suggested again.
4. To merge by hand, use **Merge** on a lead's row and search for the duplicate.

## Leads from the referral program

Anyone can refer a childcare program from the Referrals page on kiddietrac.com. Each referral arrives as a lead at
**New** with **Lead source** set to Referral and no owner — pick it up. Its notes say who referred them. If the
program's email already belongs to an open lead, the referral is added to that lead's history instead. The referred
program's first month is free, and the referrer earns a one-time $200 CAD once the program has paid for 60 days.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Sales rep | Add, work, merge and delete leads; the whole team shares the list. |
| Platform admin | Everything a sales rep can, plus sales settings. |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? I can't find a lead. | Use **Search name, company, email** at the top of **Leads**. A merged lead lives on inside the one it was merged into.
??? Should I delete a lead that went cold? | No — mark it **Lost** so it counts in the win/loss figures. **Delete** ("Delete this lead?") is for duplicates and mistakes only; it removes the lead for everyone.
??? "Could not delete" or "Could not merge" | Refresh and try again. If it keeps failing, raise a ticket from Help.
??? Does changing the stage email the prospect? | No. Stage changes are only logged on the lead.
