# Quick access and the Everything launcher

On a phone, agency admins and centre directors have a launcher called **Everything**. It holds every place in your menu as a tile, grouped the same way as the sidebar, with your favourite places in **Quick access** at the top. You choose which tiles are in Quick access.

## Where it is

On your phone, tap **🏠 Home** in the bar along the bottom of the screen.

[[open: #home | Open Everything]]

The screen shows, from top to bottom:

1. A **Search…** box that searches every place in your menu.
2. **Quick access**, your own short list of tiles.
3. Every other section of your menu, each in its own card.

## Opening and closing sections

Tap a section's heading to open or close it. The number beside the heading tells you how many places are inside.

- **Quick access** is open until you close it.
- The first section of your menu is open the first time. The rest start closed.
- KiddieTrac remembers which sections you left open or closed.

Typing in **Search…** shows matching places from every section, so you do not need to open them first.

## Customising Quick access

Quick access starts with up to eight places most admins and directors use, such as the dashboard, children, families, messages and reports. You can change it to suit how you work.

1. Under Quick access, tap **✏️ Customize**. The tiles start to wiggle.
2. To **remove** a tile, tap the red **✕** on its corner.
3. To **move** a tile, tap **◀** or **▶** under it.
4. To **add** a place, tap the **＋ Add** tile. Find the place in the list (you can type in **🔍 Find a place…**), then tap **+ Add**. Tap **✓ Added** to take it off again. Press **Done** to close the list.
5. When you are finished, tap **Done**.

You can have up to **16** tiles. The count shows while you edit, for example "9 of 16".

> **Tip:** The list of places only shows screens your role can open, so you can never add a tile that leads nowhere.

## Going back to the default

While customising, tap **Reset to default** to put back the standard tiles. The button only appears once you have changed something.

If you remove every tile, Quick access says "Nothing pinned" until you add some again.

## Who it is for

- **Agency admins**, **centre directors** and **platform admins** each have their own Quick access.
- Your choice is saved to **your account**, so it follows you to another phone or tablet.
- It is saved **per role**. If you are both an agency admin and a director, or you switch roles, each role keeps its own tiles.

??? I changed Quick access on my phone. Will my tablet show it? | Yes. It is saved to your account, so it appears on any device where you sign in with the same role.
??? A tile I added has disappeared. | Tiles only show for places your current role can open. If your role or permissions changed, that place is no longer available to you.
??? Does this change the sidebar on my computer? | No. Quick access only changes the tiles on the Everything launcher. Your sidebar stays the same.
