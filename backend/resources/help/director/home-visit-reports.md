# Home visit reports

Home visit reports are the notes home visitors write after each visit to a family or provider: who was there, what happened, strengths, concerns and next steps. This screen is the reviewer's list of every report in your agency. From here you can search and sort reports, read one, download it as a PDF, correct it (every change is logged), and record a visit you made yourself.

[[open: #home-visit-reports | Open Home visit reports]]

**Where it is:** sidebar → **Home Visitors** → **Home visit reports**.

## Finding a report

1. Open **Home Visitors** → **Home visit reports**. The table lists every report, newest visit first.
2. Type in **🔍 Filter by home visitor, family, centre…** to narrow the list as you type.
3. Press a column heading (**Home visitor**, **Family / child**, **Centre**, **Date**, **Type**, **Status**) to sort by it. Press it again to reverse the order.
4. Check the **Status** column: **Draft** (not finished yet), **Submitted**, or **Edited** (changed after it was submitted; hover to see how many edits).

The bar under the table shows how many reports are listed, how many were edited and how many are drafts.

> ℹ️ The search box and sorting show on a computer or tablet. On a phone you see the full list.

## Reading a report and downloading the PDF

1. On the report's row, press **ℹ️ View report**. The **Home Visit Report** opens with its type, date and status.
2. Read the details: **Home visitor**, **Centre**, **Family / child**, **Who was present**, **Location**, **Duration** and **Follow-up date**, then **Visit summary**, **Provider strengths**, **Concerns / observations** and **Next steps**.
3. If the report was changed, **🕓 Edit history** at the bottom shows who changed what, when, and old value → new value.
4. Press **⬇ Download PDF** to save or print a copy.
5. Press **← Back** to return to the list.

## Correcting a report

1. On the row, press **✏️ Edit** (or **✎ Edit** on the report's view). **Edit home visit report** opens with the report filled in.
2. Change what needs correcting.
3. Type why in **Add a note (optional)**. The note is saved to the report's **Activity log**.
4. Press **Save changes**. A **Report updated** message appears.

The **Activity log** under the form lists **Report created** and every later change with the date, time and the person's name.

## Recording a visit you made yourself

1. Press **➕ New home visit report** at the top of the list.
2. Under **Visit details**, choose the **Centre** the family belongs to, check the **Visit date**, and pick the **Visit type** (**Initial visit**, **Routine visit**, **Follow-up**, **Assessment** or **Other**).
3. Optionally choose the **Location** (**Family home**, **Virtual / phone**, **Community** or **Other**) and enter the **Duration (minutes)**.
4. Under **Family**, enter the **Family name**, the **Child name (optional)** and **Who was present**.
5. Under **Observations**, write the **Visit summary** (required), then any **Strengths observed**, **Concerns**, **Next steps** and **Follow-up date (optional)**.
6. Press **💾 Save as draft** to finish later, or **Submit report** when it is complete. A **Report submitted** message confirms it. The report is recorded under your name. Open **Home visit reports** again to see it in the list.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | See every report, view, download, edit any report and record their own. |
| Centre director | The same as an agency admin. |
| Platform admin | The same, in the agency they are working in. |
| Home visitor | Writes reports under **My reports**; can edit only their own drafts. Submitted reports are corrected by a reviewer. |
| Educators and parents | Do not see home visit reports. |

Home visit reports are part of the home visiting features of your plan.

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? The list says "No home-visit reports match." | Your search hides every report. Clear the search box.
??? I see "Could not load reports:" with a message. | The list did not load. Reload the page; if it keeps happening, send the message to KiddieTrac support.
??? Submit report says "Please choose a centre." | Pick the family's **Centre** at the top of the form and press **Submit report** again.
??? It says "Please set the visit date." or "Please add a visit summary." | Fill in **Visit date** or **Visit summary**; both are needed to submit. A draft can be saved without them.
??? The Centre list says "Could not load centres". | Reload the page. If it still fails, you may not have any open centres you can work in.
??? A home visitor cannot change a report they already submitted. | That is on purpose. Open the report here, press **✏️ Edit**, make the correction and add a note explaining it.
