# Parent Wallet

Store up to **six payment methods** per family — credit cards, debit cards, and Canadian ACH (bank account) — and pick one as the default for autopay.

## Adding a method

1. Sidebar → **Wallet**.
2. Tap **+ Add credit/debit card** or **+ Add bank account (ACH)**.
3. Enter card or bank details in the Stripe-secured form.
4. Optionally name the method (e.g. "My everyday card") so you can tell them apart later.

Card details never touch our servers — they go directly to Stripe.

## Setting the default

The default method is the one charged automatically when an invoice is due. The first method added is automatically set as default. Tap **Set default** on any other method to switch.

## Removing a method

Tap **Remove** on any saved method. If it was the default, the next-added method auto-promotes. Removing your only method turns off autopay.

## Limits

- Maximum 6 methods per family (matches Procare standard)
- Adding a card OR bank account does not pre-charge you — it just stores the payment instrument for future use.
