# De-enrolling a family

When a family leaves, de-enrolling closes their place properly: the children are
withdrawn, the guardians' sign-in ends, everyone is told, and the records are kept for
your retention period.

**Nothing is deleted.** De-enrolling is not a delete, and it cannot be undone from the
screen. Read [What happens](#what-happens) before you start.

## Before you start

- Settle or note any outstanding balance. The last screen will show you what is owed,
  and the family's goodbye email will itemise it.
- If the family is only pausing — a summer away, a parental leave — use **Suspend**
  instead. A suspension is reversible; this is not.
- If they are moving to another provider you run, use **Move to another provider**
  instead, so the children keep their history and their place.

## Step by step

1. Go to **Administration → Families**.
2. Find the family and open the row actions (the **⋮** at the end of the row).
3. Choose **🗑️ De-enrol**.
4. Read the summary. It lists exactly what will happen, and — if the family owes money
   — the **outstanding balance and every unpaid invoice**.
5. If a balance is shown, you are asked to confirm a second time. That second
   confirmation records that you saw the figure; it is written to the audit log with
   your name against it.
6. Confirm. The children are withdrawn, the accounts close and the emails go out.

> **A balance never blocks a de-enrolment.** You are warned, not stopped. A disputed
> invoice or a few cents of rounding must never trap a record with no way out — so the
> decision stays yours, and the system just makes sure it is an informed one.

![The de-enrol confirmation, showing the outstanding balance and exactly what will change](https://api.kiddietrac.com/help-img/de-enrol.png)

## What happens

The moment you confirm, in this order:

| | What changes |
|---|---|
| **Children** | Marked **withdrawn** as of today, with the date recorded. They leave every roster, ratio and capacity count immediately. |
| **Guardians** | Every guardian account is **deactivated**. They can no longer sign in, on any device. |
| **Emails** | Each guardian is emailed a goodbye notice. Your directors and agency admins are blind-copied. |
| **The family record** | Archived — kept, not deleted. |
| **Audit log** | Records who did it, how many children were withdrawn, how many accounts closed, and the balance outstanding at the time. |

The emails are sent **before** the accounts close, so the notice reaches a family that
still exists. **They cannot be unsent.**

## What the family receives

A warm goodbye that answers the question a departing family actually has — *what
happens to my child's records?* It states your retention period in your own numbers,
explains that licensed child care records must be kept and cannot be deleted on request
while that requirement applies, and thanks them.

If money is owed, the same email also carries:

- an **itemised ledger** — every unpaid invoice, its due date, what was invoiced and
  what is still owed, with overdue ones marked;
- a clear request to settle in full, and an invitation to talk to you if an invoice
  looks wrong or they need time;
- a plain statement that an unpaid account may be referred to collections.

The ledger is itemised rather than summarised on purpose: their portal access ends with
that message, so the invoices they could previously look up are about to become
unreachable. That email is their copy.

## Afterwards

The family has not vanished. Go to **Administration → Families** and switch from
**Active** to **Archived** to find them, along with anyone else who has left. Open the
record to read it exactly as before — see
[Archived records and retention](archived-records).

## De-enrolling several families at once

Tick the families in the table and use the bulk **De-enrol** button. Everything above
happens for each one, including an email per guardian. The confirmation tells you how
many emails that will be. There is no undo, so check the selection twice.

## If the family is going to another agency

De-enrol here, and let the receiving agency enrol them fresh. Do not delete the
children first — their withdrawal date is part of your licensing record.
