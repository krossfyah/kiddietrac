# Assigning tasks to your team

Use **Staff → Tasks** to hand out work and track it to completion — for educators, home visitors and parents.

## Assign a task

1. Open **Staff → Tasks** and click **➕ Assign task**.
2. Pick who it's for. The list is grouped by **Educators**, **Home visitors** and **Parents** in your agency (a centre director sees the educators at their own centre, plus the agency's home visitors and parents).
3. Give it a **title**, optional **details**, a **priority** (High / Normal / Low) and an optional **due date**.
4. **Assign task** — it appears instantly on that person's *My tasks* home screen, and their tile shows a red open-count.

## Track it

The Tasks screen shows counts (**Open / In progress / Done**) and a table of every task with who it's assigned to, priority, due date and current status. The assignee moves it through *Start → Done* themselves. On each row, the **⋮ actions menu** (far right) lets you **View** the full task, **Edit** it — title, details, priority, due date and status — or **Delete** it.
