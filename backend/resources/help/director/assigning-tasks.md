# Assigning tasks to your team

The **Tasks** screen is where agency admins and centre directors hand out work and follow it to completion. You can give a task to an educator, a home visitor or a parent, let them know straight away by app, email, text or call, and set a reminder for later. The person sees it on their own **My tasks** screen and marks it started and done.

[[open: #tasks | Open Tasks]]

At the top you see three counts, **Open**, **In progress** and **Done**, and below them a table of every task with the columns **Task**, **Assigned to**, **Priority**, **Created**, **Due** and **Status**.

## Assigning a task

1. Press **➕** (**Assign task**) next to the **Tasks** heading. The **Assign a task** window opens.
2. In **Assign to**, pick the person. The list is grouped into **Educators**, **Home visitors** and **Parents**, with each person's centre after their name.
3. Optionally set a **Due date**.
4. Type a **Title**, for example "Update the allergy board".
5. Add **Details (optional)** if the person needs more to go on.
6. Choose the **Priority**: **Normal**, **High** or **Low**.
7. Under **📣 Let them know now**, tick how to tell them: **📱 App** (ticked already), **✉️ Email**, **💬 Text** or **📞 Voice call**. Untick them all to assign quietly.
8. Optionally set a **⏰ Reminder** (see below).
9. Press **Assign task**. A toast says "Task assigned" and which ways it was sent, and the task appears at the top of the table.

If a message could not go out, the toast says "not sent by" that channel "(their settings or consent)". The task is still assigned.

## Setting a reminder

The **⏰ Reminder** box is in both the **Assign a task** and **Edit task** windows.

1. Open the **When** list.
2. Choose **The day before it is due, 8:00 AM**, **The morning it is due, 8:00 AM**, or **Pick a date and time…**. The first two need a **Due date**.
3. If you chose **Pick a date and time…**, set the **Date and time**.
4. Tick how to send it: **📱 App**, **✉️ Email**, **💬 Text** or **📞 Voice call**.
5. Save the task. The table shows the reminder under the title, for example "⏰ Nov 28, 8:00 AM · App, Email".

Once the reminder has gone out, the row shows "✓ Reminder sent". Change the time to send it again. Times are in your agency's time zone.

## Viewing, editing and deleting a task

Each row has a **⋮** actions menu at the far right.

1. Choose **⋮ > View** to read the whole task in **Task details**. Press **Close** when you are done.
2. Choose **⋮ > Edit** to open **Edit task**. Change the **Title**, **Details**, **Priority**, **Due date**, **Status** or the reminder, then press **Save changes**. A toast says "Task updated".
3. Choose **⋮ > Delete** and confirm "Delete this task? This cannot be undone." The task is removed for you and the person it was assigned to.

You cannot change who a task is assigned to. Delete it and assign a new one instead.

## How the person works through it

The assignee opens **My tasks** and sees each task as a card with its priority, status, due date and who it came from. They press **▶ Start** to move it to **In progress** and **✓ Mark done** when it is finished, or **↺ Reopen** to open it again. Your counts and table update the next time you open **Tasks**. A due date that has passed shows in red with ⚠ until the task is done.

## Private to-dos

To-dos you add for yourself from the My day panel also show here as **Me** with a **Private** tag. Only you see them, and they never send anything. Choose **⋮ > Mark done** on the row to finish one.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Assign tasks to anyone in the agency and manage every task. |
| Centre director | Assign tasks to the educators at their own centres and to the agency's home visitors and parents. |
| Platform admin | The same as an agency admin, for the agency they have chosen. |
| Educators, home visitors, parents | Do not see this screen. They see their own tasks on **My tasks**. |

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? The Assign to list says "No assignable people in this agency". | No educators, home visitors or parents are set up yet, or none at your centres. Invite them first, then try again.
??? I get "Pick who the task is for". | Choose a person in **Assign to** before pressing **Assign task**.
??? I get "Give the task a title". | The **Title** is required. Type one and press **Assign task** again.
??? I get "Set a due date, or pick a date and time for the reminder". | The two "8:00 AM" reminder choices count from the due date. Set a **Due date**, or choose **Pick a date and time…**.
??? I get "Choose how to send the reminder". | Tick at least one of **📱 App**, **✉️ Email**, **💬 Text** or **📞 Voice call** in the reminder box.
??? The text or call was not sent. | Texts and calls follow each person's own settings and consent. If they have turned them off, only the other channels go out.
??? The table is empty. | It says "No tasks yet" until you assign the first one. Use **➕** (**Assign task**) to start.
