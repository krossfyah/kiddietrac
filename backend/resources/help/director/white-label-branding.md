# Branding and white-label

**Branding** is where your agency's identity lives: your logo and colour, your invoice look and numbering, your contact and legal details, how your email is sent, your payslip wording, and your region and ID settings. Each of these has its own tab.

> Who can use this: **Agency admin** (for their agency), **Platform admin**.

## Where it is

**Agency overview → ✏️ Edit agency → Open Branding →**. Platform admins can also use **Reseller → Branding**. Either way, the screen works on the agency you are currently viewing.

[[open: #admin-branding | Open Branding]]

For the agency's **name, contact email and phone, and subdomain**, use the **✏️ Edit agency** button on the **Agency overview** screen itself.

![The Edit agency button on Agency overview](https://api.kiddietrac.com/help-img/edit-agency.png)

## The tabs

A row of tabs runs across the top of Branding:

| Tab | What it holds |
|---|---|
| **🏷️ White-label & logo** | Your licence status, your logo, your primary colour, and a preview of where your logo appears |
| **🧾 Invoices** | Invoice style, invoice numbering, business address, bank details, and the live invoice preview |
| **📇 Contact & legal** | Support email, privacy policy link, terms and conditions link |
| **✉️ Email delivery** | Send your agency's email from your own Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace address |
| **💵 Payslips** | The wording printed on every staff payslip |
| **🌎 Region & IDs** | Your country and compliance settings, and your reference ID prefix |

Platform admins also see **🍂 Seasonal email headers** (see below).

The **Save changes** button at the bottom saves the **White-label & logo**, **Invoices** and **Contact & legal** tabs together, whichever one is open. The other tabs have their own save buttons.

## Your logo and colour

1. Open the **🏷️ White-label & logo** tab.
2. Under **Logo**, press **Upload logo** and choose a file, or drag a file onto the box. Use PNG, JPG or WebP, up to 2 MB. A wide logo on a transparent background works best.
3. To change it later, press **Replace logo**. To take it off, press **Remove**.
4. If your logo is already online, open **Use a link to an image instead** and paste its address.
5. Set your **Primary colour**. It is used for the header of your invoices and white-label emails.
6. Press **Save changes**.

Once saved, your logo is used on invoices, emails and forms.

## The white-label licence

The **White-label licence** box at the top of the tab tells you whether your agency has it.

- **Licence off:** your logo appears on invoices and emails. The portal and app keep the KiddieTrac brand.
- **Licence on:** your logo replaces KiddieTrac throughout. Families and staff see it in the top bar (for every role, on a computer and on a phone), on the app's loading screen after sign-in, on the quick-unlock screen, and on the sign-in page of your own branded app.

The licence is something your agency buys, so only a **platform admin** can switch it. Platform admins see a **Show "Powered by Kiddietrac"** checkbox; untick it only for an agency on the white-label licence. Agency admins see the licence status but cannot change it. Ask KiddieTrac about the licence if you want your own brand throughout.

> **Tip:** Your own app icon and app name in the app stores come with a white-label app build. Ask KiddieTrac.

### Previewing it

Beside the logo settings, **Where your logo appears** shows a live preview of the **top bar** and the **app loading and unlock screen**. It changes as you change the logo, before you save. A line above the preview tells you what families and staff will see:

- with the licence on and a logo: your logo,
- with the licence on and no logo yet: KiddieTrac, until you upload one,
- with the licence off: KiddieTrac.

## Invoices

The **🧾 Invoices** tab holds:

- **Invoice style** — the layout of your invoices, chosen from twelve styles.
- **Invoice numbering** — the format of your invoice numbers and where a running number starts.
- **Business address** — appears in the invoice *From* block.
- **Bank info / payment details** — shown on invoices.

Press **↻ Refresh preview** to update the **Live invoice preview**. Choosing a different invoice style updates it straight away. See [Invoice style and numbering](invoice-numbering-and-style).

## Contact & legal

- **Support email** — shown on invoices and parent emails.
- **Privacy policy URL** and **Terms & conditions URL** — linked from the footer of your emails and marketing campaigns. Set these as part of your agency setup.

## Email delivery

By default, KiddieTrac sends your email for you. If your agency has its own Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, you can send from your own address instead, so families see mail from you. When it is set up, the card shows **Own email active**.

## Payslips

**Payslip appearance** sets an optional message that appears on every staff payslip. Your logo, name and address are added automatically.

## Region & IDs

- **Country & compliance** — pick the country you operate in. KiddieTrac applies the matching currency and shows the rules that apply to you.
- **Reference ID prefix** — the letters at the front of every child, family, staff and parent ID, such as **ILH** in ILH-C-0042. See [Reference IDs](reference-ids).

## Seasonal email headers (platform admins)

The **🍂 Seasonal email headers** tab sets the banner at the top of KiddieTrac emails for each season. It applies across the platform, so only platform admins see it. It used to be in the Settings menu.

## What appears on white-label invoices

The invoice *From* block pulls your **agency name, address, phone, and support email**, and uses your **logo and primary colour**. The same applies to real invoices, the form **Preview**, and emailed forms and announcements (sent from your agency's own address when email delivery is set up).
