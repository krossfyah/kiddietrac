# All my agencies (reseller overview)

**All my agencies** is a reseller's home screen. It shows every agency and centre you brought to the platform, as of right now: how full they are, who is here today, open tickets, plan billing and anything that needs your attention. From here you open an agency to work inside it, or look after its admins. Platform admins see the same screen as **Reseller overview**, for any reseller.

[[open: #reseller | Open All my agencies]]

## Reading the totals

1. Open **All my agencies** from the **Reseller** section of the menu (platform admins: **Reseller overview**).
2. Read the tiles across the top: **Agencies**, **Centres**, **Enrolled / capacity**, **Here today** (with the attendance percentage), **Staff on the floor**, **Families**, **Open tickets**, **Plan billing / month** and **Need attention**.
3. Scroll down to the agency cards. Each card repeats the numbers for that agency, plus **Last activity**, **Plan** and **Billing** (with any amount overdue).

> ℹ️ The numbers are live: "Here today" counts children signed in right now, and "Staff on the floor" counts staff clocked in.

## Finding the agencies that need attention

1. Open the filter next to the search box and pick **Needs attention** (or **Healthy** to see the rest).
2. Read the coloured flags under each agency's name, for example "No activity for 7+ days", "1 overdue plan invoice", "No agency admin of its own", "No centres yet", "No children enrolled" or "Children present with no staff clocked in". "At or near capacity" is for information only.
3. Use the sort box to order the list: **Sort: name**, **Sort: enrolled**, **Sort: here today %**, **Sort: last activity** or **Sort: open tickets**.
4. Type in **Search agencies or centres…** to find an agency by its name, a centre's name or a city.

## Seeing an agency's centres

1. On the agency card, press **Centres (n)**.
2. Read the table: **Centre**, **City**, **Enrolled**, **Capacity**, **Here today**, **Staff on floor** and **Families**.
3. Press **Hide centres** to fold it away again.

## Working inside an agency

1. On the agency card, press **Open agency →**.
2. The portal switches to that agency and opens its **Agency overview**. You now work as its agency admin, with its menu.
3. To come back, open **All my agencies** in the **Reseller** section of the menu.

## Looking after an agency's admins

1. On the agency card, press **Admins**. A popup lists the agency's own admins with **Status** (Active, Invited, Turned off, and "2FA" if they use two-step sign-in) and **Last sign-in**.
2. Press **Send new link** to email that admin a fresh set-password link (valid for 7 days).
3. Press **Turn off** to stop an admin managing the agency, and confirm. Press **Turn on** to give access back later.
4. To add one, fill in **First name**, **Last name**, **Email** (and **Phone** if you like) under **Invite another admin**, then press **Send invite**. They are emailed an invite to set a password.

[[show: #reseller @ Admins | Show me the Admins button]]

> ⚠️ Your own reseller access is not listed in this popup, and you cannot turn yourself off here.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Reseller | Sees only the agencies that belong to their reseller, opens them, manages their admins. |
| Platform admin | Picks any reseller in **Viewing reseller** at the top and sees that reseller's agencies. |
| Agency admin (not a reseller) | Does not use this screen; it answers "You do not manage a reseller." |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? The list says "No agencies yet". | No agency belongs to your reseller yet. Press **+ Onboard an agency** to add your first one, or ask KiddieTrac to move an existing agency under your brand.
??? I see "You do not manage a reseller." | Your account has no active reseller role. Raise a ticket with KiddieTrac ([[raise-a-ticket]]) and ask them to add you to the reseller's **People**.
??? "No agencies match." | Your search or the **Needs attention** / **Healthy** filter hides them. Clear the search and pick **All agencies**.
??? "Could not open that agency" appears. | The switch did not go through, usually because the agency is no longer yours or your session ended. Refresh the page and try again.
??? An admin says the link expired. | Press **Admins**, then **Send new link** beside them. Each link lasts 7 days and is sent by email only.
??? I get "Someone already has an account with that email." | That person already has a KiddieTrac account. Use a different email, or ask KiddieTrac to add their existing account to the agency.
??? Does turning an admin off email them? | No. They simply can no longer sign in to manage that agency. Turning them on again restores access.
