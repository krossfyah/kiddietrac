# Wellness pre-arrival screening

Before drop-off, parents can answer a quick symptom check for their child in the parent portal. KiddieTrac grades each answer as cleared, review or block, tells your staff about anything that needs a look, and gathers the day's results for each centre on the **Wellness digest**. Open the digest in the morning to see who is cleared, who needs checking at the door and who should stay home.

[[open: #wellness-digest | Open Wellness digest]]

## Reading today's digest

1. Open **Administration > Wellness digest**.
2. If your agency has more than one centre, pick one in **Select a centre…**. The digest opens for that centre.
3. Read the banner: the centre, today's date and how many screenings are in, for example "18/24 screenings in".
4. Check the four counts: **Cleared**, **Review**, **Block** and **Not submitted**.
5. Read the table. Each row shows the **Child**, the **Result**, the **Symptoms reported** and any **Notes** the parent added.
6. To see another centre, change the **Centre** list at the top of the screen.

The digest shows today only, in your agency's time zone. It starts fresh each morning.

## What each result means

A parent ticks any symptom the child has today: **Fever 🌡**, **Cough 😷**, **Runny nose 🤧**, **Vomiting 🤢**, **Diarrhea 💩**, **Rash 🟥** or **Sick contact 🦠**. KiddieTrac grades the answer straight away:

| Result | When | What the parent sees |
|---|---|---|
| **Block** | Fever, vomiting, diarrhea or a recent sick contact | "🚫 Please keep your child home today and contact the centre." |
| **Review** | Only a cough, runny nose or rash | "⚠ Submitted. Staff will review symptoms at arrival." |
| **Cleared** | No symptoms ticked | "✓ Cleared for drop-off today." |

> ℹ️ This follows common public-health guidance: fever (38 °C or more), vomiting, diarrhea and a known sick contact mean the child stays home; a cough or runny nose alone calls for your judgement. Your own health policy always comes first.

## Acting on a review or block

1. When a parent submits a **Review** or **Block**, the educators, director and agency admin at the child's centre get a notification, for example "🚫 Maya: do not admit today" or "⚠ Maya: review symptoms before admit".
2. Open the notification or the **Wellness digest** to see the symptoms and notes.
3. For a **Block**, contact the family if the child is brought in anyway, and follow your illness policy.
4. For a **Review**, check the child at the door before you sign them in.

## Following up on families who have not answered

1. Look at the **Not submitted** count on the digest.
2. Compare the table with your expected children: anyone missing has not answered today.
3. Remind those families at drop-off, or by a message, to complete the **Wellness check** before they leave home.

## How parents fill it in

Parents open **Wellness check** in their portal, choose the **Child**, tick any symptoms under "Any of these today?", add **Notes** if they want, and press **Submit screening**. They can submit again the same day; the newest answer replaces the earlier one.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Open the digest for every centre in the agency. |
| Platform admin | The same, for the agency they have chosen. |
| Centre director, educator | Get the review and block notifications for their centre. |
| Parents | Submit the **Wellness check** for their own children and see the result. |

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? The digest says "No screenings submitted yet today." | No parent at that centre has answered today. Check the **Centre** picked at the top, then remind families.
??? The screen says "No centres." | Your account cannot see any centre in this agency. Check the agency you have chosen, or ask an agency admin.
??? A child is cleared but looks unwell. | The check is the parent's own answer. Your staff can still follow your illness policy at the door.
??? Are parents emailed about the result? | No. The parent sees the result on screen when they submit. Staff get an in-app notification for a review or block.
??? A parent answered for the wrong child. | Ask them to open **Wellness check** again, pick the right child and submit. Each child has their own answer for the day.
