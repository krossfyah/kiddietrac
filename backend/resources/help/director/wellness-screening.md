# Wellness screening

A quick yes/no symptom check parents submit before drop-off. Helps staff decide whether to admit the child today.

## Parent flow

1. Sidebar → **Wellness check**.
2. Tap any symptom present today: Fever 🌡 / Cough 😷 / Runny nose 🤧 / Vomiting 🤢 / Diarrhea 💩 / Rash 🟥 / Sick contact 🦠.
3. Add any notes.
4. Submit.

The system grades the result:
- **🚫 Block** — fever, vomiting, diarrhea, or recent sick contact → please stay home.
- **⚠ Review** — cough, runny nose, or rash only → staff review on arrival.
- **✓ Cleared** — none of the above → all good for drop-off.

## Staff side

- Sidebar → **Wellness digest** (director / agency admin).
- Today's screenings grouped by centre with cleared / review / block counts.
- "Not submitted" count surfaces families who haven't yet completed today's check.
- Notifications fan out to staff on every block / review result.

## Public-health alignment

Following Ontario / Health Canada guidance: fever (≥38°C), vomiting, diarrhea, and known sick contact mandate exclusion. Cough + runny nose alone require judgment.
