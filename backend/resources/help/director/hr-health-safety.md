# HR: risk assessments, chemicals, safe work procedures, H&S duties and tasks

Part of the KiddieTrac HR add-on. Open **Human Resources > Health & safety**. Agency admins see every centre; directors see their own centres. Staff injuries, Hazards and the Drill log are in the same row of tabs.

## Risk assessments

Press **New risk assessment**, choose the centre, and start from a blank page or one of the examples: outdoor play, field trips, sleep room, kitchen or water play. The examples are a general starting point for childcare. They are not advice for your centre and say nothing about what any rule requires. Each copy is marked **Example — review for your centre** until you change it.

For each hazard, write who could be harmed, how likely it is and how serious it would be (1 to 5 each), the controls you have in place, and the risk that is left once the controls are in place. Risk = likelihood × severity: 1-4 low, 5-9 medium, 10-16 high, 20-25 very high. Add an owner and a review date. The owner and the centre's directors get a reminder 14 days before the review date, on the day, and every week while it is overdue.

A director of the centre or an agency admin signs it off by typing their name. The person who made the last change can't sign it. If anyone changes it after sign-off, it goes back to draft. Press ⋮ > **Print** for a paper copy.

## Chemicals (WHMIS)

Press **Add a product** for each cleaning product or other hazardous product kept at a centre. Add where it is stored, the hazard pictograms on its label or safety data sheet (SDS), and attach the SDS as a PDF or a photo. Add the date printed on the SDS and a review date.

The list flags products with no SDS on file, an SDS dated more than 3 years ago (ask the supplier for a current one), and reviews that are due or overdue. The centre's directors get a reminder before each review date and once when an SDS passes 3 years old. SDS files are private: staff of that centre open them from **My HR > Safety data sheets**. When a product leaves the centre, press ⋮ > **No longer on site**. It stays on file.

## Safe work procedures

These are step-by-step ways to do a job safely, for example lifting a child, using a cleaning product or changing a nappy. They live in your Policy library under the category **Safe work procedure**. In this tab, press **Add procedure**, then **Add text** to write it or upload the document. Staff open it and sign that they have read it, in **My HR > Safe work procedures** or **Policies to review**. When you publish a new version, everyone signs again. The table shows who has signed.

## H&S duties

Press **Name a duty** to record who is a first aider, CPR responder, fire warden, health & safety representative, committee member, food safety lead or another duty, at one centre or every centre. When a duty needs a certificate (First Aid for a first aider, for example), KiddieTrac checks the person's certifications every time. You see a valid, expiring, expired or missing certificate. The person and the centre's directors get a reminder 30 days before it expires, when it expires, and if there is none on file. Press ⋮ > **End duty** when someone stops; it stays on file.

## H&S tasks

Press **New H&S task** to give a named person a safety job, such as fixing a gate or getting a current SDS. You can also press ⋮ > **Create H&S task** on a risk assessment or a product. By default the task needs evidence before it can be closed: the person adds a photo or a file in **My HR > H&S tasks** (on a phone this opens the camera). The task also shows in their usual task list, but it can't be ticked off there without the evidence. You get a notice when it is closed. ⋮ > **Reopen** puts a closed task back; ⋮ > **Cancel task** removes it from every list.

## Hazards with a photo

Anyone on staff can report a hazard from **Hazards**, or from **My HR > Report a hazard**, and add a photo straight from their phone camera. Directors and admins see **📷 Photo attached** and press ⋮ > **See photo**. Photos are private files.

??? Who sees the safety data sheets? | The directors and admins of that centre, and the staff who work there (in My HR). The files are never public.
??? Can I sign off a risk assessment I just edited? | No. Another director of that centre or an agency admin signs it off.
??? Do the examples meet the rules in my province? | They are examples only, not advice or a statement of any rule. Review each line for your own centre.
