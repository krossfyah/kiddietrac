# HR: risk assessments, chemicals, safe work procedures, H&S duties and tasks

These tabs keep your workplace health and safety paperwork in one place: risk assessments, hazardous products and their safety data sheets (WHMIS), safe work procedures, who holds which safety duty, and safety jobs that need proof when done. They are part of the KiddieTrac HR add-on, under **Human Resources > Health & safety**, next to **Staff injuries**, **Hazards** and the **Drill log**. Agency admins see every centre; centre directors see their own.

[[open: #hr-safety?tab=risk | Open Risk assessments]]

## Writing a risk assessment

1. Open **Human Resources > Health & safety** and press the **Risk assessments** tab.
2. Press **+ New risk assessment**.
3. Choose the **Centre**, and under **Start from** pick **A blank risk assessment** or an example (outdoor play, field trips, sleep room, kitchen, water play).
4. Enter the **Activity or area**, the **Owner** and a **Review by** date.
5. For each line, fill in the **Hazard**, **Who could be harmed**, **Likelihood** and **Severity** (1 to 5), the **Controls in place**, then the **Residual likelihood** and **Residual severity** left once the controls work.
6. Press **Save**.

[[show: #hr-safety?tab=risk @ Risk assessments > New risk assessment | Show me]]

Risk = likelihood × severity: 1–4 low, 5–9 medium, 10–16 high, 20–25 very high. The owner and the centre's directors get a reminder 14 days before the review date, on the day, and every week while it is overdue.

> ⚠️ The examples are a general starting point for childcare, not advice and not a statement of any rule. Each copy is marked **Example — review for your centre** until you change it.

### Signing it off

1. Open the assessment's **⋮** menu and press **Sign off**.
2. Type your name under **Type your full name to sign** and press **Sign off**.

The person who made the last change can't sign it: another director of that centre or an agency admin does. A change after sign-off puts it back to draft. **⋮ > Print** gives a paper copy.

## Recording chemicals and safety data sheets (WHMIS)

1. Press the **Chemicals (WHMIS)** tab, then **+ Add a product**.
2. Enter the **Product**, **Supplier / maker**, **Where it is stored** and **Amount kept (optional)**.
3. Tick the hazard pictograms on its label or safety data sheet.
4. Attach the safety data sheet (SDS) as a PDF or photo, and enter the **Date on the SDS** and a **Review by** date.
5. Add **Notes (first aid, PPE, how it is used)** and press **Save**.

The list flags products with no SDS on file, an SDS more than 3 years old, and reviews due or overdue. **⋮ > Open SDS** shows the file. When a product leaves, press **⋮ > No longer on site**; it stays on file, and **Back on site** returns it.

## Adding a safe work procedure

1. Press the **Safe work procedures** tab.
2. Press **+ Add procedure** and give it a title.
3. Press **Add text** to write the steps, or upload the document.
4. Publish it. Staff of that centre sign that they have read it in **My HR** or **Policies to review**.

Procedures live in your Policy library under the category **Safe work procedure**. A new version asks everyone to sign again, and the table shows who has signed.

## Naming who holds a safety duty

1. Press the **H&S duties** tab, then **+ Name a duty**.
2. Choose the **Duty** (first aider, CPR, fire warden, H&S representative, committee member, food safety lead or other) and the **Person**.
3. Choose the **Centre** (or **Every centre**) and the **Certificate the duty needs** (or **No certificate needed**).
4. Set **From** and **Until (optional)**, then press **Save**.

The **Certificate** column checks the person's certifications live: valid, expiring, expired or missing. The person and the centre's directors are reminded 30 days before it expires, when it expires, and if none is on file. **⋮ > End duty** when someone stops.

## Giving someone an H&S task

1. Press the **H&S tasks** tab, then **+ New H&S task** (or **⋮ > Create H&S task** on a risk assessment or product).
2. Enter the **Task** and **Details**, choose **Assign to**, a **Due** date and the **Priority**.
3. Press **Create**.

By default the task needs evidence before it can be closed: the person adds a photo or file in **My HR > H&S tasks** (a phone opens the camera). You get a notice when it is closed. **⋮ > Open evidence** shows it, **Reopen** puts a closed task back, and **Cancel task** removes it from every list.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Everything, every centre. |
| Centre director | Everything for their own centres; signs off an assessment someone else last edited. |
| Staff | Read safety data sheets of their own centres, sign procedures and close their H&S tasks with evidence, in **My HR**. |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? Who sees the safety data sheets? | The directors and admins of that centre, and the staff who work there (in My HR). The files are never public.
??? Can I sign off a risk assessment I just edited? | No. Another director of that centre or an agency admin signs it off.
??? Staff can't tick off their H&S task. | It needs evidence first: they add a photo or file in My HR > H&S tasks. It can't be ticked off from the plain task list.
??? Do the examples meet the rules in my province? | They are examples only. Review each line for your own centre.
??? Why does a duty show the certificate as missing? | The person has no certificate of that type on file, or it has expired. Add or renew it in Certifications.
