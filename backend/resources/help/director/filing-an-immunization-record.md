# Filing an immunization record

Filing the card and recording what it says used to be two jobs on two screens, which is
why records sat on file for weeks with the child still showing overdue. It is one action
now.

## Two doors, one wizard

- **Immunizations** (sidebar) → **＋ Upload a record**. Opens with a child picker —
  "upload it for them" is the thought you have while looking at an overdue row.
- **A child's record → Immunization tab** → the same button, already fixed to that child.

Both open the same dialog and do the same thing.

## What the dialog asks

**The record** — a PDF or a photo of the card, up to 10 MB. A phone photo is normally well
under.

**What does this record cover?** — tick each dose the card shows, against your agency's
schedule.

- **A date is optional.** Leave it blank when the card is unclear; the dose still counts as
  recorded. A record on file is not the same as a recorded dose, and only the ticks move a
  child off the overdue list.
- **Anything the card shows that is not on your schedule can be added underneath** — type
  the vaccine and the dose label. A family that moved provinces, or a travel vaccine, does
  not fit a local schedule, and refusing to record it would make the card less useful than
  the paper it came on.
- A dose already on the child's record is **skipped, not duplicated**. Re-filing a clearer
  photo of the same card is a normal thing to do and must not double the history.

**Notes** — anything the record does not say: a faded line, a dose the clinic is
confirming.

## What happens when you file it

- The document is filed on the child's record, so it also appears on their **Documents**
  tab.
- Each dose you ticked is recorded against the child, **joined to the document it was read
  off** — so "was the 2nd DTaP entered from that card?" has an answer.
- The child's status updates immediately. If the ticks clear everything the schedule
  expects by their age, they stop being overdue.
- **The office is emailed**: your other directors and agency admins are told which child,
  by whom, which doses were recorded, which were skipped as already known, your notes, and
  **what is still outstanding afterwards** — the difference between "handled" and
  "handled, keep chasing". You are not emailed about your own filing.
- The team is notified in the app.
- An audit row is written **naming every dose**, not a count.

## The Immunization tab

A child's Immunization tab has two sub-tabs:

- **💉 What is due** — the schedule, and what is outstanding.
- **📄 Records on file** — the cards themselves, with the doses read off each one and any
  notes. Somebody who has just filed a card is looking for this one.

**View** opens a record in the portal's document panel, with **Print** in its header —
it does not open a new tab or leave the app. See
[Opening and printing documents](opening-and-printing-documents).

## Who can do what

- **Agency admins, directors, educators and home visitors** at the child's centre can file
  a record *and* record the doses off it.
- **Parents can send a record in**, and that is all. Deciding that a smudged line means
  "DTaP-IPV-Hib, 2nd dose" is a clinical judgement and it is the centre's to make. Their
  upload lands here for you to read and tick off.

## Worth knowing

- A record filed by a parent still shows the child as overdue until somebody records the
  doses from it. That is the point at which a compliance flag clears, and it is deliberate.
- Correct the underlying data on the child's own record. The
  [Immunization report](immunization-report) only reads.
- A filed record cannot be deleted by a parent. Remove one through the child's Documents
  tab, which is audited.
