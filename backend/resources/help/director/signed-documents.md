# Signed documents — the e-signature record

**Engagement → Signed documents** is the record of agreements people have accepted with
an electronic signature in the portal: the privacy policy and NDA, handbook
acknowledgements, enrolment contracts, photo consent, medical authorisations and field-trip
permissions. Open it to prove who agreed to what, and when, or to give someone a copy.

[[open: #signed-docs | Open Signed documents]]

> ℹ️ PDF forms sent from **Forms Manager** are kept on its **✅ Completed** tab, and PDFs from **eDocuments** under **View signatures** on each document. This screen holds the built-in agreements.

## Finding a signed document

1. Open **Signed documents**. The table under **Signed & on file** lists every signature by the people in your agency.
2. Type a name or a document in the search box above the table to narrow it down.
3. Click a column heading, **Document**, **Signed by** or **Signed (agency time)**, to sort by it.
4. Use the page buttons under the table when there are more than 25 rows.

## Opening or saving a copy

1. Find the row.
2. Press its **⋮** button.
3. Choose **View document** to read it on screen, with the signature and its stamp.
4. Choose **Download** to save the file. Its name has the document, the signer and the date in it, so it files neatly. **Download** only appears when a signed file is stored for that row.

## Reading a row

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Document** | What was agreed to, for example **Privacy policy & NDA** or **Photo consent**. "**— declined**" means the person refused it, and the refusal is recorded too. |
| **Signed by** | The person who signed. |
| **Signed (agency time)** | The date and time, in your agency's time zone. |

The signature stamp on the document itself adds the IP address and the approximate location
it was signed from (city, province or state, country). Nobody types these in: the server
records them at the moment of signing.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they see |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Every signature by people in the agency you are working in. |
| Platform admin | The same, for the agency picked at the top. |
| Parent | Their own signed documents, on their **My documents** screen. |
| Centre director, educator | No access to this screen. |

Nothing on this screen can be edited or deleted. It is a record, not a work list.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? It says "No signed documents yet." | Nobody in this agency has signed one of the built-in agreements yet, or you are working in a different agency. Check the agency picker at the top.
??? A parent's form is not here. | Check what kind of form it was. A PDF form from Forms Manager is on its Completed tab; an eDocument is under View signatures on that document's card.
??? There is no Download for a row. | No file is stored for that signature. View document still shows the full record.
??? Can I delete a signature here? | No. To withdraw a signature on a Forms Manager form, use ⋮ > Delete this sign-off on its Completed tab.
??? Do the times match what the parent saw? | Times are shown in the agency's time zone, the same as everywhere else in the portal.
