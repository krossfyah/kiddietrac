# AI chatbot

The floating 💬 button in the bottom-right of every screen opens an AI assistant that answers parent questions using your centre's help articles + policies.

## How to use it

1. Tap the orange 💬 button (bottom-right corner of any page).
2. Type your question in the chat panel.
3. The bot reads your centre's policy documents + handbook + FAQs and answers in plain English.
4. Continues a session — follow-up questions remember context.

## What it can answer

- Pickup hours, vacation policy, fee schedule
- "When is my next invoice due?"
- "What's the centre's anaphylaxis procedure?"
- "Where do I find my child's portfolio?"
- "How do I sign up for parent-teacher conference?"

## What it cannot do

- Make changes (it's read-only)
- Process payments
- See data outside your role (a parent can't query other families)
- Answer outside the centre's policy documents — if it doesn't know, it'll say so + suggest you contact the centre directly

## Privacy

Conversations are stored in `chatbot_messages` for session continuity. Only you can see your own conversation history. Staff cannot read parent chatbot exchanges.

## Requires Anthropic credits

The chatbot uses Anthropic's Claude. Each agency funds its own credits via the platform's Anthropic account. When credits run out, the bot returns an error message until topped up.
