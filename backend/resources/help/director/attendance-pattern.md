# Multi-day attendance pattern

**Attendance days** is where the office records which days of the week each child normally comes, and which part of the day. One grid shows every child down the side and Monday to Sunday across the top, so you can set a whole term's patterns in a few minutes and answer "who is in on Wednesday?" at a glance. The pattern drives room ratios, room planning and tuition projections.

**Where it is:** sidebar → **Administration** → **Attendance days**. The screen's banner reads **Attendance pattern**.

[[open: #attendance-pattern | Open Attendance days]]

## Reading the grid

- The **Child** column shows the child's name, their enrolment status (enrolled, waitlisted, withdrawn), and their centre and room.
- Each day column holds one choice: **Full** (full day), **AM** (morning only), **PM** (afternoon only), **Before** (before school), **After** (after school), **B&A** (before and after school), or **—** for a day they do not come. Each choice has its own colour; a day with nothing set is pale grey with a dashed edge, so gaps stand out.
- Under each day's name a small pill says how many of the children shown are in that day, for example **12 in**.
- Today's column is shaded and its header ends in **(TODAY)**.

## Setting one child's days

1. Type part of the child's name, centre or room in **Search**. The grid narrows as you type.
2. Under each day, open the dropdown and choose **Full**, **AM**, **PM**, **Before**, **After**, **B&A** or **—**. The row turns amber and shows **• unsaved**.
3. Check **Changes apply from**. It starts at today; pick a later date if the new days start next week or next term.
4. Press **Save 1 change** (the button counts your unsaved rows; with nothing changed it reads **Save changes** and is greyed out).
5. A green **✓ Saved 1** appears beside the button and the amber shading goes.

## Setting many children at once

Most children share one pattern, so the bulk buttons fill the grid for everyone currently shown.

1. Use **Search** and **Show** to narrow the grid to the children you want, for example one room.
2. Under **Apply to everyone shown:** press **Mon–Fri, full day**, **Mon–Fri, mornings**, **Mon–Fri, afternoons**, **Mon–Fri, before & after** or **Clear all days**.
3. A box asks *Apply "Mon–Fri, full day" to 14 children?* Press **Fill grid**. Monday to Friday are filled and Saturday and Sunday are cleared. Nothing is saved yet.
4. Change any exceptions by hand, for example a child who only comes Tuesday and Thursday.
5. Press **Save 14 changes**. The message counts up (**Saving 3 of 14…**) and ends with **✓ Saved 14**.

> 💡 Bulk fill, then fix the exceptions, then save once. Leaving the screen before you press Save throws away the unsaved rows.

## Finding children with no pattern

1. Open the **Show** dropdown.
2. Choose **Missing a pattern**. Only children with no day set are listed.
3. Set their days and press **Save changes**.

**Show** also offers **Enrolled only** (the default) and **Everyone**, which adds waitlisted and withdrawn children.

## What "Changes apply from" does

Saving starts a new pattern on that date, and the old one ends where the new one begins. The old pattern is kept, so ratios and reports for past weeks still use the days that applied then. Set the date before you save: a whole batch is saved with the same date.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | See every child in the agency, edit any pattern, use the bulk buttons. |
| Platform admin | The same, inside the agency they have switched into. |
| Centre director | Attendance days is not in the director menu. Ask an agency admin to change a child's days. |
| Parent | Sees their own children's days in the family portal, one tab per child, and can press **✏️ Edit days** to change them there. Their changes show here. |

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? The screen says "Could not load attendance patterns". | The list did not load. Reload the page; if it keeps happening, check your connection or raise a support ticket with the time it happened.
??? The grid says "No children match." | Your search or the **Show** filter hides everyone. Clear **Search** and set **Show** to **Everyone**.
??? The message says "3 saved, 1 failed". | The failed rows stay amber with **• unsaved**. Press **Save changes** again to retry just those.
??? A child comes in for one extra day. Do I change the pattern? | No. The pattern is the usual week. Check the child in that day as normal and leave the pattern as it is.
??? Why are Saturday and Sunday empty after a bulk fill? | The bulk buttons are Monday to Friday. Set weekend care by hand on the children who need it.
??? Does a parent get an email when I change the days? | No. Saving a pattern sends no email or text. Tell the family through Message Centre if the change is yours, not theirs.
