# Daily care logs (diapers, naps, meals, bottles)

**Daily log** is the quick-tap screen for the moments parents ask about: diapers, naps, meals, bottles, sunscreen, mood, medicine and changes of clothes. It also shares photos and videos, records a late pick-up or arrival, and opens an observation. Each entry rolls straight into the family's Today screen.

**Where it is:** sidebar → **Operations** → **Daily log**. The banner reads **Log a moment**.

[[open: #care-log | Open Daily log]]

## Choosing who you are logging for

1. Agency admins: pick the **Provider** first. The **Child** list then shows only that provider's children.
2. Open **Child** and pick one child, or choose **All children** at the top of the list.
3. The **Today's log** card below fills with that child's entries for the day (or everyone's, for **All children**).

Centre directors see the children in the rooms they work in; with no room of their own they see the agency's children they are allowed to see.

## Logging a moment for one child

1. With a child chosen, tap a tile: **🧷 Diaper**, **🚽 Bathroom**, **😴 Nap**, **🍽️ Meal**, **🍎 Snack**, **🍼 Bottle**, **🌳 Outdoor play**, **☀️ Sunscreen**, **🙂 Mood**, **💊 Medicine** or **👕 Change of clothes**.
2. Under **What happened?** tap one chip, for example **Wet**, **Ate most** or **Slept well**. Tap **Other** to type your own words.
3. Fill in the extra field the tile asks for, if any: **oz:** for a bottle; **Fell asleep** and **Woke up** for a nap; **Medicine and dose** for medicine (required); **Which snack?** for a snack; **🧴 Cream applied** for a diaper.
4. Add a **Note (optional)** the parent should read.
5. Leave **When did it happen? (optional)** at the current time, or set an earlier time if you are logging after the fact.
6. Optionally press **📷 Add a photo or video** and describe the moment in the box that appears; the description is required once a file is attached.
7. Press **Log it**. The entry appears at the top of **Today's log** with the time and your name.

> ℹ️ **💊 Medicine** here tells the parent what was given. The formal administration record, with the parent's consent, is still kept on the Medications screen.

## Logging for the whole room at once

1. Choose **All children** in the **Child** list.
2. Tap **🌳 Outdoor play**, **🍎 Snack**, **🍽️ Meal**, **🍼 Bottle**, **☀️ Sunscreen**, **🙂 Mood** or **😴 Nap**.
3. Fill in the details once and press **Log it**. The entry is filed for every child **checked in right now**.

Diaper, bathroom, medicine and change of clothes are never filed for a room: they describe one child's body.

## Sharing a photo or video

1. Tap **📸 Photo** or **🎬 Video**.
2. Press **📷 Choose photos** (up to 20 at a time) or **🎬 Choose a video** (up to 30 MB, about 30 seconds).
3. Tag every child in it. The child you picked is tagged already; with **All children**, nobody is tagged until you choose.
4. Type the **Description (required)**.
5. Press **Share it**. Each tagged family sees it in Photos & video, and it appears in the daily overview and that night's summary email.

## Recording a late pick-up or late arrival

1. Pick the child and tap **⏰ Late**.
2. Choose **Late pick-up** or **Late arrival**.
3. Under **How late**, tap a time such as **15m**, or type the minutes.
4. Add what happened if useful and press **Log it**.

A director reviews it on the late pick-up screen. Logging it never charges the family anything.

## Other tiles

- **🌱 Observation** opens the Observations form with the child already chosen.
- **🧺 Supplies** asks the family (or every family, with **All children**) to send in supplies.

## Looking at another day

Use **‹** and **›** beside the date, pick a date, or press **Today**. The heading changes to **Yesterday's log** or the date. You cannot move past today.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Pick any provider and child in the agency, and log everything on this page. |
| Centre director | Log for the children of their own centres. |
| Educator | Log for the children in their rooms (see the educator guide). |
| Parent | Reads the entries in the family portal. Parents do not see the Observation or Supplies tiles. |

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? I see "Pick a child to log for." | Choose a child, or **All children**, in the **Child** list before tapping a tile.
??? I see 'Pick a child to log for — "All children" does not apply to diaper.' | Diaper, bathroom, medicine, change of clothes and late are logged one child at a time. Pick the child.
??? I see "No children are checked in right now, so there is nobody to log this for." | A room entry only goes to children who are checked in. Check them in first, or log for one child.
??? I see "That time is later today. A log records what has already happened". | The **When did it happen?** time is in the future. Pick a time that has passed.
??? I see "Say which medicine and how much." | **Medicine and dose** is required for a medicine entry. Type the name and amount.
??? The file is refused as too big. | Photos and videos are limited to 30 MB. Record a shorter clip, or send it from your phone at a lower quality.
??? Can I edit or delete an entry? | Not from this screen. Log a corrected entry with a note saying what changed.
??? Does the parent get a notification? | Entries show on their Today screen straight away. Photos and videos also notify their phone and appear in that evening's summary email.
