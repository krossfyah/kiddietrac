# Sleep checks, policy and the inspector report

The **Sleep room** shows every sleeping child under 24 months in a room, counts down to each child's next direct visual check, and records each check with the child's position, breathing and colour. Directors and admins also get the **📋 Report** tab: every nap and every check for a day or a range, ready for a licensing inspector. Ontario's O. Reg. 137/15 s.33.1 requires these checks at the frequency set by **your centre's sleep policy**; the regulation does not name a number of minutes.

**Where it is:** sidebar → **Operations** → **Sleep room**. The entry only shows when sleep checks are on for your agency.

[[open: #sleep-room | Open the Sleep room]]

## Setting your policy

1. Go to **Settings → Room Controls → Sleep checks**.
2. Tick **Sleep checks** to show the Sleep room and send overdue alerts.
3. Set **Under 12 months: check every** and **12 to 24 months: check every** to match your written sleep policy. Both default to 15 minutes.
4. Choose whether to **Tell the director when a check is overdue**, and set the **Grace period before the director is told**. If the centre has no director, the agency admins are told.
5. Under **Centres with their own policy**, give a centre its own intervals if it has a different policy. Leave a centre's boxes blank to use the agency setting.
6. Press **Save**. The change applies straight away.

[[open: #educator-rooms?tab=sleep | Open sleep check settings]]

Agency admins can change the policy; directors can see it. Sleep checks are on by default for Ontario agencies and off elsewhere.

## Starting a nap

1. Open the **Sleep room**. If you work in more than one room, pick the room from the dropdown at the top.
2. Find the child. Each card shows their age and, for under-12-month-olds, a **Back to sleep** tag.
3. Press **😴 Asleep now**. A notice says the child is asleep and *Sleep checks have started.*

The card now shows **Asleep since** with the time, the check interval and **Next check in** with a countdown. It turns amber in the last three minutes and red when the check is overdue (**Overdue by**). A banner at the top says how many checks are overdue.

## Recording a check

1. Look at the child directly: breathing, colour, position. A monitor does not replace a check.
2. Press **✓ Checked** on the child's card.
3. Choose the position: back, side or tummy.
4. Leave **Breathing normal** and **Colour normal** ticked, or untick what was not normal.
5. Add a **Note (optional)** if something needs recording.
6. Press **Save check**. The countdown starts again.

When more than one child is asleep, **✓ Check all** records the whole room in one box: set each child's position and press **Save checks**.

> ⚠️ An under-12-month-old recorded on their side or tummy without a physician's direction gets a warning: turn the child onto their back, and add a note saying so.

## Ending a nap

Press **🌅 Woke up** on the card. *Sleep checks have stopped.* A child who is signed out while asleep ends the nap too.

## Physician's direction

For an infant whose physician has directed a different sleep position in writing:

1. On the child's card, press **🩺 Direction** (directors and admins only, children under 12 months).
2. Tick **A physician has directed a different sleep position in writing**.
3. Under **What the direction says**, describe it, for example the position, the doctor and the date of the letter.
4. Press **Save**. The card shows a **Physician direction** tag. Keep the signed letter in the child's Documents.

## The inspector report

1. Open the **📋 Report** tab.
2. Choose the centre, a room or **All rooms**, and the dates (up to a month).
3. Read the summary: naps, checks, and how many naps had a gap longer than your policy.
4. The table lists each nap: **Date**, **Child**, **Room**, **Asleep**, **Woke**, **Checks**, **Longest gap** and **Status** (**On time**, a count of gaps, or **Wake not logged**).
5. Press **View checks** on a row for the timeline: time, educator, position, breathing, colour and notes.
6. Press **⬇ Check log (CSV)** for a spreadsheet, or **⬇ Inspector report (PDF)** for a printable record.

Gaps are measured against your *current* policy, from when the child fell asleep to when they woke.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Educator | Start naps, record checks, end naps, in their own rooms. |
| Centre director | All of that, plus **🩺 Direction** and the **📋 Report** tab. |
| Agency admin | The same as a director, plus changing the sleep policy. |
| Parent | Sees naps in the daily summary, not the checks screen. |

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? Sleep room is not in my menu. | Sleep checks are off for your agency. An agency admin can turn them on in **Settings → Room Controls → Sleep checks**.
??? The screen says "You are not assigned to a room yet." | Ask a director to assign you to a room.
??? The screen says "Sleep checks are switched off for this centre." | This centre's policy has checks off. An agency admin can turn them on in Room Controls.
??? A child is missing from the room. | The Sleep room only lists children under 24 months. A note at the top tells you if a child has no date of birth on file, so their age cannot be worked out. Add it on the child's record.
??? The report says "No naps logged for children under 24 months in this period." | No naps were recorded for children under 24 months at that centre on those dates. Try another range or **All rooms**.
??? Who is alerted when a check is overdue? | The room's educators get a bell notice and a phone alert, then the director after the grace period you set.

## Telling parents

Your sleep policy must be posted, and parents told about significant changes in their child's sleep patterns. Naps already appear in the parent's daily summary. Use Message Centre for anything you need to discuss.
