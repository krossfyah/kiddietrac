# Sleep checks, policy and the inspector report

Ontario's O. Reg. 137/15 s.33.1 requires documented direct visual checks of every sleeping child under 24 months, at the frequency set by **your centre's sleep policy**. The regulation does not name a number of minutes. Infants under 12 months sleep on their back unless a physician has directed otherwise in writing.

## Setting your policy

Go to **Settings → Room Controls → Sleep checks**. Agency admins can change it; directors can see it. Set:

- the interval for **under 12 months** and for **12 to 24 months**. Both default to 15 minutes; set them to match your written sleep policy.
- whether the **director** is told when a check is overdue, and after how many minutes. If the centre has no director, the agency admins are told.
- a different policy for individual centres, if they have one. Leave a centre's boxes blank to use the agency setting.

Press **Save**. The change applies straight away.

[[open: #educator-rooms?tab=sleep | Open sleep check settings]]

Sleep checks are on by default for Ontario agencies and off elsewhere. When they are off for a centre, the Sleep room says so and links to this setting.

> **Tip:** This setting used to live under Settings → Notifications. It moved to Room Controls with the other room settings.

## Physician's direction

For an infant whose physician has directed a different sleep position in writing, open **Sleep room** and tap **🩺 Direction** on the child. Tick the box and describe the direction. Keep the signed letter in the child's Documents.

## The report

**Sleep room → Report** lists every nap of a child under 24 months for a day or a range of up to a month. For each nap it shows when the child fell asleep and woke, how many checks were recorded, the longest stretch between checks, and any stretch longer than your policy. Tap **View checks** for the full timeline: time, educator, position, breathing, colour and notes.

Download it as **CSV** or **PDF** for a licensing inspection. Gaps are measured against your *current* policy.

## Telling parents

Your sleep policy must be posted, and parents told about significant changes in their child's sleep patterns. Naps already appear in the parent's daily summary. Use Message Centre for anything you need to discuss.
