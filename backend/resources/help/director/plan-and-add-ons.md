# Your plan and add-ons

Your agency's plan (Starter, Growth or Agency) and any add-ons decide which features
your team and families can use. A change takes effect the next time the app opens — no
one needs to sign out.

[[open: #licence | See my plan]]

## Features that are not in your plan

- **Agency admins** see them in the menu and on tiles, greyed out with an **Upgrade**
  badge. Tap one to see what it does and which plan or add-on brings it.
- Tap **Ask about adding it** to send us a request. We'll get back to you about adding it.
- **Staff and families never see them.** They only appear once they're part of your plan.

## Add-ons

- **SMS & voice** — text and voice announcements, **Safe Arrival** check-ins and calls,
  and the **parent phone line** (calling in absent or late).
- **AI Bundle** — summaries, writing help, report-card drafts and Play Spark ideas.
- **White-label** — your own brand everywhere.
- **SMS Incremental** — each block adds one month's text allowance **and** call allowance, so extra
  texts and calls aren't billed as overage. Nothing is ever blocked.
- **Reseller bundle** — includes 5 centres. Each **Reseller extra centre** adds one more. Your open
  centres always stay open, and closed centres don't count. When you reach the limit, opening another
  centre shows an **Upgrade** popup instead.

## When a plan gets smaller

Features that are no longer included stop straight away. The exception is **invoicing
and autopay**: they keep running until the end of the period you've already paid for, so
invoices and payments that are already scheduled still go out. After that, they stop.
