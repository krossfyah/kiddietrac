# Churn risk

**Marketing › Churn risk** gives each family a score for how likely they are to leave soon, so you can reach out
before they do. The score is worked out each time you open the page from three signals: how much the family looks at
their child's observations, how regularly the child is signed in, and whether their account is behind on payments.

[[open: #ai-churn | Open Churn risk]]

## Finding the families to call this week

1. Open **Marketing › Churn risk**. The banner reads **📉 Churn risk**.
2. Read the tiles: **High risk** (with "Outreach this week"), **Medium**, **Low** and **Total families**.
3. In **All families by risk**, the families with the highest **Score** are at the top. The **Bucket** pill is high, medium or low.
4. Read the **Signals** column to see why — for example fewer sign-ins or an overdue balance.
5. Contact each high-risk family this week: a friendly call or message from their educator or director, or a meeting.

> 💡 Match the conversation to the signal. Low attendance may mean a schedule problem; an overdue balance may need a payment plan; no observation views may mean the parent is not using the app — offer help to sign in.

## Following up

1. Note what you did on the family's record (a quick note or message).
2. Open the page again in a week or two: if attendance and payments improve, the family moves down.

## Who can do what

| Role | Sees |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Families across the active agency. |
| Centre director | Families across the agency (the list is not narrowed to one centre). |
| Platform admin | The agency chosen in the agency switcher. |

The list shows the 80 highest-risk families. Families never see their score.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? Every family shows low. | Good news, or there is not much data yet: a new agency with few sign-ins and invoices scores everyone low.
??? A family with no problems shows high. | Check the signals. A child on holiday has fewer sign-ins; a balance that is paid but not recorded shows as overdue.
??? Does this send anything to families? | No. It is a list for your team only.
??? The page is empty. | There are no active families in the agency yet.
