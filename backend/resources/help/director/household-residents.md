# Household residents (home daycares)

Some jurisdictions (Ohio today) require every adult who **lives in a home where child care is provided**
to have the same background checks as staff. **Household residents** keeps that list for each home.

## Who can see it

Directors and admins see the homes they look after. A provider sees their own home.
The menu entry only appears where your jurisdiction has this rule.

## Adding an adult

1. Open **Household residents**.
2. Under the home, enter the person's **name**, **relationship** (for example spouse or adult child),
   and optionally their **date of birth** and the date they **moved in**. Press **Add adult**.
3. Record each **background check**: choose the type, add the reference and the date it was done.
   Leave **Renew by** blank and KiddieTrac fills it in from your rules (in Ohio, 5 years later).

Each check shows **Current** or **Expired**. When someone moves out, press **Remove** — they stay
in the record as removed. Every change is kept in the audit log.
