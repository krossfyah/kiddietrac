# Household residents (home daycares)

Some jurisdictions (Ohio today) require every adult who **lives in a home where child care is provided** to have the same background checks as staff. **Household residents** keeps that list for each home: who lives there, since when, and which checks are on file with their renewal dates.

**Where it is:** sidebar → **Administration** → **Household residents**. The banner reads **Household residents**, and a note under it repeats the rule: *Every adult who lives in the home needs the same background checks as staff.*

[[open: #household | Open Household residents]]

## When this screen appears

The menu entry only shows where your agency's province or state has the household rule. Each home is a card with its name at the top. Agency admins see every home in the agency, directors see the homes they look after, and a provider sees their own home.

## Adding an adult who lives in the home

1. Find the home's card.
2. At the bottom of the card, type the person's **Full name**.
3. Type their **Relationship (e.g. spouse)**, for example spouse, adult child or grandparent.
4. Optionally enter their date of birth and the date they moved in, in the two date boxes.
5. Press **Add adult**. They appear on the card, with *No background checks on file.* in amber until you add one.

## Recording a background check

1. Under the adult's name, choose the type of check in the dropdown. The list is your jurisdiction's own checks.
2. Type the check's **Reference**, for example the certificate number.
3. In the first date box, enter the date the check was done.
4. Leave the second date box (renew by) empty to let KiddieTrac fill it from your rules (in Ohio, five years later), or type a date to override it.
5. Press **Add check**. The check shows its type, a status pill, its dates and its reference.

The pill reads **Current** while the renew-by date is ahead, and **Expired** once it has passed. Add a new check when the person renews; the old one stays as history.

## When someone moves out

1. Press **Remove** beside the adult's name.
2. Confirm **Remove this adult?** (*They are kept in the record as removed.*).
3. They disappear from the card but stay in the home's record and the audit log.

## Removing a check entered by mistake

1. Press the **✕** at the end of the check's line.
2. Confirm **Remove this check?**. Only that check is removed.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | See and edit every home in the agency. |
| Centre director | See and edit the homes they look after. |
| Platform admin | The same as an agency admin, inside the agency they have switched into. |
| Provider (educator in their own home) | See and edit their own home. |
| Parent | Does not see this screen. |

Every add and remove is kept in the audit log with who did it and when.

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? I cannot find Household residents in the menu. | It only appears where your jurisdiction requires checks on household adults. If you think it should be there, ask an agency admin to check the agency's province or state.
??? The screen shows no homes. | You do not look after any home. Ask an agency admin to give you access to the homes you oversee.
??? I see a red "Could not save" message. | Something on the form was missing or refused, for example an empty **Full name**. Check the fields and press the button again.
??? Do I need to add the provider themselves? | No. The provider's own checks are kept on their staff record. List the other adults who live in the home.
??? How do I know when a check is about to expire? | The renew-by date is shown on each check line. Expiry alerts for staff checks are covered in [Background-check expiry](background-checks).
??? Does a 17-year-old need to be listed? | Follow your jurisdiction's rule for the age at which a resident needs checks. Add them when they reach that age.
