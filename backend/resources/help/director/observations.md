# Learning observations

The Observations screen collects the learning stories your team writes about each child: what the child did, a short summary for the family, and the learning goals it links to in your agency's learning framework. Use it to read and sort your team's observations, and to write a new one yourself, with or without AI help.

[[open: #observations | Open Observations]]

## Reading your team's observations

1. Open **Operations > Observations**. The page is headed **Learning Observations**.
2. Each row shows the **Child**, **When** it was observed, the **Educator**, the **Provider** (centre or room) and the **Domain**, such as **Social & emotional** or **Language & literacy**.
3. The note is open under each row: the family summary, the learning-framework links and any milestones with the evidence the educator saw. A green **SHARED** tag means the family can see it.
4. To change the order, choose from **Sort by**: **Newest first**, **Oldest first**, **Child (A-Z)**, **Educator (A-Z)**, **Provider / centre (A-Z)** or **Domain (A-Z)**. You can also press a column heading to sort by it, and press it again to reverse the order.

## Writing an observation with AI help

1. Press **+ New observation**. The **New observation** page opens at **Step 1: Capture the moment**.
2. Choose the **Child**. Children who are signed in now are listed first and marked "at Centre".
3. In **What did you observe?**, write what you saw, factually and in at least 10 characters. For example: "Maya stacked five blocks into a tower, knocked them down and laughed, then rebuilt them in a circle and asked Priya to help."
4. To talk instead of typing, press **Speak it**, speak, then press **Stop**. Check the words that appear in the box.
5. Press **Structure with AI**. It usually takes 5 to 10 seconds.
6. At **Step 2: Review and save**, check the **Domain**, edit the **Family summary (parent will see this)**, and look over the framework links that were found.
7. Leave **Share with family right away** ticked for the family to see it now, or untick it to keep it for staff.
8. Press **Save observation**. An **Observation saved** toast appears and you go back to the list.

> ⚠️ Nothing is saved until you press **Save observation**. The yellow **Not saved yet.** note at Step 2 reminds you. To throw the draft away and begin again, press **Start over**.

## Writing an observation without AI

1. Press **+ New observation** and fill in the **Child** and **What did you observe?** as above.
2. Press **Write it myself**. Step 2 opens with your words as the family summary and no framework links.
3. Choose the **Domain**, adjust the summary and press **Save observation**.

> 💡 If you leave the page before saving, the next time you open **New observation** you see "You have an unfinished observation." Press **Restore it** to carry on, or **Discard** to start fresh.

## Who can do what

- Agency admins and centre directors read every observation in the centres they can see, and write new ones.
- Educators write observations for the children in their rooms, from the same screen.
- Parents see only the observations shared with the family, in their portal.
- Platform admins open this screen through [View as](view-as) an agency admin.
- The screen needs the Observations feature in your agency's plan. The **Structure with AI** button also needs AI to be switched on for your agency; **Write it myself** always works.

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? The list says "No observations yet". | Nobody has saved an observation in your centres yet. Press **+ New observation** to write the first one.
??? I see "Please select a child." or "Need at least 10 characters." | Choose a child and write a little more in **What did you observe?**, then try again.
??? Structure with AI shows "AI failed:" and a message. | The AI could not structure the note this time. Your text is still in the box: press **Structure with AI** again, or press **Write it myself** to save it without AI.
??? The child list says "No children found in your rooms. Ask your director to assign you to a room." | You are not linked to a room with enrolled children. Ask your agency admin to add you to a room.
??? At Step 2 I see "Summary cannot be empty." | The family summary is blank. Write a sentence or two and press **Save observation** again.
??? Does the family get a message when I share an observation? | A shared observation appears in the family's portal for their child. Untick **Share with family right away** if you want to keep it for staff only.
