# Archived records

When a family de-enrols, a child is withdrawn, a staff account is closed or a provider
is archived, **the record is kept, not deleted.** Licensed child care records have to
outlive the enrolment that created them.

This is where you read them.

## Where to find them

Each section has an **Active | Archived** switch at the top:

- **Administration → Families** → *Archived* — families who have de-enrolled
- **Administration → Children** → *Archived* — children withdrawn or removed
- **Centres / Rooms** → *Archived* — providers you have closed
- **User management** → the *Show deactivated* toggle — closed staff accounts

The Archived button shows a count, so you can see at a glance whether there is anything
there.

![The Active / Archived switch at the top of the Families screen](https://api.kiddietrac.com/help-img/archived-switch.png)

## What each row tells you

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Left** | The date and time they left, in your agency's timezone |
| **State** | *Withdrawn* (still a readable record) or *Removed* — these are different things and are not merged |
| **Kept until** | When your retention period expires for that record |

Some staff rows show an **EST.** chip next to the date. That means no exact closure
date was recorded for that account, so the date shown is the last time the account
changed. It is marked rather than presented as fact — a retention date quoted to a
former employee should not quietly be a guess.

## Retention periods

The dates come from **your own** compliance settings, not a number we chose: child and
family records for your configured child-record period, staff and documents for your
document period. Change them in **Settings → Data retention**.

The banner at the top of the Archived view also tells you whether automatic clean-up is
on. If it is off — the default — nothing is ever removed, however old.

## Opening an archived record

Click any row. The record opens exactly as it did before they left, with an amber
banner saying it has left and is retained history. Everything is readable: guardians,
health information, enrolment history, documents.

## Seeing everything about a child or family

Open a child record and choose the **🗄️ Full history** tab. This is every record held
about that child, gathered from across the whole system:

- Attendance, daily events and care logs
- Observations, incidents, medications and doses given
- Immunisations, wellness screenings, absences
- Photos and video they appear in
- Chats, signed documents and files
- Report cards, awards, birthdays, field trips
- Lesson and meal plans for the rooms they were in
- Invoices and billed items

![The Full history tab: every category held for this child, with counts](https://api.kiddietrac.com/help-img/full-history.png)

Sections start collapsed with a count beside each, so you can go straight to the one
you want. Open a section to page through it — busy children have hundreds of daily
events, so it is shown 25 at a time with **Prev / Next** rather than in one wall.

This works for archived children too. That is rather the point: a retention promise you
cannot actually read is not worth much.

## What you cannot do

- You cannot un-archive a family from this screen. If a family returns, enrol them
  fresh — their history stays attached to the old record.
- You cannot delete an archived record to "tidy up". Retention is a legal obligation,
  not a preference.
