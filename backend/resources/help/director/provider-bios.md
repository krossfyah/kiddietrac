# Provider bios and bio reminders

A provider's **bio** is the first thing a new family reads about them: it goes in the family's
**welcome email** when a child joins (or moves to) that provider, and it shows on the
**provider card** in the parents' app. A provider without one leaves families with a
placeholder line: *"Your provider will share a little about themselves here soon."*

## Where providers write it

**Settings → Profile → Your bio** (at least 40 characters). Every reminder links straight there
and outlines the box. You can also write or edit a provider's bio yourself:
**Providers → the provider → Edit**.

## Reminders

**Settings → Notifications → Provider bios.** The card lists every provider who has no bio yet,
whether they have a photo, and how many reminders they have had.

| Setting | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Send reminders** | On by default. |
| **How often / time of day** | Every day, weekdays, once a week (default Monday 9:00) or every few days, in your agency's timezone. |
| **A bio counts once it has at least** | 40 characters by default. |
| **Stop after** | How many reminders one provider gets (default 6). 0 keeps reminding. |
| **Send by email** | Explains why it matters, where to write it, what to say, and asks for a photo if they have none. |
| **Show in the app** | A bell notification that opens the bio box. |
| **Push to phones** | Off by default. |
| **Show a reminder card** | A small *"Families would love to meet you"* card on every screen until the bio is written. They can hide it for the day. |
| **Send admins a summary** | One email to agency admins listing who still has no bio, when reminders go out. |

**Preview** shows the reminder a provider would get; **Send test…** sends one copy, marked [Test],
to an address you type. Reminders stop by themselves as soon as the bio is saved.
