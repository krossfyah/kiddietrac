# Developmental milestones

A structured checklist of HDLH-aligned milestones across four age bands (infant, toddler, preschool, kindergarten) and four domains (Belonging, Wellbeing, Engagement, Expression). Open from any child's record.

## Marking a milestone

Each milestone has three pill buttons:

- **Emerging** (grey) — the child is just beginning to show signs of this skill
- **In progress** (amber) — the skill is appearing but inconsistently
- **Achieved** (green) — the skill is consistently demonstrated

Tap any pill to update. The portfolio's stat tiles update immediately.

## Catalog vs custom

The built-in catalog covers ~28 milestones. Adding custom milestones, importing the full HDLH continuum, or aligning to ELECT/ELOF is on the v22p51 roadmap.

## Parent visibility

Guardians see the same checklist for their own children and can track progress over time. Milestone status changes appear in the **Today** feed when staff update them.
