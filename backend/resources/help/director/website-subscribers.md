# Website subscribers

**Website → Subscribers.** Everybody who signed up through the public site, and
everybody who has left.

## The two tabs

- **Subscribers** — who is on the list, with the date they joined and where from.
- **Unsubscribed** — who has left and exactly when, kept rather than deleted. A record
  that somebody asked not to be contacted is the point; deleting it means the next
  import quietly adds them back.

Each row has the usual **⋮** menu, and the list searches, sorts and exports like every
other table in the portal.

## What a subscriber receives

- **On subscribing** — a confirmation, with an unsubscribe link in it.
- **On unsubscribing** — a short confirmation that it is done, so nobody is left
  wondering whether the link worked.

## Worth knowing

- Unsubscribing is one click from the email and needs no login.
- Removing somebody by hand from the Subscribers tab moves them to Unsubscribed with a
  timestamp; it does not erase them.
