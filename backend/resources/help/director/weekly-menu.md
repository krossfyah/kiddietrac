# Weekly menu

The weekly menu is where you plan what each centre serves: breakfast, morning snack, lunch, afternoon snack and dinner for every day the centre is open, with allergens, notes for families, food photos and serving times. When you publish a week, families see it in their app. The screen also reminds you of every child's allergies and dietary needs while you plan.

[[open: #menu | Open Weekly menu]]

**Where it is:** sidebar → **Programs** → **Weekly menu**.

## Opening a centre's week

1. Open **Programs** → **Weekly menu**.
2. If you work in more than one centre, choose one in the **Centre** drop-down (**Select a centre…**). With one centre, it opens straight away.
3. Read the **⚠️ Allergy & dietary reminders** box. It lists each child with an allergy or dietary need; anaphylactic allergies are marked 🚨 in red. A second box, **Allergy information not recorded**, names children with nothing on file.
4. Use **◀** and **▶** to move a week back or forward. Press **This week** to jump back to the current week. Today's column is tinted.

The grid shows one row per meal and one column per open day. Each meal has a **Serves at** time.

## Planning the week

1. Press **✏️ Edit**. The grid unlocks and **💾 Save menu** appears.
2. Press a slot showing **+ Add**. A dialog opens for that meal and day.
3. Type the **Dish**, for example "Chicken and rice".
4. Enter **Allergens**, for example "dairy, wheat".
5. Optionally add **Notes for families** about what is served or a swap for a child with allergies.
6. Under **Photo**, press **🖼 Choose photo** to pick a food photo, **⬆ Upload my own** to use yours, or **Remove** to clear it.
7. Press **Done**. To clear a slot, empty the **Dish** and press **Done**.
8. Set the **Serves at** time for each meal, and add **Notes (optional)** for the whole week under the grid.
9. Choose the **Status**: **Draft** (staff only), **Published** (families can see it) or **Archived**.
10. Press **💾 Save menu**. You see **Published — families notified.** or **Saved.** and the grid locks again.

> ⚠️ A week nobody has saved yet starts as **Published**, so saving it sends it to families. Choose **Draft** first if it is not ready.

> 💡 Pressing **✓ Done** with unsaved changes saves them, the same as **💾 Save menu**.

## Saving time with copies, templates and photos

1. **⧉ Copy last week** fills this week from the week before, photos included. If this week already has dishes, you are asked to confirm with **Replace with last week**. Review the grid, then press **💾 Save menu**.
2. **💾 Save as template** keeps a saved week for your whole agency to reuse. Press **💾 Save menu** first, enter a **Template name** (for example "Fall week A") and press **Save template**.
3. **📋 Apply a template…** opens a list of templates. Pick one, set **Week of (Monday)**, tick the centres, and press **Apply**. The week is saved as a **draft** at each centre; published weeks are never changed. Tick **Replace draft menus that already have dishes** to overwrite drafts.
4. **📷 Fill photos** (in edit mode) finds food photos for dishes that have none.
5. **Add food photos automatically to new dishes**, under the grid, adds a photo to every new dish for you.

## Checking the menu against Canada's Food Guide

For Canadian agencies, **🍎 Canada's Food Guide check** under the grid shows each day as **✓ balanced** or with suggestions (vegetables or fruit at lunch, a whole-grain food, a protein food, water as the drink, sugary drinks or high-sugar foods). Press a day to read its tips. It is friendly guidance, not a compliance check.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Plan, save, publish and template menus for every centre. |
| Centre director | The same, for the centres they work in. |
| Educator | Views and edits their own centre's weekly menu. |
| Parent | Sees only published weeks for their child's centre. |

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? I see "No centre is assigned to your account yet". | Your account is not linked to any centre. Ask your agency admin to add you to a centre.
??? The slots do nothing when I press them. | The week is locked for reading. Press **✏️ Edit** first.
??? I see "Save failed." | The week was not saved and the grid stays editable with your work in it. Check your connection and press **💾 Save menu** again.
??? Save as template says "Save the menu first". | Press **💾 Save menu**, then **💾 Save as template**.
??? Copy last week says "Nothing to copy". | The week before has no saved dishes. Go back a week with **◀** to check.
??? Apply says "No templates yet". | Save a week as a template first.
??? Are families notified when I publish? | Yes. Saving a week as **Published** tells families; a **Draft** is seen by staff only.
