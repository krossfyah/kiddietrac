# Re-enrolment campaigns

**Re-enrollment campaigns** asks every enrolled family whether their child is coming back next term. You launch a campaign with a name, the term and a deadline; KiddieTrac invites every currently enrolled child in the agency and notifies their parents, then counts who answered and who renewed. Agency admins use it a couple of months before a new term or school year.

[[open: #reenrollment | Open Re-enrollment]]

## Launching a campaign

1. Open **Engagement › Re-enrollment**.
2. Press **+ Launch campaign** in the banner.
3. In **Launch a re-enrolment campaign**, check the **Campaign name** (it starts as "Fall 2026 re-enrolment") and change it if you like.
4. Set the **Target term**, for example "Fall 2026" or "September 2027".
5. Pick the **Deadline** — the date answers are due. It must be after today.
6. Press **Launch campaign**.

A message says how many children were invited (for example "Invited 84 children"), and the campaign appears at the top of the list with the status **sent**.

> ⚠️ A campaign goes to **every enrolled child in the agency** the moment you launch it, and their parents are notified straight away. There is no draft, no centre filter and no undo — check the name, term and deadline first.

## What families receive

Every guardian of an invited child gets a notification — in the portal bell and as a push on their phone — titled **Re-enrol for** the term, saying **Confirm by** the deadline. For each child, a family's answer is **yes**, **no** or **unsure**, with an optional note.

Children who are withdrawn, waitlisted or archived when you launch are not invited. A child who enrols after the launch is not added — they will be in the next campaign.

## Following the results

1. Open **Engagement › Re-enrollment**.
2. Read the campaign's row: **Campaign**, **Term**, **Deadline**, **Status**, **Invited** (children asked), **Responded** (children with any answer) and **Renewed** (children whose family said yes, in green).
3. The gap between **Invited** and **Responded** is the families still to hear from. Follow up with them by message or phone before the deadline.
4. After the deadline, plan next term's rooms from the **Renewed** count, and offer the places that were not renewed to your **Waitlist**.

## When to launch

- 60 to 90 days before the term starts, so families have time to decide and you have time to fill places.
- After any tuition increase is scheduled, so parents see the new rate before they commit — see [[tuition-increases]].
- After subsidy paperwork is renewed, so families know what they will pay.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Launch campaigns for the agency and follow every campaign |
| Platform admin | The same, in the agency being viewed |
| Centre director | Does not have this screen; ask an agency admin to launch a campaign |
| Parent | Receives the notification and answers for their own children |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? Launch campaign does nothing when I press it. | All three fields are required. Fill in **Campaign name**, **Target term** and **Deadline**, and make sure the deadline is after today.
??? It said "Invited 0 children". | No child in the agency had the status Enrolled at that moment. Check **Administration › Children** with the status filter on **Enrolled**.
??? Can I send it to one centre only? | Not from this screen — a campaign always goes to every enrolled child in the agency.
??? Responded stays at 0. | Families may not have answered yet. Remind them before the deadline by message or announcement, and note answers you get by phone in the child's record.
??? Can I close or delete a campaign? | No. A campaign stays in the list with its counts as a record; launch a new one for the next term.
??? How are families told? | Each guardian of an invited child gets a notification in the portal bell and on their phone.
