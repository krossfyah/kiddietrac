# Re-enrolment campaigns

**Re-enrollment campaigns** asks every enrolled family whether their child is coming back next term. You launch a campaign with a name, the term and a deadline; every guardian gets a notification and an email with an **Answer now** button, families answer **Yes, coming back** or **No** for each child (with a note if they like), and the screen counts who answered and who renewed. Agency admins and directors use it a couple of months before a new term or school year.

[[open: #reenrollment | Open Re-enrollment]]

## Launching a campaign

1. Open **Engagement › Re-enrollment**.
2. Press **+ Launch campaign** in the banner.
3. Choose the **Centre** (agency admins can also pick **All centres**; directors pick one of their own centres).
4. Check the **Campaign name** and set the **Target term**, for example "Fall 2027".
5. Pick the **Deadline** — the last day families can answer. It must be after today.
6. Press **Launch campaign**.

A message says how many children were invited and how many families were told, and the campaign appears at the top of the list as **Open**.

> ⚠️ Families are told the moment you launch. There is no draft and no undo — check the centre, name, term and deadline first.

## What families receive

- A notification in the app: **Re-enrol for** the term, "Are Ava and Liam coming back? Please answer by …".
- An email in your agency's own style, greeting them by first name, listing their children and an **Answer now** button. The button opens a page where they answer for each child — no sign-in needed. The link only works for their own family and only until the deadline.
- In the app, the notification opens their **Re-enrolment** screen with the same choices.

Families can change their answer until the deadline. Guardians who have not accepted their invite yet are not emailed; they see the notification when they sign in.

## Automatic reminder

Families who have not answered get **one** reminder (notification and email), the agency's reminder days before the deadline — 7 by default — from 9 am. The reminder lists only the children still waiting for an answer. Agency admins change the days with **⚙ Reminder rule** (0 turns reminders off). The **Deadline** column shows when the reminder goes, or went.

## Following the results

1. Read the campaign's row: **Invited** (children asked), **Responded** (children with an answer), **Renewed** (coming back, in green) and **Not returning**.
2. Open the row's **⋮** and choose **View answers** to see every family's answer per child, their note, when and how they answered (in the app or by email link), and who was reminded. Use the **All / No answer yet / Coming back / Not returning** buttons to filter.
3. Follow up with families still to answer by message or phone before the deadline.
4. After the deadline, plan next term's rooms from **Renewed**, and offer places that were not renewed to your **Waitlist**.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Launch campaigns for one centre or the whole agency, see every answer, set the reminder rule |
| Centre director | Launch campaigns for their own centres; sees only their own centres' families and counts |
| Platform admin | The same as an agency admin, in the agency being viewed |
| Parent | Answers for their own children in the app or from the email, until the deadline |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? Launch campaign does nothing when I press it. | All fields are required. Fill in **Campaign name**, **Target term** and **Deadline**, and make sure the deadline is after today.
??? "Choose one of your centres for this campaign." | Directors launch for one of their own centres. Pick it under **Centre**.
??? It said "Invited 0 children". | No child at that centre had the status Enrolled. Check **Administration › Children** with the status filter on **Enrolled**.
??? A family says the email link does not work. | The link stops working after the deadline, or if the family has left. They can still answer in the app until the deadline; after it, note their answer yourself.
??? A family did not get the email. | Check they have an email address and have accepted their invite. They can always answer in the app from the notification.
??? Can I close or delete a campaign? | No. It closes for answers by itself after the deadline and stays in the list as a record.
??? Older campaigns show 0 answers. | Counts come from the families' answers. Campaigns launched before families could answer have none recorded.
