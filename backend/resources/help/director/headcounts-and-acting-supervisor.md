# Headcounts and the acting supervisor

Ontario's amended O. Reg. 137/15 takes effect on 1 January 2027. Under s.11, your supervision policy must say when, how and how often children are counted, and what staff do when a child is unaccounted for. Under s.6.1(2), it must name who acts for the supervisor when the supervisor is off-site and something urgent comes up. KiddieTrac records both.

## The Headcounts screen

Open **Headcounts** from the menu. It has two tabs.

[[open: #headcounts | Open Headcounts]]

- **👀 Rooms now** shows each room: how many children are signed in, when the last count was done, and a **👀 Headcount** button. A red chip means someone was not seen at the last count. The acting supervisor for each centre is shown here too.
- **📜 Logs** lists every headcount: when and where it was done, the moment, the result, who was not seen, who counted, and the follow-up (alert sent, child found). You can filter by date, centre, room, moment and result.
- For an open count (a child not seen and not yet marked found), you can **Alert director** or **Mark found** from the logs.

The screen remembers which tab you last used. A link to a single headcount, such as the one in a missing-child alert, opens the **Logs** tab.

A missing-child alert reaches the centre's directors, the agency admins and the acting supervisors by bell and push notification. No text message is sent. Each alert links to the headcount record.

## The daily headcount report

For a document you can file or hand to an inspector, use **Reports → Headcounts**.

1. Open **Reports** and choose **👀 Headcounts**.
2. Pick a single day for the daily log, or any date range.
3. Press **Run report**, then export it to PDF, Excel or CSV, or print it.

There is one row per count: date and time, centre, room or outing, the moment, expected and counted, the result, who was not seen, who counted, the follow-up and any note. An open count shows **OPEN** in the follow-up column.

Agency admins can also **schedule** it from the Reports screen, so it is emailed to you every day. Educators can run the report too, for their own rooms and the counts they took.

## Naming the acting supervisor

On **Headcounts → Rooms now**, click **Acting supervisor…** next to a centre. Agency admins can also do this from **Centres** with the **Acting supervisor** button.

- Add one or more people who work at the centre, in the order they should be contacted.
- The names show on the dashboard's Supervision card and on every missing-child screen an educator sees ("If the supervisor is away, contact: …").
- If someone leaves or loses their role at the centre, they drop off the list on their own.

## Reminders (agency admins)

Routine headcount reminders are set in **Settings → Room Controls → Headcounts**. Tick **Remind educators after** and choose an interval from 15 to 120 minutes. If a room with children signed in goes that long without a count, the educators clocked in to that room get a bell and a push reminder. Reminders are off by default.

[[open: #educator-rooms?tab=headcounts | Open headcount reminder settings]]

Reminders stop at the end of an educator's rostered shift that day, or at the centre's closing time if they have no roster. Someone who stays clocked in after hours is not sent reminders all evening.

The **Rooms now** tab shows agency admins whether reminders are on, with a link to change them.
