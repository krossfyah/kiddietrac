# Headcounts and the acting supervisor

A headcount is a name-to-face count of the children signed in to a room, done at every
transition, washroom trip, outdoor in/out and dismissal. The **Headcounts** screen shows
each room's last count, keeps the log of every count, and names who acts for the
supervisor. Open it from the sidebar: **Operations** → **Headcounts**.

[[open: #headcounts | Open Headcounts]]

Ontario's amended O. Reg. 137/15 takes effect on 1 January 2027. Under s.11, your
supervision policy must say when, how and how often children are counted, and what staff
do when a child is unaccounted for. Under s.6.1(2), it must name who acts for the
supervisor when the supervisor is off-site. KiddieTrac records both.

## Taking a headcount

1. Open the **👀 Rooms now** tab. Each centre lists its rooms with how many children are **here**, the last count, and a **👀 Headcount** button. (Educators also have **👀 Headcount** on their Today screen.)
2. Press **👀 Headcount** on the room. The list of children signed in opens.
3. Choose the moment at the top, for example routine, transition, outdoor or dismissal.
4. Tap each child as you see them. The counter shows how many of the children signed in are accounted for.
5. If a child is here but not signed in, tick them under **Not signed in yet**. They count as arrived for Safe Arrival, so their parents aren't messaged — sign them in afterwards.
6. Optionally open **Where / note (optional)** and add where you counted, such as the playground gate.
7. Press **Save — all … accounted for** (or **Save — … not seen**). If nobody is ticked, the button asks you to tap again first.

When everyone is accounted for, you see **All … accounted for** and the count closes itself.

## When a child is not seen

1. The result shows **⚠️ … not accounted for** with each missing child. Search now: the last place seen, washrooms, cubbies, the playground and gates, the parking lot, and follow your centre's missing-child procedure.
2. Press **🚨 Alert director now**. The centre's directors, the agency admins and the acting supervisors get a bell and a push at once. No text message is sent.
3. When the child is found, press **✅ Found — all accounted for**, say where they were found, and press **Mark found**. Anyone you alerted is told.
4. Or press **Count again** to recount. **Serious occurrence report** beside a child opens a pre-filled incident report.

## Checking the logs

1. Open the **📜 Logs** tab. Every headcount is listed: **When**, **Where**, **Moment**, **Result**, **Not seen**, **Counted by** and **Follow-up**.
2. Filter by date, centre (**All centres**), room (**All rooms**), moment (**Any moment**) and result (**Any result**).
3. For an open count (a child not seen and not yet marked found), press **Alert director** or **Mark found** on its row.

The screen remembers which tab you last used. A link to a single headcount, such as the one in a missing-child alert, opens the **📜 Logs** tab on it.

## Naming the acting supervisor

1. On **👀 Rooms now**, press **Acting supervisor…** beside the centre. Agency admins can also do this from **Centres** with the **Acting supervisor** button.
2. Choose people from **Add someone at this centre…**, in the order they should be contacted. Use **↑** to move someone up and **Remove** to take them off.
3. Press **Save**. An **Acting supervisor saved** message appears.

The names show on the dashboard's Supervision card and on every missing-child screen an
educator sees ("If the supervisor is away, contact: …"). Someone who leaves or loses their
role at the centre drops off the list on their own.

## The daily headcount report

1. Open **Reports** and choose **👀 Headcounts**.
2. Pick a single day for the daily log, or any date range.
3. Press **Run report**, then export it to PDF, Excel or CSV, or print it.

There is one row per count, and an open count shows **OPEN** in the follow-up column.
Agency admins can also **schedule** it so it is emailed every day.

## Reminders (agency admins)

Routine headcount reminders are set in **Settings → Room Controls → 👀 Headcounts**. Tick
**Remind educators after** and choose 15 to 120 minutes. A room with children signed in
that goes that long without a count sends the educators clocked in to it a bell and a
push. Reminders are off by default, and stop at the end of an educator's rostered shift
(or the centre's closing time). The **Rooms now** tab shows admins whether reminders are on,
with **Change in Room Controls →**.

[[open: #educator-rooms?tab=headcounts | Open headcount reminder settings]]

## Who can do what

- **Agency admins** see every centre, take counts, act on open counts, name acting supervisors and set reminders.
- **Centre directors** do the same for their own centres, except reminders.
- **Educators** take headcounts for their rooms and walks, alert the director, mark children found, and run the report for their own rooms.
- Parents do not see headcounts.

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? The 👀 Headcount button is greyed out. | Nobody is signed in to that room right now. A headcount lists the children who are signed in.
??? It says "More children signed in" when I save. | Someone was signed in while you counted. They are added to the list marked "just signed in" — find them too, then save.
??? It says "No acting supervisor is named for this centre yet." | A director or agency admin names one with Acting supervisor… on the Rooms now tab.
??? Who gets the missing-child alert? | The centre's directors, the agency admins and the acting supervisors, by bell and push notification. No text message is sent.
??? I saved by mistake with nobody ticked. | Count again right away. Each count is kept in the logs, and the new count shows the real result.
