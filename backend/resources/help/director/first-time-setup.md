# First-time setup

The first time a new **agency admin** signs in, a short setup wizard runs
automatically. You can reopen it later from **User management → Reopen
onboarding**.

## The steps

1. **Your profile** — confirm your name and phone, and **upload an avatar**
   (JPG/PNG, max 2 MB).
2. **Address & emergency contact** — your address and an emergency contact.
3. **About your organization** — country (sets currency + compliance), business
   name, tax/registration number, billing email, number of centres.
4. **Invite your team** *(optional)* — add **directors and educators**; each
   gets an email invite to set their password. Add as many as you like with
   **+ Add a team member**. (Parents are added per family under **Families**
   once you've added children.)
5. **Make it yours (white-label)** — *only if your plan includes white-label.*
   Upload your **logo**, set your **primary colour**, **support email**, and
   **privacy / terms URLs**. These appear on invoices and campaigns.

Click **Save and continue** on the last step to finish — you'll land on your
dashboard. Everything here can be changed later (profile, **Branding**, **User
management**).

> Who sees this: every new user gets steps 1–3 for their role; steps 4–5 are
> **agency admin** only (5 only when the plan includes white-label).
