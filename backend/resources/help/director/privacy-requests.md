# Privacy requests

**Administration › Privacy requests** tracks every request from a parent, staff member or anyone else to see what your
agency holds about them or their child, to correct it, to delete it, or to withdraw consent. Under PIPEDA you must
answer within 30 days (you may extend once by up to 30 more days, with notice), and a missed deadline counts as a
refusal. Log the request the day it arrives — by email, letter, phone or in person.

[[open: #privacy-requests | Open Privacy requests]]

## Logging a request

1. Press **+ Log a request**.
2. Under **Whose information**, type a name and pick the family member, child or staff member it is about.
3. Under **The request**, choose **What they are asking**, the **Received on** date and who **Asked by** (**The person themselves**, **Parent or guardian of the child** or **Someone acting for them**).
4. Describe **What they asked for** in their words.
5. Press **Log request**. A green **Request logged** toast appears and the request reopens with its timeline and its **Answer by** date.

A request needs **Asked by**, **Received on** and **What they asked for**.

## Checking who is asking

1. Under **Identity**, confirm the person is who they say, using details you already hold (their phone number or email on file). Do not collect new identity documents just for this.
2. Enter **Confirmed on** and press **Save changes**.

## Preparing and sending the answer

1. Press **⤓ Download their records**. You get their records in this agency as one file.
2. Review the file and remove other people's information before giving anything out.
3. Send, correct or delete what was asked. Log each step in **Steps and changes** with **Add step** ("Sent the records by email").
4. Under **Answer**, write what was given, corrected or deleted — or, for a refusal, the reasons and that they may complain to the Privacy Commissioner — and the **Answered on** date.
5. Set **Status** to **Completed** (or **Refused** / **Withdrawn**) and press **Save changes**.

## Extending the deadline

1. Tell the person in writing that you need more time, and why.
2. Fill in **Extended to** (no more than 30 days after the first due date) and **Why it was extended**.
3. Press **Save changes**. The **Answer by** date moves to the new date.

## Reading the list

The tiles show **Due within 7 days**, **Overdue** and **On record**. Each row shows the **Request**, when it was
**Received**, its **Answer by** date and status. Press **Open …** on a row to work on it.

> ⚠️ An overdue request counts as a refusal under PIPEDA — answer it as soon as possible.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | See, log, answer and download. |
| Centre director, educator | Nothing on this screen: "Only an agency admin can see privacy requests." Pass any request you receive to your agency admin the same day. |
| Platform admin | The agency chosen in the agency switcher. |

Nothing is ever deleted from the tracker.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? "No one in this agency by that name." | Check the spelling, or search by another family member. The person must be on file in the active agency.
??? ⤓ Download their records does nothing. | Choose whose information it is about first.
??? "Download failed" | Try again; if it keeps failing, raise a ticket from Help.
??? Does logging a request email the person? | No. You answer them yourself; the tracker only keeps the record and the dates.
