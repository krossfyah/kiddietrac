# Background-check tracking

Track vulnerable sector (VSS), criminal record, driver and reference checks for every staff member: when each one expires, the document on file, and any notes.

## Where it is

Sidebar → **Background checks**. Agency admins and centre directors can use it.

- **Agency admins** see the checks of all current staff in the agency.
- **Directors** see and edit the checks of their own centres' staff only.

[[open: #background-checks | Open Background checks]]

At the top you see four counts: **Checks on file**, **Expired**, **Expiring in 60 days**, and **Staff with no check**.

## Add a check

1. Press **+ Add check**.
2. Choose the **Staff member**. The list shows current staff of the agency (educators, home visitors, directors and admins) with their staff reference ID.
3. Pick the **Type of check**. The types follow your province or state's rules where they are set up; otherwise VSS, Criminal, Driver, Reference or Other.
4. Enter the **Reference / certificate #**, the **Issued** date (optional) and the **Expires** date (required).
5. Add any **Notes** — for example "offence declaration on file", "renewal applied for" or "follow-up needed".
6. Press **Save check**.

The expiry date cannot be before the issue date.

## Attach the document

1. Press **View** on the row.
2. Under the record, choose the file (PDF, image or Word document) and press **Upload**.

The row then shows a **Document** link. Editing a check later keeps the document that is attached.

## The table

Each row shows the staff member, check, reference, issued and expiry dates (with "in 12 days" or "5 days ago"), status, notes, and **View**, **Edit** and **Delete** buttons. Use the **All statuses** filter to show only expired, expiring or valid checks.

Status colours:

- **Valid** (green) — expires more than 60 days from now
- **Expiring soon** (amber) — expires within 60 days
- **Expired** (red) — past the expiry date

Deleting a check asks you to confirm and cannot be undone.

## Expiry reminders

Every morning at 8:00, KiddieTrac looks for checks that expire within the next 60 days or ran out in the last 30. The staff member gets a notice in their bell, for example *Background check expires in 21 day(s) (VSS)*, at most once a week per check, until it is renewed. Use the **Expiring in 60 days** and **Expired** counts at the top of this screen to follow up.

> **Tip:** When a renewal comes in, edit the check with the new expiry date and upload the new document. The reminders stop on their own.

## On the staff record

A staff member's own record has a **Background checks** tab listing their checks, expiry dates, notes and a link to the document.

## Export

Press **⤓ CSV** for a spreadsheet with staff name, staff ID, email, check type, reference, issued, expires, status and notes. The Excel export includes notes too.
