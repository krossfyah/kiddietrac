# Adding a family

Every child belongs to a family, so a new enrolment starts here. The **New family** wizard adds the family, its parents and guardians, the children and the emergency contacts in one go.

[[open: #admin-families | Open Families]]

## Where it is

- **Agency admins:** sidebar → **Administration** → **Families** → **+ Add family** opens the **New family** wizard described below.
- **Directors and admins:** **⚡ Quick add** → **👪 New family** opens a shorter form for the family itself: family name, primary email, primary phone, country, the full address and preferred language. Email, country and the full address (street, city, province or state, postal or ZIP code) are required. Add the guardians and children afterwards.

## The five steps

The wizard has five steps: **Family**, **Guardians**, **Children**, **Emergency** and **Review**. **← Back** takes you to the previous step without closing the wizard.

### 1. Family

- **Centre** and **Family name** — required.
- **Primary phone**.
- **Primary email** — required.
- **Country** — Canada or United States. Required.
- **Street address**, **City**, **Province / State** and **Postal / ZIP code** — all required.
- **Billing split** — single payer, 50 / 50 or a custom split.
- **Internal notes** — staff only. There's plenty of room to write.

> **Tip:** Start typing the street address and pick from the suggestions. It fills in the city, province or state, postal or ZIP code and country for you. Suggestions cover Canada and the US and appear after the first few characters.

If the family name looks like one already on file, you're asked to tick **This is a different family** or open the existing one instead.

### 2. Guardians

Add one or more parents or guardians. The first one is the primary contact and billing payer.

- **First name** and **Last name** — required.
- **Email** — required for the first guardian. For anyone else it's **optional**. A guardian with no email is on the family but has no login. Each email must be different.
- **Phone**, and a **Second phone** with its type: **Mobile**, **Home**, **Work** or **Other**. A work number can have an **Ext.**
- **Gender** and **Relationship**.
- **Authorized for pickup**.

Press **+ Add another guardian** for more.

### 3. Children

Name, date of birth, room, attendance days, usual drop-off and pick-up times, allergies, diet, medical notes and the family doctor. Every child needs a room, so they show up on a roster. See [Adding a new child](adding-a-new-child).

### 4. Emergency

Optional. Add the people to call if a guardian can't be reached: name, relationship, phone, a second phone with its type and extension, and email. Tick **Authorized for pickup** for anyone who may collect the children.

Tick **Give a pick-up login** to give that person their own sign-in (their email is then required). See [Pick-up logins](pick-up-logins).

### 5. Review

Check everything. **Send the welcome email now** is ticked by default: each guardian with an email gets their sign-in details and an introduction to the centre. Untick it to add the family quietly and send the welcome later from the family card.

## Phone numbers

Every phone box formats itself as you type: **4165550199** becomes **(416) 555-0199**. Type the digits only. Anything that isn't a phone number, such as a note, is left as you wrote it.

## Parents who already have a forms account

If you sent forms to a parent before their family was in KiddieTrac, they already have a temporary, forms-only account. As you type a guardian's name or email on the **Guardians** step, KiddieTrac looks for one.

1. A yellow box appears: **Forms-only account found**, with their email and how many forms they've signed.
2. Press **Link to this family**.
3. A popup shows what's on that account — name, email, phone, gender, forms signed, and when they last signed in. Press **Use this account**.
4. The guardian is filled in from the account, and the box turns green: **Will link … forms account**.

When you finish the wizard, that account joins the family, signed forms included. Nothing on the account itself is changed. Typing the same email yourself links it the same way.

See [Forms-only parents](forms-only-parents).

## Changing a family later

Open the family and choose **Edit**. The editor has the tabs **Family**, **Guardians**, **Children** and **Emergency**, with the same fields, including country, second phone numbers and pick-up logins.

??? Why is the email required now? | The primary email is how the family receives invoices, notices and the welcome. A family can't be saved without it, or without a full address.
??? A second parent has no email. | Leave their email blank. They're on the family and can be called, but they won't have a login. Add an email later to invite them.
??? The address suggestions don't appear. | Type at least four characters of the street. Suggestions are for Canada and the US; anywhere else, type the address in full.
