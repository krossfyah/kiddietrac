# Video sharing feed

The **Video feed** collects the short videos your team shares from the centre: first steps, a song at circle time, a concert or a field-trip arrival. Staff upload a clip with a caption, everyone on the team can play it, and people react with an emoji. Use it when a moment needs sound and movement; for everything else, photos load faster and use less storage.

[[open: #videos | Open Video feed]]

The banner shows how many videos are on the feed. Below it, each video is a card with the player, the date and who shared it, the caption, and a row of reaction buttons.

## Sharing a video

1. Open **Engagement > Video feed**.
2. Press **+ Upload video** in the banner. The **Share a video** window opens.
3. Press the file box and choose a video from your computer or phone. MP4, MOV (QuickTime) and WebM files work.
4. In **🏷 Who's in this photo?**, press the name of each child in the video. It is the same picker as photo sharing: **Everyone here** tags every child who is here right now, **Clear** removes all tags, and the count beside the heading shows how many are tagged.
5. Type a **Caption**, for example "Maya's first steps in the toddler room".
6. Press **Upload**. When it finishes, a toast says "Video shared" and the video appears at the top of the feed.

> ℹ️ Every video needs at least one tagged child. The tag is what puts the video on that child's family feed, so a video with no children tagged would reach no family.

## Photo permissions and family notices

Photo permissions apply to videos exactly as they do to photos.

- A child marked **🚫** (their family asked that they not be photographed) is greyed out in the picker and cannot be tagged. The server refuses it too.
- A child marked **👪** has family-only photos: other families do not see a video that child is on. When that keeps the video from some families, the toast after **Upload** names them instead of "Video shared".
- The families allowed to see the video get a **New video shared** notice in the app (with your caption, or "A new moment is on the feed."). A parent who switched off **Photos & videos** in their notification settings is not told, but still sees the video on their feed.

Press **Cancel** to close the window without sharing anything.

> ⚠️ Keep clips short. The largest video you can share is 50 MB, which is roughly a minute or two of phone video. Trim longer clips on your phone before you upload.

## Watching and reacting

1. Find the video on the feed. The newest is first.
2. Press play on the player. Use its controls for volume and full screen.
3. Press a reaction: **❤️**, **😊**, **😮**, **🎉** or **👏**. The number beside it goes up by one.

Each reaction button shows how many times it has been pressed, so you can see at a glance which videos families and staff enjoyed.

## Which videos you see

- The feed shows the 80 most recent videos.
- Staff see the videos shared at the centres they work at. An agency admin sees every centre in the agency.
- A video is filed under the centre of the first child you tag (when you work at that centre), so it shows to the staff of that child's centre.
- Families see a video only when it tags one of their children and every child on it allows photos to be shared with them.

## Who can do what

| Role | What they can do |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Share videos, watch and react to every centre's videos. |
| Platform admin | The same, for the agency they have chosen. |
| Centre director, educator | The feed is not in their menu. |
| Parents | See only videos that tag their own child and that photo permissions allow. They do not get the **+ Upload video** button: only staff share videos. |

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? I get "Pick a video". | No file was chosen. Press the file box, choose a video and press **Upload** again.
??? I get "Tag at least one child so their family sees it." | Press at least one child's name in **🏷 Who's in this photo?**, then press **Upload** again.
??? A child's name is greyed out with 🚫. | Their family asked that they not be photographed, so they cannot be tagged in a video either. Share a clip without that child, or check the family's choice on **Photo permissions**.
??? The window says "Could not load the children to tag." | The children list did not load. Close the window, check your connection and press **+ Upload video** again.
??? I get "File too large. Max video size is 50MB." | Trim the clip or record a shorter one, then upload it again.
??? I get "Choose a centre you work at before sharing a video." | Your account is not linked to a centre in this agency. Ask an agency admin to add you to a centre, or switch to the right agency first.
??? The upload failed with another message. | Check your connection and that the file is MP4, MOV or WebM. Other formats are refused.
??? The page says "No videos yet." | Nobody at your centres has shared a video. Press **+ Upload video** to share the first one.
??? Are families told when I share a video? | Yes. The parents of each tagged child whose photo permissions allow it get a **New video shared** notice, unless they switched off **Photos & videos** in their notification settings.
??? Can I delete a video? | Not from this screen. Ask your agency admin or raise a ticket if a video was shared by mistake.
