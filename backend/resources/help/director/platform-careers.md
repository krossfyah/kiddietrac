# Careers (job applications to KiddieTrac)

**Platform › Careers** holds the job applications people send from www.kiddietrac.com/careers: who applied for which role, their resume and cover letter, where each one stands, and how hiring is going. It is KiddieTrac's own hiring, not an agency's, and only platform super-admins can open it. A copy of every application is also emailed to the KiddieTrac info inbox.

[[open: #platform-careers | Open Careers]]

The screen has two tabs: **📊 Insights** and **📋 Applications**. It remembers the one you used last.

## Seeing how hiring is going

1. Open **Platform › Careers** and choose **📊 Insights**.
2. Pick a **Period** (**Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, **Last 12 months** or **All time**) and, if you like, one **Posting**.
3. Read the tiles: **Applications**, **New this week**, **Waiting for a first look**, **In progress**, **Interview rate**, **Hired**, **Time to first response** and **Time to hire**.
4. Look at the charts: applications over time, **Pipeline**, **Where they stand now**, **By posting**, **By department**, **By location**, **Day of the week**, **Hour of the day** and **What applicants send**.
5. Check **Waiting on you**: applications still **New**, and ones in progress with no change for 7 days or more, oldest first.
6. Check **Postings on the careers page** for roles with no applicants, and **Roles by applicants** for the busiest roles.

> ℹ️ Status changes are recorded from early November 2026, so **Time to first response** and **Time to hire** only count applications after that.

## Working through an application

1. Choose **📋 Applications**. Each row shows **Received**, **Role**, **Applicant**, **Contact** and **Status**.
2. Open the row's **⋮** and choose **👁️ View resume** or **👁️ View cover letter** to read it in the portal, or **⬇️ Download resume** / **⬇️ Download cover letter** to save it. **💬 Note** shows a typed cover note.
3. Change the **Status** drop-down as you go: **New**, **Reviewing**, **Interview**, **Offer**, **Hired**, **Kept on file**, **Declined** or **Withdrawn**. A **Status updated** toast confirms it. Changing the status alone sends no email.

## Booking a meeting, keeping on file or declining

Each of these opens a window with an email preview. Nothing is sent until you press the send button.

1. From the row's **⋮**, choose **📅 Book a meeting**, **🗂️ Keep on file** or **💌 Decline politely**.
2. For a meeting, set **Date**, **Time (Toronto)**, **Length** and **How** (**Microsoft Teams video call** or **Phone call / in person**, with **Where (optional)**).
3. Add **A personal note (optional)** if you want a line in your own words.
4. Check the **Email preview** on the right.
5. Press **Send invitation**, **Keep on file & send** or **Send**.

What happens next:
- **Book a meeting** emails the invitation and puts the meeting on the team calendar in Meetings.
- **Keep on file** keeps the resume for **24 months**, then removes it automatically; the email tells the applicant the date and that they can ask for removal at any time.
- **Decline politely** sends a thank-you email.

The row then shows which email was sent and when. Emails are signed "The KiddieTrac Team" and go only to the applicant's own address.

## Who can do what

- **Platform super-admins**: everything on this screen.
- **Agency admins and every other role**: cannot see KiddieTrac's job applications. Agencies hire their own staff in **Human Resources**.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? The list says "No applications yet." | Nobody has applied from the careers page yet. New applications appear here as soon as they are sent.
??? "Could not load applications. Please try again." | The connection dropped or your session ended. Refresh the page.
??? "Saved, but the email could not be sent. Check the address." | The outcome was recorded but the applicant's email bounced. Contact them another way.
??? "Pick a date and a time." | A meeting needs both before it can be sent.
??? A Word resume looks different from the original. | Word files are shown as text, so the layout can differ. Use **⬇️ Download** to open the original.
??? Can I undo a decline email? | No. An email that has been sent cannot be recalled. You can still change the **Status** afterwards.
