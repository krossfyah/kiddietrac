# HR: open shifts and shift notices

Post a shift nobody is on yet and let the staff who can work it ask for it. Staff also hear when their own shifts change. This is part of the KiddieTrac HR add-on.

## Posting an open shift (directors and admins)

Open **Human Resources > Rota & shifts > Open shifts** and choose **Post an open shift**. Pick the room, the day (and up to 13 more days in a row), the hours, the role and, if needed, a certificate the person must hold, such as First Aid.

The board also lists the week's **ratio shortfalls**: times when a room has fewer educators planned than its children need. Each one has **Post an open shift** in its ⋮ menu, with the room, day and hours filled in. You can do the same from the shortfalls on **Human Resources > Rota & shifts > Rota plan**. An open shift that covers a shortfall is marked.

Everyone at the centre who can take the shift hears about it once (bell and phone), however many days you post.

## Who can take one

A person sees an open shift and can ask for it when they:

- work at that centre;
- are free then: no approved leave, no can't-work date, and inside their weekly availability;
- are not already on a shift at that time, at any centre;
- hold the certificate it asks for, current on that day.

If they cannot take it, My HR says why.

## Asking for an open shift (staff)

Open **My HR** and find **Open shifts you can pick up**. Open one with **View open shift** and choose **Ask for this shift**. You can add a note. You can **Withdraw my request** while it is waiting.

## Confirming (directors and admins)

Requests show on the board as **waiting** and in your activity bell. Open the shift and choose **Confirm** beside a name: the shift goes on the rota for that person, and anyone else who asked is told it went to a colleague. **Decline** tells that person. **Take it off the board** cancels the open shift and tells anyone who asked.

Nobody confirms their own request: another director or an agency admin does.

## Shift notices

When someone's upcoming shifts are added, changed or cancelled, they get **one** notice (bell, phone and email) listing all the changes, sent once the rota has been quiet for a few minutes. Building a week's rota, autofill and standing hours send one notice per person, not one per shift. A change that is undone before the notice goes is not sent, and you are never told about a change you made yourself.

**Shift notices** on the board (agency admins) turns them on or off, turns the email off, chooses how long to wait for the rota to go quiet, and decides whether the nightly rota fill (which adds one day at a time) sends notices. Each person can switch notices off in **Settings > Notifications > My shifts**.
