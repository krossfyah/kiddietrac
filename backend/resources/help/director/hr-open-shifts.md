# HR: open shifts and shift notices

The **Open shifts** board, in **Human Resources > Rota & shifts**, is part of the KiddieTrac HR add-on. Post a shift nobody is on yet, and staff who can work it ask for it in My HR; a director confirms who gets it. The board also lists the week's ratio shortfalls, and holds the settings for the notices staff get when their own shifts change.

[[open: #schedule?tab=openshifts | Open the Open shifts board]]

## Posting an open shift (directors and admins)

1. Open **Human Resources > Rota & shifts** and choose **Open shifts**.
2. Use **‹**, **›** or **This week** to pick the week.
3. Press **Post an open shift**.
4. Choose the **Room** and the **Role**.
5. Choose the **Day**. To post the same hours on following days, pick **Repeat on the next days (optional)** (up to **+13 days**).
6. Set **From** and **To**.
7. If the person must hold a certificate (for example First Aid), choose it in **Must hold (optional)**.
8. Add a **Note for staff (optional)** and press **Post it**. An **Open shift posted.** toast appears.

Everyone at the centre who can take the shift is told once (bell and phone), however many days you post.

> ⚠️ Days the centre is closed are your call: check the calendar before you post.

## Covering a ratio shortfall

1. On the board, look at **Ratio shortfalls this week**: times when a room has fewer **Educators planned** than its **Children expected** need.
2. Press **Post an open shift** on that row. The room, day and hours are filled in.
3. Check the role and press **Post it**.

An open shift that covers a shortfall is marked **Covers a ratio shortfall**. The same button is on each warning in **Rota & shifts > Rota plan**.

## Confirming who takes it

1. Requests show on the board in the **Asked** column and in your activity bell.
2. Press **View open shift** on the row.
3. Under **Who asked**, press **Confirm** beside a name. You see **Confirmed. The shift is on the rota.**
4. Or press **Decline** beside a name to tell that person.

Anyone else who asked is told it went to a colleague. To cancel the shift, press **Take it off the board**: everyone who asked is told.

> ℹ️ Nobody confirms their own request. It says "Your own request: another director or an admin confirms it."

## Who can take an open shift

A person sees it, and can ask for it, when they:

- work at that centre;
- are free then: no approved leave, no can't-work date, and inside their weekly availability;
- are not on another shift at that time, at any centre;
- hold the certificate it asks for, current on that day.

If they cannot take it, My HR says why.

## Asking for an open shift (staff)

1. Open **My HR** and find **Open shifts you can pick up**.
2. Press **View open shift**.
3. Add a **Note for your director (optional)** and press **Ask for this shift**. You see "Asked. A director confirms who takes it."
4. Changed your mind? Open it again and press **Withdraw my request**.

You are told either way.

## Setting up shift notices (agency admins)

When someone's upcoming shifts are added, changed or cancelled, they get **one** notice (bell, phone and email) listing all the changes, once the rota has been quiet for a few minutes. A week built at once, autofill and standing hours send one notice per person, not one per shift. A change undone before the notice goes is not sent, and you are never told about a change you made yourself.

1. On the board, press **Shift notices**.
2. Tick or untick **Send shift notices** and **Also by email**.
3. Set **Wait for the rota to be quiet for** (a few minutes).
4. Choose whether to **Include shifts the nightly rota fill adds (it adds a day at a time)**.
5. Press **Save**. A **Shift notice settings saved.** toast appears.

Each person can turn these off for themselves in **Settings > Notifications** (the **My shifts** topic).

## Who can do what

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Post, confirm, decline and cancel at every centre; change the shift notice settings. |
| Centre director | Post, confirm, decline and cancel at their own centres. |
| Educator, home visitor | See and ask for open shifts they can take, in My HR. |

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? A staff member says they can't see the open shift. | They are not eligible: another shift at that time, leave or a can't-work date, outside their availability, a missing or expired certificate, or not placed at that centre. My HR tells them which.
??? Can I post one shift for a whole week? | Yes: choose the first day and **Repeat on the next days**. Staff are told once.
??? I get "The shift ends after it starts." | The **To** time is before **From**. Fix the hours.
??? There is no Confirm button. | It is your own request, or the person is no longer eligible (the reason shows beside their name).
