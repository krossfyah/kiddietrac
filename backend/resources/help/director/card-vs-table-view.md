# Card vs Table view on lists

The Families and Children lists offer two ways to look at the same data. Pick the one that fits your task — Kiddietrac remembers your choice per-list.

## Cards (default)

Each row is a coloured card with the most-important fields highlighted. Best for browsing — you scan visually for the family or child you want, then click in.

- Larger touch targets (works well on tablets)
- Easier to spot warnings (e.g. outstanding balance in red, current room colour swatch)
- Slower if you have many rows to scan at once

## Table

Each row is a compact line in a structured table with sortable columns. Best for dense lists — you can see 20+ rows on screen at once.

- Faster to spot patterns (e.g. who has the highest fee, oldest child, etc.)
- Familiar spreadsheet feel for billing/audit work
- Less visual hierarchy — same compactness, easier to misclick

## Switching views

Look at the toolbar above the list — there's a small segmented control with two icons:

- **Cards** (square icon) — the default
- **Table** (lines icon) — switches to table

Click whichever you want. Your choice persists in your browser's storage and applies the next time you visit that list. Each list (Families, Children, etc.) tracks its own preference independently.

## Centres tab

The Centres tab already uses table view by design — the column-heavy data (capacity %, enrolled count, staff count, etc.) reads better in a grid. There's no card option there currently.
