# Tuition & fee plans

Reusable fee plans are the building blocks of billing — a named amount plus how
often it bills. You define them once and apply them to families to generate
invoices.

## Creating a plan

1. Sidebar → **Settings → Tuition plans**.
2. Click **+ New plan**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Plan name** (e.g. "Toddler full-time")
   - **Amount** and **billing frequency** (weekly / bi-weekly / monthly / one-time)
   - Optionally scope to a **centre**, **age group**, or add a **description**
   - **Active** — only active plans can be applied to families
4. Save. The plan appears in the list with its amount and frequency.

Click any plan to **edit** or **delete** it.

## Generating invoices from a plan (invoice automation)

1. On an active plan, click **💸 Generate invoices**.
2. Pick the **families** to bill (or Select all).
3. Choose **Draft** (review first) or **Sent** (issue now) and a **due-in days**.
4. Click **Generate invoices** — one invoice per family is created with the plan
   amount as a tuition line, in your agency's currency.

Drafts can be reviewed and edited before sending. The currency follows your
agency's **Country & compliance** setting.

> Who can use this: **Agency admin** and **Platform admin**.
