# Adding a new child

In Kiddietrac, every child belongs to a **family**. Before you can add a child, you need their family in the system.

## Step 1 — Make sure their family exists

Check the **Families** section in the sidebar. If their family isn't there, click **👪 New family** in QUICK ADD first.

## Step 2 — Add the child

Click **👶 New child** in the QUICK ADD section of the sidebar.

Fill in:

- **First name, last name** — their legal name as it appears on official documents
- **Preferred name** — what they actually go by (e.g., "Jamie" instead of "James")
- **Date of birth** — used for age and room eligibility
- **Gender** — choose "Prefer not to say" if you don't want to record this
- **Family** — the family they belong to (must be created first)
- **Room** — which room they're enrolled in
- **Start date** — typically today, but you can future-date it
- **Monthly fee** — what the family pays at full rate before any subsidy
- **CWELCC eligible** — usually true for children under 6; this enables the Ontario subsidy
- **Medical notes** — allergies, conditions, EpiPen location, action plans
- **Dietary notes** — restrictions, preferences

## Important: medical notes

If a child has a **severe allergy** (peanut, tree nut, etc.) or a **medical condition** (asthma, diabetes), enter this carefully. The medical note will appear on the educator's tablet as a red badge. This is critical safety information.

Include:
- What the allergen / condition is
- Where the EpiPen / inhaler / medication is stored
- The action plan: "If they show symptoms, administer EpiPen and call 911"

## After adding a child

The child is automatically enrolled in the room you selected. Their family will see them in the parent portal as soon as you invite the parent.

**Next step:** invite the parent(s). See **Inviting parents**.

## Common questions

**What if I don't know their CWELCC eligibility yet?**
Leave it on (the default). You can update it later. The subsidy is applied to invoices when they're generated.

**What if they're moving rooms next month?**
Add them to their current room now. When they move, you can update the enrollment.

**Can I add a child without enrolling them in a room?**
Not yet — every child must be enrolled in a room when created. If they're on a waitlist, ask your account manager about the waitlist feature.
