# Reports — run, download and schedule

**Administration › Reports** holds the ready-made reports for your centres and agency: attendance and hours, invoices
and balances, monthly attendance and fees, the record of attendance, capacity and vacancies and more. Pick a report,
set the dates and centre, and run it as a PDF or spreadsheet — or have it emailed to someone automatically every day,
week or month.

[[open: #reports | Open Reports]]

## Running a report

1. Open **Administration › Reports**.
2. Choose the tab: **Centre reports** or **Agency reports**.
3. Set the date range at the top (**Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **This month to date**, **Last calendar month**, or your own **From** and **To**).
4. Pick the **Centre** (or **All centres**).
5. Press **Run report** on the report's card. "Generating report…" shows, then the file downloads.

Some reports ask one more question first:

- **Invoices & balances** — tick the **Status** values to include (for example **Overdue**, **Voided**), choose the **Period** (**The date range on the screen**, **Specific dates** or **Every invoice, all dates**) and the **Balance** (**Any balance**, **Only invoices with money still owing** or **Only invoices settled in full**), then press **Run report**.
- **Monthly attendance & fees**, **Monthly attendance & fees by family**, **Record of Attendance** and **Capacity & vacancies** — pick the month (and the family, for the by-family sheet).

> 💡 Statuses are coloured the same way in the PDF as on screen (for example paid, partially paid, absence recorded).

## Emailing a report automatically

1. Open the **Scheduled reports** tab and press **Schedule a report**.
2. Choose the **Report** and the **Format**: **PDF**, **Excel** or both.
3. Choose the period it covers (for example **Last 7 days** or **Last calendar month**).
4. Set the **Frequency** — **Daily**, **Weekly** (pick the **Day of week**) or **Monthly** (pick the **Day of month**) — and the send time.
5. Enter **Send to (email)**.
6. Press **Save schedule**. A **Schedule created** toast appears and the schedule shows in the list as **Active**, with when it was **Last sent**.

To change one, open its row actions and choose edit, then **Save changes**. Use **Send now** to send it straight away,
the switch to pause it (**Schedule paused**), or **Delete** to remove it.

## Who can do what

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Agency admin | Run every report for every centre, and schedule reports. |
| Centre director | Run reports for their own centre(s). |
| Educator | Attendance and hours for the room(s) they are assigned to. |
| Platform admin | The agency chosen in the agency switcher. |

Scheduled emails go only to the address you type. Make sure the person is allowed to see that report.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? "Pick at least one status, or the report has nothing to show." | In the Invoices & balances questions, tick at least one status.
??? "The start date is after the end date." | Swap the dates, or pick one of the ready-made ranges.
??? An educator sees "No rooms are assigned to you yet." | Assign them to a room on their staff record; their reports follow their rooms.
??? A scheduled report did not arrive. | Check the address on the schedule, then press **Send now**. If you see "Could not send", raise a ticket from Help.
??? I want my own columns and filters. | The built-in exports on each list (Families, Children, Invoices …) give a spreadsheet of what is on screen.
