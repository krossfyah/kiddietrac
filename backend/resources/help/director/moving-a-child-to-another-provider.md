# Moving a child to another provider

Once a family is enrolled, its provider is changed only by **moving** the children, never by
editing the family record. Moving keeps the history straight and tells everyone who needs to know.

## How

1. Open the family (or the child) and use **⋮ → Move to another provider**.
2. Choose the provider (and room) they are moving to. Each option shows how many places are left.
3. Pick the date the move takes effect, whether brothers and sisters move too, and an optional reason.
4. Confirm.

## Who is told what

| Who | What they get |
|---|---|
| **The family** | A **change-of-provider letter** (who has their child, from when, and that nothing else changes), then the **new provider's welcome email** with their photo, bio and how to reach them. Plus a notice in the app. |
| **The new provider / educators** | *"👋 Joining you"* — the child is on their roster from that date. |
| **The educator the child is leaving** | *"👋 Moving on — [child] moved to a new educator, effective [date]. They have come off your roster."* The new provider's name is not shared with them. |

The welcome email is only sent when the provider actually changes: a different centre, or a room
with someone new caring for the child. A room change under the same educator sends the letter only.

> **Tip:** the welcome email uses the new provider's bio. If they haven't written one, families see
> a placeholder. See [Provider bios](provider-bios).

The move is recorded in the provider history and the audit log.
