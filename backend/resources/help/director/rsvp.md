# Event RSVPs — invitations and sign-ups

**Engagement → Event RSVPs** runs an event from invitation to head count: an open house, a
parent night, a fundraiser. You make the event, add the people to invite, send each of them
a personal RSVP link, and watch who signs up, who opts out and who has not answered. The
screen has four tabs: **Events**, **Templates**, **Sender accounts** and **Reports**.

[[open: #rsvp | Open Event RSVPs]]

## Creating an event

1. Press **＋ New event** at the top right.
2. Under **Details**, pick what to **Start from**: one of **Your templates** or a **Built-in templates** entry. It fills in the questions, the invitation email and the image.
3. Type the **Event title**, then **Starts**, **Ends** and **Where**.
4. Write **About the event (shown on the RSVP page)**. Add a **Capacity (optional)** if places are limited.
5. Leave **Status** on **Draft — link not live yet** until you are ready, or choose **Open — taking RSVPs**.
6. Under **Email & image**, check the **Subject line** and **Message**. `{{event}}` puts in the event title and `{{first_name}}` the person's first name. Add a **Confirmation message (sent to each registrant)** and **Tips for attendees (confirmation email)** if you want them.
7. Under **Sender & branding**, choose **Send from** and type the address in **Send sign-ups and opt-outs to**. That address hears about every sign-up and is the Reply-To on invitations.
8. Press **👁 Preview** to see the invitation, the RSVP page and the confirmation exactly as people will. Nothing is sent from a preview.
9. Press **Create event**. The event page opens.

> 💡 **Branding**: **Agency branded** uses your logo, header and footer. **Unbranded** shows only the event's own image, title and text, with no KiddieTrac or agency name.

## Inviting people

1. On the event page, under **Invitees**, press **＋ Add invitees**.
2. On **Paste or upload**, paste one person per line ("Name, email", "Name <email>" or a bare email), or press **Upload a CSV file**. The box counts the valid emails as you go.
3. Or press **Families & staff**, search, filter to **Families** or **Staff**, and tick people. **Select shown** ticks everyone in the list.
4. Tick **Send their invitations now** to send at once (an event must be open first), or leave it to send later.
5. Press **Add invitees**. A message says how many were added, how many invitations are on their way, and anyone skipped because they were already on the list or had opted out.

## Sending the invitations

Each event card on the **Events** tab has one main button that follows its status:
**Preview & send** for a draft, **Send N invitations** when people are waiting,
**Invite more** when everyone has been sent one, and **View report** once it is closed.

1. Press **Preview & send** (or **Send N invitations**).
2. Check the **Invitation email**, **RSVP page** and **Confirmation email** tabs. The **From**, **Reply-To** and **Subject** lines are shown above.
3. Press the send button and confirm. Anyone who opted out is skipped.

## Reminders

1. On the event page, under **Reminders**, press a quick choice: **＋ 1 week before**, **＋ 1 day before** or **＋ 2 hours before** (these need the event to have a start time), or **＋ Custom…**.
2. In **Schedule a reminder**, choose **When** (**Before the event starts** or **At a specific date and time**) and **Send to**, add an optional message, and press **Schedule reminder**.
3. To send one straight away instead, press **🔔 Send a reminder now**.

## Following the replies

1. Open the **Reports** tab and choose the event.
2. Press a view: **Everyone**, **Sent**, **Signed up**, **Opted out**, **No response** or **Not sent**. Each shows its count.
3. The table shows each person's status, when they were sent, opened, signed up or opted out, and their answers. Times are in your agency's time zone.
4. Press **⬇ CSV** to download the view.

The **⋮** on an event card also offers **✏️ Edit**, **🔗 Copy link** (a shareable RSVP link),
**📄 Duplicate**, **⏸ Close event**, **💾 Save as my template** and **🗑 Delete**.

## Who can do what

- **Agency admins** use every tab for their agency.
- **Platform admins** can also work across agencies.
- Centre directors, educators and parents do not see this screen. Invited people only see the invitation email and the RSVP page.

## FAQs and when something goes wrong

??? Why is Send their invitations now greyed out? | The event is still a draft. Open it for RSVPs first, or send from Preview & send, which opens it and sends in one go.
??? My invitations went to junk. | Send from a sender account on your own domain, or connect Gmail on the Sender accounts tab. A "Town of ..." name sent from a free email address is often treated as junk.
??? Someone who opted out wants invitations again. | On the Reports tab, scroll to Unsubscribed from all event invitations and press Resubscribe beside their email. Do it only when they have asked.
??? What happens when I close an event? | The RSVP link stops taking sign-ups. Everything already received stays in the report, and you can reopen it.
??? What does deleting an event do? | Its RSVP link stops working, anything not yet sent is held back and the report is no longer available. Close it instead if you might need the report.
??? Who gets an email when someone signs up? | The address in Send sign-ups and opt-outs to, and the person who signed up gets the confirmation email.
