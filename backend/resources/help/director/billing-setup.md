# Billing

Everything that governs how your agency bills lives under **Settings → Billing**,
organised into two tabs.

## Setup tab

- **Billing defaults** — the default frequency, **deposit**, and **registration
  fee** pre-selected when you create invoices and fee plans.
- **Late fees** — percentage charged on overdue balances, a cap, and grace days.
- **Accepted payment methods** — tick which ways families may pay (card, bank
  transfer/ACH, Interac e-Transfer, cash, cheque, other).
- **Autopay** — enrol new families by default, require a card on file, and an
  optional card surcharge %.

Click **Save billing setup** to apply.

## Settings tab

- **Enable email** / **Enable SMS** for the agency (invoices, reminders,
  announcements, alerts).
- **Default language** — English, Français, Español, or **हिन्दी (Hindi)**.

## Related

- **Tuition plans** — define reusable fee plans and generate invoices.
- **White-label branding** — put your logo, address, and colours on invoices.

> Who can use this: **Agency admin** and **Platform admin**.
