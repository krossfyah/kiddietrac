# Assigning educators to rooms

**Room assignments** puts every educator and home visitor on one page with the rooms
they work in, and saves the lot in one go.

## Why it matters

Several screens work room by room: who sees which children, whose attendance and
sign-out figures are counted against them. Without an assignment those screens fall
back to showing the whole centre, which is why an educator can see more children than
they teach, and why the daily digest scorecard reports sign-outs per centre rather
than per person.

## Using it

1. **Staff → Room assignments.**
2. Each row is one person. Tick the rooms they work in.
3. The banner at the top counts how many people still have no room.
4. **Save changes** — only the people you actually changed are written.

The chips offered on each row are the rooms at **that person's own centre**. Offering
every room in the agency is how somebody ends up attached to a room three sites away
by a mis-click.

## Worth knowing

- Saving **replaces** a person's rooms rather than adding to them: the row shows the
  complete set, so what you submit is the complete answer.
- Re-saving without changing anything writes nothing, so the record of who assigned a
  room and when is not churned.
- An educator can hold several rooms.
