# Room assignments and room rotations

Two room jobs live here. **Room assignments** (Settings → Room Controls → Room
assignments) says which rooms each educator works in. **Room rotations**
(Administration → Room rotations) lists the children who will soon be old enough to move
up to the next room, so you can plan the move.

[[open: #room-rotations | Open Room rotations]]
[[open: #educator-rooms | Open Room Controls]]

## Assigning a person to rooms

Room assignments is the first tab of **Room Controls**, which also holds the headcount,
sleep check, daily records, auto sign-off and walk settings. See [Room Controls](room-controls).

1. Open **Settings** → **Room Controls** → **🚪 Room assignments**.
2. Choose the person in the **Educator** list. Their current rooms are shown straight away, read-only.
3. Press **✏️ Edit assignments**. The screen stays read-only until you do: these decisions control who can see which children.
4. Tick the rooms they work in, and untick the rooms they no longer work in.
5. Press **Save changes**. **Cancel** puts it back exactly as it was when you pressed Edit.

Switching to a different person mid-edit asks first whether to discard your changes.

## Why it matters

Several screens work room by room — who sees which children, and whose attendance and
sign-out figures are counted against them. Without an assignment those screens fall back
to showing the whole centre, which is why an educator can see more children than they
teach. Room assignments also decide who gets headcount reminders and which daily records
count towards an educator's lesson plan check. A banner counts how many people have no
room and how many rooms have nobody — worth getting to zero.

## Every room in the agency is offered

The list is not limited to the person's own centre. Their own centres are **marked and
sorted to the top**, so reaching outside them is visibly deliberate. Someone who works
across several sites can be assigned correctly, and the save is always checked against
the whole agency.

- Saving **replaces** a person's rooms rather than adding to them.
- Re-saving without changing anything writes nothing, so the record of who assigned a room and when is kept clean.
- A person can hold several rooms, at several centres.
- In a home-childcare agency, where the provider is also the room, the centre name is not repeated on the room.

## Planning room moves with Room rotations

1. Open **Administration** → **Room rotations**. The **🔄 Room rotation candidates** table lists children who reach the next age band within 60 days.
2. Read each row: the **Child**, their **Current age**, the age they reach and the date (**Reaches threshold**), **Days until** and the **Centre**.
3. Start with the red chips (14 days or less), then amber (30 days or less). Blue chips have more time.
4. Tell the receiving room, then move the child's room on their record when the day comes.

The list is for planning only: it does not move anyone or send any message. When nobody
is due to move, it says **No upcoming rotations.**

## Who can do what

- **Agency admins** can open both screens and change anyone's rooms.
- **Centre directors** can change room assignments in Room Controls. Room rotations is in the agency admin's **Administration** menu.
- **Educators** cannot change room assignments; they see the children of the rooms they are assigned to.

## FAQs and troubleshooting

??? An educator sees children from rooms they don't work in. | They have no room assigned, so screens fall back to the whole centre. Assign their rooms and press Save changes.
??? I can't tick any rooms. | Press ✏️ Edit assignments first. The screen is read-only until you do.
??? It says "No educators in this agency yet." | Add staff first. Only people with a staff role can be assigned to rooms.
??? Room rotations says "No upcoming rotations." but a child is about to move up. | The list uses each child's date of birth. Check that it is filled in on the child's record.
??? Does Room rotations move the child for me? | No. It only lists who is due. Move the child's room on their record.
