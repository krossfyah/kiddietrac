CWELCC Compliance Checklist — 2026

The 18-point CWELCC compliance checklist

Everything Ontario childcare operators need to stay audit-ready under the Canada-Wide Early Learning & Child Care program.

✅ Tip: tick each box as you confirm it. Re-run quarterly to stay inspection-ready.

🍁 Enrolment & Eligibility

Confirm each child's age category (infant, toddler, preschool) for the correct funding stream.
Verify eligibility and enrolment dates for the CWELCC base-fee reduction.
Keep a signed parent enrolment agreement on file for every child.
Track licensed capacity vs. actual enrolment for each room.

💰 Fees & Subsidy

Apply the correct CWELCC fee reduction to every eligible child.
Reconcile subsidy amounts against the current provincial rate table.
Maintain a transparent fee schedule (gross fee, reduction, net parent fee).
Document any fee changes with their effective dates.

✅ Attendance & Records

Record daily check-in/check-out times for every child.
Retain attendance and financial records for the required retention period.
Log staff-to-child ratios for each room throughout the day.

📊 Reporting & Submission

Prepare monthly/quarterly CWELCC funding reports.
Submit enrolment and attendance data by every ministry deadline.
Track allocation vs. actual spend of CWELCC funding.
Keep payroll & wage-enhancement (WEG) records aligned with funding.

🔒 Audit Readiness

Store all records securely with role-based access.
Run a quarterly self-audit against this checklist.
Keep digital backups of agreements, invoices and reports.

CWELCC subsidy math, submission reports and audit-ready records — generated for you automatically.

This checklist is a general guide and not legal advice. Always confirm current requirements with your ministry and licensing authority.

How we protect you

Security at every layer

All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2+ and encrypted at rest. Sensitive fields, such as two-factor secrets, are additionally encrypted at the application layer.

Your data is hosted in North American data centres and never leaves the continent — important for PIPEDA and provincial residency requirements.

Automated backups run twice daily with point-in-time recovery, so your records stay safe even in the rare event of an incident.

Two-factor authentication and granular, role-based permissions ensure staff only see what they need. Platform admins, agency admins, directors, educators and guardians each have scoped access.

Your data is always yours. Export to PDF, CSV or Excel anytime, and request deletion in line with our documented data-retention policy.

We monitor the platform around the clock and follow a documented incident-response process, including timely notification where required by law.
