Meet KiddieTrac

One place for your whole program

KiddieTrac is an all-in-one platform for childcare centres, home child care agencies and multi-site organizations. It brings the office, the classroom and families together.

  • Invoices, subsidies and payments that reconcile themselves.
  • Check-in, attendance and ratios in real time.
  • Observations, lesson plans and report cards your educators enjoy writing.
  • Parents who always know how their child’s day went.
