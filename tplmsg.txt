Document templates: a safe renderer plus a Blade importer

Printable documents (payslips, invoices) get a template store, so a layout can be brought
in from elsewhere rather than living hard-coded in a controller.

THE RENDERER EXECUTES NOTHING. It understands values, if/else and each, and no more. A
Blade file is PHP: rendering an uploaded one would hand anyone who reaches the import
screen code execution on the server. The importer TRANSLATES Blade into the safe subset
and reports what it could not translate, rather than passing anything through to an
evaluator.

Getting that translation right took four passes against iLearn's real payslip, and each
failure is worth recording:

  * Replacing a field inside a formatting call left nested braces and orphan fragments all
    through the output. Expressions are now taken WHOLE and their wrappers peeled, instead
    of substrings being swapped.
  * Arithmetic a template cannot perform - hours times rate, gross plus vacation - resolves
    to the named total we already supply. That is why regular_amount, ot_amount and
    gross_with_vacation exist on the payslip kind.
  * The fallback rule demanded ?? and so never matched a plain ?:, silently dropping the
    recipient, type and status lines.
  * A Blade condition holds raw PHP and nothing had rewritten it, so fragments of it stayed
    visible; and trimming parenthesis CHARACTERS mangled a cast into nonsense.

iLearn's payslip now converts with three notes - all of them things that genuinely cannot
carry over - and renders every field, including the overtime line KiddieTrac was missing.

An agency template beats the platform default; activating a default forks a copy, so one
agency's choice never changes another's. The active template cannot be deleted, because a
payslip already issued has to stay reproducible.
