iLearn payroll syncs into KiddieTrac, history and onward

iLearn runs the payroll; KiddieTrac had none of it. 67 staff payroll documents worth
$118,496.86 now sit in the ledger alongside the 62 reconstructed from punches, so an
educator opens one place to see what they were paid and an admin reads one ledger.

THE PAY PERIOD IS THE RECORD, not the invoice. iLearn raises a provider/contractor
invoice for a pay period that already exists - 51 of 55 carry a record_no pointing
straight at one, categorised "Provider Payroll". Importing both would have booked $213k
against a real payroll of $112k. An invoice that resolves to a pay period contributes
only its number to that payslip; only the four whose pay periods were later soft-deleted
are stored in their own right.

Deductions come across as recorded - CPP, EI, income tax, vacation - and the payslip PDF
shows the breakdown and net where a payroll was actually run, staying gross-only for the
reconstructed rows where no such calculation exists.

Payees are matched on EMAIL, never name: iLearn's names are free text and already carry
"Cassandra"/"Cassanda" for one person. A match also requires an active role in the
receiving agency, so an email resolving to somebody in another agency is left unlinked
rather than filed across the boundary. Where a record has no email at all, an exact and
unique name match within the agency is accepted; an ambiguous name links nobody. 15
documents stay unlinked because those payees have no KiddieTrac account - their history
is still in the admin ledger, because the agency's payroll has to be complete.

Being a parent says nothing about how somebody is paid: where the only KiddieTrac role is
guardian, iLearn's payroll designation wins, so a provider's payslip no longer reads
"Parent" on the wrong payroll.

Ongoing sync is the established pattern: a PayPeriod observer pushes on save (deferred,
best-effort, never blocking the payroll save), the invoice observer now routes staff
invoices to payroll instead of dropping them at its parent_id check - which is precisely
why nothing ever crossed - and kiddietrac:sync-payroll reconciles nightly at 02:40. Every
push is an idempotent upsert on external_key.
